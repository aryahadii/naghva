# Definitions

Naghva covers music that is both Iranian and independent. Nothing else
decides it: not genre, language, period, popularity or anyone's view of
the music.

Scope is judged release by release, since an artist can move between
independent and mainstream work. An artist belongs in Naghva when some of
their work is in scope.

## Iranian – ایرانی

Made in Iran, or by Iranians anywhere, including the diaspora, in any
language.

## Independent – مستقل

Released by the artist, or by a label, platform or collective that is
neither a major label nor state-affiliated.

Evidence comes from sources: one calling the release, or the act at that
time, independent (مستقل), or a release fact that shows who published it.
