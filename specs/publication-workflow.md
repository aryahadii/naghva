# Publication workflow

A valid record is not yet a good entry. These gates apply to every new entry,
enrichment and correction; the other specs set what may be claimed and how it
is written.

## Coverage

Before an entry is published, check:

- **Listening.** Every platform in the
  [listen link policy](data-policies.md#listen-links), linking the exact
  release or track, not only the artist's profile.
- **Images.** An artist photograph, and cover art for every included release.
- **Connections.** Memberships, collaborators, performers, contributors,
  labels and events, linked to existing items rather than duplicated.
- **Catalogue.** Official catalogues and independent sources, compared with
  what is already captured. A partial catalogue says where it stops and why.
- **Life.** Interviews, programmes and reporting, read for the artist's story,
  with its moments kept as [life events](data-framework.md#life-events).
- **Interviews.** A long interview with the artist is heard in full, not read
  from its description or automatic captions. It is transcribed by two
  independent speech-to-text models, and only passages they agree on are
  captured.
- **Dates.** When sources date an event differently, look for the earliest
  report before choosing.
- **Provenance.** Every captured passage says where in the source it is and
  when it was read, and every web source gets an archive link.

A search that finds nothing is recorded as a search, not as proof that nothing
exists.

## Review

Someone other than the author checks the source behind every claim, looks for
what is missing, reads both languages against
[Artist writing](artist-writing.md), and looks at the built pages: images,
links, credits and relationships as readers will see them. A video description
or search snippet is not an interview, and automatic captions are checked
against the audio before they carry a claim.

## Acceptance

The owner sees the Persian and English pages, what changed and where coverage
stops, and decides. Passing checks or a positive review is not acceptance.
Nothing is merged or released without the owner's word.
