# Design

## Identity

Naghva has an Iranian identity. Its look comes from Iranian miniature painting
(نگارگری مینیاتوری ایرانی): painted scenes, illumination and ornament. It is
not minimalist; minimalism is not very Iranian.

The look is dark, like a garden at night: soft fades and blends rather than
hard borders, cohesive colours and smooth edges.

The idea behind the look is a mural of Iranian independent music
(دیوارنگارهٔ موسیقی مستقل ایرانی). It sets the mood and is never used as a
title or caption.

## Pages

Each page is in one language, English or Persian, as the [vision](vision.md)
treats both equally. Names appear in both scripts wherever a source gives them.
Every page works on mobile and desktop.

- **Homepage.** Search first, in the night sky of a painted garden, then the
  latest releases.
- **Artist page.** An encyclopedia entry for one act or person, made for easy
  reading: the article, Recordings and Events as rows in date order, and
  Sources. An outline lets readers go straight to the part they want.
  Miniature art keeps the mood at the edges without drawing attention.
- **Release.** A panel on its artist's page, with its own address, rather than
  a page of its own.
- **Timeline.** Everything Naghva records, from the earliest to now: releases,
  events, formations, members joining and leaving, and life events.
- **Artists, Search and About.** The list of artists, a search page, and what
  Naghva is.
