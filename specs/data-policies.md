# Data policies

## Reliability

- Every published claim is tied to a source and a verbatim quote from it.
  Editorial display spellings are the one exception, under Names below.
  When in doubt, leave it out.
- Cite only sources actually opened and read, never a URL or quote from
  memory.
- Keep the source's precision: a year stays a year, circa stays circa, and
  a date keeps its source's calendar.

## Neutrality

- Record what sources say. No ranking, reviewing or taste, in the data or
  the articles.
- When sources disagree, keep each version with its source.

## Sources

- Preference: the artist's or label's own pages; discographies (Discogs,
  MusicBrainz); reputable journalism and interviews; academic work;
  Wikipedia, as a secondary source whose citations come first.
- A person's account of their own career is a primary source.
- Don't cite AI-generated encyclopedias, content farms, lyrics sites,
  uncited fan wikis, third-party social posts or forums.
- Sources may be in any language.

## Names

- Persian and English are equal. Use an attested spelling in each
  language where one exists, and reuse it across records and articles.
- When no Persian form is attested, a clear Persian display spelling of a
  name or title may stand beside the sourced original. It is marked as
  editorial, and the original keeps its reference. When identity or
  pronunciation is unclear, keep the original alone.
- Never invent aliases, legal names or identities from a transliteration
  or a translated title.

## People

- What a source has already published about a person may be recorded,
  including a legal name, birth date or birthplace.
- Anything about a person comes down when that person asks.

## Listen links

- Official uploads by the artist or label, or by the organizer of the
  documented event, on YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, Apple Music
  or Telegram. Verify the account, the artist and the exact recording or
  version. Fan reposts and namesake accounts don't qualify.

## Images

- Only images that the artist, their label or the event's organizer
  published, or that carry a free licence.
- Shown whole, as published: never cropped, retouched, upscaled or
  generated.
- Credited to whoever made them when the source says, otherwise to where
  they were published.
- Taken down when the person who made the image asks.

## Reuse

- The record is CC0 1.0. Articles are CC BY 4.0.
- Quotes and images belong to their authors and are covered by neither.
