# Naghva vision

Naghva exists to preserve and make sense of the history of independent Iranian
music. What counts as independent and as Iranian is set out in
[definitions.md](definitions.md).

What it preserves is the record: who made what, with whom, when, how, why, and
whatever else the sources support. Every fact backed by its source, with links
out to where the music can be heard. It brings scattered evidence about
artists, works, scenes, and relationships into that record, equally in Persian
and English, for listeners in Iran and abroad, researchers, journalists, and
the artists themselves to explore and return to. It spans every period, from
the earliest roots to music being made today, without pretending the history
is complete.

Naghva records what sources say. It does not rank, review, or carry anyone’s
taste.
