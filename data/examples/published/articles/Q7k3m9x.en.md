Badnama is a band from Tehran. A Persian magazine interview says it formed
in 1383 of the Solar Hijri calendar;[^formed] an English-language roundup of
the city's rock scene says 2005.[^roundup]

## Nameless Street

Nima Rad has played guitar in the band since 1383.[^guitar] Its album
Nameless Street was released in November 2012 by Meh Records.[^album]
