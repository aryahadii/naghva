Sanam Pasha is an Iranian singer, composer and vocal teacher working in rock and metal. Born in Tehran in 1978, she learned Iranian music and classical piano before turning to rock. She has sung with 5grs and formed an all-female band bearing her own name in the early 2010s.[^early][^5grs][^band]

## Learning and teaching

Pasha grew up learning Persian folk music, then studied piano, singing and solfeggio.[^early] She later taught blues, rock and metal singing. In 2008 she began a vocal competition to give singers, particularly women, more opportunities to perform.[^teaching]

## A band of her own

After years of singing backing vocals in other groups, Pasha wanted to perform her own music. She began working with electric guitarist Bita Sadeghi, and they formed the band Sanam Pasha.[^backing][^start][^guitar][^own] She has also sung with the metal band 5grs, performing at Arm Fest in Yerevan and the Persian Metal Festival.[^5grs]

On 25 August 2018 her all-female band gave its first concert at Niavaran Cultural Centre in Tehran, for an audience of women.[^show][^women]

## Later work

In 2020 Pasha introduced Barahoot, a new musical project, with “[Day and Night](#day-and-night-single-scs),” setting a poem by Forough Farrokhzad.[^barahoot][^forough]

In 2025 Pasha sang on composer Orod Anzabipoor’s jazz-fusion album *[Incidents](#incidents-feat-sanam-pasha-xbr)*.[^incidents-vocal][^incidents-composer][^jazz][^incidents-date]
