Sanam Pasha is an Iranian singer, composer, songwriter, pianist and vocal coach specialising in rock and metal. In her RSL Awards profile she says she was born in Tehran on 16 July 1978 and began learning Persian folk music as a child before studying classical piano, singing and solfeggio.[^identity][^early]

## Bands and performance

Pasha says she founded an all-female rock band bearing her name in 2012.[^band] In the same profile she describes her work as lead vocalist with 5grs and performances at Arm Fest in Yerevan and the Persian Metal Festival.[^5grs]

## Teaching and recordings

She describes teaching blues, rock and metal vocals and initiating a vocal contest in 2008 to create opportunities, particularly for women.[^teaching] Apple Music lists her as a featured artist on Orod Anzabipoor’s Incidents.[^incidents]
