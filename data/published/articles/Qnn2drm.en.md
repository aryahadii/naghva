Pouya Ehsaei is an Iranian electronic musician, producer and sound designer who began making music in Tehran’s underground scene. In London he leads the band Ariwo and organises Parasang, a club night for live electronic music.[^bio][^beginnings][^ariwo]

## Playing live

Ehsaei recorded and produced two studio albums with Ariwo. For his own album [*RocRast*](#rocrast-jtt), released in 2020, he worked differently. He played live using hardware built around a modular synthesiser and kept the recordings close to the performances, with little editing afterwards.[^studio][^date][^album][^composition][^process]

During the lockdowns of 2020 and 2021, Ehsaei developed a way for musicians, dancers and visual artists in different places to perform together in real time. He produced twelve such shows for Parasang and streamed them online.[^realtime][^streams] In another project, *From the Lips to the Moon*, he and the writer and performer Tara Fatehi bring poets, musicians and visual artists together for live shows.[^moon][^tara]
