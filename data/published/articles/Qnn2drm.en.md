Pouya Ehsaei is an Iranian musician, sound designer, producer and curator based in London. He leads Ariwo and runs the electronic and live music night Parasang.[^bio][^ariwo]

## Collaboration and RocRast

Ehsaei also founded and co-organises *From the Lips to the Moon*, a collaborative project bringing together music, poetry and live visuals.[^moon]

Zabte Sote released *[RocRast](#rocrast-jtt)* on 30 October 2020, with music composed by Ehsaei. Its tracks were recorded from live performances using an all-hardware setup centred on a modular synthesiser. The release description specifies minimal editing and post-production.[^album][^date][^composition][^process]
