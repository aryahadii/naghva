Dastgâmachine is a Toronto trio that arranges melodies from Iranian classical music using techniques from dub and techno. Mahmood Schricker plays electric setar, Caleb Klager handles electronics, and Jason Rule plays turntables. The three gave their first concert in August 2023, before the group had a name.[^members][^music][^first]

## The nights at Tapestry

At their monthly Infinite Jest residency at Tapestry, a venue in Toronto’s Kensington Market, the trio recorded their sets in stereo. Those recordings became the four tracks of [*Tapestry*](#dastgamachine-tapestry-jtv), released in January 2025. Mani Bayat mixed and produced “Dar Sobh.” The others went straight from the live recordings to mastering. For “Shahnaz,” Schricker followed the order of melodies in an improvisation by the Iranian tar player Jalil Shahnaz and played them on setar with the group.[^monthly][^album][^mixed][^dar][^shahnaz][^melody]

## Returning to the first concert

On the January 2026 EP [*Bâzsâzi*](#dastgamachine-bazsazi-9p4) the trio included an excerpt of “Zolfonoon” from their first concert, with words and vocals by Sina Salimi. Klager, under his Wavemap name, remixed the same recording. At the release concert Schricker and Rule performed as a duo.[^edition][^raw][^first][^sina][^alias][^duo][^pair]

The group played Toronto’s Wavelength festival in March. Its August single “Araq o Anar” comes from that concert. The piece takes its melody from Delkash, part of the classical Iranian mode of Mahur, and combines it with sounds from modular electronics.[^gig][^live][^delkash][^araq]
