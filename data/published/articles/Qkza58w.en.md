Kourosh Yaghmaei is a singer and composer. In the final year of his sociology studies, his first professional work, “Gole Yakh,” brought together his composing, orchestration, playing and singing.[^first-work]

## Regional melodies at orchestral scale

Yaghmaei says his voice was barred from broadcast for 17 years beginning in 1358 SH.[^ban] During that period, [*Diar*](#دیار-5pm) presented Iranian regional melodies as an instrumental orchestration.[^diar-bio] The album brings together ten regional melodies arranged for a modern large orchestra.[^diar-detail]

After that seventeen-year period, [*Sib-e Noghreii*](#سیب-نقره-ای-ftf) was his first vocal album.[^first-vocal] “Raz-e Del” is a nine-part set: six vocal pieces, two chaharmozrab and one tasnif, arranged for large and electronic orchestras, chorus and traditional Iranian instruments.[^raz]

## The long studio passage

Yaghmaei says he began work on [*Malek Jamshid*](#malek-jamshid-q7e) nearly fourteen years before his account and spent close to three years on it.[^malek-start][^malek-duration] The recording dates from 2003–2006, and the album was released on June 10, 2016.[^malek-recording][^malek-release]

[*Rebel*](#rebel-qx3) was released worldwide on February 9, 2024, and has twelve songs.[^rebel-date][^rebel-product] The two-track [*Rebel - Single*](#rebel-single-vsd) is a separate product. Yaghmaei’s official channel calls its title track the album’s second single.[^rebel-single]

## Track-specific credits

Several credits name individual tracks: Kamil Yaghmaei is credited for piano or keyboard on “مهرورزان” from [*Maho Palang*](#ماه-و-پلنگ-ce4); Fereydoun Foroughi is named as singer and composer on [Sol 2](#سل-2-nq2)’s “قوزک پا”; the [*Arayesh-e Khorshid*](#آرایش-خورشید-n7m) page credits [Kaveh Yaghmaei](/en/kaveh-yaghmaei-cba/) on keyboard and Satkin Yaghmaei in the chorus for “سر کوی دوست” and “کلات”; and a Telegram caption credits Kambiz Yaghmaei on keyboard and bass for “مستُم مستُم و اسمر اسمر.”[^maho-track][^sol2-track][^arayesh-track][^telegram-track-name][^telegram-track-credit] These credits apply to the named tracks.

## Caltex catalog dispute

In a public statement, Yaghmaei says Caltex retitled an album he calls “Kourosh Yaghmaei’s New Concert” as [*Parandehe Mohajer*](#parandehe-mohajer-s48) and removed two instrumental tracks. He also contested the distribution of “Gole Yakh.”[^caltex-dispute]
