Ensemble Mehr brought seven Iranian women to Venice in September 2007 for the eight-hundredth anniversary of Rumi’s birth. Composer and artistic director Azar Zargarian played tar. Her group surrounded singer Mehrbanu with kamancheh, qanun, barbat, tanbur and percussion.[^mehr][^director][^date][^players][^lineup][^rumiyear]

The programme at Centro Culturale Candiani opened in Mahur, alternating instrumental passages with poetry by Rumi and Attar. The second half moved into Bayat-e Tork. In “Tasnife Ghadah,” Mehrbanu sang Hafez. The presenter filmed that performance, along with “Pardey-e Nou,” “Janam Janam” and “Hilat Raha Kon.”[^mahur][^bayat][^attar][^rumi][^hafez][^ghadah][^fkplmbltsuva][^fsh6rg9rzhbm][^foqxbzolmh1c]

The ensemble toured Italy again in May 2008. Its presenter photographed the musicians onstage in Bologna on 17 May and at Florence’s Teatro Puccini a week later. Kamancheh player Parisa Kashefi appears alongside Zargarian and Mehrbanu in the photographs from this tour.[^bologna][^florence][^year][^cast][^inst]
