Jilliz Williz is an alternative and fusion band from Tehran. Singer and guitarist [Arian Shariat](/en/rian-vhg/) and trombonist Sepehr Abbasi started the group. Trumpeter Mahan Mohammadi, bassist Navid Ghaleb and drummer Saleh Khan Mohammadi joined before the band formally began playing in 2024–25.[^origins][^lineup][^place]

## Horns and guitar

Abbasi came to the band from classical and orchestral music. Mohammadi had learned the dotar of southern Khorasan, setar and tombak as well as trumpet. Ghaleb had played live with indie bands.[^sepehr][^mahan][^navid]

The five musicians arranged their first single, “[Jaye To Mimiram](#jaye-to-mimiram-nwr),” with trumpet and trombone alongside electric guitar, bass and drums. Shariat wrote the words, sang and played the guitar.[^first][^jaye] On “[Iran Khube](#iran-khube-y2t)” the group kept that combination, with Shariat also playing keyboards and acoustic guitar.[^iran] They also rearranged older songs, including “[Amad Nowbahar](#آمد-نوبهار-txc)” and “Raftam.”[^amad][^raftam]
