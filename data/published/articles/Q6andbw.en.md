Jilliz Williz is an alternative/fusion group formed in Tehran around [Arian Shariat](/en/rian-vhg/), its guitarist and singer, and trombonist Sepehr Abbasi. Trumpeter Mahan Mohammadi, bassist Navid Ghaleb and drummer Saleh Khan Mohammadi joined before the group began its formal activity in 1403 (2024–25).[^origins][^lineup][^place]

The members’ backgrounds span several musical settings. Abbasi studied trombone and worked in classical and orchestral music; Mohammadi learned southern-Khorasan dotar, setar and tombak alongside trumpet. Ghaleb gained live-performance experience in indie bands before Jilliz Williz.[^sepehr][^mahan][^navid]

## Songs and arrangements

[Jaye To Mimiram](#jaye-to-mimiram-nwr) was the group’s first single.[^first] Alongside it, [Iran Khube](#iran-khube-y2t) and [Taarif Kon](#taarif-kon-9ym) bring trumpet and trombone together with guitar, bass and drums. Arian Shariat wrote the lyrics and sings all three; on *Iran Khube* he also plays keyboards and acoustic guitar. The band arranged each track.[^jaye][^iran][^taarif]

The band’s Telegram channel also shared rearrangements of [*Amad Nowbahar*](#آمد-نوبهار-txc) and *Raftam*, alongside a *Qamari Joon* clip captioned as unreleased.[^amad][^raftam][^qamari]
