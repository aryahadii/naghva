Haleh Seyfizadeh (born 1984) is an Iranian singer, songwriter and improviser who began performing with the composer and tar player Ali Ghamsari in 2000.[^identity][^birth][^start][^ghamsari-role][^ghamsari-composer] Her work includes Iranian classical music, music theatre and collaborations with the Nederlands Blazers Ensemble. She has also written melodies for her own recordings.[^early-work][^nbe-works][^shahr]

## Early music and Ali Ghamsari

Haleh first learned the tombak, an Iranian goblet drum.[^instrument] She began performing with Ghamsari at sixteen and went on to sing for documentary films and theatre in Iran. She later performed abroad with the Yade-Doost women's ensemble.[^birth][^early-work]

With Ghamsari, she moved gradually from singing alongside other voices to passages for a solo voice. In a 2018 interview, she said that she valued their continuing partnership because he knew her vocal range and she understood his way of composing. She preferred parts written specifically for a woman's voice to simply joining the other singers.[^solo][^partnership][^vocal-lines][^date]

## Shatt-e Ranj

In 2018 Haleh sang and acted in Ghamsari's concert theatre production *Shatt-e Ranj*. She had sung in play readings before, but had no acting experience. The movement on stage was difficult at first; she came to welcome it and felt that singers needed to understand acting and their own bodies.[^acting][^instrument][^movement][^date] Some moments developed through improvisation with the actress Sara Rasoulzadeh, including an exchange of looks that became part of their performance.[^improvise]

## With the Nederlands Blazers Ensemble

In 2017 the Nederlands Blazers Ensemble invited Haleh and Ghamsari to Amsterdam's Turning East festival for a programme combining Bach with Persian music. The announced programme paired them with the soprano Elisabeth Hetherington and theorbo player David Mackor, and included connecting music by Mathilde Wantenaar.[^festival][^festival-date][^programme][^nbe-music]

Haleh and Ghamsari continued with *Bach & Sufi* in 2018–19 and *Stories from the East* in 2023.[^nbe-works] The live album [*Bach & Sufi*](#bach-sufi-live-d75) includes her singing in Ghamsari's works and in Bach's “Agnus Dei.”[^bach-ghamsari][^bach-tracks] In February 2025 the ensemble announced them as guest soloists for *In Hogere Sferen – Bach, Soefi & Pärt*, adding Arvo Pärt's music to the programme.[^nbe-2025][^nbe-guests]

## Composing and new collaborations

In spring 2020 Haleh recorded [“Shahr Khalist”](#shahr-khalist-live-vqe) with an ensemble. She wrote its melody and sang words by Alireza Koliai, with an arrangement by Amir Mohammad Nazar.[^shahr] For [“Khaneh Koo”](#khaneh-koo-feat-aeen-ahmadifar-rouzbeh-esfandarmaz-single-tzx), a memorial to the passengers of Ukraine International Airlines Flight 752, she set a poem by Qeysar Aminpour to music. Aeen Ahmadifar arranged it, and Rouzbeh Esfandarmaz played clarinet.[^khaneh]

She developed *Hidden Songs* with the guitarist [Golfam Khayam](/en/golfam-khayam-ran/). Haleh described the music as entirely improvised after a year of isolation during lockdown, using poetry from Saadi to Nosrat Rahmani.[^hidden-duo][^hidden] She later collaborated with [Kābin](/en/kabin-fwf/) on the EP [*Mikhahamat*](/en/kabin-fwf/#mikhahamat-an4).[^kabin]
