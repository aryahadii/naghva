Sheen Trio takes its name from ش, the first letter of clarinetist Shabnam Parvaresh’s name. She dates the group’s founding in Osnabrück to 2020. Ula Martyn-Ellis plays electric guitar and Philipp Buck plays drums. The trio has no bass player. Parvaresh can use her bass clarinet to lay down a repeating bass line, then hand the pulse to one of the others and take up a melody.[^name][^year][^players][^pitch]

[*Gozar*](#gozar-5qh), their 2023 debut on Berthold Records, opens with “5 Days 8 Hours and 35 Minutes.” That was how long Parvaresh remembered waiting for music to download in Tehran in the 1990s. When the electricity failed, the wait began again. The closing piece, “Pendulum,” follows another unreliable timekeeper. Her grandmother’s clock would go out of time, and Parvaresh wrote the piece in five beats to catch its irregular swing.[^gozar][^internet][^clock]

The Swiss label Unit Records issued the trio’s second album, [*Transitory*](#transitory-nvt), in January 2026. On “Maroggia,” Parvaresh evoked the misty Iranian valley where her grandfather had lived. The trio brought the music of *Transitory* to Jazzfest Berlin that year.[^date][^mountain][^berlin]
