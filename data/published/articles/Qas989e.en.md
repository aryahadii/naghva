Sussan Deyhim is a composer, vocalist and performance artist.[^profile] She says her view was shaped between two worlds. As a child, she joined Pars National Ballet, whose work brought contemporary classical choreography together with Iranian folkloric dance and music. At the Shiraz Arts Festival, she encountered experimental international work alongside indigenous forms.[^ballet-start][^ballet][^festival]

## The House Is Black

In *The House Is Black*, a multimedia stage work inspired by Forough Farrokhzad’s writing, Deyhim and Richard Horowitz co-composed the music.[^house] Deyhim describes a process that starts with the poem: she develops vocal melodies first, then builds rhythms, textures and grooves around its nuances. In sessions with musicians, she revises material that feels too literal, sometimes moving it toward more abstract or electronic sounds. She and Horowitz also exchange sounds and groupings, while leaving room for silence and atmosphere.[^process-poem][^process-revision][^process-exchange][^process-silence]

## Collaboration across recordings and images

The recordings gathered on [*The Invisible Road: Original Recordings, 1985–1990*](#the-invisible-road-original-recordings-1985-1990-h6n) are credited to Deyhim and Horowitz, with all compositions attributed to both.[^invisible-title][^invisible-compositions] On [*Majoun*](#majoun-p6q), Deyhim is billed as vocalist and Horowitz as composer; she later called it mostly his album, on which she sang.[^majoun][^majoun-voice]

When a remix of her [*Madman of God*](#madman-of-god-fcw) was planned, Deyhim asked to work with Bill Laswell. He felt the album did not need remixing, but he and Karsh Kale came up with ideas instead; [*Shy Angels*](#shy-angels-s7m) became Laswell’s reinterpretation of the full album.[^madman-title][^madman-laswell][^shy-angels]

Her sound also reached Shirin Neshat’s video installations: [*Soliloquy*](#soliloquy-axk) is music and sound design for Neshat’s work, while [*Rapture*](#rapture-7gd) gathers Deyhim’s scores and sound design, with music composed alongside Horowitz and inspired by Attar’s *Conference of the Birds*.[^soliloquy][^rapture][^rapture-composition]

On [*Songs from UR*](#songs-from-ur-2ta), Deyhim and Torsten Rasch share composition, with Deyhim credited for vocals and drones.[^songs-composition][^songs-vocals] For the [*Once Within A Time*](#once-within-a-time-original-motion-picture-soundtrack-dp3) soundtrack, Deyhim and Philip Glass share album billing, with Deyhim credited for additional vocals and sonic collaborations. On “Clarion Call,” both have a specific composer credit.[^once-billing][^once-contribution][^once-clarion]
