Arash Akbari, also known as Odepth, is a musician, producer and new media artist described in 2014 as based in Tehran. His work combines electric guitar, acoustic sounds, field recordings and digital synthesis across ambient, drone, noise and experimental music.[^bio] He also makes still and generative visual art and interactive audiovisual installations.[^visuals]

## Cracked Echoes

[Cracked Echoes](#cracked-echoes-sbk) is offered on his own Bandcamp page.[^album] The page gives the release date as November 9, 2014. Akbari composed, performed and produced the music and created the artwork. Lawrence English mastered it at Room40, and Soft Recordings published it.[^credits]
