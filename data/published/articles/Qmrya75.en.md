Arash Akbari, also known as Odepth, is a musician, producer and visual artist who was working in Tehran in the 2010s. He makes ambient music with electric guitar, field recordings and synthesized sounds. He also makes interactive installations in which sound and images share a space.[^bio][^visuals]

## From recorded sound to data

In 2014 Akbari composed, performed and produced [*Cracked Echoes*](#cracked-echoes-sbk). He made the artwork himself. Soft Recordings released the album after Lawrence English mastered it.[^album][^credits]

For [*Amnestic Continuum*](#amnestic-continuum-xgh) in 2022, Akbari turned numerical data into sound. He also used the data to generate sculptures. Chris Doherty-Ingram responded in verse. Akbari wanted listeners to think about what the numbers represented and the lives behind them.[^year][^sounds][^sculpture][^poems][^purpose]
