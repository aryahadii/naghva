Angband began in 2004 as guitarist, songwriter and lyricist Mahyar Dean's instrumental solo project. In a 2020 interview he described it becoming a band with the addition of drummer Ramin and singer Ashkan.[^origin]

## IV

The 2020 album *IV* features Tim Aymar on vocals, Dean on guitar and bass, and Ramin Rahimi on drums. Pure Underground Records released the CD edition on 24 July 2020.[^lineup][^record]

Dean also mixed and mastered the album. In the interview, he related this work to his decision to develop his production skills after the band's earlier records.[^production]
