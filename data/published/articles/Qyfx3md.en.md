Angband is an Iranian progressive metal band that guitarist Mahyar Dean started in 2004 as an instrumental solo project. It became a group when drummer Ramin Rahimi and singer Ashkan joined him. An exchange with Pure Steel on Myspace led to a recording contract.[^origin][^lineup][^genre][^demo]

## Finding a label

In 2007 Dean sent the demo to record labels. After a message arrived from Pure Steel through Myspace, he sent the label the band’s music and they signed with it.[^demo] The following year they released [*Rising from Apadana*](#rising-from-apadana-2tt).[^apadana][^debut-date]

In 2017 Dean asked the metal singer Tim Aymar to join Angband on vocals. The album took three years to finish. On [*IV*](#iv-5j6), Dean played guitar and bass and Rahimi played drums. Dean also mixed and mastered the record himself. Dissatisfied with the sound of earlier albums, he had been working on his production skills since 2015.[^invited][^lineup][^record][^production] One track, “Mirage,” took its verse from a translation of a poem by Rumi.[^rumi]
