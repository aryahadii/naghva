Cheshmak is a Tehran trio of keyboard player Kaveh Ghaffari, percussionist Farhad Asadi and bassist Arif Mirbaghi. A Toronto recording session on a snowy December afternoon gives a sense of their repertoire. They played “Shushtar Reng” alongside an instrumental called “Kaveh’s Tune,” and the label Local Dish cut the music straight from tape to a seven-inch record.[^group][^keys][^drum][^bass][^snow][^vinyl][^reng][^tune]

## The Tehran recordings

During the pandemic the three played with singer [Neda Mohamadpour](/en/neda-mohamadpour-bb4/) in Tehran. They also recorded under their individual names. The first volume of *Nostalgie* came together in several locations during lockdowns in 2021, with guests including clarinetist Mehrpouya Daneshvar. Violinist Aso Kohzadi joined them on electric violin for the second volume in 2022. In August 2024 they issued a third. That October they released five tracks as Cheshmak on the EP *Avalin Cheshmak*, opening with “Boro Keh Berim.”[^neda][^series][^line][^clar][^two][^violin][^three][^first][^one]

In December 2024 the trio presented a workshop at Zameen Art House on the successes and setbacks of making independent music in Iran. The players followed the workshop with a concert called *Boro Ke Berim*. In August 2025 the Dami Jabren series released a film of the band playing at Abad Art House on Qeshm Island. The following month they played at SaBar in Berlin.[^zdate][^zshow][^show][^iran][^iranfilm][^berlin][^concertdate]

## Toronto sessions

Local Dish released the trio’s seven-inch in January 2025. A day later it issued another record on which Mohamadpour rejoined them, this time to sing one Turkish and one Afghan song. The same year they played a studio set for the Persian-language programme *Gooshgir*, with Ghaffari on synthesizer, Asadi on drums and Mirbaghi on electric bass.[^epdate][^duedate][^duet][^goosh]

When Canadian singer and guitarist Friendly Rich heard the group had three days free during a tour, he booked a Toronto studio. They recorded his songs in June 2025 and produced the album together. On “One by One,” Ghaffari’s organ and accordion play against hand drums. On “Memorial Bench,” Mirbaghi plays a walking bass line. The album, [*A Bruised Sunset*](#a-bruised-sunset-n95), came out in May 2026.[^break][^session][^written][^joint][^song][^bassline][^issued]
