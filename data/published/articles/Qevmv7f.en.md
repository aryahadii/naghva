Jamin is an Iranian recording artist and producer. He has released work under his own name and made records with rapper Justina, working with her in Tbilisi and later performing with her in northern Sweden.[^iran][^sard][^jam][^fest][^prod]

## Recordings and production

Jamin released a remix of *Sard* in 2019 and the R&B single *Deviation* the following year. In October 2019 he mixed and arranged the electronics for a BBC Persian performance recorded in Tbilisi.[^bbc][^session] In 2020 he played from Tbilisi for Justina’s streamed concert with Diaspora Arts Connection. His partnership with her extended to the studio. He produced her song *Darim Miaym*, released in October 2021.[^sard][^deviation][^jam][^prod][^pdate]

For *Chashm*, Jamin drew on the melody of *Baba Karam* in the Persian mode of chahargah. Justina wrote the words.[^melody][^words]

## Playing in Sweden

Jamin and Justina took their band on a ten-day tour of schools in Norrbotten, playing for teenagers across the region. His recordings during this period included *Residim Tehran* with Koosh in 2024. In May 2025 he released *Noosh* with Justina, and in June the two took the stage at the Festspelen music festival in Piteå.[^tour][^guests][^tehran][^noosh][^fest][^year]
