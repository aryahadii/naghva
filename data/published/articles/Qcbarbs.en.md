Kaveh Yaghmaei is an Iranian rock and pop-rock singer, guitarist and composer, and the son of the musician [Kourosh Yaghmaei](/en/kourosh-yaghmaei-kza/). He began releasing solo albums in the early 2000s, lived in Vancouver and later returned to Iran. His albums include [*Scarecrow*](#scarecrow-w6g), [*Sokoote Sard (Cold Silence)*](#sokoote-sard-cold-silence-myq) and [*Manshour*](#manshour-x6t).[^profile][^return][^first][^manshour]

## Musical training

Yaghmaei grew up in a family of musicians and began studying piano. After the revolution, the conservatory closed. When he returned to music school, there was no piano teacher, so he chose flute as his principal instrument and later continued with guitar.[^family][^learning]

## Scarecrow and Cold Silence

Yaghmaei released [*Scarecrow*](#scarecrow-w6g) in the early 2000s. He worked with the lyricist Mazda Shahani, his sister Satgin and brother Camil, and other musicians. He later recalled that obtaining permission to release the album had been difficult.[^first][^metrasek]

For [*Sokoote Sard (Cold Silence)*](#sokoote-sard-cold-silence-myq), he composed the music and worked mainly with the lyricist Roozbeh Bemani. He described support from the Taraneh record company and freedom from the stringent censorship he had faced as important differences from his previous album: he could make the music he wanted.[^cold][^freedom] Choosing words was part of that process. A lyric had to affect him and move him to compose.[^lyrics]

## Return to Iran

After living in Vancouver, Yaghmaei returned to Iran to continue working there.[^return] Difficulties in obtaining permission delayed his next album, [*Manshour*](#manshour-x6t), which was released in Tehran in 2016, nine years after his previous album.[^delay][^profile][^manshour]

He composed its songs and recorded them in Canada, playing electric and acoustic guitar and keyboards himself.[^recording] His collaboration with Bemani continued, and he worked with the lyricists Hossein Ghiasi and Niloofar Farzandshad.[^manshour]

In 2021 he released [*Adamhaye Shabzadeh*](#adamhaye-shabzadeh-3bk).[^night]

In 2026 he released [“Taghat Biar”](#taghat-biar-single-r98), composing, singing and playing guitar. Farzandshad played keyboards, with Shane Gaalaas on drums and Pouya Khani on bass; Javad Mahdavi wrote the lyrics.[^taghat-date][^taghat-self][^taghat-keys][^taghat-drums][^taghat-bass][^taghat-lyrics]
