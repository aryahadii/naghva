Kaveh Yaghmaei is an Iranian singer, instrumentalist and composer working in rock and pop rock.[^profile]

## From Scarecrow to Manshour

His recorded catalogue includes [Scarecrow](#scarecrow-w6g), [Sokoote Sard (Cold Silence)](#sokoote-sard-cold-silence-myq) and [Manshour](#manshour-x6t).[^first][^cold][^manshour] Reporting on Manshour's release in Tehran, VOA described it as his third album, following a nine-year interval. The report credits Yaghmaei with composing the album's songs and playing electric and acoustic guitars and keyboards during recording in Canada.[^context][^recording]

Manshour's nine songs draw on rock and pop rock.[^profile] Apple's catalogue dates the album to 2016 and also carries [Adamhaye Shabzadeh](#adamhaye-shabzadeh-3bk) in 2021.[^manshour][^night] Catalogue dates are kept at their stated precision and are not used to infer a complete release history.
