Yas is the performing name of Yaser Bakhtiari, an Iranian rapper born in 1982.[^identity][^fa-born] He is counted among the founders of Persian rap, and in 2013 he became the first Iranian rapper allowed by the Islamic Republic to perform legally in Iran.[^wk-first] His songs have dealt with disasters, scandals and official neglect in Iran, and he is one of the few Persian rappers who do not swear in their songs.[^wk-bam][^wk-cd][^wk-azchi][^wk-swear] He has worked with the American rapper Tech N9ne and, in his later songs, with the producer Farbod Arsh.[^unity][^beem][^baaq]

## Early life

Bakhtiari spent his childhood in the Naser Khosrow, Ekbatan and Shamshiri neighbourhoods of Tehran.[^fa-childhood] His father introduced him to rap by bringing back Tupac CDs from trips to Germany.[^father] He has said he was shaped as a young man by those albums and by classical Persian poetry, especially Rumi.[^wk-influence] His father died when he was eighteen. As the eldest child, he left a university course in civil engineering unfinished and went to work to support the family, at his uncle’s trading company and as a driver taking passengers in a Paykan.[^fa-father] In a 2006 interview he described working from morning until six in the evening, then at a second job, and trying to find time and money for the studio in between.[^work]

## *Bam* and *CD Ro Beshkan*

He began his career after the 2003 Bam earthquake, which led him to write his first well-known song, “[Bam](#bam-3fz),” composed by Mita Maleki.[^wk-bam][^fa-bam] He said the song came from his own feelings and from scenes he had witnessed.[^bam] AaMin, a friend who played guitar and sang on it, became his partner on later songs.[^bamcollab][^aamin]

In 1385 (2006) Yas and AaMin released “[CD Ro Beshkan](#cd-ro-beshkan-mz4)” (“Break the CD”) after a private video of the actress Zahra Amir Ebrahimi was circulated. They criticised the people who had spread the video and asked listeners to destroy their copies.[^cd][^fa-cd][^wk-cd] AaMin later said that in their work together his own lines stayed general, leaving Yas to go into the details.[^roles]

That same year Yas sang on “Yadet Nare” with the rapper Majid Ghafouri, a track from Ghafouri’s licensed album. It became the first song with his voice to be released with official permission.[^fa-yadet] Yas said he had spent two or three years trying to get a permit himself, without success, before asking the Barbad music company to obtain it.[^permit] He answered the film *300* with “Hoviate Man” (“My Identity”), protesting its distortion of history.[^fa-hoviat]

## Concerts and protest songs

In 1386 (2007) he spent six weeks in the United States and gave several concerts there.[^fa-us] In Ordibehesht 1387 (April–May 2008) he took part in a national conference on youth problems in Shiraz, where he defended rap against critics who blamed it for violence and permissiveness.[^fa-shiraz] In 2011 he released “[Sarbaz E Vatan](#sarbaz-e-vatan-soldier-h5w)” and “[Az Chi Begam](#az-chi-begam-what-can-i-say-mqs)” (“What Can I Say”), composed and arranged by Saeed Ansar. The latter was about the fire at a school in the village of Doroudzan and the schoolgirls hurt in it, and blamed the state for the old oil heater that caused it.[^sarbaz][^sarbaz-name][^azchi][^azchi-name][^fa-azchi][^wk-azchi] On 21 December 2011, MTV Iggy’s voters chose him as artist of the week.[^wk-mtv] “[Man Mijangam](#man-mijangam-ill-fight-on-ym3)” (“I’ll Fight On”) followed in 2012.[^mijangam][^mijangam-name]

## Tech N9ne and the United States

On 19 Esfand 1391 (9 March 2013) Yas left Iran for the United States to work with Tech N9ne.[^fa-left] In Kansas City, Tech N9ne received the “Persian Tupac” with open arms, and the two recorded a song together at Chapman Studios.[^sm-tupac][^sm-studio] That November they shot a video there for “[Sound of Unity](#sound-of-unity-sedaye-ettehad-r2k),” which came out on 20 February 2014. In it the two trade verses across the Kansas City landscape, and Tech N9ne sings the closing chorus in Persian.[^sk-video][^unitydate][^unity-video][^unity-chorus] In 2013 and 2014 Yas toured cities in the United States and Canada, among them San Francisco, Vancouver, Toronto and Montreal.[^fa-tour]

A music writer later noted that part of his appeal to the authorities was his use of traditional Iranian poetic forms.[^mt]

## Return and censorship

Back in Iran, he released “Bad Shodam” on 21 Esfand 1393 (12 March 2015) and then “Mosafer,” about himself and his travels of the previous two years.[^fa-return] He sang the closing-credits song for the film *Barcode*, but when the film opened on 19 Khordad 1395 (8 June 2016), his voice had been removed: the authorities had made that a condition of its screening permit. In protest, audiences sang his song “[Barcode](#barcode-y9w)” in Iranian cinemas as the credits rolled.[^fa-barcode][^fa-sing][^barcode-song][^barcode-song-name]

## Farbod Arsh and later songs

“[Sarkoob](#sarkoob-5z9),” composed by Farbod Arsh, came out on Radio Javan on 30 Esfand 1395 (20 March 2017); at the time it was one of the longest songs in Persian rap.[^sarkoob][^fa-sarkoob] Later songs include “[Bande Naaf Ta Khatte Saaf](#bande-naaf-ta-khatte-saaf-qy6)” with Moer and “[Agaah](#agaah-8pg),” also with Moer and composed by Arsh.[^bandenaaf][^bandenaaf-name][^agaah][^agaah-composer] He released “[Sefareshi](#sefareshi-57g)” in 2022.[^sefareshi][^sefareshi-name]

“[Beem](#beem-y38),” produced by Arsh and released on 27 May 2022, was his first political single. About twelve minutes long, it is built in five verses over three different beats.[^beem][^fa-beem] “[BAAQ](#baaq-63h),” released in March 2025, is the longest piece in his catalogue. Using a garden as a metaphor for Iran, it tells the political and social events of 1401 to 1404 in four parts.[^baaqdate][^baaq][^fa-baaq] That June he released four singles named for those years, from “[1401](#1401-hyy)” to “[1404](#1404-ket).”[^y1401][^y1401-name][^y1404][^y1404-name] In October 2026 he joined Reza Pishro and Tech N9ne on “[SEPAR (Shield)](#separ-shield-9pc).”[^separ][^separ-name]
