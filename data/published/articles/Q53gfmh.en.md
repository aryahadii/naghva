Hypernova is a band from Tehran that drew international press attention on reaching the United States in April 2007.[^iran] In an interview with Bidoun, vocalist Raam said that the musicians wanted listeners to engage with their music rather than only the story of their Iranian origins.[^view]

## Exit Strategy

The band’s *[Exit Strategy EP](#exit-strategy-ep-c66)* is available on its Bandcamp page.[^album] Recorded live at Machines With Magnets, it credits production to Keith Souza and Hypernova, recording, mixing and mastering to Souza and Seth Manchester, songwriting to Kodi and lyrics to [King Raam](/en/king-raam-z9w/).[^recording] The release date is August 17, 2011.[^date]
