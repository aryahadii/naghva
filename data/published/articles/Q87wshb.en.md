Sepehr Pirasteh and Sina Homaee founded Shiraz Ensemble in Philadelphia in 2023. They named it for the Iranian city where they both grew up. That August at University Lutheran they played as a duo in Chahargah and Homayoun. Homaee played santur and tombak, while Pirasteh paired kamancheh with electronics. Santur player Parsa Ferdowsi and oud player Bahar Badiei later joined the core group. Kamancheh player Noushin Nowroozilarki also performed with the ensemble.[^found][^name][^duo][^instruments][^where][^core][^noushin]

For a Philadelphia concert in March 2024, poet Fatemeh Shams chose contemporary Persian poems for the musicians to set to music. [Mahya Hamedi](/en/mahya-hamedi-3yt/) sang with the ensemble at the Asian Arts Initiative. One of the published excerpts uses a poem by Simin Behbahani. Hamedi also sang and played keys with the group for PhillyCAM’s *People Powered Sounds*.[^poetry][^date][^simin][^broadcast]

On November 12, 2024, at Temple University’s Cybersounds concert, the ensemble performed Pirasteh’s “Shur-Afshar” with visuals by Pegah Saebi. Pirasteh wrote passages in which the boundary between Shur and Afshari gradually becomes hard to hear.[^templedate][^templework][^templevisual][^templemode][^templepair]

A collaboration with Medukha in June 2025 brought Polish folk poetry and Iranian instruments together in an improvised set. The two ensembles played together again that November at The Perch. In June 2026 Shiraz premiered Pirasteh’s “A Thousand Canary Fall Silent In My Throat” at the same venue. Badiei played oud, Ferdowsi santur and Homaee tombak, with Pirasteh on kamancheh and electronics.[^med][^nov][^canary][^band][^month]
