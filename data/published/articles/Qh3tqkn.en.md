Sogol Mirzaei is a Tehran-born tar and setar player and composer. At the Tehran Conservatory she studied tar with Houshang Zarif and Arshad Tahmasebi. In 2006 she moved to France, where she pursued the setar with Reza Ghassemi in Paris and earned a master’s degree in musicology at the Sorbonne.[^city][^school][^setar][^study]

In 2014 she founded Ensemble Chakâm to give Iranian instrumental music a place on stage. With its first lineup she opened a Lausanne concert for Kayhan Kalhor and Ali Bahrami Fard. When the ensemble changed shape, she wrote for her tar alongside a Palestinian qanun and a French viol. She also took the two lutes into film music, playing tar and setar for Gilles Sivilotto’s score to the five-part ARTE documentary series Un jour à Téhéran.[^group][^concert][^three][^film][^score]

At Geneva’s Ateliers d’ethnomusicologie she met singer and percussionist Hossein Rad. In 2021 they founded Rokh Quartet to revisit Iranian classical repertoire and music from neighbouring regions. On its studio album Bostântchi she plays a tar solo and shares the billing for “Pirouz” and “Mille rosiers”. In other ensembles, her tar met different voices. With Sowal Diabi she played alongside Malian singer Mamani Keïta and Iranian singer Aïda Nosrat. She also performed with Jordi Savall’s Orpheus XXI and with the quintet Atine.[^geneva][^rokh][^tracks][^sowal][^others] In November 2021 she recorded at the Palais de la Porte Dorée in Paris with Alexis Paul, who played street organ and electronics. She joined qanun player Christine Zayed for “Lucia” and improvised with Paul in F sharp.[^organ][^lucia][^improv]

One of her compositions for Chakâm, “Didâr”, was heard in the group’s Manchester WOMEX performance and on its 2025 debut album. She subsequently toured Ode au Cyprès with singer Maliheh Moradi. Their October 2026 itinerary included Lille, Zurich and Ghent.[^didar][^debut][^tour]
