The Muckers began in Tehran in 2011. At a charity show they remember as their first concert, they played songs by Creedence Clearwater Revival, Jimi Hendrix and the Beatles. They sang in English and used iTunes to reach listeners outside Iran.[^prague-language][^prague-store] By August 2012 their own “Attitude” and “Shoot Me Again” were circulating alongside a cover of ZZ Top’s “Tush.” Mani Tabibzadeh sang and played guitar in the group, with Emir Mohseni on guitar, Sadra Farvahi on bass, Kamiyar Keramati on synthesizer and Arash Nourozi on drums.[^iran-name][^interview-charity][^iran-old][^iranshoot-h][^irantush-h][^iran-mani][^iran-emir][^iran-sadra][^iran-kamiyar][^iran-arash]

Mohseni’s journey from Tehran to the United States coincided with the travel ban in 2017. In New York he fronted a new quartet under the old name. Anthony Azarmgin played bass, Chris Cawley rhythm guitar and John Zimmerman drums. The nine-minute title track of their first New York EP, *It’s Better Without You*, appeared in October 2018. A month later they issued it alongside “Memories” and “This Town Will Drag Us Down.” A year later the quartet played those three songs at Paste’s Manhattan studio, opening with an early live performance of “Suspended.”[^world-trip][^bcendeavor-emir][^bcendeavor-anthony][^bcendeavor-chris][^bcendeavor-john][^apple1438423759-date][^apple1441114249-date][^apple1441114249-track1][^apple1441114249-track2][^paste-t71][^paste-title]

“Roll the Dice,” Mohseni said, grew out of the feeling that something you love can be worth risking everything for. It reached listeners as a single in 2020 and appeared on the band’s first album, *Endeavor*, the following February. In between, they revisited Frank Zappa’s “Trouble Every Day” in protest against police violence, pledging the proceeds to the NAACP Legal Defense Fund.[^bctrouble-every-day-fund][^bctrouble-every-day-cause] Their later recordings include a GUM & Ginoli remix of “So Far Away” and the 2023 single “Suffocation.”[^bcendeavor-roll][^apple1497604893-date][^apple1531973256-date][^bctrouble-every-day-why][^apple1550696465-remix][^apple1696979871-date]
