Kourosh Babaei started out on violin before he took up the kamancheh, the Iranian bowed instrument he later studied with Ardeshir Kamkar. Born in Abadan in 1971, he was twelve when he began violin. His recordings in Iran include *Afshari Morakab*, with Parviz Meshkatian’s Aref Ensemble and singer Iraj Bastami. In 2016 he moved to Toronto.[^who][^early][^study][^classical][^tor]

In Toronto, the musicians he played with sometimes changed the role of the kamancheh. On *Experimental Link 003*, released in 2023, he played in a trio with Chernilo on electronics and Steve Mancuso on percussion.[^record][^link][^percussion]

## The instrument that replaced a cello

Pianist Houman Dehlavi had been writing music for an unfinished documentary about the painter Hamidreza Andarz. At first he heard the music for piano and cello. After meeting Babaei and hearing his shahkaman, another bowed instrument, he rewrote the cello part for it. On *Lights and Shadows*, released in 2025, the two players introduce two themes separately and then improvise on them as the lines start to intertwine.[^film][^cello][^impro][^years]

Babaei also took up a problem familiar from years of playing and teaching. The spike at the foot of the kamancheh would slide across a smooth floor. His Kamanyar support holds the instrument in place and adjusts for playing seated or standing.[^slip][^stand]
