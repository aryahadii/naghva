Marjan Farsad is an Iranian singer, songwriter, animator and illustrator born in Tehran in 1983. She studied visual art in Iran and Canada and began releasing albums while living abroad. Her songs and animated videos draw on personal experiences; her albums include [*Blue Flowers*](#blue-flowers-7f5) and [*White Tree*](#white-tree-cx9).[^bio][^studies][^move][^albums][^visual][^scenes]

## Iran and Montreal

Farsad studied graphic design in Iran and film animation in Montreal. She left Iran after the 2009 election and settled in Montreal.[^studies][^move] Animation and design remained her livelihood as she made music. Her lyrics grew from things she had experienced, and she often used them to picture a scene or tell a story.[^visual][^scenes]

In 2014 she released her first album, *Blue Flowers*.[^albums] That year she also published the video for “Lullaby for Bunnies,” writing its music and words and creating its illustrations.[^lullaby-visual][^lullaby-upload]

## White Tree

Farsad returned to recording for *White Tree*, released in 2020. She had been dissatisfied with an earlier version and did not want to release it as it was. Support from listeners paid for a new production.[^albums][^funding]

Working with the musicians changed the songs. Farsad said that their ideas became a substantial part of the album, beyond what she had brought to the sessions herself.[^team]

## Mahtab and later collaborations

By 2021 Farsad was living in New York. That year she wrote [“Mahtab”](#mahtab-7ka) in response to the imprisonment of Keyomars Marzban and Niloufar Bayani, people close to her. The Center for Human Rights in Iran produced the song. Farsad considered it more personal than political: their imprisonment had compelled her to speak out.[^bio][^mahtab][^friends][^response][^personal] The image of stars disappearing night after night expressed her response to events in Iran.[^mahtab-image]

She released the [*Be Yadam* EP](#be-yadam-ep-tkf) in 2022.[^ep][^ep-date] She later worked with the musician Shervin Najafian on [“Nameh Baroon”](#nameh-baroon-yyf) and [“Parie Gheseha”](#parie-gheseha-mvb). She wrote their music and lyrics and made the illustrations and artwork, while Najafian produced the recordings and played guitar.[^rain-credits][^fairy-credits]
