Bahram Nouraei, known as Bahram, is an Iranian rapper born in Tehran on 25 April 1988, according to his official biography.[^rapper][^bio]

## Music and words

In an interview with Metal Magazine, Bahram recalls his father collecting music cassettes and singing at family gatherings, while his mother read poetry and novels. As a child he read works by Forough Farrokhzad and Sadegh Hedayat.[^family][^singing][^mother][^reading]

Bahram recalls Ahmadinejad saying the government should focus on the economy rather than women’s clothing, while morality-police patrols arrested women over their appearance. He says the contradiction prompted him to write [Nameyee be Rayees Jomhoor](#nameyee-be-rayees-jomhoor-hfq) as an open letter, aiming for serious and empathetic social criticism.[^nameyee-subject][^nameyee-priority][^nameyee-promise][^nameyee-arrests][^nameyee-contradiction][^nameyee-letter][^nameyee-tone] His official discography lists [24 Sa'at](#24-saat-8bh) as an August 2008 album, with Atour credited for its music production.[^early-card]

## Sound and storytelling

Looking back on his work with beatmakers, Bahram says many, especially early in their careers, made music through sampling; many beats he selected contained existing music. He felt familiar sounds could connect with listening tastes in Iran, but says some beats they used had not been cleared for representation and reproduction rights, causing copyright issues.[^beats][^picked][^familiar][^clearance]

For the 2015 album [Eshtebahe Khoob](#eshtebahe-khoob-dej), Bahram and Mahdyar Aghajani are credited as producers.[^good-card] Asked about its reverse-chronological storytelling, Bahram says the technique brought his story to life; he describes its inspiration as the fractal-like process of confronting difficult decisions and its expression in personal growth.[^good-question][^good-mistake][^good-inspiration] For the 2023 album [Khodha](#khodha-34h), he describes wanting to redirect anger toward a constructive force and encourage people to confront their inner shadows or suppressed selves.[^khodha-intent][^khodha-inner][^khodha-card]

## Working together

Bahram says he and Ghogha started the Peeleh artist collective in 2019 to build an inclusive, cross-genre network of experts and creatives.[^peeleh-cofounder][^peeleh-network] The official discography credits Ali Sorena as a featured artist on [Khoone Khorshid](#khoone-khorshid-ra4), with music production by Peymandegar and Ashkan Mousavi.[^collab-card]
