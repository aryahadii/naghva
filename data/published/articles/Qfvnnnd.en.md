Arshia Samsaminia is an Iranian composer who writes for instruments and electronics. He began by writing down his improvisations on the setar. Later, in pieces for European chamber ensembles, he used electronics to help musicians find intervals described by the Persian scholar Ibn Sina.[^identity][^setar][^score][^avicenna][^which][^result]

## From Tehran to Tbilisi

Born in Tehran in 1989, Samsaminia shared his brother’s interest in rock and learned electric guitar and piano as a teenager. Setar lessons opened a route from improvising to composing. In 2013 he performed his own piece [“Gravity”](#gravity-for-prepared-guitar-and-laptop-batumi-contemporary-music-festival-2013-zjb), for prepared guitar and laptop, at a contemporary music festival in Batumi.[^birth][^rock][^keys][^setar][^score][^guitar][^batumi]

A journey on the Tbilisi metro supplied the sounds for an audiovisual work in 2015. He sampled the noise he heard entering a station, boarding a train and travelling between two stops. That year he and three other performers played a prepared piano at the Tbilisi Conservatoire. They used materials including videotape tied to the bass strings.[^metro][^station][^sample][^piano][^joint][^tbilisi]

In December 2019 the duo silo portem made four improvisations in their home studio using recordings Samsaminia had given them permission to rework. They edited the sessions into an album named after him and released it in June 2020.[^silo][^improv][^sdate]

## Composing with historical tunings

In Berlin, Samsaminia studied microtonal composition with Marc Sabat. For *FREQ.EXP*, part of his 2022 residency at the Klangteppich festival, he calculated eighteen frequencies from Ibn Sina’s account of the octave. The string players change instruments over the course of the piece. Johannes Fink moves from double bass to cello, and Nikolaus Schlierf from violin to viola. Samsaminia also drew on Ibn Sina for [*Shifā*](../yalda-zamani-rte/#the-cure-shifa-collegium-novum-zurich-2022-k9y), performed that April by Collegium Novum Zürich under Yalda Zamani.[^school][^residency][^avicenna][^which][^instruments][^players][^zurich][^cure]
