Pasha Hanjani is a ney player and composer from Tehran. He has played the solo part in Hossein Alizadeh’s *Ney Nava* with orchestras in Iran and abroad, and has recorded chamber music with Iranian, Norwegian and Turkish musicians.[^pacon][^painter][^paak]

## Training and Ney Nava

Hanjani began his musical education at Tehran’s National Conservatory, where his teachers included Abdolnaqi Afsharnia and A. Kianinejad. He continued at Tehran University of Art and taught ney there while studying. He first played *Ney Nava* with Parsian and Zahiraldini, then performed it with a Ukrainian chamber orchestra. In 2017 he took the solo part with Iran’s National Orchestra under Mehdi Vojdani.[^pacon][^pateach][^pau][^pain2][^painter][^pain3]

## Recordings and ensembles

Hanjani arranged songs with oud player Ali Pajooheshgar for Payam Azizi’s albums *Maleka* and *Payam-e Asheqan*. He toured as the ney player in the Mastan Ensemble.[^maleka][^asheqan] On [*Three Wise Men*](#tre-vise-menn-xjt), he played with Norwegian trumpeter Mathias Eick and Turkish duduk player Ertan Tekin. He recorded with Mahsa and Marjan Vahdat on [*I Am Eve*](#i-am-eve-3dk) and [*Twinklings of Hope*](#twinklings-of-hope-yrm), and with percussionist Zakaria Yousefi on *Stray Dust*.[^pacon][^paak][^pasim][^past] Erik Hillestad produced the trio’s instrumental versions of Christmas songs.[^xpprod][^xpchrist]
