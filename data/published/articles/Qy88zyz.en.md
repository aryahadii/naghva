Erwin Khachikian grew up in Tehran hearing his father play guitar and organ. He began studying classical music at eleven, and was thirteen when his Armenian family moved to Los Angeles. He kept studying music through school and took a degree in psychology at California State University, Northridge.[^interview-birth][^interview-early][^interview-school][^bio-move][^interview-degree]

At the keyboards in Slow Motion Reign, Khachikian helped shape bassist and singer Narek Pogosyan’s songs into the piano arrangements on the group’s self-titled full-length. An earlier EP also bore the band’s name, and the full-length followed in 2006.[^ep][^smr-full] He also produced and arranged two albums for Siavash Ghomeshi, *Neghab* and *Bi Sarzamin Tar Az Bad*. With Serj Tankian’s backing band, the F.C.C., he toured as a keyboardist and musical director.[^slow-keys][^slow-arrangements][^bio-produce][^bio-persian][^bio-fcc][^bio-musical]

In his own project [Karmandan](/en/karmandan-zjh/), he wrote songs for an ensemble whose musicians changed from country to country. With Niaz Nawab he recorded the EP *Terminal 1*. The two also played “Jahane Man” as an acoustic duet in Vancouver.[^interview-local][^terminal][^session]

Khachikian directed music for the first season of *The Voice Persia* and organized European and American tours for Pallett. He produced the 2023 Glendale concert *Zan: Voices of Freedom*, which brought Niaz Nawab, Golazin and Gelareh Sheibani together to sing “Sharghiye Ghamgin.”[^zan] When the band performed “From Eastern Lands” in Los Angeles in 2024, he joined them onstage as their guest.[^bio-director][^bio-tour][^guest]
