Dara Daraei is a bassist, composer and bandleader. He played in the ensemble of the composer [Peter Soleimanipour](/en/peter-soleimanipour-mgp/) and spent years studying the music of southern Iran. In 2014 he co-founded [Damahi](/en/damahi-ep6/), whose members drew on the music of Iran’s southern coast for their first album.[^ensemble][^southern][^band-en][^debut]

## Ensemble work

Daraei played Soleimanipour’s compositions in the [ensemble](/en/peter-soleimanipour-ensemble-2vr/) that Soleimanipour formed in 2005–06.[^ensemble] By 2010 Daraei was recording with the Beyond the Moon Ensemble, Reza Rohani and other musicians on [*Fragile Silence*](#fragile-silence-pwz).[^solo][^solo-date]

## Damahi

The singer [Reza Koolaghani](/en/reza-koolaghani-kwr/) recalled travelling with Daraei and the other musicians to ports and islands in southern Iran. The idea of playing together took shape on those trips. Daraei became Damahi’s bandleader and played bass in the group.[^southern][^band-en] The group released its [self-titled debut](/en/damahi-ep6/#damahi-d3a) in 2015.[^debut][^debut-title]

Daraei composed the melody of [“Bi Aman”](/en/damahi-ep6/#bi-aman-qzd). In the arrangement, he pointed out, Ebrahim Alavi’s oud sometimes follows Koolaghani’s voice. He also set words by Ebrahim Monsefi to music for “Tark-e Diyar”, sung by Majid Salari and arranged by [Hamzeh Yeganeh](/en/hamzeh-yeganeh-373/).[^bi-aman][^bi-aman-account][^composer]
