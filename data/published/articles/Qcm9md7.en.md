Dara Daraei, also credited as Dara Daraee, is a bassist and bandleader. Damahi’s bilingual official biography identifies him among its founders in 1393 of the Solar Hijri calendar, also given there as 2014.[^band][^band-en][^solo]

A 2018 retrospective published by the Iranian Musicians Union names Daraei among the musicians who performed Soleimanipour’s compositions with the ensemble Soleimanipour formed in 1384 of the Solar Hijri calendar.[^ensemble]

## Recordings and collaboration

Daraei appears on [Fragile Silence](#fragile-silence-pwz) and the single Happiness. Apple Music includes him among the artists on both releases and dates them to 8 June 2010 and 21 April 2021 respectively.[^solo][^solo-date][^happy][^happy-date] Majid Salari’s own recording credits for Tark-e Diyar identify Daraei as the composer, with Salari on vocals, [Hamzeh Yeganeh](/en/hamzeh-yeganeh-373/) arranging, and lyrics by Ebrahim Monsefi.[^composer]
