Hooshyar Khayam is an Iranian pianist, composer and conductor who grew up in Tehran. He recorded solo and collaborative works before founding Tehran Records in 2015 to publish contemporary Iranian music. In 2020 he moved to Ottawa, where he later taught at Carleton University.[^bio][^tehran][^hermes][^label][^move][^carleton]

## From music lessons to Tehran Records

Khayam learned music privately while attending school in Tehran. He went on to study music at Tehran Art University and earned a doctorate in composition at the University of Cincinnati.[^lessons][^college][^doctorate] He made records with the label Hermes, among them [*All of You*](#all-of-you-bva). On his own label he released [*Music for Tar and Piano*](#music-for-tar-and-piano-33f), a collaboration with Milad Mohammadi.[^hermes][^all][^tar][^mohammadi]

Tehran Records’ last release in 2020 was [“Noor”](#noor-yzg). Khayam and Soheil Mokhberi made the piece in memory of the people who died on Flight 752.[^years][^flight][^noor]

## Raaz

For [*Raaz*](#raaz-kef), a commission from the Iranian Heritage Foundation in London, Khayam worked with the composer and electronic musician Bamdad Afshar. They wrote for live singers and a Baluchi ensemble alongside piano, strings and electronics, bringing recordings of music from Baluchestan into the performance. The album came out in 2020.[^raaz][^commission][^forces][^date]
