Hooshyar Khayam is an Iranian musician active as a composer, conductor, pianist and producer. He founded his own label, Tehran Records, in 2015 to produce and promote contemporary Iranian music. His official biography dates the label's activity to 2015–2020.[^bio][^label][^years]

## RAAZ

His discography lists *RAAZ* under 2020 and 30M Records. Khayam developed the project with composer and electronic musician Bamdad Afshar around the music of Baluchestan, on a commission from the Iranian Heritage Foundation in London.[^date][^raaz][^commission]

The work brings prerecorded Baluchi music together with live voices, a Baluchi ensemble, piano, string quartet, prepared piano and electronics.[^forces]
