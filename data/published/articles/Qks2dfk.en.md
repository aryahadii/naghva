Ava Rasti is an Iranian pianist, bassist and composer based in Berlin. In Tehran she helped form the underground rock band The Finches and played bass with them from 2015 to 2020. When she began recording solo, she returned to the classical music she had continued listening to throughout her years with the band. She has also played bass and electronics with Mehdi Saki in the trio Kamakan.[^piano][^berlin][^formation][^five][^solo][^kamakan]

For [*Ginestra*](#ginestra-hm6), Rasti sampled Bach, Sibelius, Beethoven and Mahler. She was thinking about the Woman, Life, Freedom movement in Iran. The title comes from a flower that survives on volcanic slopes, an image she associated with rebuilding after destruction. Both *Ginestra* and *The Living Room* appeared in 2023.[^sample][^flower][^protest][^room]

While in residence at Fabrica in Treviso, Rasti used to visit the Piave with friends. Only later did she learn that battles had been fought there during the Napoleonic wars and the First World War. The distance between her memory of those peaceful visits and what she learned about the site led to [*The River*](#the-river-yb4).[^river][^history][^treviso] In Braga in October 2025 she performed *The River* with violin, viola and two cellos in a commission from the Semibreve festival. The film of that performance premiered a year later.[^braga][^quartet][^filmdate]

Rasti also composed music for *Double Life* and Mehrnoush Alia’s film *1001 Frames*. For the soundtrack to *A Butterfly Fly From Each Fingertip*, Saki sang on “Butterflies Hugging Each Other.” Rasti’s eight-piece album *No One Knows How Wild the Wind Blows* followed in October 2026.[^double][^film][^saki][^later]

Rasti had written “Tehran” on her last visit to the city. She wrote the title track in Berlin during the twelve-day war in Iran in 2025, unable to return. On “The Wind Will Take Us Away,” named for a poem by Forough Farrokhzad, a voice she recorded in a Freiburg church enters the music. She had been there to compose for a stage adaptation of Kafka’s *The Trial*.[^visit][^war][^poem][^theatre]
