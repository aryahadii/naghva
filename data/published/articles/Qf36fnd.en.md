Sevdaliza, born Sevda Alizadeh in Tehran in 1987, is an Iranian-Dutch singer, songwriter and producer who grew up in the Netherlands.[^identity][^birth] At 24 she began making music with the Rotterdam producer Mucky, and released two EPs in 2015 and her debut album, [*ISON*](#ison-e85), on her own label in 2017.[^at-24][^early-ep][^silk-ep][^own-label] After a second album, [*Shabrang*](#shabrang-d8b), she reached a wider audience with [“Alibi”](#alibi-ft-pabllo-vittar-yseult-4q9), recorded with Pabllo Vittar and Yseult in 2024, and released her third album, [*HEROINA*](#heroina-7m4), in 2025.[^shabrang-date][^alibi-artists][^alibi-date][^heroina-guests]

## Early life

Sevda Alizadeh was born during the Iran–Iraq War, and later said her earliest memories were of instability and a constant, low fear.[^war] Her father was a political prisoner.[^father] When she was four or five, she and her mother left Iran as refugees; her father was still in prison, and her mother had to rebuild their life in the Netherlands alone.[^age-four][^refugees][^mother-alone] They lived in asylum seekers’ centres before settling in Hardinxveld-Giessendam.[^migration]

At seven she found her way into music through pirate radio. She recorded golden-age rap, learned the lyrics by heart and counted the Notorious B.I.G. among her heroes.[^radio] At fifteen or sixteen she left home on a basketball scholarship, and went on to play for the Netherlands at national level.[^moved-out][^sport][^youth-team] She paid her own way through a master’s degree in communication. Her first job, at MTV, brought her into contact with artists, and there she realized she wanted to make music herself.[^youth-team][^degree][^mtv]

## First records

Alizadeh started making music of her own at 24. She had no training in reading music or playing an instrument, so she spent hours in the studio teaching herself to sing and to use Ableton.[^at-24][^teach] It took a year of intense work with the producer Mucky before her sound took shape, and the two became, in her words, like a family.[^at-24][^mucky]

Her first EP, [*The Suspended Kid*](#the-suspended-kid-4fy), came out in January 2015 after a year and a half of work. She named it after the way people had responded to her in social situations, which convinced her to choose a different path.[^early-ep][^suspended-time][^suspended-title] [*Children of Silk*](#children-of-silk-feat-rome-fortune-4ac) followed that November.[^silk-ep] Her videos were as much part of the work as the songs: in [“Human”](#human-8rs) she wore a costume that transformed her body, to comment on the fetishization of women’s bodies and the male gaze.[^human-visual]

## Bebin and ISON

In January 2017 the United States barred entry to citizens of Iran and six other countries, and Sevdaliza could no longer travel there.[^ban-scope][^bebin-travel] In response she wrote [“Bebin”](#bebin-vmf), Persian for “look”, in about four days; she and Mucky finished it within 48 hours. It was the first song she had written in Persian, and it felt natural to her.[^bebin-days][^bebin-farsi][^bebin-date] She gave its sales to people facing racial exclusion.[^bebin-own]

Her debut album, *ISON*, came out that April on her own label, Twisted Elegance. It is named after a sungrazing comet.[^ison-date][^own-label][^comet] She called it her first real body of work and an emotional diary, written by beginning without a set idea and balancing control with letting go.[^ison-intent][^subconscious] She had not grown up listening to Iranian music and did not think of her music as Iranian, but, she said, some things came back “in weird ways”.[^persian-notes]

## Shabrang and Dahlia

[*Shabrang*](#shabrang-d8b) followed in 2020. Where she had spent at least two years in the studio on *ISON*, she made the new album between stretches on the road.[^shabrang-date][^tour-process] Its title is the name of Siyavash’s black horse in Ferdowsi’s *Shahnameh*. On it she covered Googoosh’s “Gole Bi Goldoon”, a song she had heard again and again at emotional moments in her life.[^shabrang-title][^googoosh-cover][^googoosh-memory]

In 2021 she introduced Dahlia, a robot she had spent two years building, seen in the video for “Homunculus – Oh My God”. She made it in response to an industry whose standards she did not meet, in sound, looks or behaviour.[^dahlia] The project led to the EP [*Raving Dahlia*](#raving-dahlia-k44) in February 2022.[^raving-date]

In October 2022, as protests over the death of Mahsa Amini spread across Iran, Sevdaliza released [“Woman Life Freedom”](#woman-life-freedom-jey), written in support of Iranian women. In a statement she said she had become aware at a young age of how women were forced into obedience through violence and intimidation.[^protests][^wlf][^wlf-statement]

## Alibi and HEROINA

Sevdaliza began studying Spanish and became friends online with the Puerto Rican rapper Villano Antillano. Their song [“Ride or Die”](#ride-or-die-feat-villano-antillano-7g4) came out in June 2023, and the Dominican rapper Tokischa joined them for [a second part](#ride-or-die-pt-2-d3x), recorded together in Paris and released in April 2024.[^spanish][^ride-date][^ride2-date] She also recorded [“Nothing Lasts Forever”](#nothing-lasts-forever-xkc) with Grimes.[^nlf-artists]

By then she had two albums behind her but little money. As a new mother, she later said, she was struggling and could not support her family.[^struggling] She began the demo of “Alibi” with her six-month-old child on her lap, meaning to write an anthem that celebrated women’s strength and to save herself from her situation.[^alibi-demo][^alibi-save] Recorded with the Brazilian singer Pabllo Vittar and the French singer Yseult, it came out in June 2024, and that September the three sang it during Karol G’s set at Rock in Rio.[^alibi-artists][^alibi-date][^rio] She described this as the point where she stopped using music mainly as therapy and began sharing joy and power, especially with women and queer listeners.[^joy] In October she released [“No Me Cansare”](#no-me-cansare-fnc) with Karol G.[^karol-artists][^karol]

In June 2025 she released [“Messiah”](#messiah-9xz), which she described as a fantasy of placing women at the centre of religion without shaming their sexuality.[^messiah-date][^messiah] Its video, with erotic scenes and Christian symbols, drew an outcry in Armenia, and that August the Coca-Cola Music Fest there cancelled her performance.[^armenia-video][^armenia]

*HEROINA*, her third album, came out in October 2025, with Karol G, Kenia Os, Pabllo Vittar and Tokischa among its guests. The title is the Spanish word for heroine. She said the album was about wanting to be free while feeling how limited that freedom can be.[^heroina-date][^heroina-guests][^heroina-word][^heroina-free] On it, [“Strong Because You Are”](#strong-because-you-are-wcx) is a song for her mother.[^mother-song]
