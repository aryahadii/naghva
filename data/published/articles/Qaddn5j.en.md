The Allophones are an alternative rock band formed in Florence in 2010 by a quartet that included Iranian singer and guitarist Peyman Salimi. The group won Tuscany’s T-Rumors competition in 2011 and made the records *Stalemate* and *Muscle Memory*. Years later the band accompanied Salimi at Maida Vale Studios for his project Self and Apolide.[^alf][^alline][^alt][^als][^alalb][^alses]

## Florence

Salimi sang and played guitar. Andrea Deidda played guitar, Bernardo Cicchi played bass and Federico Franchi played drums, with all three contributing vocals. The band drew on new wave and alternative rock while keeping a direct melody at the centre of its arrangements. After winning T-Rumors, the four recorded [*Stalemate*](#stalemate-dae) at Florence’s Larione 10 studio with Antonio Aiazzi. The 2013 EP included six originals and a cover.[^alline][^alf][^alt][^als]

## Later sessions

In 2017 The Allophones released [*Muscle Memory*](#muscle-memory-w8b), their first full-length album. Tiziano Borghi and Eduardo de la Paz mixed it.[^almix] They played on Salimi’s [“Can’t Play Standing Up”](#cant-play-standing-up-a3c), issued jointly in 2022. In September 2025 they joined him at BBC Maida Vale Studios in London for the first performances of Self and Apolide. One of those sessions became that project’s first single, “Ira.”[^alalb][^alses]
