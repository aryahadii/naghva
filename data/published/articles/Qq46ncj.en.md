Hamsaz Ensemble formed around the Iranian composer and tar player Ali Samadpour. With singer [Bahar Movahed](/en/bahar-movahed-wxq/), the group explored how Persian songs had changed over the centuries. In spring 2013, they performed a programme on the history of the Persian ballad at Symphony Space in New York and at Berkeley City College.[^orig][^ny][^broad][^berk]

In March 2015, at Town Hall Seattle, *Iran Through the Centuries* traced a history from music attributed to the medieval musician Abd al-Qadir Maraghi to pieces by Samadpour himself. Aida Meftahi supplied choreography. Nine of the programme’s 31 pieces included movement, and projections accompanied the music. The [filmmaker’s concert excerpt](https://vimeo.com/179002684) shows the staging.[^mar][^when][^title][^modern][^pieces][^dancer]

The ensemble returned to Town Hall in May 2017 for *Neishapur Nights*, the closing concert of a University of Washington conference. Its programme drew on poetry by Rumi and Omar Khayyam. Movahed published the stage performance of “Baange Naay.” She also released a rehearsal of Faramarz Payvar’s “Ba Man Sanama” in Samadpour’s arrangement, with [Payam Yousefi](/en/payam-yousefi-57c/) on kamancheh.[^uw][^date][^shay][^stage][^rehearsal][^payvar][^ali][^payam]
