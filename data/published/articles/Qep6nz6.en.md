Damahi is an Iranian band founded by bassist and bandleader [Dara Daraee](/en/dara-daraee-cm9/), [Hamzeh Yeganeh](/en/hamzeh-yeganeh-373/), Ebrahim Alavi, singer [Reza Koolaghani](/en/reza-koolaghani-kwr/) and Shayan Fathi.[^founders] The band’s official biography gives 2014 as its founding year, with 1393 in the Persian text; the introduction to Cadence’s interview with Koolaghani instead dates his formation of the group with Daraee to 1392 of the Solar Hijri calendar (2013–14).[^founders][^formation-official][^formation-cadence]

## Travels and a first album

For Koolaghani, the group grew out of trips to the south rather than a plan to form a band. He and Alavi are southerners; Yeganeh and Daraee, though not from the region, had spent years working in southern groups and researching its music. Koolaghani says he and Alavi considered Daraee’s knowledge of that music greater than their own. Their journeys together to southern islands and ports gave them the idea of working together and, in his account, produced half of the first album.[^southern]

That debut, [*Damahi*](#damahi-d3a), reached Iran’s music market in September 2015. It brought southern coastal music into contact with jazz, rock, reggae, flamenco and other musical forms.[^debut][^debut-title] Daraee describes the band’s music as pop in a broad sense, with fusion and jazz as more specific categories; he says balance was something they wanted from the outset.[^genre]

## Two routes into the second album

The second album, [*Dar Man Boro Shekar*](#dar-man-boro-shekar-w9z), appeared in 2019.[^second-album][^album-date] In a pre-release interview, Daraee explained that two of its tracks, “Houmba” and “Bi Aman,” were already circulating online.[^early-tracks][^bi-aman-account]

[*Houmba*](#houmba-feat-ghanbar-rastgoo-jmx), featuring Ghanbar Rastgoo, takes its words and melody from the older «هووم», associated with children’s music in Hormozgan. Damahi arranged the piece, with Rastgoo singing and playing ney-jofti and Koolaghani on backing vocals.[^houmba-title][^houmba]

[*Bi Aman*](#bi-aman-qzd) has a melody by Daraee and lyrics by احسان گودرزی, sung by Koolaghani, with guests including Hooman Namdari on clarinet.[^bi-aman] Daraee describes its musical language as drawing on popular music and singles out Alavi’s oud, which sometimes follows the vocal melody, as a characteristic part of Damahi’s sound.[^bi-aman-account]

## An anniversary and a dedication

[*Mahi*](#mahi-single-qyy) came in 2024 as the band’s offering for its tenth birthday, with lyrics by امیر مهدی اصغری.[^mahi][^mahi-date] The same writer supplied the lyrics for [*Octavio Paz*](#octavio-paz-single-j8k) in 2025, with a melody by Koolaghani. Damahi dedicated that recording to the Hormozgan islands, invoking their history and natural beauty while lamenting tourists’ litter and human neglect.[^octavio][^octavio-date]
