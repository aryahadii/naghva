Hura Mirshekari is an Iranian singer and visual artist based in Paris. She studied mathematics in Zabol and painting in Zahedan before moving to France in 2016. Her first melodies grew out of a conversation with Payam Sistani, an Iranian poet living in Norway. He invited her to try setting poems in her own language, Sistani, to music. As she read them she began to hum, following the rise and fall of the words.[^study][^move][^poet][^mel][^language]

In Paris she and musician Babak Amir Mobasher founded Aïka Ensemble. She sang with the group in Marseille in 2022 and at events in solidarity with the Woman, Life, Freedom movement. One of her films credits Payam Sistani with the words, Amir Mobasher with the music and Habib Meftah as a musician. The next year her collaboration with Flugen, “Mashaal,” appeared on the compilation *JIN JIYAN AZADÎ: Voices for Iran*.[^found][^paris][^mucem][^singer][^vidtidtpvcucvc][^collection][^dateap1693117123]

French producer Renaud Satre worked with her on *Hura*, her six-song debut EP, in 2024. That year she won the Prix Habib Sharifi. In her prize performance “Dâd,” she stood on sand to evoke the encroaching dunes of Sistan. A cast of her face also recurs in her stage work.[^dad][^sand][^sistan][^cast] In 2025 her voice joined prepared piano and kalimba on Crystal Sound Project’s “Lut’s Calling.” She also sang alongside actor Alice Rahimi in readings of *Badjens*, a novel by Delphine Minoui. Satre wrote music for the stage production, and in April 2026 Mirshekari and Satre released a five-song EP under the same title, with guest appearances by Siân Pottok and Fiona Sanjabi.[^producer][^six][^dateap1751911655][^award][^lut][^read][^perform][^badjens][^albumap1888643548][^dateap1888643548]
