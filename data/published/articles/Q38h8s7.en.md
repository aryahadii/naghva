Farzane Zamen is an Iranian singer-songwriter and producer from Tehran. She recorded her early songs in Iran and moved to Glasgow in 2017. There she made the electronic EP [*Z Bent*](#z-bent-tmq) and began performing folk songs alongside her own music.[^identity][^producer][^singles][^move][^ep][^electronic][^local][^folk]

## Recording in Tehran

Zamen took up classical guitar at seventeen. When she began writing songs, her father suggested she sing them herself. Her first studio recording, in 2008, was in English: “You've Gone Cold Tonight”. She planned to call her album *A Few Steps to Morning*. The online platforms she relied on dealt in singles, so she released the songs one by one.[^guitar][^dad][^studio][^title][^market]

## Glasgow

An Artist Protection Fund fellowship took her to Glasgow’s Centre for Contemporary Arts in 2017. On *Z Bent*, released in 2018, she sang in English while Habiba Makhlouf contributed lyrics in Arabic and French.[^residency][^move][^ep-date][^voices]

Zamen first performed folk songs after moving to Britain. Her [2019 Solas Festival set](#farzane-zamen-live-at-solas-festival-2019-zt2) included two Azerbaijani songs. For her later project on songs from Azerbaijan, she returned to music she remembered hearing her parents and grandparents sing. In April 2023 she played those songs with a band at Glasgow Women’s Library and sold copies of [*Folksongs of Azarbaijan*](#folksongs-of-azarbaijan-jmn) there.[^folk][^solas][^solas-track0][^solas-track1][^family][^launch][^band][^copies][^folkname]
