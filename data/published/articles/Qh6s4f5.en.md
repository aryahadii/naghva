Meshcut is the stage name of Meshkat Mosavat, an Iranian singer, songwriter and producer based in Paris. Their musical life began in Tehran’s underground scene. They sang with Ash on [“Wasted”](#wasted-hp5) and met the other members of Stereotype at weekly jam sessions in a Tehran house. During lockdown in 2020, Mosavat, Zhina Ardalan and Asal began making synthwave together.[^artist][^iran][^underground][^ash][^voice][^new]

Stereotype’s work continued in Istanbul and then Paris. Alongside the group, Mosavat released “Dream in a Dream” on Apranik Records’ *Woman, Life, Freedom* compilation and recorded an acoustic version of “Cheapskate” with Mehrdad Azizi. In March 2024, they sang at the POUYA Cultural Centre in Paris at a gathering where Iranian musicians met to improvise together.[^band][^guest][^video][^concert][^song]

In Paris Mosavat met producer Christine Guin, who worked with them on their first solo album, [*REBIRTH*](#rebirth-sec), released in 2025. Guin also performs as Oui Future. Together they formed the darkwave duo Salvaation. Its opening track “LOOP” places Mosavat’s voice in front of Guin’s electronics. For a separate residency at La Générale in Paris, Mosavat proposed an EP for voice and piano.[^paris][^first][^album][^other][^loop][^res]
