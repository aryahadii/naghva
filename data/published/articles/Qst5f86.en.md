Soul Nidus is a Canadian alternative rock group formed by Iranian-born brothers Ehsan and Salman Ashrafi. Ehsan sang and played guitar, Salman played guitar, and Nick Sansalone was the drummer in the trio that appeared at Toronto’s Tirgan festival in 2008. The brothers now describe Soul Nidus as a duo, with Salman also playing piano and drums.[^iran-brothers][^fest-iran][^fest-membere][^fest-members][^fest-membern][^own-lineup]

The brothers date the band to the early 2000s. Ehsan had written a few songs and wanted to release them under a name other than his own. They initially looked for a record deal, then grew attached to choosing for themselves what to record. Their first album, *Stoic*, was released in 2005. In 2007 they took it to the UK for concerts and acoustic sessions on the BBC and Kerrang! Radio.[^own-members][^interview-name][^interview-independent][^ap81634471-issue][^ap81634471-date][^fest-visit][^fest-radio][^fest-stations]

For *Looking Back Facing Forward* (2010), Ehsan wrote lyrics and song structures, and Salman developed melodies as they played together. Salman recalled that before recording a song Ehsan tried to recover the feeling he had when he first wrote it. Two songs on the album describe different moments in a relationship. “Cigarettes and Butterflies” is about longing for someone, while “I Can Only Sing for You” celebrates being with them. Graham Brewer helped produce the record and Geoff Pesche mastered it at Abbey Road.[^ap395720185-issue][^ap395720185-date][^interview-songwriting][^interview-melody][^interview-process][^interview-contrast][^interview-connection][^interview-producer]

In 2020 filmmaker Sam Javanrouh used the band’s music in *Godspeed*, his memorial to the victims of Flight PS752. In 2024 the brothers released “The Ark”, “Live to Love” and “Jetlag”.[^motion-film][^motion-music][^motion-title][^ap1748921924-date][^ap1763276615-date][^ap1777971414-date]
