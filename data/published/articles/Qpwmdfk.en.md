Aïka Ensemble formed in Paris around Iranian singer Hura Mirshekari and musician Babak Amir Mobasher. Mirshekari sang in Sistani, the language of her home region in southeastern Iran. The sounds of speech shaped her melodies. A recording by trumpeter Elia Chalom shows how the group took those songs onto the stage at Marseille’s Mucem in October 2022. Chalom also published their performance of “Man agar barkhizam.”[^found][^paris][^language][^mucem][^videytvszwong]

In December 2022 the ensemble published nine recordings, including “Del Katah,” “Dishna Baresh” and “Nana me kenjake tono.” Mirshekari also filmed “Del katah shadak.” In March 2023 the organisers of the Assises du journalisme in Tours invited her and Aïka to open an evening at the Théâtre Olympia in solidarity with Iran, before a discussion with journalists.[^scaikaensembledelkatah][^scaikaensembledishnabaresh][^scaikaensemblenanamekenjaketono][^vid6rkm3vt2js0][^tours][^theatre]

In 2025 the ensemble accompanied a reading of Delphine Minoui’s novel *Badjens* with actor Alice Rahimi in Le Havre. They brought the reading to Toulouse’s Marathon des mots in June. A later Paris programme describes Mirshekari as a former member.[^read][^festival][^former]
