Darawish first performed in Madrid in 2013. In the quintet, Syrian oud player Hames Bitar and Iranian musician Kaveh Sarvarian played alongside saxophonist Pablo Hernández Ramos, percussionist Luis Taberna and double bassist Fernando Leria Jiménez. Sarvarian moved between ney, concert flute, tombak and voice. The musicians wrote their own pieces around melodies and rhythms from the eastern Mediterranean and Iran.[^start][^folk][^players][^wood][^ou][^bass][^tab]

“Persarab,” the first track on their January 2020 album *Madar*, started at a rehearsal. Bitar, Sarvarian and Taberna improvised while the other musicians were running late. The piece became the starting point for their later collaboration as Cardamom Trio. Sarvarian sings on four tracks on *Madar*, including “Persarab” and “Bahare Delkash.” In September that year Darawish played the album in the garden of Madrid’s Casa Árabe, with Syrian singer Linda Alahmad and Iraqi percussionist Mustafa Sabea as guests. The presenter filmed the concert.[^imp][^jam][^trio][^album][^voc][^date][^special]
