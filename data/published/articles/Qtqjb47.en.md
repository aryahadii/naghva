Negar Bouban is an Iranian oud player, singer, composer and musicologist born in Tehran in 1973. Her solo music brings the sound of the oud into conversation with the sung voice. As a researcher she has studied how rhythm works in spoken Persian and in the classical repertoire of the dastgah.[^birth][^year][^firstvoice][^study]

## Finding the oud

Bouban grew up listening to music with her mother, the violinist Sorour Zerehgar, and her brother Babak, who played the santur. At eighteen she heard Abdolvahab Shahidi singing and playing the oud on one of her mother’s records and decided to learn the instrument. She began studying with Mansour Nariman in 1992. While studying architecture at the University of Tehran, she gave her first solo radio performance, which she dates to about 1996.[^mother][^violin][^brother][^heard][^eighteen][^teacher][^arch][^radio][^radioage]

## Solo recordings and teaching

For her first album, *Continu*, Bouban took a small-bodied oud built by Mohammad Taghi Arafati into the studio on two days in July 2007. She had worked out some melodies in advance and improvised the passages between them as she played. Mahriz released the recording in Tehran in 2008.[^instrument][^sessions][^first][^year1]

Her 2014 Tehran concert became the album and film *A Tale, Foretold*. One piece had been written by her brother for voice and oud. On the original recording Bouban sang it herself. For the concert in Tehran, where women could not sing solo in public, Babak sang and she played the oud.[^live][^album][^voice][^firstvoice]

In her teaching, Bouban asks oud students to sing melodies so they can hear a phrase before playing it. In Freiburg she co-directed *Nah* with Ralf Schmid and Leonie Fritsch. The February 2024 concert placed works for singers and Middle Eastern instruments between movements of Duke Ellington’s *Far East Suite*.[^teach][^nah][^ell][^show] In 2025 the Freiburg Filmforum paired her voice and oud with Murat Coşkun’s percussion for Esther Kinsky’s reading from *Heim.Statt*.[^poetry][^performer]
