Kābin is a Tehran-based recording project.[^tehran]

## Songwriting and work with singers

Kābin wrote the music and lyrics and arranged [*Ahesteh*](/en/kabin-fwf/#ahesteh-feat-dariush-azar-yz5), sung by Dariush Azar.[^ahesteh-writing][^ahesteh-vocals] On [*Mara Bekhan*](/en/kabin-fwf/#mara-bekhan-5bj), Kābin again supplied the music and lyrics, with Haleh Seyfizadeh on vocals.[^mara]

The collaboration with Seyfizadeh continued on the six-track EP [*Mikhahamat*](/en/kabin-fwf/#mikhahamat-an4). She shares composition credits with Aeen Ahmadifar on its title track and “Dayere Soda.”[^ep-name][^ep-tracks][^ep-title-composition][^ep-dayere-composition] Azar also returned to sing on Kābin’s single [*Shekastehtar*](/en/kabin-fwf/#shekastehtar-feat-dariush-azar-bj5).[^shekastehtar-vocals][^shekastehtar-billing]

## From Nobahaari to Magnetism

[*Nobahaari*](/en/kabin-fwf/#nobahaari-qf3) is an earlier collaboration between Kābin and [Mohsen Namjoo](/en/mohsen-namjoo-t6w/).[^nobahaari][^nobahaari-upload] Their single [*Kisti?*](/en/kabin-fwf/#kisti-pmk) was released on 27 March 2025. Namjoo introduced it as part of their forthcoming album; Kābin composed and arranged the song, with Namjoo on vocals.[^kisti-date][^kisti-preview][^kisti-roles] The nine-track album [*Magnetism*](/en/kabin-fwf/#magnetism-yvf), billed to both artists, followed on 21 December that year.[^magnetism][^magnetism-date]

## Returning to the past

Kābin describes the SoundCloud set “به روایت کابین” as a tribute to the past with a sound belonging to today.[^set-title][^set-intent] It brings together [*Asir*](/en/kabin-fwf/#asir-feat-delkash-xv8), [*Zendegi*](/en/kabin-fwf/#zendegi-feat-reza-saghaee-f5q) and [*ShahedeGham*](/en/kabin-fwf/#shahede-gham-feat-emad-raam-elahe-vyd).[^set-asir][^set-zendegi][^set-shahed]
