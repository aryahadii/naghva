Bahar Dehkordi, who records as Bahar Atish, is an Iranian rapper who started performing in Tehran’s underground music scene at fifteen. She writes about working people and the restrictions women face. After studying theatre she returned to music, recording *Az Jense Paeiz* before the Woman, Life, Freedom protests began.[^name][^age][^place][^class][^school][^autumn]

## Rap and theatre in Tehran

Atish credits her father with teaching her to speak out against injustice. She was arrested in 2005. She stepped away from rap to study theatre at university, spending her evenings rehearsing in studios on Enghelab Street. Her father followed workers’ protests at the Haft Tappeh sugar company, and their conversations about the protests drew her back to writing music.[^father][^arrest][^school][^practice][^return]

## Songs of protest

When the Woman, Life, Freedom protests began, *Az Jense Paeiz* had already been recorded. Atish later recounted that the song made her a target and she had to leave Iran. In *Shekaf* she turned to the distance between rich and poor. The song opens with a chorus and single notes on a piano before the rap begins. In August 2026 she gave her first performance in Norway.[^autumn][^flee][^class][^chord][^concert][^year]
