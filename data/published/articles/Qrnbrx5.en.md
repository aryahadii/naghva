Heavy As Texas grew out of the partnership between Houston guitarist Marzi Montazeri, who moved to Texas from Tehran as a child, and singer Kyle Thomas. Both also played in Exhorder. They made the first Heavy As Texas album with a studio rhythm section: Anup Sastry played drums and Chris Collier played bass. Montazeri produced the record, while Collier mixed and mastered it.[^born][^kyle][^stud][^mix][^texas]

## How the band started

Montazeri started writing the songs while trying to sing and play intricate guitar parts himself. He brought in Kyle Thomas so he could focus on writing and playing guitar. The name Heavy As Texas came from a line in one of Montazeri’s earlier songs.[^why][^nameorigin]

The band’s [self-titled album](#heavy-as-texas-psy) came out on Crunchy Western Records in April 2019. By then the touring lineup featured Lenwood Sonnier on bass and James Goetz on drums. “To Keep a Promise” was the first song to get a video. For the next one, “King of Fools,” the group worked with filmmaker Mark Borchardt.[^album][^dig][^tour][^tourtwo][^promise][^video]
