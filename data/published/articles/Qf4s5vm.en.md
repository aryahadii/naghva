Parastoo Ahmadi (born 21 March 1997) is an Iranian singer from Nowshahr, a coastal city in Mazandaran, who is based in Tehran.[^birth][^tehran] She became widely known in 2022, when her version of Aref Qazvini's song "Az Khoon-e Javanan-e Vatan" spread on social media during the protests that followed the death of Mahsa (Jina) Amini.[^khoon-2022][^khoon-spread] In December 2024 she posted *[Karvansara Concert](#karvansara-concert-pjn)*, a performance filmed without an audience in a caravanserai in Qom province, in which she sang without a hijab.[^karv-post][^no-audience][^no-hijab] The judiciary opened a case against her the next day. In June 2026 a court in Qom sentenced her and eight members of her band and production team to 74 lashes each, and on 23 November 2026, her lawyers said, she and four members of her team were flogged.[^case][^sentence][^flogged-date][^flogged-iinov]

## Training

Ahmadi began taking lessons in singing, voice training and solfège at thirteen, then stopped from the age of sixteen until she was twenty-two.[^lessons][^lessons-own] She has said she knew the limits and difficulties from the day she first thought of working in music, and that her doubts had delayed her start; her family's support helped her carry on.[^hesitation][^family] She went on to study film directing at Soore University.[^soore]

She has always loved Iran's folk music, and what inspired her most was learning the folk songs of Kurdistan, Azerbaijan and Mazandaran. By singing the songs of Mazandaran, her home province, she wanted to pass them on to people of her own generation.[^folk][^mazandaran] For years she played the piano and posted covers of songs on Instagram.[^instagram][^instagram-en]

## First recordings

Her first professional work, in the summer of 2020, was a cover of the Mazandarani folk song "Aziz Joon".[^first-work] In June 2021 her song "[Balal Balalom](#balal-balalom-24n)" appeared on *This Is Tehran?*, a compilation of independent Iranian musicians from 30M Records.[^tehran-comp][^balal][^balal-date] That August she released "[Heyrani](#heyrani-7b7)", a poem by Baba Taher that she set to music with the pianist Ehsan Beyraghdar, and in March or April 2022 "Lahze Didar", on a poem by Mehdi Akhavan-Sales.[^heyrani][^heyrani-credits][^heyrani-date][^lahze]

## Woman, Life, Freedom

Ahmadi first made her own version of "Az Khoon-e Javanan-e Vatan" in November 2019.[^khoon-2019] In the autumn of 2022, as protests spread after Mahsa (Jina) Amini died in the custody of the morality police, she sang it again. The performance spread widely on social media and brought her voice to a much larger audience.[^khoon-2022][^khoon-spread][^wider] In May or June 2023 she released "[Havaye Azadi](#havaye-azadi-qan)", her own music to a poem by Fatemeh Dogoharani, and dedicated it to the Woman, Life, Freedom movement.[^havaye]

On 23 September 2023 security agents entered her home in Tehran and seized her personal belongings, including her phone and computer.[^raid][^raid-dw] Her Instagram page went offline for a time, and she was summoned to the prosecutor's office.[^summons] About four months later she began releasing music again with "Che Sazam", on a poem by Houshang Ebtehaj. She sang it again at the caravanserai a year later, in a setting by the guitarist Soheil Faghih Nasiri.[^che-return][^che-concert][^che-title][^che-poet]

## Before the concert

Through 2024 she recorded mostly with Beyraghdar and Faghih Nasiri, who would later play with her at the caravanserai. In March 2024 she posted a live version of Fereydoon Farrokhzad's "[Haft](#haft-fhy)", dedicated to the people of Iran, with Faghih Nasiri on electric guitar and Beyraghdar on piano.[^haft][^haft-piano][^haft-date] In October she released "[Nafas-e Shab](#nafas-e-shab-zpy)", her own song to words by Alireza Koliai, and the folk song "[Vasoonak Shirazi](#vasoonak-shirazi-v76)", both with Beyraghdar.[^nafas][^nafas-date][^vasoonak][^vasoonak-date]

## Karvansara Concert

At nine in the evening on 11 December 2024, Ahmadi posted a 27-minute video, *Karvansara Concert*, on her YouTube channel.[^karv-post] It was filmed without an audience at the Deir-e Gachin caravanserai, an old trading post, now an events venue, in the desert outside Qom.[^karv-place][^no-audience] She sang seven songs, from Mazandarani and Afghan folk songs to "Az Khoon-e Javanan-e Vatan", with Beyraghdar on piano, Faghih Nasiri on electric guitar, Amirali Pirnia on bass and Amin Taheri on drums.[^karv-songs] She appeared without a hijab, which Iranian law requires women to wear in public, and sang solo, which, as The National noted, Iranian authorities have in effect banned women from doing since the 1979 revolution.[^no-hijab][^solo-ban]

"I am Parastoo, a girl who wants to sing for the people I love," she wrote under the video. "This is a right I could not ignore." She asked listeners to hear her voice in "this imaginary concert".[^karv-words] The video was watched hundreds of thousands of times within a short time, and by November 2026 it had been viewed more than 3.2 million times.[^views][^views-2026]

## Arrest and trial

On 12 December, the day after the video appeared, the judiciary announced that it had opened a case against Ahmadi and the production team for performing without a permit and without regard for "legal and religious standards".[^case][^case-bbc] On 14 December she was arrested in Mazandaran. The same day Beyraghdar and Faghih Nasiri were arrested at their music school in Tehran.[^arrest][^arrest-iien] All three were released early the next morning, but the case went on.[^released] On 23 December she went with her lawyers to a Tehran prosecutor's office for moral security, where she was charged and allowed to stay free on bail of three billion tomans until the end of the proceedings.[^bail] Three days later officials announced that the caravanserai had been sealed and that two cultural heritage managers in Qom province had been dismissed.[^sealed]

For about nine months she released no new songs. "Months passed in an imposed silence," she wrote in August 2025, when she returned with "[Me Jaan](#me-jaan-t4d)", a song of her native Mazandaran composed by Faghih Nasiri to words by Yaser Amiri. Its video was filmed on the slopes of Mount Damavand.[^mejaan][^mejaan-en][^mejaan-credits][^mejaan-caption] In October she released "[Az To Neveshtan](#az-to-neveshtan-ay5)", by Ramin Darestani and Meisam Yousefi, and wrote that under the present circumstances she could share only one audio piece.[^azto-en][^azto-credits]

On 17 June 2026 a criminal court in Qom convicted Ahmadi and eight others from the band and production team of "offending public decency by producing and publishing vulgar and immoral content online", charges they denied. Each was sentenced to 74 lashes, a two-year ban on leaving the country and a two-year ban on artistic and musical work.[^sentence][^charge][^sentence-bbc] Four days later the composer and tar player Hossein Alizadeh spoke at the Iranian Artists' House in Tehran: "Unfortunately, I am speaking on a day when a number of our artists have been sentenced to flogging; the Stone Age is before our eyes." He proposed that the lashes "be divided among us".[^alizadeh][^alizadeh-divide] At the end of September an appeals court in Qom rejected the appeal of Ahmadi and seven others and upheld the sentence.[^appeal][^appeal-bbc][^appeal-nat]

## Flogging

On 23 November 2026 Ahmadi, Faghih Nasiri, Taheri, Pirnia and the concert's organizer, Mohsen Lashkari, reported to the sentence enforcement office of the Qom prosecutor, where each was given 74 lashes, their lawyer Mostafa Nili said.[^flogged][^flogged-date][^flogged-iinov] A member of the defence team said the lawyers had tried to have the punishment postponed, but the enforcement branch insisted on carrying it out at once.[^postpone] It remained unclear whether the sentences of the others would be carried out.[^others]

The UN human rights chief, Volker Türk, called the flogging "abhorrent", and Iran's Music House condemned it and called for an urgent review of the case.[^turk][^music-house] The singer Mehdi Yarrahi, who had been given 74 lashes in March 2025 over a protest song, shared footage of her concert in solidarity.[^yarrahi] On the day of the flogging Ahmadi posted "[Mara Beboos](#mara-beboos-concert-version-q9j)" from the concert on her channel, and two days later a video of "Che Sazam" with Beyraghdar, Faghih Nasiri, Pirnia and Taheri.[^mara][^mara-date][^che-2026][^che-2026-date]
