Lowen formed in London in 2017 after singer Nina Saeidi and guitarist Shem Lucas met at an Akercocke show. Saeidi, born in Britain to Iranian parents, brought the turns of Persian tahrir singing into the band’s doom metal.[^formed][^show][^home][^sound]

## From the studio to a Brighton stage

In October 2017 the band recorded *A Crypt in the Stars*, with Saeidi on vocals and lyrics. They released it the following July. Their next record began as a benefit for the Green Door Store in Brighton. On *Unceasing Lamentations* Lucas played acoustic guitar while Saeidi sang and played daf and shruti box. They improvised the first two pieces onstage.[^debut][^debutdate][^lyrics][^venue][^benefit][^instruments][^guit][^imp]

## A chapter of the Shahnameh

For their second album, Lowen took a warning from the *Shahnameh* as their title. *Do Not Go to War With the Demons of Mazandaran* came out in October 2024 through Church Road Records and Lay Bare Recordings. In December 2025 they returned to that chapter in a different form. On *The Legend of Kay Kavus*, Saeidi reads the story behind the record aloud.[^record][^second][^book][^spoken]

At Bloodstock in August 2025 the band played the Sophie Lancaster Stage. The festival released the full performance the following year. At Download in 2026, a Persian daf stood among candles and incense on the Dogtooth stage before the musicians came on.[^blood][^filmdate][^dl][^daf]
