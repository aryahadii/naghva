Sejar Percussion Band is an Iranian ensemble built around the daf, a frame drum. [Zanyar Hesami](/en/zanyar-hesami-je5/) founded the group and wrote for its daf players. In 2021 the musicians released “Dafa Daf E Daf Mah Ha” with Sadolah Nasiri and Jivar Shikholesalami. At the closing concert of a Tehran festival for daf soloists that year, the ensemble played Hesami’s “Dlovan.”[^origin][^score][^single][^singers][^festival][^dl]

Working with the Nasl-e Khalil tanbur players brought the drums together with an ensemble of long-necked lutes. The musicians recorded a rehearsal in May 2022 before their two-night programme at Vahdat Hall that summer. Hazhir Mehr Afrouz sang and composed, Hesami arranged for the daf players, and Zohreh Kazemi managed Sejar. At Vahdat Hall, Hesami also played the solo part in his own “Butterflies Die in Two Stages.” He recalled his 2022 performance with Sejar there as his last in Iran.[^rehearse][^concert][^concertday][^site][^honour][^score][^manager][^solo][^year]

In the recording of “Yar Mara Ghar Mara,” the two ensembles accompanied Mehr Afrouz singing verses by Rumi. He arranged for the tanburs himself, while Kazemi directed the daf players in Hesami’s arrangement.[^words][^singer][^arrangement][^dir]
