When Saeid “Natch” Nadjafi was fifteen or sixteen, a friend gave him a Metallica cassette. By 2001 he was playing music with guitarist Shayan Amini.[^asylum-tape][^asylum-duo] Nadjafi began making electronic music on a computer after the police confiscated his instruments at an underground concert in Iran in 2007. With The Plastic Wave he made *[RE]action*. He then invited Amini to work with him again in [The Casualty Process](/en/the-casualty-process-mdm/). Their US visas had been denied in 2009. In March 2010, Nadjafi watched from Iran over Skype as an American band played The Plastic Wave’s music in Brooklyn. He and Amini moved to New York in 2011.[^stanford-denied][^stanford-skype] Nadjafi also wrote music for film. His 2013 piece “There Is Something Here” was written for Rodin Hamidi and Tony Comas’s documentary *Breaking the Devils Back*.[^iran-name][^dignity-instruments][^dignity-rock][^dignity-invite][^dignity-moved][^sc80788448-film]

Nadjafi had met Hana Sabri in Iran shortly before leaving on tour. She joined him in New York, and the two later put on *Exhale* in Austin, bringing aerial dance and a techno party into the same room. Some of their next sets took place at home. They had planned to take out their kitchen island, but put a mixer and decks on it instead. Friends began bringing friends. The gatherings gave their name to Kitchen Techno, the collective Nadjafi and Sabri founded in 2023.[^kitchen-place][^kitchen-join][^kitchen-dance][^kitchen-island][^kitchen-found]

The collective records its nights in Austin, including Sabri and Nadjafi taking turns at the decks. In October 2026 Nadjafi opened its record label with *Structura I*. Its four tracks run from “Vekta” to “Tyias.” The same season he worked behind the scenes on Blue Lapis Light’s aerial dance production *Carried by the Winds* as a production assistant.[^labelmusic-original][^labelmusic-number][^apple6818258616-track0][^apple6818258616-track3][^performance-credit]
