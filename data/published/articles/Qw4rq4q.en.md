Taraamoon is the Persian-language electronic duo of singer and lyricist Sara Bigdeli Shamloo and composer and producer Nima Aghiani. The two musicians were already recording together as [9T Antiope](/en/9t-antiope-yv3/) when they formed Taraamoon in Paris in 2020. Their album *Bādbān* brought seven Iranian rappers into a story about the survivors of a shipwreck.[^formation][^based][^sound][^vocals][^prod][^ship][^guests]

## The first songs

Living away from Iran, Shamloo and Aghiani missed working in Persian. They wanted a separate project for the songs they could write in that language. They explained the name Taraamoon as the diameter of a circle, joining a word for the distance from here to there with another for measure. Low-Zi released “Shezān” in April 2020 and “Zahāz” in July. That August they appeared with Atour on “Parākandeh-Rooz,” released by Divar.[^lang][^separate][^word][^meaning][^april][^july][^atour][^aug]

## The voices on Bādbān

The story of *Bādbān* begins when a storm tears the sails of a ship and scatters its passengers. For each rapper, the duo sketched music around the sound of the person’s voice and the cadence of their speech. REZ opened the album with “Pilār.” Aghiani produced, mixed and mastered the recordings, while Shamloo wrote and sang the passages that thread through the different narrators’ accounts. Amir B. Ash made the artwork and visuals. Low-Zi issued the album digitally and on CD in March 2021.[^ship][^sketch][^pieces][^prod][^vocals][^art][^cd][^album]

The duo continued to release individual songs, including “Hermās” in 2022, “Tamāh” and “Tāvā,” and “Malāll” in March 2026.[^single][^year][^tamah][^tava][^late]
