Abdi Behravanfar is a singer-songwriter born in Mashhad in 1975. A 2012 interview describes his music as bringing rock, country and blues together with Iranian folk traditions.[^bio] His own artist profile lists guitar, harmonica and dotar among his instruments.[^instruments]

## Collaboration and recordings

In the interview, Behravanfar recalls first meeting Mohsen Namjoo through Reza Khakshur around 2000.[^collaboration] His album Vel is available on the Abdi Behravanfar/MUD Bandcamp page,[^album] with a release date of January 11, 2024.[^date]
