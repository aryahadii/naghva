Abdi Behravanfar is a singer-songwriter from Mashhad, born in 1975. He plays guitar, harmonica and dotar and brings Iranian folk music into rock, country and blues.[^bio][^instruments] He formed the band MUD in Mashhad and later sang blues in Persian in London.[^mud][^london]

## A band in Mashhad

Behravanfar bought his first guitar at 23. His house became a meeting place for local musicians.[^guitar][^home] Around 2000 he met the singer [Mohsen Namjoo](/en/mohsen-namjoo-t6w/). The two sang Iranian folk songs together, and Namjoo began bringing his setar to play with him.[^collaboration][^ensemble] MUD gave its first concert in 2002 at Golestaneh Hall in Mashhad.[^concert]

## Shalamroud and the dotar

In Armenia, Behravanfar played his album [*Shalamroud*](#shalamroud-dwb) for a musician who told him that he could not hear which part of Iran Behravanfar came from. The question stayed with him. Back in Iran he began learning the dotar.[^armenia][^exchange][^dotar] He released *Shalamroud*, his first album, in 2008.[^debut][^debut-date] MUD followed it in 2009 with [*Kokheo & Kalakhet*](#kokheo-kalakhet-rh6).[^mud-album][^mud-date] In London, Behravanfar sang blues in Persian. His later recordings include [*Vel*](#vel-khj), released in 2023 and issued on Bandcamp in 2024.[^london][^vel-apple][^album][^date]
