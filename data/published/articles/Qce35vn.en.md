Loga Ramin Torkian is an Iranian-born composer and multi-instrumentalist who co-founded Niyaz and Axiom of Choice. The latter ensemble began with singer Mamak Khadem in 1992, drawing on the Persian radif while developing new compositional structures.[^identity][^ensemble]

## Mehraab

His solo album *[Mehraab](#mehraab-p4s)* features Persian classical singer Khosro Ansari and draws on Iranian melodies and poetry. In the label’s account, Torkian describes Ansari’s voice, vocal melodies and choice of poetry as integral to the album.[^voice]

Torkian says he used modern sound processing to extend the frequency range of his acoustic instruments.[^processing]
