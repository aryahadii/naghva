Loga Ramin Torkian is an Iranian-born composer and multi-instrumentalist. In the 1990s he built compositions around the Persian radif with the group Axiom of Choice. He later founded [Niyaz](/en/niyaz-9bb/) with the singer and composer [Azam Ali](/en/azam-ali-m7d/), bringing acoustic instruments and electronics together in settings of folk songs and Sufi poetry.[^identity][^ensemble][^niyaz]

## From Axiom of Choice to Niyaz

In 1992 Torkian formed Axiom of Choice with the singer [Mamak Khadem](/en/mamak-khadem-3wp/). He used the radif, the traditional repertoire of Persian music, as the basis for new compositions.[^ensemble] Torkian and Ali founded Niyaz in 2004. Their repertoire drew on folk songs from Iran and other countries around the Persian Gulf. In 2009 they decided to move from Los Angeles to Montreal.[^niyaz][^move]

## Mehraab

For his solo album [*Mehraab*](#mehraab-p4s), Torkian worked with the Persian classical singer Khosro Ansari. Ansari chose poems and shaped the vocal melodies. Torkian processed the sounds of his acoustic instruments to extend their range.[^voice][^processing][^recording]
