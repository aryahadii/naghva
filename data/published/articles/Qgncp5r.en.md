Nima Aghiani is a Tehran-born violinist, composer and producer based in Paris. On his 2013 album [*Pacific Ocean*](#pacific-ocean-a4s), he worked with samples of prepared piano, cello and violin. At the 2024 Eavesdrop festival he used a different pairing, his voice and a baritone violin. He also records with Sara Bigdeli Shamloo in [9T Antiope](/en/9t-antiope-yv3/) and in the Persian-language electronic duo Taraamoon.[^home][^ocean][^samples][^performance][^date][^ninety][^tara]

## From violin lessons to the Tehran groups

As a child Aghiani played with his parents’ cassettes like building blocks before he began listening to them one at a time. He took up the violin at Tehran Music School, continued his studies in Armenia and then in Odesa, and returned to Iran. He played in the Camerata Orchestra before forming bands. With Shamloo and Pouya Pour-Amin he played in the trio Migrain Sq., which performed at Madrid’s Veranos de la Villa festival in 2013. That December the Norwegian label Petroglyph Music released the five parts of *Pacific Ocean*. In a Persian review musician Hadi Bastani singled out the prepared piano and string samples.[^first][^tapes][^school][^travel][^orchestra][^trio][^madrid][^ocean][^samples][^critic]

## Solo recordings and collaborations

Aghiani began working with Shamloo as 9T Antiope in 2014. His solo album [*REMS*](#rems-9zp), released in 2018, took its title from rapid eye movement sleep. As he described it, the music grew out of the feeling of being caught between deep sleep and wakefulness. On the 2019 *Convergence Zone* he turned to the relationship between a player and an instrument, asking at what point a machine might feel like an extension of the body.[^ninety][^year][^sleep][^zd][^zone]

In 2016 Aghiani and Shamloo composed and performed live music for Ali Moini’s *Man Anam Ke Rostam Bovad Pahlavan*. Onstage Moini moved a life-size puppet through a system of ropes and pulleys. The duo later built an audiovisual performance with Rainer Kohlberger. They took it to Tehran’s Azadi Concert Hall in 2018 and to the CTM festival in Berlin the following January.[^puppet][^creation][^pulley][^stage][^teh][^berl][^rainer]

For [*Kandoo*](#kandoo-7n2), released in December 2023, Aghiani produced, mixed and mastered Safir’s rap songs. The two built the album around six smaller hives inside one large hive. In 2024 Aghiani played violin, octave violin and octave mandolin on 9T Antiope’s *Horror Vacui*. An excerpt from his Eavesdrop performance was issued by Karlrecords in February 2026.[^engineer][^guest][^album][^year2][^played][^nine][^performance][^reissue]
