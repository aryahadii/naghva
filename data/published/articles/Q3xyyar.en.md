In Madrid, Iranian flutist Kaveh Sarvarian brought ney, tombak and voice into a jazz ensemble with saxophone, keyboards, bass and drums. He named it Parsinava, joining the Persian words for “Persian” and “melody.” Their concert recordings range from a Kurdish song to a tasnif in Mahur. In 2018 they performed “Bidad” at Madrid’s Café Berlín, and later returned to it at El Intruso.[^name][^lead][^sounds][^early][^kurd][^tas]

In May 2024 Parsinava played El Intruso with Iranian singer Navid Norouzi. The group used the concert to present songs prepared for its first album. Sarvarian’s subsequent film from Camaleón Studio shows the recorded project taking shape with Norouzi on vocals, Sarvarian on flute, Pablo Hernández Ramos on saxophone, Javier Geras on bass, Denis Bilanin on piano and Miguel Gonzalo on drums. Shayan Fathi recorded the session.[^later][^new][^studio][^bass][^piano][^drums][^shayan]
