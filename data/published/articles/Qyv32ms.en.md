9T Antiope is the Iranian duo of Sara Bigdeli Shamloo and Nima Aghiani. Both came out of Tehran’s experimental music scene and have worked together as 9T Antiope since 2014. Based in Paris, they combine acoustic instruments, singing and electronics.[^bio][^history][^start][^paris]

## Songs and stories

In 2015 the duo released their debut album, [*Syzygys*](#syzygys-9z6), on Unperceived Records.[^history][^start] They usually begin an album by discussing the worlds of their stories, down to textures and the lives of individual characters. They cut up the written material to make lyrics. For Shamloo, part of the attraction is fitting words and melodies together.[^writing][^words] In January 2017 they released their own EP, [*Of Murk and Shallow Water*](#of-murk-and-shallow-water-khr).[^history][^date]

## A trilogy

Later in 2017 they released [*Isthmus*](#isthmus-q58) through the French label Eilean. It became the first in a trilogy with [*Nocebo*](#nocebo-3ef) and [*Placebo*](#placebo-xmz).[^history][^trilogy] The duo first conceived *Nocebo* as a live performance. Aghiani recalled that the prospect of premiering it in Tehran brought their feelings about the city into the music.[^live] They made *Placebo* in Paris for the 2021 Présences électronique festival.[^festival]
