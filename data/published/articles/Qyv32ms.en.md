9T Antiope is the Iranian duo of Sara Bigdeli Shamloo and Nima Aghiani. Their work combines acoustic instruments, voice and atmospheric electronics.[^bio] According to their CTM festival biography, the duo began working in Tehran’s arts scene before moving to Paris.[^history]

## Recordings

The debut album Syzygys appeared on Unperceived Records. The pair then self-released [Of Murk and Shallow Water](#of-murk-and-shallow-water-khr) before Isthmus appeared on Eilean Records.[^history] Their own Bandcamp page offers Of Murk and Shallow Water,[^album] with a January 2, 2017 release date and artwork credited to Sara Bigdeli Shamloo.[^date]
