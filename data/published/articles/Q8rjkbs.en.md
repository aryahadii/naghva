Naima is an ensemble associated with Iran’s jazz scene. The Cadence biography of [Hamzeh Yeganeh](/en/hamzeh-yeganeh-373/) places his work with the group after Abrang, among his ensemble activities in Iranian jazz.[^groups]

## Sahel-e Tehran

Discogs identifies the Persian name نایما with Naima and records the album title «[ساحل تهران](#sahel-e-tehran-persian-jazz-music-bdq)».[^identity][^title] Apple Music lists the album as Sahel-e Tehran - Persian Jazz Music, by Naima Band, Makan Ashkvari and [Mahan Mirarab](/en/mahan-mirarab-7hs/), and dates it to 2012. The listing names the artists for the album as a whole and does not specify their roles on individual tracks.[^album]
