Naima was an Iranian jazz ensemble led by [Hamzeh Yeganeh](/en/hamzeh-yeganeh-373/). After playing in Abrang, Yeganeh led Naima in performances of his own compositions.[^groups][^leader] For the group’s 2012 album *[Sahel-e Tehran](#sahel-e-tehran-persian-jazz-music-bdq)* he also arranged familiar Iranian songs for jazz musicians.[^album][^settings][^edition-title]

## Sahel-e Tehran

Yeganeh’s arrangements included the songs “Shekar-e Ahoo,” “Baroon Barooneh” and “Morgh-e Sahar.”[^settings] [Makan Ashgvari](/en/makan-ashgvari-n8j/) sang lead vocals on the album, which also brought the ensemble together with [Mahan Mirarab](/en/mahan-mirarab-7hs/).[^singer][^album]
