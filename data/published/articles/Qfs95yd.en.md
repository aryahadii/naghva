Dariush Azar is a singer, composer and double bassist from Tehran, and a former member of [Pallett](/en/pallett-q53/).[^profile][^birth][^pallett-status] His solo work includes the electronic arrangements of *[Blue Gray](#blue-gray-738)* (2019) and the acoustic music of *[40](#40-sat)* (2025).[^albums][^blue-description][^forty-description]

## Training and group work

Dariush was born in Tehran in 1983. He began music lessons at ten and entered Tehran University of Arts in 2002 to study double bass. After graduating, he joined Mohammad Reza Shajarian’s ensemble and later worked with Homayoun Shajarian.[^birth][^study][^ensembles]

His group work included Pallett, [Dang Show](/en/dang-show-8cs/) and Dal. By June 2018, he had left Pallett.[^ensembles][^departure]

## Solo recordings

In 2019, Dariush released his debut album, *Blue Gray*. He drew on a personal love story, combining his voice with electric instruments and electronic sounds.[^albums][^blue-description] On “[Vâveylâ](#vaveyla-f5c),” he sang with only double bass for accompaniment. He arranged the song and played every bass part himself.[^vaveyla-vocals-arrangement][^vaveyla-bass][^vaveyla-arrangement]

His second album, *40*, followed in 2025. With vocals and acoustic instruments, he used sparse arrangements to reflect on four decades of his life and musical work.[^albums][^forty-description] In May that year, he gave the first concert for *40* at Oscar Peterson Hall in Montréal.[^montreal][^first-forty]
