Afarin Nazarijou plays the qanun, a plucked zither, in Persian classical music and in improvised ensembles. In a 2021 solo in Chahārgāh she dedicated the music to women who had composed and played before her and whose names had been forgotten.[^instrument][^yearsolo][^hommage]

She began with the piano at five in Tehran. She went on to study qanun, played tombak at the Tehran Music School and took her first two degrees in Iranian music at the University of Tehran. Her early recordings include a piece by Ardavan Kamkar. On Siamak Jahangiri’s *Hamnavaz*, a book and album of eighteen pieces for ney and other instruments, she played qanun.[^tehran][^school][^tombak][^degrees][^early][^ham][^ney][^q]

In 2019 she entered the contemporary improvisation programme at the New England Conservatory in Boston, where she received the Peter Row Award. With singer Isabel Crespo Pardo, she rearranged Ali Akbar Sheida’s “Sourtgar-e Naghsh-e Chin.” Sheida wrote the song in Chahārgāh. Hearing Marzieh’s radio performance led Nazarijou to set her own arrangement in Mahur.[^boston][^award][^sourt][^marzieh][^radio][^sing]

The two musicians used to rehearse and listen together every week. After they moved to different cities, they began composing pieces that left their form open in performance, drawing on Persian modes and on poems in Persian and Spanish. Their [performance for New Music Edmonton](https://vimeo.com/686813948) sets the voice against the strings of the qanun. On Crespo Pardo’s *el rostro (des)cubierto*, guitarist Skyler Hill and bassist Seajun Kwon joined them. The quartet recorded in Boston over two days in May 2021, and the album came out in 2023.[^weekly][^moved][^texts][^quartet][^album][^bass][^session][^release]

In [two recordings with violinist Lucy Little](https://lucy-little-m7ly.squarespace.com/listen-music), both made in 2021, the qanun takes a different place in the ensemble. Little recorded their duet “Untitled, no. 1” on electroacoustic octave violin in her home studio. On the live “A trio, take two,” singer Delfina Cheb joins the two instrumentalists.[^little][^little3] Nazarijou also recorded qanun for Brendan Angelides’s score for the 2023 video game *Assassin’s Creed Mirage*. The opening theme is one of five cues on which Australian broadcaster ABC identifies her instrument.[^game][^abc]

Nazarijou completed a further degree at the University of Alberta in 2023. She has taught piano in Tehran and at Windermere Music Academy in Edmonton. On the title piece of PAN Project’s 2024 album *Borderless Flows*, her qanun shares the improvisation with Deepak Paramashivan’s sarangi and Jeff Roberts’s guqin. In 2025 she and Nağme Yarkın released a live recording of “Ayrılık.”[^alberta][^teacher][^instructor][^players][^sarangi][^guqin][^pan][^duo][^live][^year]
