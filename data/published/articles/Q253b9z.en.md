Gabgoo began in 2006 with Reza Abaee, [Hamid Saeedi](/en/hamid-saeedi-zen/), Henrik Nagi and Habib Meftah Bushehri.[^formation] Its archived website describes a project combining regional Iranian tunes and instruments with musical contributions from elsewhere in the world.[^approach]

## Multimedia performance

Farshid Behzad joined to develop performances incorporating music, dance, projected images and light.[^media] The first project took place in July 2006 with guest musicians Dara Daraee, Darshan Jut Singh and Arash Eslami and a performance by Mehdi Saki.[^first] The archived website says members continued collaborating online after moving to different parts of the world.[^distance]
