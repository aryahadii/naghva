Gabgoo was a band based in Tehran. Reza Abaee, [Hamid Saeedi](/en/hamid-saeedi-zen/), Henrik Nagi and [Habib Meftah](/en/habib-meftah-49p/) formed it in 2006 to play music that brought tunes and instruments from different regions of Iran together with music from elsewhere in the world.[^tehran][^formation][^approach]

## The first show

Farshid Behzad joined the musicians with an idea for staging their music with dance, projected images and light. For their first project, in July 2006, [Mehdi Saki](/en/mehdi-saki-3gk/) performed with the group and guest musicians including [Dara Daraee](/en/dara-daraee-cm9/).[^media][^first] Later, when the members were living in different countries, they continued working together online.[^distance]
