Marzi Montazeri was born in Tehran and moved to Houston with his family when he was nine. He got his first guitar at twelve. He went on to play lead guitar with Philip H. Anselmo and, later, with Exhorder. On Exhorder’s “My Time” he trades lead lines with Vinnie LaBella. On his own EP, *The Uprising*, he put his name on the sleeve and brought in Tim “Ripper” Owens to sing.[^born][^first][^exh][^solo]

With Heavy As Texas, Montazeri took on the producer’s job as well as the guitar parts. He and singer Kyle Thomas worked with drummer Anup Sastry and bassist Chris Collier on their 2019 album; Collier also mixed and mastered it. Montazeri later contributed guitar to “After Goliath” by the Iranian–Norwegian band Confess, appearing alongside drummer George Kollias.[^kyle][^stud][^texas][^mix][^guest]
