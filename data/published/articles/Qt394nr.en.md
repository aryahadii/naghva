Esfand is the electronic duo formed in The Hague in 2020 by Iranian musician Rouzbeh Esfandarmaz and New Zealander Patrick Stewart. They met at the conservatory, where Stewart sold Esfandarmaz a book on audio production. For his master’s thesis, Esfandarmaz was turning the music of Iranian rituals into dance tracks. He wrote each one around a particular region or custom. Stewart joined him to work out how to play the music live.[^duo][^year][^studies][^book][^degree][^region][^stage]

Their name comes from both the last month of the Persian calendar and the seeds burned as incense. In 2021 the duo remixed “Asfer” for the Tunisian artist Nuri. [^nuri][^tunis] Shouka released their first five tracks together on *Hélé* in March 2022. Habib Meftah played percussion on the title piece and Mohsen Namjoo sang on “Quiescence.”[^name][^calendar][^issue][^perc][^vox]

In early 2024 the duo spent a week at The Grey Space in The Hague preparing a concert using the Osmose, an expressive keyboard. On 1 February they played the first version for an audience.[^residence][^osmose][^test]

*Piltan* followed in June 2024. Its first two tracks draw on the repeated rhythms of southern Iran’s zār ceremonies. On “Bibi Sāghi,” hand drums play alongside a techno beat. Namjoo sings verses by Daqiqi and Farrukhi Sistani on “Shawl,” and Misagh Moradi sings a text by Rumi on “Without Foot, Without Head.” In August the duo appeared at Berlin’s Pop-Kultur festival on a programme with Stereotype and a conversation about Iranian music called “Sound of the Revolution.”[^album][^rhythm][^drums][^poetry][^rumi][^day][^venue][^concert]
