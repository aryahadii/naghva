Rana Farhan moved to New York after graduating from Tehran University. There she explored lacquer painting and became interested in jazz, particularly the music of Harlem.[^new-york]

## Albums and Persian poetry

With guitarist and producer Steven Toub she made her first full-length album, [The Blues Are Brewin’](#the-blues-are-brewin-kca), a collection of American jazz and blues standards.[^first] Their later work brought in the poetry of Rumi and Hafez.[^poetry] Her official discography gives [I Return](#i-return-g6f) the Persian title باز آمدم.[^return]

Farhan appeared in Bahman Ghobadi's No One Knows About Persian Cats.[^film] [Persian Soul](#persian-soul-q8h) was released on March 28, 2025.[^soul]

In its August 2018 interview introduction, VOA identified Talking ’bout Love as Farhan’s first experience of songwriting and described the influence of 1960s music.[^songwriting]
