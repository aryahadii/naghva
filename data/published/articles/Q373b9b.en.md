Hamzeh Yeganeh is a pianist and composer who draws on Iranian regional music in his jazz compositions and arrangements. He studied Baluchi music for his degree and played keyboards and banjo in the original lineup of [Damahi](/en/damahi-ep6/), founded in 2014. He has also arranged Persian melodies for people learning the piano.[^study][^band][^tunes]

## Playing in ensembles

Yeganeh played with Abrang and then [Naima](/en/naima-8rj/). His collaborators included [Peter Soleimanipour](/en/peter-soleimanipour-mgp/) and [Arin Keshishi](/en/arin-keshishi-ged/). He explored the music of Bandar Abbas in his work with Damahi and Majid Salari. In Damahi’s [“Neighborhood of the Sleepers”](#neighborhood-of-the-sleepers-r9k) he played piano alongside singer [Reza Koolaghani](/en/reza-koolaghani-kwr/).[^groups][^south][^recording]

## From the studio to the trio

Yeganeh recorded his compositions on [*Hand In Hand*](#hand-in-hand-feat-idin-sadeghzadeh-ssw) with electric bassist Idin Sadeghzadeh. He played piano, keyboards and synthesizers and produced the album himself. For a [live performance at Hatra Music Institute](#hand-in-hand-live-at-hatra-2024-e2j) in 2024, he and Sadeghzadeh played the material as a trio with drummer Amin Taheri. They also played “Mahabadi” and “Jaam-e Darvish”.[^studio][^live][^liveworks]

## Teaching through melodies

On [*Persian Tunes For Piano*](#persian-tunes-for-piano-remastered-nmy), Yeganeh arranged familiar melodies as a sequence of piano lessons. In pieces such as “Majnoon Naboodom”, beginners can practice left-hand rhythms. The patterns become more demanding as they progress, and chord symbols give them room to improvise.[^tunes][^tunescredits]
