Mahya Hamedi is an Iranian singer, pianist and composer based in New York. Born in Tehran in 1999, she started voice and piano lessons as a child after her mother heard her singing along to an animated film.[^iran][^voice][^us][^home][^keys] As a teenager, she turned to composition so she could keep working in music in Iran.[^focus]

## Writing for the theatre

Hamedi began studying classical piano at the University of Tehran in 2017. While she was a student, she wrote music for Neda Shahrokhi and Jean-Louis Heckel’s play *Après Cela* at Molavi Hall. With Diba Beihaghi she co-wrote *Is, Will Be* for Fanous Art.[^univ][^theat][^play] She released “Boyer” in 2019. The following January, as tensions rose between Iran and the United States, she released the anti-war song “Deng.” She sang and played piano on her arrangement of Amir Talebi’s words. The makers of *The Female Voice of Iran* interviewed her and used the song in their documentary.[^boy][^deng][^lyr][^film]

## Boston and New York

A scholarship took Hamedi to Berklee College of Music, where she studied jazz composition and performance. In a 2022 session for Berklee she joined the 1920s song “Daughters of Cyrus” to an anthem of the Woman, Life, Freedom protests. She also played dayereh, a frame drum, for oud player Bahar Badieitabar. The two belonged to Memoried Quartet with guitarist Guy Gottesfeld and percussionist Rafael Heredia.[^schol][^cy][^vid][^day][^quart]

That November she sang alto with the Pletenitsa Balkan Choir and helped prepare the scores for its concert with Tigran Hamasyan. She was also one of the choir’s section leaders. The programme paired David Halajian’s “Kondas” with Hamasyan’s arrangement of “I Should Care.”[^choir][^leader][^score][^kondas][^care]

In January 2024 she and pianist Ava Nazar gave the premiere of Niloufar Nourbakhsh’s *Dawn*, which the composer wrote for them.[^dawn][^piece][^composer][^premiere] In April Hamedi and Badieitabar curated *Peyvand* in New York. Hamedi played piano in her contribution. She graduated the following month and began teaching and working as a freelance composer and performer.[^pey][^peydate][^pia][^gradu] In December she released “Kakhe Goman,” produced by Amir Mankovski. She had set Fereidoun Tavallali’s poem to music before leaving Iran and had performed the song at *Let Her Sing* in 2022.[^record][^prod][^zur][^fest] In September 2026 she sang at the Cologne Philharmonie in *Female Voices of Resilience*.[^col][^coly]
