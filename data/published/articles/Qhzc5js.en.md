When Ehsan Matoori began playing with the musicians who became Borderless Band in 2017, the plan was to bring new works based on the Iranian radif to audiences in North America. Kamancheh player Kourosh Babaei was in Canada, percussionist Homayoon Nasiri in Iran. Neither could enter the United States for the planned tour. Five American dates were cancelled. The musicians chose the name Borderless Band after the cancellations.[^act][^start][^idea][^names][^ban][^tour][^name]

## The Berkeley concert

Matoori put together another lineup in the United States. On 20 July 2019 the group played Freight & Salvage in Berkeley. Sara Zare sang, and the sound of Matoori’s santoor sat alongside Mike Block’s cello, Ali Montazeri’s low-pitched bam tar and Jonathan Jones’s clarinet. Michael Nesuda played guitar and Yahya Alkhansa played drums.[^day][^place][^who][^sounds]

The concert survives in a full film published by Matoori and in individual numbers. One of them is “In the Name of You,” with Zare on vocals. The camera and sound capture the seven-piece US lineup assembled for that concert.[^film][^cut]

In December 2018 Matoori had asked listeners to help the musicians record *Dust & the Moon* independently. Babaei lists an album called *Borderless* in his discography for 2018. The surviving credits leave open whether those titles refer to the same recordings.[^fund][^fundtitle][^indy][^funddate][^disc]

In Canada, Babaei also performed under the Borderless Band name. His concert list places the group in Vancouver in April 2023. That July, Toronto’s Tirgan Festival billed Babaei and vocalist Fariba Davody together, with a programme that included Afghan, Kurdish and Persian music.[^vancouver][^tordate][^torwho][^torlang]
