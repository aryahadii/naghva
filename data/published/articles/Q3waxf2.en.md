Soheil Peyghambari is an Iranian clarinetist, composer and recording engineer. He plays bass clarinet and soprano saxophone with [Quartet Diminished](/en/quartet-diminished-h8f/) and works with electronics in his solo music. In [“Duet for Clarinet and Vacuum Cleaner”](#duet-for-clarinet-and-vacuum-cleaner-x42), a household appliance shares the billing with his instrument.[^iran][^studio][^quartet][^reeds][^electronics][^vacuum]

## Tehran recordings

Born in 1985, Peyghambari studied clarinet and composition at the Tehran Conservatory of Music and began working as a sound engineer at Kargadan Studio in 2008. His first album, [*Gamaaneh*](#gamaaneh-64z), appeared in 2014 under the English title *Assumption*. Radio France played music from it on *Les contes du jour et de la nuit*. He joined Quartet Diminished in 2016.[^iran][^study][^studio][^debut][^year][^broadcast][^quartet]

When Spanish percussionist Vasco Trilla visited Tehran in 2018, Peyghambari recorded a quartet with him, guitarist Ehsan Sadigh and drummer Roozbeh Fadavi at Kargadan. He played bass clarinet and soprano saxophone and mixed the recording himself. That September he recorded an improvisation with guitarist Franck Dadure at Radio France’s Studio 107.[^set][^mix][^reeds][^radio][^franck]

## Improvisation and film

In February 2020 Peyghambari met Trilla again at Soda in Barcelona. With double bassist Àlex Reviriego they played the improvised set that became [*Möhit*](#mohit-nf3). The trio recorded together again in autumn 2021. Those recordings came out in 2026 as [*Sacred Birds*](#sacred-birds-37p), with all three musicians credited for the music.[^barca][^trio][^drums][^rec][^issued][^own]

On *The First Realm* he played bass and B-flat clarinets alongside Kevin Kastning’s extended-range guitars, including a 36-string double contraguitar. The Slovak label Hevhetia released his second solo album, *Dysphoria*, in 2021. For Sadaf Foroughi’s 2022 film *Summer with Hope*, he played soprano saxophone and electronics and recorded, mixed and mastered the soundtrack at Möhit Studio.[^kast][^guitar][^solo][^director][^date][^film][^record]

In 2023, on Ehsan Sadigh’s *Impromptu and Friends II*, Peyghambari switched between soprano saxophone and bass clarinet. His 2025 duo recording *Parsec Modulator B1* pairs his two clarinets with William Doran’s brass, bass and drums.[^impromptu][^doran]
