Rameshgar is a band that [Golsa](/en/golsa-h74/), an Iranian singer and songwriter, began working with around 2023. When she spoke about the band that year, she said it was only a few months old.[^band][^early]

## The album named Rameshgar

In 2026 Golsa and the guitarist [Taha Mazaheri](/en/taha-mazaheri-66k/) released an album under their own names, [*Rameshgar*](/en/golsa-h74/#rameshgar-feat-hamed-hamidzadeh-mehrdad-mcy-farid-farzianpour-92e), a seven-song album with Hamed Hamidzadeh, Mehrdad Mcy and Farid Farzianpour.[^album][^guitar] Golsa wrote and sang “Ey La Morovvat”. Mazaheri arranged it and played guitar, with Hamidzadeh on bass, Mcy on keyboards and Farzianpour on drums.[^voice][^guitar][^bass][^keys][^drums]
