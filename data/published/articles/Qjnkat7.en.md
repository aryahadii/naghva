Hadi Pakzad is a singer and lyricist whose SoundCloud biography places his work in electronic, alternative and rock music in Iran. In that self-description, he dates his first album recording to 2002 and describes seven concept albums.[^self]

## Recording with Masoud Fayyazzadeh

Radio Zamaneh's introduction to an episode of منهای یک names Pakzad and Masoud Fayyazzadeh discussing their work in the underground music scene.[^interview] The connection also appears in the recording catalogue: [Darkness](#darkness-846) is jointly credited to Hadi Pakzad and Masoud Fayyazzadeh, rather than to Pakzad alone.[^credits] Apple's entry dates that album to 2015.[^darkness]
