Hadi Pakzad (1982–2016) was an Iranian rock singer and lyricist from Mashhad. He recorded and distributed concept albums independently, working for many years with composer Masoud Fayyazzadeh. After more than a decade of underground work, he released *[Darkness](#darkness-846)* as his first licensed album in Iran in the winter of 2015–16.[^birth][^self][^fayyaz][^licensed][^death]

## Starting in Mashhad

Pakzad learned piano and guitar with Naser Tofighi.[^lessons] In 2001–02 he recorded his first collection in a home studio. He later described it as an early, unpolished attempt and recalled the difficulty of obtaining permission to release an album.[^home] His early album *[Fear Land](#سرزمین-ترس-pqk)* circulated independently.[^fear]

Pakzad worked closely with Masoud Fayyazzadeh, a rock composer and producer who also played guitar in Minus One.[^fayyaz] In 2005–06 he and sound engineer Dariush Daneshnia established a studio together.[^studio]

## Albums and writing

Pakzad built his albums around shared musical or lyrical themes.[^self] His collaboration with Fayyazzadeh continued through the years in which he made *[Living Underground](#زندگى-زیرزمینى-5m9)*, *[Doctor](#دکتر-t8e)*, *[Aflatoon](#افلاطون-drg)* and *[For Four](#براى-چهار-fqx)*, distributing his work on the internet.[^fayyaz][^living][^doctor][^aflatoon][^four] He described his own approach as keeping the music simple and avoiding crowded arrangements.[^restraint]

He fitted recording around his other work. He said he kept musical and written notes during the year, gathered them each Esfand, the final month of the Iranian calendar, and then recorded. He tried to complete the songs in the following spring; the result might be a few tracks or an album.[^routine]

## The licensed release

After years of independent releases, Pakzad brought out *Darkness* with an Iranian publication licence in December 2015–January 2016.[^licensed] He died on 27 May 2016.[^death]
