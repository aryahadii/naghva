Rite of Strings is a quartet led by santoor player Ehsan Matoori and bassist Edward Perez. Violinist Eylem Basaldi and drummer Engin Kaan Günaydın complete the group. In May 2026 the four recorded their first album, *Dust and the Moon*, over two days in Long Island City, New York. Bright Shiny Things released the album that August under licence from Matoori. The New York launch was booked for 30 September at Joe’s Pub.[^direct][^santur][^bass][^violin][^drums][^studio][^when][^license][^launch]

The title piece takes its name from two images. In the introduction to its filmed performance, the Farhang Foundation describes dust as the traces left by people who have moved away. The moon, visible at a distance, stands for the hope that persists after a departure.[^theme][^moon]

The nine tracks give different members a turn at arranging the quartet. On “Ağıt (Lament)” it was Basaldi who wrote for the four instruments. Perez arranged the first *Gnossienne*. For “Qeshm,” the group brought in clarinettist Ismail Lumanovski. He appears with them in the filmed performance presented by Farhang.[^agn][^satie][^qeshm]

Matoori dedicated “Xur,” the album’s second single, to the memory of Bassam Saba.[^single][^dedication]
