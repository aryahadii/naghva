Psychic Bloom is a psychedelic rock group that began in Tehran and later settled in New York City. Its guitarist and singer, Aref Ahmadi, records as Hashill. He started the group with his schoolfriend Keivan, who played bass and organ. Their first album grew out of recordings they made themselves in Tehran.[^name][^tehran][^ny][^school][^ke][^ah][^tape]

## Recording the first album

Hashill liked the ringing guitar lines he heard in spaghetti westerns. He began writing his own songs, singing and playing the guitar parts while Keivan played alongside him. For the self-titled album, they recorded on tape in a bedroom. They took the drums to an unfinished building on the outskirts of the city and recorded them with a single microphone.[^western][^ah][^ke][^tape][^drums]

The album appeared in May 2017. Wiener Records issued it on cassette, and Detriti Records later put out a cassette edition of its own. Oracle Records issued the twelve songs on a numbered LP.[^y1][^wiener][^det][^vinyl]

## Mysterious Temple

By the time he made the second album, Hashill had sold much of the equipment he had used for the first. He borrowed a friend’s Vox amplifier for the guitar parts and wrote the album in about two weeks. He was then working largely on his own, with Michael Villiers playing drums. *Mysterious Temple* came out in July 2019.[^sale][^amp][^weeks][^solo][^mike][^y2]

The project continued as a band in New York City. Treefort Music Fest named Psychic Bloom in the first group of artists announced for its 2027 festival.[^ny][^date][^festival]
