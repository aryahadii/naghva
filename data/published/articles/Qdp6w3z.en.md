Peyman Salimi is a singer-songwriter, guitarist and music producer of Persian and Italian heritage. His work spans solo songwriting and the Florence indie-rock band The Allophones.[^bio][^band]

## Guitar studies and The Allophones

Salimi studied guitar with Alfonso Borghese at Florence’s Conservatorio Luigi Cherubini and the Fiesole Music School. He holds a BA in classical guitar performance and an MA in music and technologies from the conservatory.[^study][^degrees][^masters]

After his debut solo album, *[The Curtain](#the-curtain-5qy)*, he founded The Allophones in Florence. The band won the T-Rumors contest in 2011. Its first EP, *[Stalemate](#stalemate-dae)*, followed in 2013 with Antonio Aiazzi’s involvement; *[Muscle Memory](#muscle-memory-w8b)* appeared in 2017, co-produced by Tiziano Borghi and Eduardo de la Paz.[^band][^band-work]

## Returning to Persian songwriting

After nine years with the band, Salimi returned to solo recording with the self-produced *[Won Now](#won-now-2f7)* in 2019, his first album to include Persian songs.[^won] In a 2019 interview, he described a way of writing across languages: composing in English and then translating into Persian helped him find words that fitted his music. He also described recording and producing his material in a home studio, with the exception of the drum parts.[^writing][^home-studio]

## Production and collaborations

In 2020 he established Justaudio Studio in Brussels.[^studio] His collaborations with [Sanam Maroufkhani](/en/sanam-maroufkhani-a84/) include *[Nahan](#nahan-hqx)*, on which he rearranges a song credited to Ahmad Wali and contributes vocals, guitar, bass and synthesizer. Maroufkhani sings and plays piano.[^nahan] He also plays electric guitar and handles mixing and mastering on her *[When You Left Home](#when-you-left-home-4yc)*.[^when-guitar][^when-mix]

*[The Psychology of Letting Go](#the-psychology-of-letting-go-smz)*, his third solo album, was released in 2023. Salimi describes it as a challenge to idealized images of love. He recorded, produced and mixed the album; its credits place that work at Justaudio in Brussels and Brooklyn.[^psychology][^production] [Sara Naeini](/en/sara-naeini-b23/) sings on “Tab,” and Zoe Guigueno sings lead on “Livin’ It Up.” His former Allophones bandmates also contribute.[^sara][^zoe][^guests]

Salimi and The Allophones share writing and performance billing on *[Kian](#kian-28e)*, released on 15 January 2026 and recorded at London’s Maida Vale Studios.[^kian][^kian-date][^kian-studio]

## Music technology

His studies also extend to music technology: Georgia Tech lists his MS in the subject in 2026. At its Computational and Cognitive Musicology Lab, his research examines how lyrics affect the perception of music.[^technology][^research]
