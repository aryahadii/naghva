Blurred Vision began in Canada with two Iranian brothers, Sepp and Sohl Osley. During a gig, they changed the refrain of Pink Floyd’s “Another Brick in the Wall” to “Hey Ayatollah, Leave Those Kids Alone” in response to the protests in Iran in 2009. Sepp recalled sending the recording to Roger Waters before releasing it. With his blessing, the brothers made a video with filmmaker Babak Payami. It went online in February 2010.[^brothers][^line][^green][^debut][^video][^year]

## From a video to a trio

The brothers still needed a drummer. Producer Terry Brown introduced them to Ben Riley, who joined Sepp on guitar and vocals and Sohl on bass. In 2012 they released the six-track *Democracy* EP on their own label.[^early] The three recorded *Organized Insanity*, released by Cherry Red in 2015. Four of its songs, including “The Keeper” and “Promise”, were remixed for *Manhattan Undying*. The soundtrack album also preserves music written for the film that never made it into the final cut.[^ben][^trio][^deb][^sound][^songs][^bonus]

In 2018 Bentley Levy took up the bass and Jake Bradford Sharp joined on drums. For their single “Magdalena”, Sepp built the melody around the call of a collared dove.[^dove] Two years later, Blurred Vision backed the singers at the *Dear John* concert for War Child, playing songs by John Lennon and the Beatles. Sepp ended the band in 2023 and began releasing records in his own name.[^new][^benefit][^end]
