Sote is the recording name of Ata Ebtekar, an Iranian experimental electronic producer.[^alias] His work includes Sacred Horror In Design on Opal Tapes, developed from a commission by Berlin's CTM festival and combining electronic processing with traditional Iranian music.[^practice]

## Ornamentalism

His Bandcamp page presents Ornamentalism as a collaboration between Sote / Ata Ebtekar and the Iranian Orchestra.[^album] The material was recorded with the orchestra in Tehran and reworks scores by Alireza Mashayekhi through Ebtekar's electronic treatment.[^process] Bandcamp gives 10 March 2017 as the release date of this edition.[^date]
