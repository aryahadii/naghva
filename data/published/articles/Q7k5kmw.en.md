[Ash Koosha](/en/ash-koosha-gfa/) and Negar Shaghaghi formed the indie electronic pop duo Take It Easy Hospital in Tehran. Their songs found listeners on Myspace and their experiences helped shape Bahman Ghobadi’s film *No One Knows About Persian Cats*.[^origins][^duo][^film]

## From Tehran to Manchester

Koosha started the duo with Shaghaghi after his earlier band, Font, stopped playing. They recorded a demo of “[Human Jungle](#human-jungle-wtj)” in July 2008. Listeners encouraged them to keep going, and they made a second song, “Television Blues.” A drummer and a bassist joined them onstage.[^duo][^song][^origins]

Their Myspace audience brought an invitation to Manchester’s In The City festival. Around the same time Ghobadi asked them to work with him on his film about underground musicians in Iran. The film won the Un Certain Regard Special Jury Prize at Cannes in 2009. While playing further dates in the United Kingdom, Koosha and Shaghaghi decided to seek asylum there.[^duo][^film] The duo released “[Hidden Spaces](#hidden-spaces-single-d27)” in 2019.[^hidden]
