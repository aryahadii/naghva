Take It Easy Hospital is an indie, electronic and pop duo formed in Tehran by [Ash Koosha](/en/ash-koosha-gfa/) and Negar Shaghaghi.[^duo][^origins] Its early biography describes a two-person core joined by a drummer and bassist for performances.[^origins]

## From Tehran to the screen

The band's early account dates the demo recording of [Human Jungle](#human-jungle-wtj) to July 2008 and names Television Blues as the next song.[^song] Koosha's label biography places the duo after his work with Font and describes its growing audience on Myspace and an invitation to Manchester's In The City festival.[^duo]

Koosha and Shaghaghi appeared in No One Knows About Persian Cats. Ninja Tune's biography recounts the film's success at Cannes in 2009 and the musicians' decision to seek asylum while in the UK.[^film] The duo's later catalogue includes Hidden Spaces, a 2019 single.[^hidden]
