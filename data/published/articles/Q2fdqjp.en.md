Justina is a rapper, singer and songwriter from Tehran. She wrote classical Persian verse as a child and began writing rap in her teens. Her songs address the rights of women in Iran. After leaving Iran for Georgia in 2018, she continued making music in Piteå in northern Sweden.[^birth][^poetry][^teens][^text][^move][^res]

## From poetry to rap

Justina was already listening to American rap when, at sixteen, she heard the Iranian group Zedbazi. Hearing rap in Persian gave her another way to write about what she felt. In Tehran she worked with the underground hip-hop group Tanbe10.[^teens][^group]

## Tbilisi and Piteå

For a concert streamed in 2020, Justina worked with musicians in three countries. Jamin took part from Tbilisi, Yasmin Peyman from Russia, and the Lemonoise Band from Iran. Later that year Justina moved to Piteå for a two-year residency with the International Cities of Refuge Network.[^tbilisi][^guests][^res]

In northern Sweden, Justina and Jamin toured schools across Norrbotten with their band, performing for teenagers over ten days. In June 2025 the two played together at Piteå’s summer music festival, Festspelen.[^school][^festival][^year]
