In 1384 SH, Peter formed the Peter Soleimanipour Ensemble to perform his compositions. Across the concerts covered by the Iranian Musicians Union obituary, performers included [Mahan Mirarab](/en/mahan-mirarab-7hs/), [Habib Meftah](/en/habib-meftah-49p/) and [Dara Daraee](/en/dara-daraee-cm9/).[^formation]
