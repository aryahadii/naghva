The composer and wind player [Peter Soleimanipour](/en/peter-soleimanipour-mgp/) formed the Peter Soleimanipour Ensemble in 2005 or 2006 to perform his own music.[^composer][^formation] By the summer of 2008 he was working with the ensemble in workshops in Tehran.[^workshops]

Different musicians played Soleimanipour’s pieces in concert. Among them were [Mahan Mirarab](/en/mahan-mirarab-7hs/), [Habib Meftah](/en/habib-meftah-49p/) and [Dara Daraee](/en/dara-daraee-cm9/).[^formation]
