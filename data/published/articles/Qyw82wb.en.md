Nikan Khosravi was a teenager when he started Confess in Tehran in 2010. He began with guitar and came to singing and writing his own words later. For a group working outside Iran’s licensed music industry, getting a recording to listeners was part of the job: in 2014 he founded Opposite Records. The band had already issued its debut, *Beginning of Dominion*, and its next release was a six-track EP.[^start][^beg][^opposite][^ep][^band]

The writing of *Revenge at All Costs* followed him from one country to another. Released on bail in Iran, he worked on the music there, then wrote lyrics in Turkey. In Norway he recorded the guitars, bass and vocals. The finished record opens with Iranian news audio flowing into “EVIN.” On *Destination Addiction*, Khosravi also produced the album and shared executive-production duties with his bandmate Arash Ilkhani.[^turkey][^mix][^writer][^intro][^nikan][^exec]
