Roham Sobhani is a composer and tar player born in Saqqez and raised in Tehran. He began playing the tombak at six and took up the tar at nine. He studied Persian literature before studying composition. In 2004 he formed the [Zhouan Music Ensemble](/en/zhouan-music-ensemble-tyg/), hoping to draw its repertoire from different parts of Kurdistan.[^born][^start][^training][^formation][^aim]

Zhouan won a prize at the Fajr Music Festival in 2008. That recognition did little to secure its concerts. Sobhani told Rudaw how officials in Sanandaj agreed to let the group perform only after changing the announced dedication from singer Mazhar Khaleghi to Imam Ali. The first show went ahead and the two that followed were cancelled. A later tribute to singer Hassan Zirak in Bukan was also called off.[^award][^posters][^shows][^bokan]

Sobhani composed the albums *گفت‌وگوی دل*, *فسانه* and *پیر میخانه*. He also wrote for individual voices in his ensemble. For *Desire to Fly*, released during its 2018 European tour, he set a poem by Forugh Farrokhzad for [Arezoo Banipour](/en/arezoo-banipour-pzd/).[^old][^albums][^poem][^program]

After the November 2019 protests, Sobhani argued in an interview that artists should speak alongside other people. He set Farrokhi Yazdi’s words for Banipour in “The Cage Which Traps You,” dedicated to the victims of those protests and of the Ukrainian airliner shot down two months later. For “The Banner of Tresses,” dedicated to Mahsa Amini, he wrote the lyrics himself.[^response][^prison][^lyrics][^singer][^dir][^words]
