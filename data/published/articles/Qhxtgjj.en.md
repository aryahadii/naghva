Farzad Golpayegani is a guitarist, composer and visual artist born in Tehran. He combines progressive metal with Eastern musical traditions.[^origin][^style][^practice] He made his first electric guitar records in Iran and later moved to Istanbul, where he could distribute his music more widely.[^delay][^turkey][^istanbul]

## The first records

Golpayegani grew interested in rock and metal through the music his older brother played. As a teenager he bought his first electric guitar.[^brother][^guitar] His first album, [*One*](#one-tpc), came out in 2004 after a two-year delay. He made some CDs himself, but most listeners found his recordings through his website.[^delay][^online]

By November 2009 he had moved to Istanbul.[^turkey] On [*Six*](#six-afz), released in 2012, he returned to music he had written for the video games *Quest of Persia: Nader’s Blade* and *Garshasp*.[^six][^six-date]

## Music and painting

Golpayegani gives many of his compositions and paintings numbers so that a title will not tell the listener or viewer what to expect.[^titles] On [*Nine*](#nine-hsq) in 2022 he sang, played guitar and five-string violin, mixed the recordings and designed the cover. Scott Collins joined him on acoustic guitar for “Reaching The Horizon”.[^nine][^credits] In 2026 Golpayegani released [*Ten*](#ten-m2v).[^ten][^ten-date]
