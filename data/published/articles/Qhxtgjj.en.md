Farzad Golpayegani was born in Tehran, Iran.[^origin] He combines progressive metal with eastern musical elements. His biography describes an independent practice in which he produces his own work, plays electric and acoustic guitar, bass and violin, and collaborates with guest musicians.[^style][^practice]

## Numbers as titles

Many of his compositions and visual works carry numbers as titles. He explains this choice as a way to let listeners and viewers encounter the work without a verbal title setting their expectations.[^titles]

## Nine

His ninth album, *Nine*, was released on October 14, 2022. Golpayegani performed the vocals and instruments, arranged, mixed and mastered the album, and created its artwork. Scott Collins contributes acoustic guitar to “Reaching The Horizon.”[^nine][^credits]
