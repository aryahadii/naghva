Sarakhs is an Iranian alternative rock band. Its SoundCloud profile dates its formation in Tehran to 1998 and names Behzad Khiavchi, Yuri Akop and Peter Akop as its members.[^band]

Ebrahim Nabavi’s آوازهای زیرزمین dates the formation to 1378 of the Solar Hijri calendar, differing from the band profile’s 1998 date.[^book-formation]

According to Nabavi, the band recorded its first album, [از خاک](#از-خاک-6p7), at «بم» studio between 1378 and 1380. It circulated informally and later online without a formal release.[^first-album]

Nabavi identifies مرداد 85 as the second album. Recorded live at «آفتاب عاَلمتاب» studio, it was released by «بمآهنگ» in 1386.[^mordad]
