Sarakhs is an alternative rock band that formed in Tehran around the turn of the millennium. Singer Behzad Khiavchi started the band with brothers Yuri Akop on drums and Peter Akop on bass.[^band][^book-formation] Their first album passed from listener to listener. For their second they recorded live in the studio.[^first-album][^mordad]

## The first two albums

The trio recorded [*Az Khak*](#az-khak-6p7) at Bam studio around 2000. Copies circulated underground and later online.[^early][^first-album] Their second album, [*Mordad 85*](#mordad-85-weh), reached listeners through the label Bamahang in 2007.[^mordad][^second-date]

## Returning to the band

After a long break, Khiavchi began working with Sarakhs again in 2021–22.[^returning] In 2023 the trio released two singles, [“Rooye Mah Ghadam Gozashtim”](#rooye-mah-ghadam-gozashtim-mej) and [“Be Man Begoo Chera?”](#be-man-begoo-chera-qje).[^moon][^why][^titles][^single-dates-moon][^single-dates-why]
