Sepidar Ensemble is an Iranian classical music group led by tar player [Siavash Imani](/en/siavash-imani-mkh/). Formed in the mid-2010s, it plays pieces by earlier composers alongside music by Imani and its santur player [Aria Mohafez](/en/aria-mohafez-cvf/). Their music is based on the dastgāh, the system of modes and melodic phrases that shapes Persian classical composition and improvisation.[^ifound][^date][^contrasting][^repertoire][^tc]

## Tash

Imani brought together musicians who had been friends for years and shared an interest in the music of the Qajar period. For [*Tash*](#tash-2021-edition-m7x), released by Mahoor in 2021, he worked with singer Mehdi Emami. He conceived the album as a *majles*, a gathering in which musicians move between instrumental pieces and exchanges between voice and instrument. The sequence leaves them room to depart from the usual order. [Houshmand Ebadi](/en/houshmand-ebadi-xwe/) played ney and [Sina Danesh](/en/sina-danesh-q6a/) played kamancheh, with Mohafez on santur and Pedram Khavarzamini on tombak.[^contrasting][^tcover][^tdate][^tintent][^tform][^tln][^tc]

## With Sepideh Raissadat

In November 2021 the group accompanied singer Sepideh Raissadat at the Alhambra in Geneva. Imani arranged a programme that set the ensemble’s own music alongside pieces by early twentieth-century composers including Parviz Iranpour and Reza Mahjoubi. Sina Khoshk Bijari played tombak, and Imani played both tar and setar. Swiss radio recorded the concert.[^igeneva][^gdate][^gprog][^gprog2][^gprog3][^gcast]

By 2026 oud player Yasamin Shahhosseini had joined the concert lineup. The group and Raissadat played together again at Transart in Bolzano. The Musica Sacra programme in Maastricht billed dastgāh improvisations and compositions alongside a work by Mazyar Kashian for voice, quarter-tone piano and percussion, for performance with Ensemble Musikfabrik.[^toud][^tcdate][^salto][^mat][^mo]
