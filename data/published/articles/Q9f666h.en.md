Arash Ilkhani helped found Confess in Tehran with Nikan Khosravi. On the 2015 album *In Pursuit of Dreams* he appears as “Chemical,” the DJ and sampler player alongside the guitars, bass and drums. On *Revenge at All Costs* he worked at the turntables again. The record begins with fragments of Iranian broadcasts; the band lets those voices lead into the opening song, “EVIN.”[^band][^nickname][^decks][^intro]

The 2015 arrest of the two musicians followed the release of *In Pursuit of Dreams*. After Khosravi had left Iran via Turkey, the musicians reunited in Norway. There, playing for school audiences meant learning when to put the instruments down and speak. Producers David Chocron and Gine Heien worked with the group on that part of the performance.[^arrest][^two][^school]

Ilkhani joined Khosravi in Harstad after his bandmate secured a residency there. In a Persian-language interview he recalled the surprise of Norwegian listeners when they learned that the musicians playing metal were Iranian. By the time of *Destination Addiction*, Ilkhani and Khosravi were the album’s executive producers. The personnel list still places Ilkhani at the turntables and sampler, with George Kollias on drums.[^res][^audience][^exec][^arash][^greece]
