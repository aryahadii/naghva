Cyminology is a Berlin jazz quartet that builds its music around the sound of Persian verse. Singer Cymin Samawatie founded it in 2002 with a repertoire that grew out of her studies in Berlin. Her teacher Jerry Granelli had encouraged her to try new arrangements for Persian poetry. Pianist Benedikt Jahnel, bassist Ralf Schwarz and drummer Ketan Bhatti joined her in the quartet.[^group][^teacher][^quartet]

## From the reed flute to Saburi

Samawatie recalled that the group’s first self-made recording was a live set sung entirely in English.[^live] The quartet’s first two albums, *Per Se* and *Bemun*, appeared on Cologne jazz label Double Moon. For their first ECM release in 2009 they chose a title from the opening of Rumi’s *Masnavi*. *As Ney* begins with his song of the reed flute and also includes settings of Hafez and Forough Farrokhzad.[^first][^next][^early][^ecmyear][^reed][^poems]

On *Saburi* the lyrics were all Samawatie’s. She wrote the title piece and *Shakibaai* for the 2009 Morgenland Festival in Osnabrück while following the suppression of the post-election protests in Tehran. A change in the group’s instrumentation came with *Phoenix* in 2015. Violist Martin Stegner, a collaborator since 2011, joined the quartet on record for the first time. They dedicated the album to Farrokhzad.[^lyrics][^songs][^protests][^viola][^ded][^year]

Cyminology also performed with Tobias Backhaus on drums. In 2019 Berlin’s Pergamonmuseum invited musicians to respond to objects in its collection. For *Echoes Stereo Bounce*, the quartet chose the carved façade of Mshatta.[^backhaus][^museum][^mdate]
