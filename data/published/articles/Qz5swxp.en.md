Sara Bigdeli Shamloo is a Tehran-born singer, lyricist and composer based in Paris. She records as SarrSew and sings with [9T Antiope](/en/9t-antiope-yv3/) and [Taraamoon](/en/taraamoon-w4r/). Her own recordings include songs for plays, film music and a stage work about the deaths of two of her brothers.[^where][^jobs][^alias][^duos][^score][^wood][^lost][^plan]

## From the orchestra to the theatre

Shamloo was born in 1991. Her mother wrote poetry and books, and two of her brothers played in metal bands. She learned classical violin and met the composer Kayvan Mirhadi through a friend in his Camerata Orchestra. Joining the orchestra brought her into contact with Nima Aghiani and Pouya Pour-Amin. In 2011 she became the singer of their trio, Migrain Sq. She also studied theatre directing at the University of Tehran before leaving the course.[^where][^mother][^metal][^violin][^orchestra][^meeting][^trio][^year][^school]

In 2013 she released music she had written for *Pinocchio*, including a piece for the scene in which the wooden boy is made. She also composed the closing credits for the short film *Summer Afternoon*. As half of 9T Antiope, she wrote the words and sang on the duo’s 2015 album *Syzygys*. In 2018 her solo piece [“Farewell, Warden.”](#farewell-warden-q8g) appeared on Zabte Sote’s *Girih: Iranian Sound Artists*.[^wood][^date][^score][^syzygys][^cd][^girih][^firstdate]

## Songs for the stage and on record

For the 2019 Forecast Forum in Berlin, Shamloo developed *Loss in the Living Room*, a performance that grew from her experience of losing two brothers. She planned to bring a moving machine and projections onto the stage alongside music and spoken monologues. Her collaborations with the theatre company kainkollektiv also produced separately released songs. She wrote “Reptile” and “Last Body Standing [Falls]” for *GAIA: A Cyborg Opera* and “La Laliki Land” for *The Golden Age of Extremes*.[^lost][^plan][^berlin][^time][^oper][^opera][^stage]

Shamloo also wrote the libretto for Ali Moini’s stage work [*±*](https://vimeo.com/537631017), adapting Moini’s original texts for music by 9T Antiope.[^libretto][^musicmoini]

Apranik Records included her song “Wronka” on its 2023 collection *Woman, Life, Freedom*. For BBC Music Life that December, she chose recordings by Arvo Pärt and Ben Frost for a programme she called [*Beloved Sorrow Blue My Blood*](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p0gzlcf6).[^mix][^music] In 2025 she appeared with Saeed Dehghan on two songs from Ali Sorena’s album *Mojassameh*, “Khaneye Sorkh” and “Charkheshe Rangha.”[^label][^wronka][^red][^colour][^guest][^latest]
