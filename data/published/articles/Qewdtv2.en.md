Ensemble Delgosha is an Iranian classical group led by ney player and arranger Siamak Jahangiri. In February 2012 the musicians recorded two suites at Delârâm Studio, one in Afshari and one in Dashti. Singer Pantea Alvandipour had spent eight years studying the style of Qamar-ol-Moluk Vaziri, particularly the breaks in the voice that gave a phrase its dramatic force. On the Afshari suite she exchanges phrases with Hamid Khansari’s oud. In Dashti the group gives a solo to kamancheh player Elmira Mardaneh.[^director][^group][^studio][^afshari][^suites][^vaziri][^voices][^khan][^kamancheh]

The recording came out as *Iran: Music of the Qajar Era* on 12 May 2014. Five days later, Alvandipour sang at the Louvre auditorium in Paris. The following evening the same ensemble accompanied Maede Tabatabai Niya in Jahangiri’s own compositions. Alongside his ney were Maryam Gharasou on tar, Ali Bahrami Fard on santur and Pasha Karami on tombak. For both singers, whom restrictions on women’s public performances had kept from the stage in Iran, the Paris concerts marked their stage debuts.[^album][^date][^dates][^sunday][^maede][^parisplayers][^debut][^irani]

In January 2018 Delgosha performed at Tehran’s Rudaki Hall with singer Hosein Alishapour. He shared a minute of the final rehearsal for the concert, which drew on the album *Tanha*. The group opened its set with an instrumental introduction in Homayun. A short santur improvisation led into a longer one on ney, before the oud and tar players took their turns in the opening section of the mode.[^concert][^rehearsal][^hall][^datef][^mode][^sequence][^solos]
