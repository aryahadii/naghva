Balzan is an indie electro-rock duo formed by singer Amir Balafshan and guitarist
and composer [Nima Ramezan](/en/nima-ramezan-3cs/).[^duo] They began working
together around 2018 and released their debut album, *[Balzan](#balzan-b5x)*,
in early 2019.[^started][^esfand] They set poems by Nosrat Rahmani to music throughout the album
and returned to his writing in later songs.[^poems][^sorood][^parande][^boose]

## Starting Balzan

Balafshan, a singer and multi-instrumentalist from Rasht, had worked with the
city’s indie-rock band [Makhoola](/en/makhoola-v24/) and had a solo project called
Davvar.[^rasht] Ramezan had worked with [Comment Band](/en/comment-band-ec3/), Pulse
and [O-Hum](/en/o-hum-6v5/).[^earlier-bands] By April 2018 the two were working
under the name Balzan.[^channel] Balafshan said of the partnership: “Balzan isn’t my project or Nima’s; it took
shape through our collaboration.”[^collaboration]

## The debut album

During 2018, Balafshan and Ramezan released songs they had composed together using
Rahmani’s poems.[^payan][^mahtab] They spent more than a year and a half making
their first album, which Javan published in 2019. They launched it in Tehran and
Isfahan and performed at its launch in Rasht.[^launches][^credits][^started][^rasht-launch]

Speaking after the release, Ramezan said he was no longer interested in playing
solos. While making the album, he had not thought about genres or how many people
might listen to it.[^approach]

## 2020–2021

During the stay-at-home period in April 2020, the duo reworked
“[رنگین‌کمان](#رنگین-کمان-bfg)” with Mahbod Shafinejad.[^home][^rangin]
That June they released “[سرود گم‌شده](#سرود-گم-شده-fdr),” which they composed
together using Rahmani’s poem “Pandora.”[^sorood]

Balafshan and Ramezan recorded a [concert at Tehran’s Ruberu Mansion](#balzan-live-in-tehran-2021-fjd)
with bassist Arash Saeidi, drummer Bardia Amiri and cellist Parsa Hadi.[^online-stage][^video][^lineup]
The recording was scheduled to stream online in January 2021.[^online-date][^online-stage]

## Later work

Balzan continued performing in Tehran, including concerts in December 2024 and
at Kakh Honar in January 2025.[^azar][^kakh] In June 2025 they released
“[Parandeye Abi](#parandeye-abi-vjt),” for which Balafshan set Rahmani’s poem
“Namak” to music and sang it. Ramezan arranged the song and played the instruments;
Amiri played drums.[^parande][^parande-date] Later that year,
“[Kissed by the Wind](#kissed-by-the-wind-vq7)” drew on Rahmani’s poem “Mard-e
Bi-sar.”[^boose]
