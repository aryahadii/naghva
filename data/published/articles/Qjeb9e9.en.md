Shervin Hajipour is an Iranian singer and songwriter. He performed his own song *Shayad Behesht* on the Iranian television talent show *Asr-e Jadid*. In 2022, as protests spread across Iran after the death of Mahsa Amini in custody, he turned a series of social media posts into the song *Baraye* (“For”).[^singer][^show][^protest][^tweets]

## Learning to write for his own voice

Hajipour began violin lessons at eight. By twelve he was inventing melodies when he was supposed to be practising his exercises. His teacher sent him to another instructor, and he took up composition. He taught himself guitar and piano.[^violin][^teacher][^composer][^guitar]

He began writing songs on commission and sang the demos himself so clients could hear the melodies. When one asked why he did not sing professionally, he started practising his voice. In 2019 he appeared on *Asr-e Jadid* on Iranian television.[^demo][^voice][^tv]

## A song written from tweets

People were writing on Twitter about what had brought them into the streets. At his piano at home, Hajipour set their words to music. In the video, the posts appear on screen as he sings.[^piano][^screen] Released online, *Baraye* became an anthem for the Woman, Life, Freedom protests. In February 2023 the Recording Academy gave the song its first award for Best Song for Social Change.[^tweets][^protest][^date][^award]

## Recording after Baraye

Hajipour was arrested after the song came out. In *Ashghal* (“Garbage”), he sang about the restrictions imposed by the security services and the courts. In September 2024 he announced that he had been pardoned and that his case was closed.[^song][^case][^when]

He sang *I Have You*, the title theme for the Persian version of the Pakistani drama *Meray Paas Tum Ho*, for Dot Republic Media.[^ost][^pak] His voice was also heard in the Iranian series *Vahshi*.[^filming]

In December 2025 he announced a licensed album called *Vaqei* (“Real”), with distribution through the Arad Cultural and Artistic Institute. When listeners questioned his decision to seek a permit, he said he wanted to stay in Iran and keep working there.[^album][^dist][^work]
