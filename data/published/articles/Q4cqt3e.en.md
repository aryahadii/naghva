Adrienne Shamszad is an Iranian American singer and songwriter from the San Francisco Bay Area. She accompanies herself on guitar, piano and setar. She made her first album, *A New Life*, after returning from India and later studied Persian singing with Mahsa Vahdat and setar with Atabak Elyasi.[^adrkit][^adrsess][^setsight][^atclass]

## First songs and A New Life

Shamszad was twelve when she first picked up a guitar. Her mother showed her two blues songs, “Trouble in Mind” and “St. James’ Infirmary.” A year later she was writing her own. After college and five months singing and travelling in India, Shamszad recorded *A New Life* with producer Phil Ludwig between 2006 and 2008. The sessions drew on blues, jazz, bluegrass, reggae and Indian classical music.[^adr12][^adrblues][^adrsess][^adralb]

## Persian singing and setar

Shamszad met Mahsa Vahdat through a 2016 project with the Kitka Women’s Vocal Ensemble. In 2018 she received a Living Cultures grant to study Iranian singing with Vahdat and the next year presented her bilingual repertoire at Freight & Salvage in Berkeley. She also took up the setar.[^actagrant][^freight] In 2020 she worked with Elyasi on instrumental radif, including unmeasured pieces and the ornaments she would use in improvisation. *Love You Without Fear* gathered songs from concerts and sessions held between 2015 and 2018. In 2023 she released her second full-length album, *Wash It All Away*.[^adrkit][^setsight][^atclass][^adrlove][^adrwash]
