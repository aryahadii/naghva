Yalda Zamani is an Iranian-German conductor based in Berlin. Born in Algiers and raised in Tehran, she first stood in front of an orchestra as a singer in a choir. She later took up live coding, writing computer code onstage to make music with electronic musicians including Hadi Bastani and Rojin Sharafi.[^identity][^born][^choir][^conduct][^two][^code][^instruments][^rojin]

## From the choir to the orchestra

Her father was an architect. In one of his books on Le Corbusier, Zamani discovered the convent of La Tourette. Its patterns of light and shadow drew her to the music of Iannis Xenakis, who had helped design the building.[^architecture][^xenakis]

In Tehran in 2009, Zamani studied composition and music theory privately with Mehran Rouhani and Houshang Ostovar. The following year she met Vijay Upadhyaya while singing in his choir at the Austrian Cultural Forum. One day he asked her to conduct the orchestra. The piece she was handed was Schubert’s Fifth Symphony. In 2011 she passed the entrance examination for Georg Mark’s conducting class in Vienna.[^study][^mentors][^invite][^choir][^conduct][^first][^vienna]

## Electronics and ensemble playing

At Berlin’s tak Theater Aufbau Kreuzberg in June 2021, Zamani and Hadi Bastani improvised together using electronic modules and live code. The performance came out in two parts in August 2022 as [*TWO*](#two-live-at-tak-theater-aufbau-berlin-pcb).[^two][^code][^release]

A disagreement about rhythm between Karlheinz Stockhausen and Aphex Twin gave Zamani the idea for the [*Goat Song Project*](#goat-song-project-1-m5a). ORF musikprotokoll commissioned the piece. At its October 2022 performance, Zamani wrote code live, Rojin Sharafi played electronics and Shiva Feshareki used turntables.[^goat][^players][^instruments][^rojin][^shiva][^time]

Zamani also founded the Chamber Orchestra Elbe. On 12 December 2023 she conducted its debut at Hamburg’s Elbphilharmonie with harpsichordist Mahan Esfahani. The next month she joined Pierre Bleuse at Ensemble intercontemporain in Paris as assistant conductor. At the La Maestra competition in March 2024 she rehearsed Fanny Hensel’s Overture in C major with the orchestra before conducting it. The [Philharmonie’s film](https://philharmoniedeparis.fr/en/live/concert/1165024-la-maestra) includes both moments.[^hensel][^quarter] She also recorded Amen Feizabadi’s chamber opera *Deep Siesta* with Zafraan Ensemble and Neue Vocalsolisten Stuttgart. The recording came out in September 2026.[^ensemble2022][^recital][^debuthall][^debut][^paris][^pierre][^opera][^ensemble][^operadate]
