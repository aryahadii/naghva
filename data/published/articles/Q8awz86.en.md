Mohammad Laryan joined the Bushehr ensemble Siriya around 2017. In his own introduction to listeners, he called himself the one who was always trying to make the other players laugh, even on stage. When he sat down to write for *A Table for Two*, he imagined two people in a café. For Laryan, the intervals of Bushehri music could tell a story about everyday life in the city without a boat or a seascape in sight.[^joined][^jokes][^cafe]

Laryan sang the songs on the album. On “Sher Yadom Ra,” recorded at Abnous Studio, he wrote the lyrics as well. For an earlier Siriya song, “Sedam Mal-e To,” he supplied the words and Sajad Bahadori the music and arrangement. Laryan later shared the billing on “Delom Dar Asheghi” with Ehsan Matoori and Siriya. His voice also appears outside the ensemble’s catalogue, on “Jabel” and “Leyli & Shirin”; the 2026 single “Jombeisa” brings him together with DJ Phellix, Seventh Soul and Ario Vibes.[^singer][^poem][^studio][^early][^delom][^otherb][^otherl][^otherj]
