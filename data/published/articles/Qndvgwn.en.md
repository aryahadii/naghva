Badieh are a group formed in Mashhad by Iranian tar and setar player Mohammad Miraghazadeh and Spanish oud and rubab player Michel Gasco. Their name means “steppe” or “desert” in Persian. They arrange songs from Greater Khorasan, drawing on a repertoire shared across the border between Iran and Afghanistan. Afghan tabla player Ramin Ahmadi played on their debut and was part of the trio that recorded their second album.[^lisbon-formed][^music-loc][^one-member0][^one-member1][^sbs-meaning][^one-sides][^one-member2][^two-trio][^two-member2]

## Finding a shared repertoire

Gasco met Miraghazadeh on a visit to Mashhad to study the Afghan rubab. His teacher was Nasim Khushnawaz, a player of the Herati style, while Miraghazadeh had studied with the Iranian tar master Mohammad Reza Lotfi. The two began playing folk pieces they both knew from Khorasan and formed Badieh in 2018. When choosing instruments for a piece, they allowed the arrangement to depart from the version they had learned.[^one-meeting][^two-teacher][^two-study][^one-shared][^lisbon-formed][^one-choice]

The two sides of the border can be heard on [their first record](#badieh-vhf): “Paresh-e Jal” comes from Herat in Afghanistan, while “Leyla Dar Vakon” is an Iranian song. They recorded the six tracks in Mashhad and Madrid. The Australian label Worlds Within Worlds released the record in 2021.[^one-sound][^one-studio][^one-date][^two-debut]

## A trio in Mashhad

On [*Badieh II*](#badieh-ii-q6z), recorded in Mashhad and released in 2023, the two founders again arranged the pieces. Miraghazadeh and Gasco brought together singers and instrumentalists with whom they had studied or played. On “Asheq Bodom Leili,” Gasco’s rubab teacher Khushnawaz plays alongside singer and harmonium player Jawad Tabesh. The opening song, “Yar Golakom,” pairs the voices of Ehsan Nasibi and Golnaz Hariri with bass tar and daf.[^two-date][^two-recording][^two-arranged][^two-teacher][^two-member11][^two-member12][^two-member6][^two-member7][^two-member8][^two-member9]

For an April 2024 concert in Rabat, the programme named a different trio: Gasco, Ahmadi and double bassist Toño de Miguel.[^rabat-trio][^rabat-date] In October 2025 Miraghazadeh and Gasco played at the OzAsia Festival in Adelaide with Pranav Ramji on tabla. Miraghazadeh sang as well as playing setar.[^adelaide-show][^adelaide-year][^adelaide-guest][^adelaide-sang][^adelaide-setar]
