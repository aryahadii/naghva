Nesa Azadikhah is an Iranian DJ, producer, composer and sound artist born in Tehran in 1984. She founded Deep House Tehran in 2014 and later co-founded Apranik Records with AIDA. Her work includes solo electronic music, remixes and production for singers. She moved to Paris in October 2023.[^bio][^deeprhythms-0][^trans-1][^polychrome][^groove-1][^mixmag-0]

## First sounds

Azadikhah began learning tombak at six and went on to explore daf, santur and tar. In her teens she began DJing and then producing music with software.[^trans-0][^mixmag-2][^innate-0] She learned much of electronic production independently, through the internet and friends. Her interest in noise and atmosphere also led her to record the sounds of the city and use them in her music.[^questions-0][^questions-1][^mixmag-1]

## Deep House Tehran

In 2014 Azadikhah started Deep House Tehran, a platform through which she could promote electronic artists and make material available in Persian. The project grew beyond online activity to public events in Tehran’s art galleries.[^trans-1][^trans-2][^groove-0] Travel was harder: she recalled repeated visa refusals before an invitation in 2019 finally brought an accepted application.[^deeprhythms-1][^deeprhythms-2][^deeprhythms-3]

Azadikhah’s own productions continued alongside that work. When she remixed Johanna Burnheart’s “Never Let Me Go”, she was dissatisfied with her first result and made another version with a different atmosphere and genre. Both were released, as the 4am and 9am remixes; she preferred the latter.[^tanz-0][^tanz-1][^tanz-2]

## Apranik and collaborations

Azadikhah and AIDA’s label Apranik began releasing music with *Woman, Life, Freedom* in January 2023. The label grew out of the protests in Iran in 2022, and the two planned to direct the proceeds to charities helping women in Iran. Azadikhah contributed “Sarnevesht”.[^cause-0][^cause-1][^compilation][^compilation-date][^sarnevesht] Later that year she moved to Paris and released *Truth Will Out*. In 2024 she and AIDA released *Polychrome*, which included her pieces “Where Can Be Home” and “Levitate”.[^mixmag-0][^truth][^polychrome][^polychrome-date][^home][^levitate]

She also makes music for singers through the Peeleh collective. Her production work includes Bahram’s album *Heech*.[^groove-1][^bahram]
