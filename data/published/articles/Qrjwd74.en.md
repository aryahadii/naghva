Nesa Azadikhah is an Iranian DJ, producer, composer and sound artist. Mixmag describes her work as blending field recordings with techno, ambient, breaks and IDM.[^bio][^sound]

## Releases and platforms

Alongside her solo work, she has established labels with AIDA and supports Iranian artists through the editorial and streaming platform Deep House Tehran.[^platform]

Her Bandcamp page dates *[Truth Will Out EP](#truth-will-out-ep-yac)* to December 23, 2023. She offers the EP through SoundCloud and Bandcamp, with mastering by Nima Aghiani and artwork by Kiana Bahrami.[^album][^date][^own][^credits]
