Jetpack is a band from Tehran that mixes electronic production and guitar with funk and disco. Keyboardist and producer Mohammad Shariat began it in 2017 with guitarist Majid Heidari; his younger brother [Arian Shariat](/en/rian-vhg/) became its singer and songwriter, and Erfan Aseminejad its drummer. After the English-language [*Joystick EP*](#joystick-ep-h5c) in 2019, the band turned back to Persian songs on [*Kafi Nist*](#kafi-nist-qvv) in 2022.[^identity][^founders][^joining][^sound][^joystick][^kafi][^persian]

## Beginnings

Mohammad had played in so many bands that broke up that he called himself a master of them. Tired of it, he proposed an electronic project to Majid, and the two meant to work as a duo.[^disband][^duo][^founders] They recorded with a vocoder, and it did not turn out as they wanted. The songs were meant to be in Persian, so Mohammad asked Arian, six years younger than him, to sing. Jetpack was the first project the brothers made together.[^vocoder][^persian-plan][^six-years][^brothers]

The two Persian tracks they put out first pleased neither the band nor many listeners.[^language][^persian-tracks] Arian sang mostly in English and could write lyrics in English, so Jetpack became an English-language band. He wrote his first English lyric for it in a shared taxi from Sanat Square to Resalat. Pop in Iran, Mohammad said, was getting worse by the day, and they chose to make the music they enjoyed.[^arian-english][^english-lyrics][^taxi][^pop-worse] Their first English song was [“Forbidden Love”](#forbidden-love-yk4); Mohammad remembers the Persian [“Shab”](#shab-n54) as the very first track they released.[^language][^shab-first]

## Joystick

In May 2019 Jetpack released the *Joystick EP*, six songs it produced itself.[^joystick][^joystick-production][^ep-tracks] Each member brought a part. On “Off the Road”, Majid wrote the riff, Mohammad the production, Arian the vocal melody, and Erfan played acoustic drums. From the start Mohammad was the producer, and since the band has no bass player he plays the bass parts himself. Fewer than five bands in Iran, he said, produce all their own music this way.[^quarter][^producer][^bass][^few-bands] Erfan had joined to play drums and electronic percussion for their live shows.[^drums]

Only Mohammad made his living from music in 2021. Arian was a student, Majid had another job, and Erfan ran his own studio. The others were older and already played many concerts, so Jetpack rarely played live; that year they decided to plan shows, on their own terms.[^living][^jobs][^older][^concerts] Its House No.4 profile counted only two live shows since the band began, at the Tehran Electronic Music Festival and at Ruberu Mansion.[^two-shows] Because they sang in English, many listeners abroad could not tell where the band was from or that its members were Iranian.[^where-from][^not-iranian] Mohammad rejected the label “the Iranian Daft Punk”.[^daft]

## Persian songs

In June 2020 Jetpack released [“Noghteh Ha”](#noghteh-ha-feat-sanam-maroufkhani-single-z2e) with the singer [Sanam Maroufkhani](/en/sanam-maroufkhani-a84/).[^noghteh][^noghteh-date] Later that year the band played a [set](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yY-m1-JgAc) for Gate of Tehran, a month-long virtual festival of live and recorded performances.[^gate][^gate-video]

With *Kafi Nist*, released in April 2022, Jetpack returned to mostly Persian lyrics and worked with singers from different backgrounds: Makan Ashgvari, Sharyar and [Golsa](/en/golsa-h74/).[^kafi][^persian][^album] Golsa wrote the music and words of [“Khakestar Shod”](#khakestar-shod-feat-golsa-single-zzr), which Mohammad produced.[^khakestar] Ali Eshqi recommended the album in beehype’s selection of Iranian albums from 2022, and the radio host Siavash Rokni chose it among his best of the year for CISM.[^beehype][^beehype-author][^cism][^cism-context]

## Later work

In November 2024 the brothers said Jetpack had not ended or broken up; they were taking some distance before deciding what came next.[^not-ended] Arian had started his own projects.[^own-project] In January–February 2025 the band played in the Bijar series, organised by Made in Iran and 8Box, which filmed its [performance of “Kafi Nist”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ttnu2Etkg6I).[^bijar][^bijar-title] That April it released [“Joone Jaddet”](#joone-jaddet-single-5ym), composed by Arian and Mohammad.[^joone-music][^joone-date]
