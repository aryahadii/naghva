Raz-e Shab was an Iranian band founded by keyboard player Ramin Behna. Behna played several concerts with the group, whose first album, already in circulation by 2003, was *[Dar-E-Qali (The Rug Weaver’s Loom)](#dar-e-qali-the-rug-weavers-loom-phb)*.[^profile][^concerts][^credits][^album][^feature-date][^roster]

## Dar-E-Qali

By 2003 the band had recorded the album with singers Houman Javid and Farzam Rahmi. Behna played piano and synthesizer, [Arash Sobhani](/en/arash-sobhani-eca/) played electric and acoustic guitars, and Shahrooz Khajeh Molaie played drums.[^credits][^roster][^feature-date] The members later went their separate ways.[^split]
