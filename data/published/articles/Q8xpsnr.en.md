Raz-e Shab is an Iranian band documented by Iranian Alternative Radio's artist feature in 2003.[^profile][^feature-date]

## Daar-e Qaali

The feature lists the musicians on Daar-e Qaali: Ramin Behna on piano and synthesizer, Shahrooz Khajeh Molaie on drums, [Arash Sobhani](/en/arash-sobhani-eca/) on guitars, Houman Javid and Farzam Rahmi on vocals, and Zohreh Davoodi, Niloofar Paidar and Farnaz Amini Rad on backing vocals. Ardavan Anzabi-Pour and Homayoon Nasiri are explicitly identified as guest musicians on bass and percussion.[^credits]

The recording is available as [Dar-E-Qali (The Rug Weaver's Loom)](#dar-e-qali-the-rug-weavers-loom-phb) in Apple's catalogue.[^album] Its 2010 platform date describes that catalogue entry; it cannot be the beginning of the band's history, since the earlier feature already discusses the album.[^catalogue][^profile][^credits]
