Avin Ahmadi plays the oud and sings. Born in Tehran in 2004, she now lives in Vienna. At seven, walking past a classroom, she heard someone playing the oud. When the time came to choose an instrument, her parents had to search for one small enough for her to play. In 2015 she entered the Tehran music conservatory, where her teachers included Negar Bouban and Hojat Farajian. She later moved to Austria to study jazz improvisation at Anton Bruckner University in Linz.[^origin][^heard][^instrument][^school][^teachers][^study]

## A song from Portugal and a solo set in Vienna

Ahmadi wrote “Dopelk” on a trip to Portugal. On the recording she sings her own verse and plays oud alongside Michael Alves on violin. She released it in August 2023. That October she gave a solo concert at Porgy & Bess in Vienna. Two pieces from the set came out as a live single. The concert also gave rise to her solo project Qayeq, the Persian word for a boat.[^travel][^lyrics][^duo][^debut][^venue][^two][^boat]

## Poems and musical partnerships

In June 2025 Ahmadi recorded *Liminal* in Vienna. The words that run through its first three tracks come from one poem, Masiha Barzegar’s “Ab‘ād-e Vojūd-e To.” On the seventh and final track, “November,” pianist Reza Tavakoli shares the composition credit. For Hideout Records’ *Carte Blanche* series, the label paired Ahmadi with trumpeter Lorenz Widauer. The two had never met before making *Until We Meet Again*.[^studio][^lyrics2][^seven][^trumpet][^lorenz]

Ahmadi first met singer Sanam Maroufkhani in person on a visit to Amsterdam. On their live recording “Beneath the Wooden Roof,” Maroufkhani sings her own words, Ahmadi plays oud and sings, and Tavakoli plays piano. All three share the composition credit.[^sanam][^roof]
