Aria Mohafez began learning both the santur and tombak at seven in Tehran. With Majid Kiani he spent five years studying the santur style of Habib Somāi. In 2007 he joined Mohammad Reza Lotfi’s Hamnavazan-e Sheyda as a santur player, recording with the ensemble and travelling to concerts outside Tehran. The films of those years preserve his introductions in Homayun and Dashti.[^r1][^r2][^r3][^r4][^r5][^r6]

After Thursday rehearsals with Sheyda, Sanaz Sattarzadeh encouraged him to join her for lessons on the dotar with Babak Rahati. Mohafez later traced his song “If Only Her Eyes” to those lessons and the music of northern Khorasan. He also recorded duets with tombak player Pedram Khavarzamini on Sarva and Peyda. At the Mugam Festival in Baku in 2018 he won second prize for solo instrumental performance.[^r7][^r8][^r9][^r10][^r11]

For the Ān Ensemble he wrote Luminous Sunshine, a lament for Lotfi sung by Mehdi Emami. Among its pieces is a chaharmezrab that Lotfi had improvised and Mohafez adapted for the whole ensemble. The group performed the programme at Niavaran in 2018, before its studio release. In 2021 he toured Switzerland with Sepidar, directed by Siavash Imani with Sepideh Raissadat on vocals. In Araz Salek’s 2025 Peripheries of Nahavand his santur joins a project shaped by Iranian, Ottoman and Arab composition.[^r12][^r13][^r14][^r15][^r16][^r17][^lotfi][^arrange][^lament]
