Zambra Collective is a quintet whose five singers each play a string instrument. Tehran oud player Bahar Badiei met bassist Devon Gates, guitarist Ria Modak, cellist Queralt Giralt Soler and violinist Ángela Varo Moreno at Berklee in Boston. They took their name from an Arabic word for a gathering.[^members][^met][^name]

They played at Berklee’s Red Room in May 2025. In June, clarinetist Jahnvi Madan joined them at Roundhead Brewing in Boston.[^may][^june]

The Detroit Jazz Festival commissioned their song cycle *Songs for Our Mothers* for its 2025 edition. They dedicated it to the women who raised them, drawing on the Catalan sardana dance and boat songs from India’s west coast.[^cycleidea][^sounds] On August 31 the five played on the Riverfront stage. For the sound crew, amplifying the quiet wooden instruments together posed a challenge. Two films from the performance show how different musicians wrote for this lineup. Gates brought “A Room,” and Badiei brought “Embraced by the Flowing Water.”[^detroit][^cycle][^date][^river][^wood][^room][^water]

Varo Moreno wrote “Bulla,” whose title evokes the noise and excitement of people gathering in Andalusia. Kara Snethlage engineered the quintet’s performance at The Record Co. The musicians released the film on their own channel in February 2026.[^bulla][^noise][^engineer][^rec][^issued]
