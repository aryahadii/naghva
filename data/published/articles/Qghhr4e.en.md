ASADI grew up in Columbus, Ohio. As a Persian American producer, he builds electronic pieces from instruments he plays himself, including Persian setar, Kurdish tambour and Turkish saz. The drum pad is also a stage instrument: he triggers the layers of a beat by hand, bringing the work of arranging a track in front of the audience.[^ohio][^instr][^rhythm]

That approach travels between the concert stage and the game score. ASADI composed the music for *The Rogue Prince of Persia*; the complete soundtrack has 24 tracks, including a song with Xye. The pair also performed the music live at Gamescom. During his 2024 residency at Shangri La in Honolulu, he worked with ukulele player Taimane Gardner on a new piece for an appearance at Capitol Modern.[^game][^score][^gamescom][^museum][^xye]
