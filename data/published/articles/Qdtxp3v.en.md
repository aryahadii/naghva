When Saeid Shanbehzadeh founded his ensemble in 1990, he brought the public music of Bushehr to the concert stage: wedding songs, religious music and dances in which people join the rhythm with their hands. The group’s roster has included percussionists Habib Meftah and Naghib Shanbehzadeh. Depending on the occasion, the ensemble could bring out a dammam drum, cymbals and a horn alongside Saeid’s bagpipe and double pipe.[^troupe][^hab][^pipes][^public]

In Paris in 2007 the ensemble performed music associated with the zâr ritual and the ornamented singing of sharveh. For globalFEST in New York in 2009 Saeid and Naghib performed a five-piece set that moved from “Ney Tofti” to “Sharvel.” A few months later the Orient Festival in Tallinn recorded the two live, with Naghib on tombak and zarbetempo.[^paris][^ritual][^global][^est]

The group’s 2009 Buda recording includes Mahmoud Bardaknia on dammam and singer Abdollah Moghateli Motlagh. On the 2011 album *Jazz & Music from South Iran*, the players made room for another set of instruments. In “Choupi,” Saeid sings and plays bagpipe alongside Matthieu Donarier’s soprano saxophone and Manu Codjia’s guitar. The ensemble also played the Shanghai World Music Festival that May.[^buda][^jazz][^years][^choupi][^guitar][^choupipipes][^festival]
