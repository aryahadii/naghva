King Raam is the stage name of Ramin Seyed-Emami, an Iranian rock singer and songwriter who began with the post-punk band [Hypernova](/en/hypernova-53g/). He turned from English-language group songs to Persian solo recordings in 2011. After returning to Tehran for several years, he continued his career abroad, extending his music into theatre and the Persian podcast *Masty o Rasty*.[^identity][^iranian][^career][^return][^ny-news][^departure][^podcast]

## Hypernova and Persian songs

Raam began teaching himself guitar after a friend left an instrument at his home in Iran. Friends looking for a singer recruited him because he spoke English well.[^left-guitar][^guitar][^recruitment] Around 2004 he formed Hypernova, which played in Tehran’s basements without official permission.[^basements]

After years of singing in English, he wanted to try Persian. He launched his solo career in Toronto in 2011 with *[Songs of the Wolves](#songs-of-the-wolves-jb6)*, setting words by Tara Aghdashloo to music. Its softer acoustic rock differed from Hypernova’s post-punk.[^english][^persian][^solo][^lyrics][^career]

## Back in Tehran

Raam returned to Iran in 2014 and began working with a new band. He performed in theatre but could not obtain permission for an official concert.[^return][^theatre-iran][^permission] He recorded *[A Day & a Year](#a-day-a-year-247)* in Tehran, then released *[Until a Thousand and ...](#until-a-thousand-and-3gm)* in 2017, his last album made while based there.[^tehran-recording][^last-tehran]

“A Day Will Come,” from *A Day & a Year*, looks back on a circle of friends scattered around the world. The writer Ali Eshqi described it as a song of nostalgia.[^song][^song-album]

## Music and storytelling

His father, environmentalist Kavous Seyed-Emami, died in Evin Prison after being detained in 2018. Raam recalled that he had been back in New York for several weeks when he heard the news.[^father][^ny-news]

During a 2019–20 residency in New York, Raam developed *Departure*, a solo storytelling performance joining his music to his family’s experience and his father’s legacy.[^residency][^development][^family-music][^departure] In 2020 he began the Persian podcast *Masty o Rasty*.[^podcast][^podcast-start]

He was booked to appear as musician and storyteller in Javaad Alipoor’s *Things Hidden Since the Foundation of the World* at Sydney Opera House during Sydney Festival in January 2024.[^sydney-role][^sydney-venue][^sydney-date]
