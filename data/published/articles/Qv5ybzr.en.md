Moody Moussavi, also known as Mohammad Moussavi, is an Iranian guitarist, singer, composer and music producer.[^identity] His recordings range from songs named after Gilaki dishes to settings of Baba Taher’s poetry and a joint album with Mehrad Hidden.[^food-reading][^oryan][^zoo]

## Early work

He grew up in Rasht, the son of a religious eulogy singer, reciting the Qur’an and hearing Shi‘i devotional music. Rock and heavy metal reached him through *Guitar Hero*; he began playing guitar at twelve, inspired by *Guitar Hero III* and Slash.[^childhood][^guitar] His band work included singing and lead guitar in Makhoola, a connection reported by the newspaper *7Sobh*.[^band] On the 2014 single [“Nadari”](#nadari-bsv), he composed the music and Saeid Moussavi sang.[^nadari-date][^nadari]

## Food Court and collaborations

His solo debut [*Food Court*](#food-court-xjp), released across 2019–2020, brought together his composition, playing and singing.[^food] The dishes supplied more than titles: writing about the record, researcher Alireza Doostdar described its use of northern Gilaki food to tell stories of love, loss, desire and violence.[^food-reading] On [“Khorous Fesenjan”](#khorous-fesenjan-yqs), Moody and Arash Monsef wrote in *bahr-e tavil*, a poetic form used for both humour and mourning. Moody told Doostdar that its structure closely followed a song he had found in his father’s eulogy collection, redirecting its musical and emotional force toward humour.[^metre][^intention]

On [“Mirza Ghasemi”](#mirza-ghasemi-srf), he worked with bassist Maziyar Khavajiyan and drummer Borna Shafizadeh.[^mirza] In 2020 he sang with [Golsa](/en/golsa-h74/) on [“The Poet”](/en/golsa-h74/#the-poet-feat-golsa-single-xf7), their reworking of the Martik and Googoosh song with words by Zoya Zakarian.[^poet-date][^poet-voices][^poet]

## Later albums

In 2022 he and Mehrad Hidden released [*Zoozanaghe*](#zoozanaghe-n69).[^zoo-date][^zoo] That July, [“Nish”](#nish-z9p) introduced his forthcoming solo album [*Oryan*](#oryan-24g), which followed in April 2023. Its words were drawn from Baba Taher Oryan’s poetry; Moody supplied vocals, guitars and songwriting, Sina Saeidi produced, and Shafizadeh played drums.[^nish-date][^nish][^oryan-date][^oryan] The [title track](#oryan-feat-aida-shahghasemi-tgr) featured Aida Shahghasemi and bass by Khavajiyan.[^oryan-guests]

He returned to Baba Taher’s poetry on [“Khoongar”](#khoongar-vw8) in 2024.[^khoongar-date][^khoongar] He continued working with Saeidi on the 2026 album [*Songs for the Sunken*](#songs-for-the-sunken-xqq). He played guitar and wrote the music; Saeidi produced the album.[^sunken-date][^sunken]
