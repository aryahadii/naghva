Babak Amir Mobasher began teaching himself classical guitar at sixteen in Tehran and gravitated towards flamenco. With Shahin Alvai and Aïda Nosrat he played in Ea. He and Nosrat went on to form Manushan, in which his guitar shared the melody with her voice and violin. At Brecon Jazz Festival’s online edition, the opening number gave him two jobs: starting the song alone and supplying its percussion by striking the guitar.[^birth][^guitar][^early][^met][^duo][^stage][^brecon] Alongside guitar he also played cello. In May 2008 he appeared with Nader Mashayekhi and the Tehran Chamber Orchestra at Die Bühne im Hof in St Pölten, Austria.[^strings][^austria][^city] In November 2015 he and Nosrat left Tehran on a French skills-and-talents visa. They settled in Ivry-sur-Seine.[^move][^ivry]

Mobasher lists three Manushan CDs in his own discography. Dreyer Gaido issued *Beyond the Vision*. Manushan released *Swing Me to Your Song* itself, with Kayvan Jahanshahi producing. The album billed to Aïda & Babak followed on Accords Croisés. In France Mobasher also founded Aïka with singer Hura Mirshekari, a separate partnership from his work with Nosrat.[^beyond][^swing][^producer][^newcd][^aika]
