Soheil Nafisi is an Iranian singer, composer and guitarist born in Tehran in 1967. Self-taught as a musician, he spent more than twenty years in Hormozgan, whose musical traditions influenced his work.[^bio] He sets Persian poetry to music, from Hafez to Ahmad Shamloo and Nima Yooshij, with a spare, narrative approach centered on a voice and a single instrument.[^poetry]

## Recordings

His recordings include Rira, Songs of the South, Chang and Sorood, and The New Design.[^albums] Rira is available in his Apple Music catalog,[^rira] with a listed release date of December 25, 2005.[^date]

Concertzender identifies a Hermes Records CD, catalog HER-027, with a 2013 date. This differs from the 2005 date in the digital listing; these catalogs do not settle the original issue date.[^hermes]
