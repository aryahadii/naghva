Pouya Mahmoodi was ten when his father introduced him to the acoustic guitar in Tehran. As a teenager in France, he began picking out Iranian melodies he remembered from home. He would later sing and play guitar in the Tehran rock group [Barad](/en/barad-s2n/). In a film of a Tehran concert, he opens the set alone on electric guitar. At Moods in Zurich he plays a ten-string guitar, improvising with bassist Luca Leombruni and drummer Remo Signer.[^press-age][^press-tehran][^press-france][^press-roots][^sit-group][^vsqqxrthlpn4-concert][^vexwmrn430k8-zurich][^vexwmrn430k8-bass][^vexwmrn430k8-drums] He also played acoustic and electric guitar on Soheil Nafisi’s *Rira*. In early 2006 he joined [Meera](/en/meera-s38/) on electric guitar for a concert of new material at Tehran’s Taraneh Music School.[^rirasleeve-guitar][^meera-guitar][^meera-show][^meera-form]

On his 2008 solo album *Mehr*, Mahmoodi used melodies from the southern Iranian zaar ceremony for “Dingomaro” and “Noban.” He built “Si Pareh” around a pattern of fourteen beats, grouped in fives, fives and fours. Billy Cobham played drums on that recording and on “Bamdad.” Negar Rostami Nejad sang and played violin on the album.[^edition275174365-date][^press-zaar][^press-metre][^vew2ovaelzq8-title][^vivwstqbl9wu-title][^band-violin] He released *Mehr* on Faryaad, the independent label he started with Hamed Derakhshan.[^labelbio-label]

In 2016 Mahmoodi took Karaj Collective to the Fiddler’s Elbow in London with bassist Saleh Zarei and percussionist Ali Nourbakhsh. The trio improvised on Kurdish, Azerbaijani, Afghan and Armenian melodies. Their 2024 album *In the Moment* includes “Sari Galin” and two pieces featuring Sahar Zibaei. Mahmoodi also wrote and played the guitar part for Naghmeh Sharifi’s live “Darya,” with Habib Meftah on percussion. In 2022 he joined Roya Arab and Phil Davies at Rich Mix in London.
