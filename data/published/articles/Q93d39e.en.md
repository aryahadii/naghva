Guitarist and singer Pouyan Khajavi and drummer Shahram Khosraviani formed Arashk in Iran in 2000 or 2001. The band made hard rock songs in Persian and instrumental progressive rock and metal.[^formation][^alternative][^members][^albums]

## Two kinds of record

When guitarist and keyboard player Salim Ghazi Saeedi joined in 2004, he brought his own compositions to the band. They recorded his instrumental albums *[Abrahadabra](#abrahadabra-wty)*, *[Sovereign](#sovereign-vfd)* and *[Ustuqus-al-Uss](#ustuqus-al-uss-e64)*. Khajavi and Khosraviani wrote the music for *[Yell](#yell-zzw)*, the group’s Persian-language hard rock album, released in 2008.[^albums][^members]

The musicians stopped working as Arashk that year. They said they had been unable to play concerts in Iran. Ghazi Saeedi went on to make music as a solo artist.[^inactive]
