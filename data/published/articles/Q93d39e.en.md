Arashk is an Iranian hard rock and progressive rock/metal band founded by Pouyan Khajavi and Shahram Khosraviani. Its own biography gives 2001 as the founding year, while Khosraviani's page gives 2000; both accounts remain recorded.[^formation][^alternative]

## Two recording projects

Salim Ghazi Saeedi joined in 2004. The band's biography distinguishes his three instrumental albums, [Abrahadabra](#abrahadabra-wty) (2006), [Sovereign](#sovereign-vfd) (2007) and Ustuqus-al-Uss (2008), from YELL (2008), a Persian-language hard rock album composed by Khajavi and Khosraviani.[^albums] Its member listing credits Khajavi with guitars, bass and vocals, Khosraviani with drums and Ghazi Saeedi with guitars and keyboards; Amir JahanShahloo is listed as a former bassist.[^members]

The biography says the band became inactive in 2008 and Ghazi Saeedi pursued solo work.[^inactive] Digital catalogue dates differ from that chronology: Apple lists Abrahadabra in 2007 and Sovereign in 2003. These dates are preserved as source disagreements, without replacing the band's account.[^albums][^abrahadabra][^sovereign]
