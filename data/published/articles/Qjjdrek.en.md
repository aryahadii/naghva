Idlefon is the recording name of Hesam Ohadi, an Iranian Canadian electronic musician born in Tehran in 1987 and based in Calgary. A drummer before taking up computer music in 2006, Ohadi helped found both the experimental label BITROT and Tehran’s SET festival.[^alias][^born][^city][^drum][^start][^collective]

## From a band to a home studio

In 2001 Ohadi and Siavash Amini formed a band. A few years later they and Nima Pourkarimi, who records as Umchunga, began making tracks at home as they learned to use recording software. They played privately and put together [*Spotty Surfaces*](#spotty-surfaces-4wa), issued by Mahriz in 2010 under the Persian title *Gereh*. Amini and Pourkarimi later appeared on separate tracks of Idlefon’s [*Intensive Collectivity Known As City*](#intensive-collectivity-known-as-city-dsv), released in 2014.[^first][^friend][^tools][^early][^pressed][^persian][^guests][^guests2][^solo]

In 2009 Ohadi scored Arash Radkia’s animated short *The Radiologist’s Room*. He contributed four pieces to *Gereh*. He and Pourkarimi also made techno together as Bitodes. His post-rock band Photomat released *Windowsill* in 2013 on the Madrid label Oído Records.[^short][^four][^duo][^madrid][^window]

Life in Tehran supplied material for Ohadi’s electronic music. Independent label Tympanik Audio issued *Submarine* in 2015. After moving to Calgary, Ohadi also drew on the landscapes of Alberta. The label n5MD released [*COLDSTREAM*](#coldstream-4t8) in November 2021, with artwork and design by Amir B Ash.[^tehran][^ind][^sub][^city][^sound][^cover][^release][^label]

## Voice and performance

For the live set *Aesthetics of Survival*, Ohadi gave singing and lyrics a more prominent place in music that already used processed vocals. Calgary station CJSW also filmed an Idlefon set for its collaboration with Black Ice. Under the name Kino, Ohadi makes kinetic sculptures and interactive installations alongside the recordings.[^show][^voice][^radio][^visual][^sculpture]

For MUTEK, Ohadi and Ash turned *COLDSTREAM* into an audiovisual performance inspired by Adam Curtis’s short film *Oh Dear*. In Calgary, Ohadi played electronics alongside Zhina Ardalan’s guitar and voice in the duo Herr Spectre, which released *All Things Consoled* in 2025. The same year, he wrote and sang the words to ArtSaves’ “Abyss.”[^avshow][^filmconcept][^pop][^herrdate][^guestlyrics]

Ohadi began Hailstones on his own in 2023. With guitarist Mehraun Ehsani and bassist Bo Scavvies, he took on vocals and keyboards. The band released *The Weariness of Hope* in September 2026.[^newband][^hailvoc][^album26]
