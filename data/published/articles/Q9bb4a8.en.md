Niyaz is a musical project founded by vocalist and composer [Azam Ali](/en/azam-ali-m7d/) and multi-instrumentalist and composer Loga R. Torkian. Their music combines medieval Sufi poetry and folk songs from Iran and neighboring Persian Gulf countries with acoustic instruments and electronics.[^bio]

## Recordings and performance

The group’s biography names Six Degrees Records as the label for four of its albums.[^records] *[The Fourth Light](#the-fourth-light-kzw)* is available on the band’s Bandcamp page,[^album] which gives its release date as March 24, 2015.[^date] On stage, the founding pair perform with additional musicians and a female whirling dervish.[^stage]

## Later recordings

The group’s Bandcamp catalogue also lists *[The Best Of Niyaz](#the-best-of-niyaz-fm5)*, released on 17 March 2017, and *[SEVEN REALMS OF ATTAR](#seven-realms-of-attar-jkk)*, released on 17 September 2026.[^best-date][^attar-date] The latter is a studio album inspired by Attar’s *Conference of the Birds*. Alongside Azam Ali and [Loga Ramin Torkian](/en/loga-ramin-torkian-ce3/), its credits name Sinan Cem Eroglu on kopuz, kaval and voice.[^attar-work][^attar-credits]

## Earlier albums and the acoustic EP

Apple Music’s catalogue lists [*Niyaz*](#niyaz-m3n) (2005), [*Nine Heavens*](#nine-heavens-epy) (2008) and [*Sumud*](#sumud-fzj) (2012).[^original-niyaz][^original-nine-heavens][^original-sumud] Six Degrees separately identifies [*Sumud Acoustic EP*](#sumud-acoustic-ep-4kc) as a 2013 release: it contains three acoustic performances of songs from *Sumud* and three new songs.[^acoustic-year][^acoustic-scope] On “Naseem”, Azam Ali’s voice is paired with Habib Meftah Boucheri’s flute.[^naseem]

For *The Best Of Niyaz*, the label’s press biography gives 10 March 2017, while the band’s Bandcamp page gives 17 March. Both source dates are retained here.[^best-label-date][^best-date]
