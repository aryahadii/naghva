Yadha Trio brought together violinist Ehsan Kalhor, santur player [Soheil Hosnavi](/en/soheil-hosnavi-76q/) and tombak player Amir Kheiri. They began playing together in 2018, drawing on the improvisations heard on the Iranian radio programmes *Golha* and *Taknavazan*. Their first arrangement in a series of music films was Fazlollah Tavakkol’s “Se-Zarbi-e Esfahan.”[^radio][^film] At Emarat-e Ruberu in January 2019, they performed a piece in Abu Ata by Kalhor. A second concert that year included a chaharmezrab, a rhythmic instrumental piece, in Shushtari.[^group][^people][^start][^repertoire][^first][^venue][^date][^second][^secondwhen]

The trio recorded its first album, *Memories*, at Paeez Studio in the Iranian year 1399. They grouped the music into four suites, each in a different mode. Each begins with a prelude, makes room for improvisation, takes up the melody of a song and closes with a reng, a rhythmic dance piece. The recordings came out in 2023. In January 2024 the musicians played a third concert under the Yadha name.[^record][^suites][^parts][^release][^third][^concert][^two]
