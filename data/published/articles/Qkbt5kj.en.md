Soofi Namazi (born 1996–97), who releases music as Soofi, is a singer and teacher of Persian classical singing who grew up in Shiraz. She began by recreating avaz performances of earlier singers, and in 2026 released a run of songs with the composer Arash Fathi and the lyricist Ehsan Haeri.[^born][^name][^teaching][^origin][^homayoun-origin][^collection][^labat-date]

## Early life and training

Soofi was born in Isfahan and raised in Shiraz. Her father played the tar and setar. She began formal music lessons at seven, and went on to study classical piano, solfège and Western classical singing.[^origin][^father][^seven][^classical]

In 2016–17 she turned to Persian avaz, studying with Saeedeh Bozorgmanesh. She learned Razavi Sarvestani’s vocal radif with Ms Shayan and Majid Rouzitalab, and from 2021–22 taught it herself under Rouzitalab’s supervision.[^avaz-study][^radif-study][^teaching]

## Recreating the old avaz

In January 2021 she posted [“Avaz-E Bayat Esfahan”](#avaz-e-bayat-esfahan-wr7) on SoundCloud, her recreation of an avaz from Razavi Sarvestani’s album *Rang-e Farah*.[^esfahan-upload][^esfahan]

For [“Avaz-e Homayoun”](#avaz-e-homayoun-ng8), she and the tar player Farid Forooghi set out to rebuild a performance by the singer Younes Dardashti with the tar of Ebrahim Sarkhosh. Circumstances forced them to record it at home on a mobile phone. She shared it on her Telegram channel in July 2022.[^homayoun-origin][^homayoun-tar][^homayoun-credit][^home][^homayoun-date] A day later she posted an avaz in [Dastgah-e Nava](#dastgah-nava-ajg) there, and in March 2023 she put [“Avaz E Bayat Tork”](#avaz-e-bayat-tork-5zr) on SoundCloud.[^nava][^nava-date][^tork][^tork-date]

## Collaborations

In 2023 Soofi sang backing vocals for Jaana Ensemble’s [*Baade Saba*](#baade-saba-w2h), a film devoted to the folk music of Iran that the Farhang Foundation premiered that December.[^baade-vocals][^baade-context][^baade-date] On [“Descent”](#descent-as8), released in December 2024, she sang a poem by Saghir Esfahani; Milad Dezfoulian composed the music and played the tanbur, with electronic design by Jahandar Sheybani.[^descent][^descent-date]

## Songs with Arash Fathi

In 2026 Soofi released a series of songs with music by Arash Fathi and words by Haeri.[^collection] The first, [“Labat Che Gooyad?”](#labat-che-gooyad-qnm), came out in June.[^labat][^labat-date] In August came [“Geryeh Nakon”](#geryeh-nakon-tmh), which she dedicated to all those who gave their lives for Iran, and [“Jadoo”](#jadoo-chx).[^geryeh-credit][^geryeh-date][^geryeh][^jadoo][^jadoo-date] Fathi joined her as a singer on a [piano version](#labat-che-gooyad-piano-version-jkw) of “Labat Che Gooyad?” in September, and again on [“Aseman Be Aseman”](#aseman-be-aseman-xf2) that November.[^piano][^piano-date][^ase-man-singers][^aseman-date]
