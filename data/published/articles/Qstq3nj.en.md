Ajam began in London in 2010 as a project of Amin Mohammad Fouladi, who performs as Amin Ajami. He drew on music heard in markets, in traditional sports and in ceremonies across Iran. The musicians chose their name partly in recognition of Iranian communities on the southern shores of the Persian Gulf who call themselves Ajami. The first album, *Raghse Mardooneh*, appeared that year.[^origin][^creator][^genre][^poetry][^sports][^name][^begin] At the Spring Festival in Cairo in May 2012, Ajam introduced each of their thirteen songs to the audience and closed with a piece from the zār healing tradition.[^egypt][^zaar]

Arash Fayyazi worked on the melodies, Nariman Akrami on production and [Sara Fotros](/en/sara-fotros-vp2/) on the percussion arrangements. Touring gave the band a chance to develop the songs that became *Rag o Risheh*, released in Iran in 2016. For one of them, “Tayyebeh Jan,” Ajami made three research trips to the foothills of the Alborz. Jamal Mohammadi, founder of the Kayer Ensemble, helped him work out the piece. Members of the Mohammadi family recorded in Ghaemshahr, the core group worked on the structure in London, and Mazandarani singer Abolhassan Khoshru joined the recording.[^core][^arr][^rag][^cab][^visits][^kayer][^studio][^london][^vocals]

“Gole Iran” supported Iran’s national football team during the 2014 World Cup. In 2020 another song reached audiences through television: “Bia Dastam Begir” was the theme for *Berim Besazim*. For the video, the band edited together films made by its listeners, viewers of the show and the people appearing in it.[^soccer][^tv][^when][^people]

After the paired EPs *Tarze Tehran* and *Tarze Landan*, Ajam filmed a two-part summer performance in Shiraz in 2025. Ajami sang and played oud, Mirza Masoud Mousavi played violin and tar, and Mohammad Athari Boroujeni sang and played kamancheh. For “Minab Golestane” in 2026, Ajam worked with New York rapper Mazzi. In this elegy for the children of Minab, a melody sampled from Khaloo Ghanbar Rastgoo runs alongside Fotros’s percussion.[^register][^register2][^shiraz][^shiraz2][^oud][^violin][^kaman][^when2][^mazzi][^melody][^bass][^source]
