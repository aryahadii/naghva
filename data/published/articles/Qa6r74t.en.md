Kasra Saboktakin played bass in two Tehran rock groups that approached Iranian music from different directions. He helped found [Barad](/en/barad-s2n/), which arranged regional Iranian melodies for electric guitars, bass and drums. With [O-Hum](/en/o-hum-6v5/) he played on *Aloodeh*. Shahram Sharbaf brought in different musicians for different songs on that album. Saboktakin was the one who played the bass line on all twelve.[^brown-found][^bio-folk][^interview-job][^interview-bass]

Born in Tehran in 1976, Saboktakin moved to the United States in 2008. In Berkeley he studied music, taught bass and worked as a session player. In 2012 he joined percussionist Greg Ellis and Namjoo for a live album taping at the Colburn School in Los Angeles. Two years later he played bass on “Project Sinister” for the metal band Whispers in Crimson.[^la-date][^la-concert][^la-bass][^la-guest][^la-tape][^crimson-bass][^crimson-date][^sinister-track] Brown University’s *Underground Iran* concert put [Mohsen Namjoo](/en/mohsen-namjoo-t6w/) and [Kiosk](/en/kiosk-tgn/) on the same stage for the first time, with Saboktakin playing bass for both. He also recorded with tar player Milad Derakhshani and toured and recorded with singer Kian Pourtorab. His work with the Istanbul progressive-rock band Modal Waves includes “Now or Never.”[^bio-birth][^bio-arrival][^brown-berkeley][^brown-both][^brown-bass][^epk-kian][^epk-milad][^epk-istanbul][^music-now] In Tehran, composer Mahyar Alizadeh recruited him for the 2019 stage work *Istgah*, in which Omid Nemati sang a story about children in a disused railway station. Saboktakin also played bass on Pallett’s “Dream Away With Me.”[^istgah-bass][^istgah-d1][^istgah-d2][^istgah-story][^istgah-vocals][^dreamclip-bass]

Saboktakin composed the score for Shiva Sanjari’s documentary *A Pot Full of Dreams*. The film follows a Stockholm restaurateur back to Iran to learn how different religious communities prepare nazri, food cooked to be given away. It screened at the Religion Today Film Festival in Trento in 2025.[^own-film][^festival-dir][^festival-plot][^festival-nazri]
