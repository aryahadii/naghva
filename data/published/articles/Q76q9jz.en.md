Soheil Hosnavi grew up in Tehran and began learning santur at eleven. He studied with Arash Farshid and Siamak Aghaei. He later credited fellow santur player Siavash Kamkar with helping him find an audience.[^city][^young][^study][^improv][^friend]

His first album, *Turbulence*, appeared in 2015. Hosnavi kept the same tuning for all nine pieces, using the Iranian mode of Shushtari. For *The Banquet of Companions* he and percussionist Shayan Riyahi made a set of seven duets. On *Do Not Stay Alone* in 2022, Hosnavi returned to songs he had played in private, writing santur variations on melodies that included “Gol Gandom,” the first song he had learned on the instrument.[^first][^tuning][^mode][^banquet][^seven][^wheat][^variation][^year]

Hosnavi formed [Yadha Trio](/en/yadha-trio-4eq/) with violinist Ehsan Kalhor and tombak player Amir Kheiri. He also performed a concert series devoted to Faramarz Payvar’s chamber music. His 2025 album *Kooye Rendan* moves from Segah into Chahargah, two systems of melody in Iranian classical music. Under the same title, he published a collection of twenty-one pieces for santur.[^trio][^show][^rendan][^ending][^work][^book]
