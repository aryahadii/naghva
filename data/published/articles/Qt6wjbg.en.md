Mohsen Namjoo is an Iranian singer-songwriter, setar player and music researcher, born in Khorasan in 1976. He combines Persian classical scales and singing with electric guitar and the vocal techniques of rock and blues.[^bio][^sound] His songs circulated in Tehran’s underground music scene before he continued his career abroad, touring internationally and teaching Iranian and Persian music in the United States.[^tehran][^us][^teaching]

## Early life and Maad

Mohsen began studying Persian classical music at twelve. He took up setar shortly before university, then studied theatre and music at the University of Tehran.[^training][^setar][^university] While he was a student, his songs spread through the city’s underground music scene.[^tehran]

During military service, a guitarist introduced him to the blues. In 2002 the two began performing with their rock band, Maad, but the authorities stopped the group’s activity.[^blues][^maad] He went on to make [*Toranj*](/en/mohsen-namjoo-t6w/#toranj-b8x) with his friends from Mashhad in the band Maad. Released in 2007, it brought together pieces he had written in Iran in the year or two before recording.[^toranj-process][^toranj-date]

## Work abroad

Mohsen moved to the United States in the late 2000s and went on to tour internationally.[^us][^us-earlier] He released recordings of his university concerts: [*Alaki*](/en/mohsen-namjoo-t6w/#alaki-rdm) captures a February 2011 performance at Stanford, while [*13/8*](/en/mohsen-namjoo-t6w/#13-8-bme), released in 2012, was recorded live at Berkeley.[^alaki-performance][^berkeley] In 2014 he taught “Tradition and Protest: Iranian and Persian Music” as a visiting professor of Iranian Studies at Brown University.[^teaching]

## Later work

In 2019 Mohsen worked with the composer and santoor player Ehsan Matoori on [*Phantasm*](/en/mohsen-namjoo-t6w/#phantasm-g9n), singing music composed by Matoori.[^phantasm][^matoori][^phantasm-roles] For his own album [*Motantan*](/en/mohsen-namjoo-t6w/#motantan-ang), released the following year, he returned to some of his earlier compositions. He recalled playing “Khashm Kardam” on setar as a university student in his early twenties.[^motantan-date][^khashm]

On *Motantan*, Mohsen expanded his usual combination of guitar, bass, drums and setar with additional instruments.[^ensemble] He wanted some words to convey a feeling through their sound without carrying a definite meaning.[^words]

In 2025 Mohsen and [Kābin](/en/kabin-fwf/) released [*Magnetism*](/en/mohsen-namjoo-t6w/#magnetism-yvf) together.[^magnetism][^magnetism-date] Mohsen returned to Tehran in August 2026 after years in the United States.[^return]
