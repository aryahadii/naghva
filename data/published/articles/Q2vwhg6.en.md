Kaveh Salehi is a guitarist and founding member of [Pallett](/en/pallett-q53/). According to the biography for his Stockholm concert, he was born in Saqqez in 1985 and graduated in music from Tehran Art University.[^bio][^pallett]

## Pallett and independent work

Pallett’s Matrix profile names Salehi as the group’s guitarist alongside Omid Nemati and Mahyar Tahmasbi.[^pallett] The introduction to his 2024 Stockholm concert describes Night Bus as a series in which he performs his own work as a songwriter, composer, instrumentalist and singer.[^solo]

Pallett’s official site now names Salehi and Darius Azar as former members, without specifying when they left.[^current]

## Pallett recordings

Pallett’s recordings include *Mr. Violet*[^pallett0], *Tehran, Smile!*[^pallett1], *Endless Ending - EP*[^pallett2] and *Prime Meridian*[^pallett3]. These recordings are credited to the band; the listings do not specify each member’s role on individual tracks.
