ArtSaves is the recording name of Arash Mollakarimi, an Iranian producer who grew up in Tehran. He began DJing at house parties there in 2012. He has since worked between Tehran and Vancouver. His label, Kopi, releases his own records and music by other Iranian and diaspora artists.[^name][^birth][^start][^parties][^places][^kopi]

Mollakarimi produced all eleven tracks on [*SEUIL*](#seuil-8na), released in March 2016. Kopi later paired it with *SEUIL Remixes* in a handmade edition of 130 double CDs. In Tehran he also played keyboards and synthesizer in a Fajr Music Festival concert with singer Kian Pourtorab. Red Bull Music Academy selected him for its twentieth-anniversary edition in 2018.[^solo][^date][^double][^press][^fajr][^stage][^academy]

## From Chimera to the stage

Mollakarimi described [*Chimera*](#chimera-ypg) as the story of a being made of different parts that belongs nowhere. He has also drawn on the music of southern Iran and its historical ties to Africa. He recorded [*Home Is Where the Hatred Is*](#home-is-where-the-hatred-is-x6g) in Tehran between April and September 2023, with Nima Aghiani mastering the album.[^chimera][^belong][^south][^studio][^master]

On the 2025 single “Abyss,” Hesam Ohadi wrote the lyrics and sang over Mollakarimi’s production. MUTEK Montréal billed *Survival of the Weakest* as a world premiere. For this live work, Mollakarimi used a sampler and synthesizer to break up dance rhythms and let ambient textures dissolve around them.[^vocal][^song][^live][^gear][^premiere]
