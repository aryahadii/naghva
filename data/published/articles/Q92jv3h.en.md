Mahdyar Aghajani, who records as Mahdyar, is a Tehran-born producer, musician and film composer. Trained in classical violin and Middle Eastern music, he began producing for Iranian rappers whom he met through graffiti. After leaving Iran in 2009, he continued working with Iranian musicians from Europe and developed his own experimental electronic music. His solo album *Seized* drew together recordings and compositions made over a decade.[^bio][^study2][^graffiti][^cannes][^assemble]

## Learning and producing

As a child Mahdyar wanted an electric guitar, inspired by Pink Floyd. Offered a choice between piano and violin, he chose the violin because it looked more like a guitar. He studied classical music for six years and Middle Eastern music for four.[^study][^study2]

As a teenager he met rappers through graffiti. Many were using downloaded instrumental tracks; he offered to make music with Iranian instruments so that they could develop a sound of their own. The work led him into electronic music and film composition.[^graffiti]

## Leaving Iran

In 2009 Mahdyar used a visa for Cannes to leave Iran.[^cannes] He later recalled closures of the studios they used and arrests of his collaborators.[^closures][^arrests] From Europe he continued producing for Iranian rappers. He composed [Hichkas](/en/hichkas-gyv/)’s “[Dastasho Mosht Karde](/en/hichkas-gyv/#dastasho-mosht-karde-vv4)” and worked with [Bahram](/en/bahram-nouraei-k4w/) on *[Eshtebahe Khoob](/en/bahram-nouraei-k4w/#eshtebahe-khoob-dej)* in 2015.[^hichkas][^bahram]

His film work included *No One Knows About Persian Cats*, for which he composed and produced music. He mixed and mastered the documentary *Life Is Waiting*.[^film][^film2] Hearing electronic music through powerful venue speakers in Europe, rather than only monitors, cars and headphones, changed how he imagined sound.[^europe]

## Seized

Alongside commissions, Mahdyar made experimental tracks as a place to test ideas freely. Encouraged by his friends, visual artist Hirad Sab and musician Ash Koosha, he selected and reworked older pieces and composed new ones for *[Seized](#seized-zvv)*, released in 2018.[^experimental][^assemble][^album]

“Seize” began in 2011, soon after his arrival in Europe, with an interest in electronic textures and unpredictable arrangement.[^seize] For “Vow,” he shaped recordings of the singer Bidad’s voice into quarter-tone melodies over a 6/8 rhythm. He avoided a verse-and-chorus structure, drawing on paintings and films as models for storytelling and thinking about extremism in different parts of the world.[^vow][^extremism]

“Money Money” grew from an unused composition for a performance in a Paris museum. Mahdyar recorded violin over a 3/4 beat, then reshaped Ash Koosha’s recorded voice to suggest the words “money money.” He connected its recurring cycle to class, status, luxury and elitism.[^money-theme][^money] He presented a one-off NTS guest show in January 2018, around the album’s release.[^nts][^ntsdate]
