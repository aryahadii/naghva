Ali Ghamsari is an Iranian tar player and composer. He founded three ensembles and took his tar through Iranian provinces for *Iranian Tar*, a series of filmed performances that draws on regional tunes and the work of local musicians.[^ghgroup][^ghname][^ghtar][^ghmas]

## Ensembles and composition

Ghamsari was born in 1983 and studied in the Faculty of Fine Arts in Tehran. He founded Sarmad at eighteen and went on to form Hamnavazan-e Hesar and the Iranian String Quartet.[^ghstart][^ghstudy] His arrangements for string groups grew alongside his work as a soloist.[^ghgroup][^ghname]

In 2018 Ghamsari joined bağlama player Coşkun Karademir’s *Tellerin Aşkı* project in Turkey after the two met in Iran and began improvising together.[^ghco]

## Iranian Tar

Ghamsari travelled with his tar, learning how players in different parts of Iran handled their melodies and accompanying musicians he met on the road. He filmed the performances, sometimes leaving the choice of what to play open. In Isfahan he recorded with Ali Ahmadian, who joined him without knowing in advance what they would perform.[^isfahan] In December 2023 he brought the project to Théâtre de la Ville in Paris with Homayoun Nasiri on tombak and Mohammad Jaberi on daf. The series also gives room to new pairings. For its 151st instalment, *Hidden Hues*, Ghamsari played tar opposite qanun player Farah Fersi in Tehran.[^ghtar][^ghmas][^ghlive][^gh151]
