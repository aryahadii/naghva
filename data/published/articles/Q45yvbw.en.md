Confess started in Tehran in 2010, drawing on death metal and 1990s hardcore. Nikan Khosravi sang and played guitar; Arash Ilkhani brought a DJ’s turntables and sampler into the band. They released *Beginning of Dominion* in 2012. By the six-track *Back to My Future* EP in 2014, Khosravi had founded Opposite Records to put their music out in Iran.[^beg][^sound][^band][^nickname][^opposite][^ep]

The move to Norway changed both the lineup and the sound. The musicians experimented with seven-string guitars and recorded *Revenge at All Costs* again there. Fragments of Iranian broadcasts open the album and lead into “EVIN,” named for the prison where Khosravi and Ilkhani were held. On the later *Destination Addiction*, Khosravi recorded voice, guitars and bass, Ilkhani handled turntables and samples, and George Kollias played drums. Guitarist Marzi Montazeri guests on “After Goliath.”[^strings][^res][^turkey][^intro][^nikan][^arash][^greece][^guitar]
