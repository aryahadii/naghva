Erwin Khachikian got the name **Karmandan** over a late breakfast in Dubai in 2006.[^dubai] A woman warned him that the office workers would soon fill the place. He thought of the invoices, emails and appointments that filled a musician’s day too. The name stuck. Khachikian wrote and produced the songs and played them with local musicians in different countries.[^zh-name][^zh-locals][^tirgan-songs]

Early on, the band appeared on the BBC’s *Kook* as a quartet. Erwin, Sophie, Peter and Jerry talked about working together in the studio, and the group performed “Nardeboon” for the programme. Their first album, *Karmandan*, came out in November 2010. On “Pelastiki” they sang about the pressure to get plastic surgery. A plastic surgeon and an elderly jeweller were among the characters the musicians played onstage.[^quartet][^bbc][^debut][^pelastiki][^tirgan-costumes]

Khachikian carried the costumes from one city to the next in a suitcase. In 2011 the musicians who wore them in Yerevan shared the stage with singer Anush Hovhannisyan.[^costumes][^anush] Tirgan billed Karmandan with Rana Mansour in Toronto in July 2013 and described a repertoire in Persian, English and Armenian. In Yerevan, Mane Tonoyan sang “Yeraz” with the band at the Puppet Theater. The second album, *Gozargah*, followed in February 2018.[^tirgan-duo][^toronto][^tirgan-lang][^armenia][^yeraz][^gozargah]
