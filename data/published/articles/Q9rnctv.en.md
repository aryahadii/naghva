Umchunga is the recording name of Tehran composer and producer Nima Pourkarimi. He took it from “Umchunga Locks,” a track by Mira Calix. Before his first solo album, he was one of two singers in the electronic and post-rock band Photomat. He also made techno with Hesam Ohadi, known as Idlefon, in their duo Bitodes.[^city][^alias][^song][^voices][^duo]

In 2008, Pourkarimi, Ohadi and Siavash Amini played two private shows in Tehran for friends and family. Mahriz released their split album *Spotty Surfaces*, also known as *Gereh*, in 2010. Their music began reaching listeners beyond those gatherings. Musician Hadi Bastani recalled discovering Pourkarimi’s “Streets 1” through a friend’s link to SoundCloud.[^events][^three][^friends][^issue][^persian][^discovery]

## The solo recordings

For *Should Have Been Done By Now*, Pourkarimi recorded guitar, synthesizers, his voice and sounds from the field, then processed them electronically. He chose six pieces that reflected a particular state of mind. Hibernate released the album in July 2015 with Azin Nafarhaghighi’s photograph of Bandar-e Anzali on the cover. The PUSH warehouse session *CyTor* documents a different collaboration, with Tegh, filmed and edited by Amir B. Ash.[^sound][^six][^debut][^photo][^sea][^session][^collab][^director]

On *The Brightest Winter Sun* (2018), Pourkarimi and Amini revisited piano music from the late eighteenth and nineteenth centuries. They removed the progression of themes and keys. Each of the five tracks bears the year the composer who inspired it died. In 2021 Pourkarimi released *black square*, a piece he had composed for a group exhibition curated by Tehran’s Emkan Gallery.[^winter][^piano][^theme][^dates][^emkan][^black]
