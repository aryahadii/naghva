Herr Spectre is the duo of Iranian musicians Zhina Ardalan, who records as Xeen, and Hesam Ohadi, known as Idlefon. Ardalan sings and plays distorted guitar. Ohadi builds the electronic parts around her voice and guitar, drawing on synth-pop and coldwave.[^people][^music][^roles]

SET Festival selected Herr Spectre for Calgary’s Sled Island in 2023. The duo had to cancel, and the festival named Roya as its replacement. Herr Spectre released the four-track EP [*All Things Consoled*](#all-things-consoled-6t7) in September 2025. It opens with “Beginning” and ends with “Fakeout.”[^festival][^cancel][^date][^start][^end]

In November 2026 the duo issued a film for “Fakeout,” created by Ohadi under his visual-art name Kino.[^kino] The musicians composed and produced the track, with additional composition and mixing by Shahin Entezami. That month Tehran Contemporary Sounds billed Herr Spectre for its Saturday programme at the Betonhalle in Berlin’s silent green.[^vid][^film][^soundtrack][^mix][^dateber][^venue][^programme]
