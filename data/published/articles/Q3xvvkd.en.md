Brothers Arash Rahbary and Arsalan formed the rock band TarantisT in Tehran in 2000. The musicians played secret shows in the city’s basements before moving to Los Angeles in 2008. They continued singing in Persian in the United States.[^identity][^formed][^brothers][^surname][^basements][^los-angeles][^independent]

## From Tehran to Los Angeles

Friends began recording the group’s basement shows and sharing them. An invitation to play in Europe followed.[^shared][^basements][^europe] It took the band two years to get visas to attend the South by Southwest festival in the United States. They reached the festival in 2008 and settled in Los Angeles.[^festival][^los-angeles]

The band continued to draw on the sounds of Iranian instruments. On their 2017 album *[Not A Crime](#not-a-crime-w9b)*, they played zarb and santoor alongside electric guitars, bass and synthesizers.[^los-angeles][^instruments][^album][^date]
