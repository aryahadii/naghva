TarantisT is a rock band from Tehran. A 2019 interview describes the group's continuing independent approach after its move to the United States.[^identity][^independent]

## Not A Crime

The band's 2017 announcement presents *[Not A Crime](#not-a-crime-w9b)* as a full-length album offered through digital music services. The musicians describe combining traditional Persian instruments, including zarb and santoor, with electric guitar, bass, synthesizers and heavy riffs.[^album][^instruments]
