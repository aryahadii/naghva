Abdulrahman Surizehi learned the benju from his father Joma in Iranian Balochistan. The Balochi instrument has a doubled melody string and accompaniment strings on either side. Joma helped develop it, and Abdulrahman expanded his repertoire through meetings and recordings with musicians on both sides of the Iran–Pakistan border. After moving to Norway, he played it alongside Anne Hytta’s Hardanger fiddle and Rostam Mirlashari’s voice in Padik.[^father][^strings][^first][^bal][^fiddle]

The Norwegian independent label Etnisk Musikklubb issued *Love Songs and Trance Music from Balochistan* as a double CD. Its next project with Surizehi, *Folk Songs and Contemporary Songs from Balochistan*, also documents his work as a composer and arranger. Singers Kaseim Hamin and Es Haq Balochnasab join him on that record. At a Norwegian album concert the benju was heard with fiddle, tabla, song and spoken poetry.[^first][^benju][^bal]

The sound archive of the Musée de l’Homme preserves eight sowt recordings made with a young ensemble from Saravan in November 1980. The catalogue identifies Surizehi on benju. In June 1996 he recorded for Radio France, which issued his playing on *La Tradition instrumentale* the following year. A 1998 tape from a small concert in Paris pairs him with Omar Sor on the bowed sorud. That year he also recorded in Oslo with santur player Javid Afsari Rad and their group Karvan. Etnisk Musikklubb released the concert on CD in 2001.[^tape][^sar][^field][^air][^omar][^day][^ocora][^issuefr][^rad][^oslo][^karv]

In Norway Surizehi received the 2006 folk music prize, and in Germany his work received the recording critics’ award. He played as a guest of Seven Winds at a benefit for a hospital in Bhopal and opened the Tromsø International Film Festival in 2012. [A 2021 UCLA programme](https://archive.org/details/calauem_200201_omvf0000120_ac) preserves nearly an hour of music with Essaq Baloch, Ali-Mohammad Baloch on saruz and Musa Baloch on tanburag. Surizehi played benju.[^norprize][^german][^winds][^bhopal][^tromso][^festyear][^symp][^film][^trio][^time]
