Aeen Ahmadifar is an arranger and a member of [Chaartaar](/en/chaartaar-b3n/). A report on the group’s Isfahan concert names him as its arranger alongside Ehsan Haeri, Arash Fathi and Arman Garshasbi.[^band][^role]

## Chaartaar

Ahmadifar’s credited role within Chaartaar is arrangement. The band’s music has been described as combining electronic music with Persian vocals; poetry, composition, singing and arrangement are credited to different members.[^role][^sound][^band]

## Recordings with Haleh Seyfizadeh

Ahmadifar appears as a guest on [Haleh Seyfizadeh](/en/haleh-seyfizadeh-wdt/)’s *Meyzadeh*.[^meyzadeh] He and Rouzbeh Esfandarmaz also appear as guests on her *Khaneh Koo*. The release titles name the collaborators without specifying what instruments they play or whether they contributed to production.[^khaneh]
