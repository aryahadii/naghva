Aeen Ahmadifar is an arranger and one of the four founders of [Chaartaar](/en/chaartaar-b3n/). In 2011 he formed the group with his friend, the composer Arash Fathi, singer Arman Garshasbi and lyricist [Ehsan Haeri](/en/ehsan-haeri-r62/). Ahmadifar gave their songs an electronic sound.[^founders][^friends][^band][^melody]

## Chaartaar’s first song

When Fathi composed a melody in a traditional Iranian style, Ahmadifar made an electronic arrangement of it. Haeri wrote lyrics for the melody, and the group’s first song became “Dar Hasrate Mah.”[^melody][^lyrics][^first] The four kept working together. For a concert announced in Isfahan, Ahmadifar was the arranger and Garshasbi the singer.[^band]

He also worked with the singer [Haleh Seyfizadeh](/en/haleh-seyfizadeh-wdt/). He and Rouzbeh Esfandarmaz were guests on her single [“Khaneh Koo”](/en/haleh-seyfizadeh-wdt/#khaneh-koo-feat-aeen-ahmadifar-rouzbeh-esfandarmaz-single-tzx), and he later appeared on [“Meyzadeh”](/en/haleh-seyfizadeh-wdt/#meyzadeh-feat-aeen-ahmadifar-single-nyb).[^khaneh][^meyzadeh][^kd][^md]
