Babak Khangholi is an Iranian singer and composer born in Lorestan in 1988–89.[^profile][^background] His 2021 song [“Fig”](#fig-single-5f3) brought him to a wider audience. He also composed for the theatre.[^fig][^fig-audience][^theatre]

## Songs and theatre

Khangholi sang a version of Javad Asari’s [“Sabre Ayoub”](#sabre-ayoub-single-6nx). In 2022 he released [“Darmoon”](#darmoon-single-vbj) with Amin Ataei and followed it with [“Ghalandar”](#ghalandar-single-tt3).[^theatre][^darmoon][^ghalandar] His work for the stage included music for the play «مختلفیم». In a 2024 interview with Cadence he performed another of his songs, «سرخ», which he had yet to release.[^theatre][^sorkh][^episode-date]
