Babak Khangholi is a singer and composer.[^profile]

According to the introduction to his Cadence interview, he was born in Lorestan in 1367 of the Solar Hijri calendar.[^background]

## Recordings

Apple Music gives a release date of September 9, 2021 for *Fig*.[^fig] It names Khangholi and Amin Ataei as the artists on *Darmoon*[^darmoon] and dates *Ghalandar* to June 27, 2022.[^ghalandar]

His work expanded into theatre music, including مختلفیم. The interview introduction also names his cover of جواد اثری’s صبر ایوب.[^theatre]
