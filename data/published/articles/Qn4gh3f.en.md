Photomat was a Tehran band that moved between electronic music and post-rock. Hesam Ohadi (Idlefon), Siavash Amini and Nima Pourkarimi (Umchunga) began playing together in the summer of 2009. Bassist Pooya Bakhsheshi joined them. Pourkarimi was one of the group’s two singers. After working on songs built around the voice, the musicians developed instrumental pieces for live shows in 2010.[^band][^city][^origin][^members][^bass][^singer][^words][^shows]

## Windowsill

The group shared demos of “White Rain” and “Vague” on SoundCloud. In June 2012, “Night” appeared in the programme of the Iranian Arts Now festival in Paris. PUSH Sessions also filmed the band playing. In November the musicians posted “Steady Dive,” describing it as a track from their forthcoming album.[^demos][^vague][^paris][^night][^live][^nov][^release]

Madrid’s Oído Records issued [*Windowsill*](#windowsill-7w7) on 14 March 2013. The eight-track album brought “Vague,” “White Rain,” “Night” and “Steady Dive” together with four other pieces.[^madrid][^march][^eight] The musicians disbanded after the album, according to Hadi Bastani’s study of experimental music in Iran.[^end]
