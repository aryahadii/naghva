Cymin Samawatie is a German-Iranian singer, pianist, composer and conductor. Born in Braunschweig in 1976 to Iranian parents, she spent part of each childhood year in Iran. She studied percussion, piano and singing in Hanover, then jazz singing in Berlin. There her teacher Jerry Granelli encouraged her to try new arrangements for Persian poetry. In 2002 she founded the quartet Cyminology, in which she sang Persian verse with piano, bass and drums.[^born][^iran][^school][^jazz][^teach][^quartet]

## Conducting an improvisation

In 2013 Samawatie and Cyminology drummer Ketan Bhatti founded the Trickster Orchestra. One way she worked with the ensemble was to conduct an improvisation through gesture. At rehearsals in Braga for the 2023 Beethovenfest, the orchestra played alongside students from Afghanistan’s National Institute of Music, the German National Youth Orchestra and the Barenboim-Said Academy. The players had no written score. Raising her hands slowly could ask them to play higher or louder.[^orchestra][^scores][^pitch][^students]

## Duets in Istanbul

In December 2018 Samawatie recorded a series of duets at Yıldız Technical University in Istanbul. Playing piano and singing, she improvised with one partner at a time, among them the kamancheh player Arslan Hazreti. The ten duets came out in June 2025 on *Temas*.[^studio][^instrument][^duos][^partner][^date]
