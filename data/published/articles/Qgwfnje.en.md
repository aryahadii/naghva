When Bosnian-Australian singer Nela Trifković was growing up in Yugoslavia, 8 March was a day to celebrate women. In Melbourne in 2020 she and her SARAY Iluminado Femme colleagues Irine Vela and Kelly Dowall began organising The Resonant Heart. Their first programme for International Women’s Day in 2021 brought the Mexican mariachi group Queen of Hearts together with Palestinian artist Aseel Tayah and Zimbabwean artist Tariro Mavondo.[^r1][^r2][^r3][^r4][^r5][^r6]

After that first programme the artists streamed a five-part series from a yoga studio in Thornbury. The fourth episode featured Eisaei.[^studio][^ep] The lineup that came together in 2022 included Iranian qanun player [Vahideh Eisaei](/en/vahideh-eisaei-p75/) and puppeteer Elnaz Sheshgelani, South Sudanese poet Lizzy Kuoth, Indian sitar player Sarita McHarg and the three SARAY musicians. On 8 March 2023 they premiered The Heart Whispers and Whirls at the Melbourne Recital Centre. In its five recorded pieces the heart prays, listens to its ancestors, falls in love and grieves. Eisaei and the group arranged the traditional “Shah Sanaam” for the moment when the heart falls in love.[^r7][^r8][^r9][^r10][^r11]

The EP was recorded at York Street Recorders and released in June 2025. For the stage work Forest Living Room the collective turned to exile, family and new beginnings. The Bowery Theatre booked the work for November 2026, and Melbourne Recital Centre announced it for March 2027.[^bowerybooking][^mrcbooking] In the recital centre’s programme “HIRA MAE / COME HERE” and “For My Ancestors” sit alongside a Greek kalamatianos dance, with songs, movement and shadow puppetry sharing the stage.[^r12][^r13][^r14][^r15][^r16][^r17]
