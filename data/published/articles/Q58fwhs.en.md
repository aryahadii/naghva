Hamed Nikpay is an Iranian singer and composer associated with fusion music. Born in Tehran in 1977, he listed tar, setar, tanbor, oud, baghlama and daf among the instruments he plays in a 2011 interview.[^identity][^birth][^instruments]

## Words and melody

Discussing his choice of poetry, Nikpay named Rumi, Emad Khorasani and Sohrab Sepehri among writers he particularly favored. He said he chose words for how well they expressed the melody and moved him.[^poetry]

## Reaching You

His official upload of the title song from *[Reaching You (Vasl-e To)](#reaching-you-vasl-e-to-nsn)* links listeners to the album's CD Baby edition. Spotify names Zang Records in the release information and identifies Nikpay himself as the holder of the recording copyright.[^album][^rights]
