Hamed Nikpay is an Iranian singer, composer and multi-instrumentalist born in Tehran in 1977. He later moved to the United States. Alongside singing, he plays plucked instruments including the tar and setar.[^birth][^move][^instruments][^identity]

## Learning music

Nikpay began playing music when he was seven. His mother taught him the techniques of traditional music and singing. He went on to play several instruments, including tar, setar, tanbur and daf. Around 2005 he moved to the United States.[^start][^mother][^instruments][^move]

## Poetry and the band

When choosing words for a melody, Nikpay said he looked for poetry that moved him and suited the music. His favourite poets included Rumi, Emad Khorasani and Sohrab Sepehri.[^poetry] He released the album *[Reaching You (Vasl-e To)](#reaching-you-vasl-e-to-nsn)* in 2013.[^solo-album][^solo-date][^edition-album]

For the 2019 Tirgan Festival, Nikpay and his band were booked to perform new arrangements of earlier work alongside new pieces in a style he called Sufi funk, with oud, electric guitar, duduk, saxophone and drums.[^identity]
