Sepideh Meshki studied graphic design in Mashhad and built her musical life around the setar. In a long interview for the Iranian magazine Cadence, recorded with her instrument beside her, she spoke of learning from setar player Masoud Shoari, singer Hengameh Akhavan, and musicians Hossein Alizadeh and Mohammad Reza Lotfi. In 2002 or 2003 she played solos and duets at Ferdowsi University in Mashhad. In 2004 she performed with Alizadeh’s Hamavayan at the University of Ilam.[^r1][^r2][^r3][^r4]

Meshki played in Lotfi’s women’s ensemble and in Hamnavazan-e Sheyda. With the women’s group she played music from the Chavosh series at the Fajr festival in Tehran and travelled to a Sufi music festival in Turkey. She also became a setar teacher at the Mirza Abdollah school. On her 2014 record A Dialogue Without Words, the exchange is between her setar and Ahmad Mostanbet’s tombak.[^r5][^r6][^r7][^r8][^r9][^r10]

After Lotfi’s death she and five former members of Sheyda founded the Ān Ensemble. Meshki composed its first record, Impatient of Separation, for the ensemble and singer Mojtaba Asgari. The label issued it on CD in 2017. Her 2018 recital at Tehran’s Rudaki Hall survives in a recording from the Chand Shab Setar festival. In summer 2022 she filmed a short solo in Segah with Hafdang Records.[^hafnotes] On *Quartal*, released in 2023, she plays alone, moving from Darāmad to Delkash, Shekasteh and Erāq.[^r11][^r12][^r13][^r14][^r15][^r16][^r17]
