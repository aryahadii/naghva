Hysterrae are an Italian-Iranian ensemble based in Lecce in southern Italy. Iranian setar player Shadi Fathi and Italian musicians Cinzia Marzo, Silvia Gallone and Irene Lungo began playing as a group at a residency in Salento in 2018. Producer Emanuele Flandoli later joined them, adding electronics to their voices, flutes, drums and strings.[^songlines-countries][^album-location][^songlines-date][^songlines-salento][^songlines-voices][^songlines-electronics][^album-line1][^album-line2][^album-line3][^album-line4]

## A residency in Salento

The four women already knew each other and had worked together before Giorgio Doveri, artistic director of the Irregolare Festival, invited them to prepare a concert in Salento. Fathi recalled that the response to that first performance encouraged them to start building a shared repertoire. They first used only their voices and acoustic instruments.[^songlines-salento][^songlines-before][^songlines-response][^songlines-voices]

On their debut album, [*Hysterrae*](#hysterrae-bf3), Marzo sings and plays flute, Gallone sings and plays percussion, and Lungo sings. Fathi plays setar, tar and daf. Flandoli produced and recorded the group in Lecce, and Linfa Records released the seven-track album in December 2023.[^album-line0][^album-line1][^album-line2][^album-line3][^album-line4][^album-studio][^album-label][^album-released] The album reached eighth place on the Transglobal World Music Chart in February 2024 and ranked forty-eighth in its chart for the 2023–24 season.[^month-position][^season-position]

## Writing “Ruvida”

Lungo described [“Ruvida”](#ruvida-s5r) as the first new song the group wrote together. It follows a woman who feels her partner’s aggression and tries to recognise the point at which trust gives way to fear.[^songlines-new][^songlines-anger][^songlines-trust]

The group released [“Strade”](#strade-k2j) as a single in July 2024. Later concert lineups have varied. The 2026 Bravetta Music Fest programme lists Fathi and Pejman Tadayon as alternatives on tar and daf.[^apple-1755506264-date][^festival-dates]
