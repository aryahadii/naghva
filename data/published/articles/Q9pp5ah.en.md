[Arash Zarabi](/en/arash-zarabi-3k2/), a Tehran-based bassist and composer, founded Unlucky Ninjas. The group made its first studio album, *[Tehran-Kabul](#tehran-kabul-q6f)*, with Soraya Husaini and her father Ibrahim. Together they explored music from Iran and Afghanistan.[^career][^ninjas][^first]

## From Tehran-Kabul to Micro Tehran

The label Made in Iran released the nine songs of *Tehran-Kabul* in 2021.[^songs][^first-date] Two years later, on *[Micro Tehran](#micro-tehran-y4d)*, the group turned to the experience of feeling trapped in Tehran. They used microtonal sounds. The album also drew on the sounds of 1980s games consoles and the experience of playing, losing and starting again.[^city][^second][^second-date][^games]
