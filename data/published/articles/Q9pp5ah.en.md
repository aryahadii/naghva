Unlucky Ninjas is a group whose first studio album, [Tehran-Kabul](#tehran-kabul-q6f), explores Iranian and Afghan cultures through a collaboration with Soraya Husaini and her father Ibrahim Husaini.[^first]

## Albums

Made in Iran released the nine-song Tehran-Kabul on 22 November 2021.[^songs][^first-date] The label identifies [Micro Tehran](#micro-tehran-y4d) as the group’s second album and dates it to 11 July 2023. Its description links the record to life in Tehran, microtonal sounds and the repeated struggles of 1980s video games.[^second][^second-date][^city][^games]
