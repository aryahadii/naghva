Hailstones began in Calgary in 2023 as a project by Iranian Canadian musician Hesam Ohadi, who also records as Idlefon. After more than a decade making electronic music, Ohadi started writing with just piano and voice. Guitarist Mehraun Ehsani and bassist Bo Scavvies joined, with Ohadi singing and playing keyboards.[^origin][^city][^piano][^years][^trio][^keys]

The band appeared at GROUND HUM + ANTiPODE in Seattle and at Black Ice in Calgary in 2023. That October, they shared a live recording of “Cannibal Song.” The studio single came out in November 2025. In the later lineup Jill Shannon played bass, and Matt Doherty joined on drums in the winter of 2025. The group combined drum kit and electronic percussion. Its filmed Sled Island performances include a 2026 version of “Cold Summer.”[^seattle][^rail][^gigs][^club][^early][^single][^newbass][^winter][^drums][^mix][^video]

Hailstones took the title of its debut album, [*The Weariness of Hope*](#the-weariness-of-hope-cb6), from a line in Nuri Bilge Ceylan’s film *About Dry Grasses*. Pat Palardy recorded the vocals at Public Lunch Studios and Shahin Entezami contributed additional mixing and mastering. The band released the six songs digitally and on cassette in September 2026.[^turkey][^record][^master][^six][^format][^debut]
