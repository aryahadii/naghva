Marjan Vahdat draws on classical Persian singing and the regional vocal traditions of Iran. As a child, she heard her father and grandmother sing and recite poetry. At eight she began studying with singer Pari Maleki in Tehran, and later took classes in regional styles with singer Sima Bina.[^maleki][^bina-classes] She took the same singing classes as her older sister Mahsa, and the sisters first went onstage together.[^childhood][^firststage][^familymusic] Erik Hillestad first recorded them for KKV’s *Lullabies from the Axis of Evil*. The label then recorded their concert in a Tehran garden for *Songs from a Persian Garden* in 2007.[^kkvstart][^gardenfirst] On her first solo album, *Blue Fields* (2013), Marjan sang in a setting that brought Western instruments into the Persian repertoire.[^serene] For *Our Garden Is Alone*, recorded across three continents, she wrote most of the melodies and words. During the pandemic she recorded her voice first. Producer Bugge Wesseltoft and the other musicians then added their parts one by one from different studios.[^pandemic][^vo][^blue][^sister][^together][^garden]

## Twinklings of Hope

Marjan was humming a Kurdish tune at the home of the poet Mohammad Ebrahim Jafari when he started reciting a poem about hope. That pairing became [“Twinklings of Hope”](#twinklings-of-hope-hpe). The sisters recorded the album with Erik Hillestad at the Italian Embassy in Tehran. For the [title song’s film](#mahsa-vahdat-marjan-vahdat-twinklings-of-hope-ypd), they took the band to a Tehran rooftop, where the two could sing in the open at a time when women’s solo performances in public were restricted.[^mvstory][^mvgard][^mvroof]

In California the Alliance for California Traditional Arts paired her with singer Heranoosh Talebzadeh for a 2023 apprenticeship in Persian singing.[^apprentice] On *The Eagle of My Heart* she set the instrumental ensemble aside and sang a cappella. Recordings of voices she had grown up with also appear, bringing other singers into the album without an accompanying band. In 2026 she recorded again with Mahsa on *Heart’s Endless Garden*.[^acap][^heard][^bina][^latest]
