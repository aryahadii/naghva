Marjan Vahdat draws on classical Persian singing and the regional vocal traditions of Iran. Her work with her older sister Mahsa brought the two voices to *Songs from a Persian Garden* and *I Am Eve*. On her first solo album, *Blue Fields*, Marjan sang in a setting that brought Western instruments into the Persian repertoire. For *Our Garden Is Alone*, recorded across three continents, she wrote most of the melodies and words. Bugge Wesseltoft played piano, accordion and keyboards, alongside bass, percussion, kamancheh and ney.[^vo][^blue][^sister][^together][^garden]

On *The Eagle of My Heart* she set the instrumental ensemble aside and sang a cappella. Recordings of voices she had grown up with also appear, bringing other singers into the album without an accompanying band. In 2026 she recorded again with Mahsa on *Heart’s Endless Garden*.[^acap][^heard][^bina][^latest]
