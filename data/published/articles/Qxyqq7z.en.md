The musicians who formed Siriya in Bushehr in 2016 drew on the music they heard around the port. About a year later singer Mohammad Laryan, percussionist Behrouz Sharifian and Mahdi Monfared joined. The credits for “Sher Yadom Ra” give a sense of the lineup on their first record: drum kit, bass and guitar played alongside oud, percussion and Monfared’s neyanban, the bagpipe heard across southern Iran. The band arranged the piece together and recorded it at Abnous Studio.[^formed][^joined][^music][^studio][^poem]

Their first album, *A Table for Two*, opens an imaginary café door. Laryan pictured two people sitting at a table and wanted to tell a Bushehr story that could begin away from the water. The musicians sold the physical CD themselves. “Sher Yadom Ra” later returned to the stage in Shiraz and in Tehran’s Vahdat Hall. For “Bomooni” they invited a fellow Bushehri musician, percussionist Habib Meftah. In “Wretched” the words come from a ghazal by Rumi.[^cafe][^sale][^live][^bom][^rumi]
