Navid Arbabian (نوید اربابیان) is a musician, composer, singer and instrumentalist from Mashhad, born in 1985. His official biography identifies bass guitar as his main instrument, alongside electric, acoustic and classical guitars and mandolin.[^identity][^birth][^instruments]

## Groups and collaboration

Arbabian joined ماد after early work in Mashhad’s bands. His biography credits him with bass guitar and a role in developing the music of محسن نامجو’s album ترنج. He later formed کیلومتر ۲۳ while expanding his work as a singer, songwriter and composer.[^maad][^toranj][^km]

## Solo work and دمنوش

The biography identifies خط خنده‌ش as his first formal solo album and describes it as folk rock combining rock with Iranian and folk elements.[^solo] His site’s chronology is inconsistent: the homepage lists [Khat-e Khandash](#khat-e-khandash-wj4) as 2016, while the biography places its publication in a timeline entry for 1392. Apple Music dates the album to 22 November 2023; the date of the original release remains unclear.[^catalog-own][^timeline][^album0][^date0]

Apple Music dates Vaheh to 14 June 2021, 13 to 13 November 2023, and Damnoosh 11 (Damnoosh-e Boomi) to 6 September 2024.[^album1][^date1][^album2][^date2][^album3][^date3] Arbabian describes دمنوش as a collaboration with رضا عابدین‌زاده spanning music, poetry, literature and narrative, later expanding into visual work.[^damnoosh]
