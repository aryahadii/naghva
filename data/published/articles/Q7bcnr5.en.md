Navid Arbabian is a singer, bassist and composer born in Mashhad in 1985. He played in bands in his hometown before forming Kilometer 23 and writing and singing his own songs.[^identity][^birth][^instruments][^maad][^km]

## From the bass to songwriting

After playing with several groups in Mashhad, Arbabian joined Maad. He played bass on *[Toranj](/en/mohsen-namjoo-t6w/#toranj-b8x)* and helped shape its music. He then formed Kilometer 23 as he developed his work as a singer and songwriter.[^maad][^toranj][^km]

In his first solo album, *[Khat-e Khandash](#khat-e-khandash-wj4)*, Arbabian brought Iranian folk sounds and instruments into rock music. Later he worked with Reza Abedinzadeh on *Damnoosh*, combining music, poetry and storytelling. The collaboration grew to include visual work. Arbabian released the EP *[Damnoosh 11](#damnoosh-11-damnoosh-e-boomi-ep-qaw)* in 2024.[^solo][^edition-catalog-own][^damnoosh][^album3][^date3]
