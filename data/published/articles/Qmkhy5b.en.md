Siavash Imani is an Iranian tar player, composer and teacher, born in Tehran in 1979. He has recorded solo tar, starting with music drawn from Abdollah Davami’s radif, and writes for [Sepidar Ensemble](/en/sepidar-ensemble-m6w/), which he founded to play Persian classical music.[^iborn][^iv1][^ifound]

## Learning the tar

Imani studied with Mohammad Reza Lotfi, Arshad Tahmasbi, Houshang Zarif and Zeidollah Toluei. He played a solo improvisation at a young musicians’ festival organised by Daryoush Talai in 2001. Two years later he joined Hossein Dehlavi’s National Plectrum Orchestra.[^iteacher][^italai][^idehlavi]

From 2005 to 2008 he worked with Tahmasbi on a Farhang Radio series introducing the radif, the repertoire of melodic phrases passed from teacher to student. They also played together on [*Shahrood*](#shahrood-2006-edition-6rj), issued by Avaye Barbad in 2006. In 2013 Imani and santur player [Aria Mohafez](/en/aria-mohafez-cvf/) began running the Baghe Naghmeha music school together.[^iradio][^ish][^ischool]

## Davami’s radif

In 2015 Mahoor issued the first volume of [*The Spring Bare Stalk*](#the-spring-bare-stalk-vol-1-snc). Imani takes Davami’s versions of melodies in Chahargah, Afshari, Bayat-e Tork and Kord Bayat as a starting point for his own playing. Mahoor released a second volume with Sina Khoshk Bijari on tombak in 2018 and a third in 2023. For [*Irā*](#ira-fyf), Imani played alone, following two modes in two extended pieces, Bayat-e Esfahan and Shour.[^iv1][^ivan][^iv2][^iv3][^ira][^irarad]

## Sepidar

With Sepidar, Imani wrote [*Tash*](#tash-2021-edition-m7x) for a small group and the singer Mehdi Emami. He drew on the customary order of a musical gathering, leaving space to vary its sequence. Later in 2021 he put the group’s own pieces alongside early twentieth-century compositions in a Geneva programme with Sepideh Raissadat. He returned to a smaller setting for [*So Faraway*](#so-faraway-hx7), a 2024 release from a Delhi concert with tombak player Pedram Khavarzamini.[^tcover][^tintent][^tform][^igeneva][^gprog3][^ifar]
