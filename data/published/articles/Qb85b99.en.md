Arash Fouladvand is an Iranian composer and conductor who founded [Bahar Choir](/en/bahar-choir-s4s/) in Paris. While studying civil engineering at the University of Tehran, he organised piano recitals to raise money for the Mahak charity. In 2006 he moved to France for doctoral studies at the French Institute of Petroleum. At the conservatoire in Aubervilliers he studied harmony, orchestration and conducting.[^bio-work][^academy-tehran][^bio-early][^bio-study][^bio-conservatory][^bio-training] He helped establish the East Paris Philharmonic Orchestra in 2010. From 2012 he served as its composer and principal conductor.[^orch][^creation][^established]

To arrange Iranian repertoire for his singers and orchestra, he listened to earlier recordings, picked out the individual voices and instruments, and wrote down their parts. For “Bu-ye Juy-e Muliyan” he drew on two arrangements from the same family. Ruhollah Khaleghi had scored it for orchestra with male and female soloists. His daughter Golnoush arranged it for choir and piano. Fouladvand said combining the two took about four months.[^press-listening][^press-piece][^press-father][^press-daughter][^press-combined][^press-four][^press-duration]

Fouladvand’s *Voice of Peace* concerts brought performers from Iran to work with the choir. In 2018 he worked with tar player Majid Derakhshani and singer Hourvash Khalili. In 2019 he invited Keyvan Saket and singer Vahid Taj. He later set poems by Rumi and Hafez to music for concerts in Paris and London. The Hafez programme was performed at UNESCO in Paris and at Central Hall Westminster in London in 2025.[^academy-guests][^academy-guestsb][^bio-rumi][^bio-hafez]
