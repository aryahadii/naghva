Peter Soleimanipour (پیتر سلیمانی‌پور) was a composer and wind-instrument player, born in Tehran in 1347 Solar Hijri (SH). As a teenager, he studied piano with کارمن اوسوریو and experimented with guitar; while studying painting at the University of Tehran, he took up flute with احسان سامعی.[^identity][^birth][^learning][^flute]

## Jazz and ensemble work

The Iranian Musicians Union dates his move into professional music to 1373 SH, when he began performing with the group called آتین. That year, he and three other musicians formed a jazz quartet called پرسیکو کوارتت. They explored jazz by playing pieces from its history.[^atin][^persiko]

In 1384 SH, he formed the [Peter Soleimanipour Ensemble](/en/peter-soleimanipour-ensemble-2vr/) to perform his compositions. The Union obituary lists performers including [Mahan Mirarab](/en/mahan-mirarab-7hs/), [Habib Meftah](/en/habib-meftah-49p/) and [Dara Daraee](/en/dara-daraee-cm9/) across different concerts.[^ensemble]

## Recorded work

His own album [*Egosystem*](#egosystem-q8a) sits alongside guest work: on the 2002 various-artists compilation *Qeshm Island*, he performs the 7:30 track [“Daryadaad”](#daryadaad-tdg).[^catalog][^qeshm][^qeshm-date][^qeshm-billing][^daryadaad][^daryadaad-credit][^daryadaad-album][^daryadaad-duration]

On Soheil Nafisi’s 2011 *Lyra & Hymn*, Peter appears as a featured musician on [“Taraneh”](#taraneh-4dm).[^lyra][^lyra-date][^lyra-track]

On [Makan Ashgvari’s “Bi Saropa”](#bi-saropa-q4r), Peter plays bass clarinet, Soheil Peyghambari plays clarinet, and [Ashgvari](/en/makan-ashgvari-n8j/) plays piano and sings.[^bi-saropa]
