Peter Soleimanipour was a Tehran-born composer and wind player who helped form a jazz quartet in the 1990s. The quartet explored earlier jazz repertoire. He went on to form an [ensemble of his own](/en/peter-soleimanipour-ensemble-2vr/) to play his compositions.[^identity][^birth][^atin][^persiko][^ensemble]

## From painting to jazz

Born in 1968–69, Soleimanipour studied piano as a teenager and learned guitar by experimenting. While studying painting at the University of Tehran, he took up the flute.[^birth][^learning][^flute] In 1994–95 he began playing professionally with Atin and formed a jazz quartet with three other musicians.[^atin][^persiko]

## Writing for an ensemble

Soleimanipour formed his own ensemble in 2005–06. Its performers included [Mahan Mirarab](/en/mahan-mirarab-7hs/), [Habib Meftah](/en/habib-meftah-49p/) and [Dara Daraei](/en/dara-daraee-cm9/). In 2008 he was still working with the ensemble in Tehran, where the musicians met in workshops.[^ensemble][^workshop]

Alongside his own album [*Egosystem*](#egosystem-q8a), Soleimanipour recorded with other musicians. He played on [“Taraneh”](#taraneh-4dm) from [Soheil Nafisi](/en/soheil-nafisi-hev/)’s 2011 album *Lyra & Hymn*. He played bass clarinet on [“Bi Saropa”](#bi-saropa-q4r), accompanying [Makan Ashgvari](/en/makan-ashgvari-n8j/) on piano and voice and Soheil Peyghambari on clarinet.[^catalog][^lyra][^lyra-date][^lyra-track][^bi-saropa] Soleimanipour died in September 2018, aged fifty.[^year][^day][^death]
