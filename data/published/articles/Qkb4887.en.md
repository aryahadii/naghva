Ahoora is an Iranian rock band formed in Tehran in late 2001.[^formed][^group] The band recorded its [self-titled debut](#ahoora-nvg) in Tehran in the final months of 2005 and released it independently the following year.[^studio][^debut] Its fourth album, *[We Were Here: Tehran 2011](#we-were-here-tehran-2011-zjd)*, came out in 2026, some fifteen years after the sessions.[^sessions][^archivedate]

## Recording in Tehran

The musicians spent two years trying to set Persian words to their music before they began singing in English.[^lang] In 2004 they recorded the demo *[The Call of Beneath](#the-call-of-beneath-x6f)* in a home studio in Tehran, with Kiavash Kia on lead guitar. The Ministry of Culture had asked for a recording before deciding whether they could perform. Permission was refused.[^demo][^kia][^permit][^refused]

On their debut album Ashkan Hadavand sang, Milad Tangshir played guitar and Mamy Baei played bass.[^lineup] Tangshir recalled a concert at which about 120 listeners filled the venue. A second night had been booked, but was cancelled. The ethnomusicologist Laudan Nooshin recorded that Basij militia members broke up an Ahoora concert at Tehran’s Rahil Hall in September 2006. The group gave away much of its music online.[^rahil][^disrupted][^gig][^cancel][^free]

While the musicians were recording *[All In Blood With You](#all-in-blood-with-you-fjf)*, released in 2007, they heard from a Russian fan club putting together a tribute to the metal band Slayer. Ahoora recorded Slayer’s “Blood Red” for the project.[^invite][^blood][^second] The label Zirzamin released Ahoora’s third album, *[Awkward Diary](#awkward-diary-pgj)*, in 2010.[^third]

## The unfinished fourth album

In 2010 and 2011 Ahoora recorded new pieces in Mosha and Tehran. The musicians had intended to add vocals, but the sessions ended before they finished that version. The band went on hiatus and its members went their separate ways.[^sessions][^unfinished][^hiatus] In 2026 they released the instrumental recordings as *We Were Here: Tehran 2011*.[^instrumental][^archivedate]
