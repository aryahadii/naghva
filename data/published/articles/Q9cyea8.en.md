Regard Persan is a Montreal trio formed by three Iranian Canadian musicians. At their first concert together, in Montreal’s Garage Concerts series in 2013, Pooria Pournazeri played the tanbour, a long-necked lute associated with Kurdish dervishes. Saeed Kamjoo plays two bowed instruments, the kamancheh and the gheychak of Baluchistan. Ziya Tabassian draws on percussion instruments from several regions of Iran.[^identity][^debut][^member0][^member1][^member2][^lute][^bow][^drums]

Their first album, *Regard Persan*, came out in September 2016. The following July they played for the opening of Toronto’s Tirgan Festival with Rumi Canada’s whirling dancers. A collaboration with Al Golpe del Guatimé brought the trio into contact with Veracruz’s son jarocho music. After touring in Canada, the groups were billed to present their joint record *Bote Jeghe* in Veracruz in August 2019.[^issue][^date][^ceremony][^mexico][^canada][^jarocho]

On *Invisible*, released that October, each musician brought something of his own experience to the writing. Pournazeri wrote “Winter in Quebec” about emigrating and making a life in Montreal. Kamjoo’s “Crossed Parallels” sets the tanbour and kamancheh in conversation. Tabassian composed “Invisible Circle,” and on “Ocean of Dreams” Habib Hosseini sings a poem by Rumi set to music by Pournazeri.[^dateap1483041196][^winter][^move][^cross][^dialogue][^circle][^ocean][^voice]

The trio also plays on all ten tracks of Al Golpe del Guatimé’s *Ecos del Radif*, released in 2020. Their jointly billed *Bote Jeghe* reached streaming services in 2021. For the concert *Hendoustan*, the trio arranged a second set with sarod player Aditya Verma.[^albumap1533449642][^dateap1533449642][^albumap1586165677][^dateap1586165677][^sarod]
