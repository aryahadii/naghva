Golnar Shahyar is an Iranian-Canadian vocalist, composer, poet and producer based in Vienna. Austrian Music Export describes her music as combining improvisation, storytelling and activism, and names Choub, Gabbeh, Golnar & Mahan Trio and GolNar among her projects.[^bio][^bands]

## Tear Drop

Bandcamp gives the release date of her album *[Tear Drop](#tear-drop-gbn)* as November 25, 2022. Shahyar composed and arranged every song and produced the album. It was recorded live at RKH in Vienna in December 2021.[^album][^date][^songs][^production][^recording]

The debut album received a German Jazz Prize nomination in 2023.[^nomination]
