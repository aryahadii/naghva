Golnar Shahyar is an Iranian-Canadian singer, composer and songwriter based in Vienna. After studying biology in Toronto, she turned to music, first training in classical singing and then developing her own songs and improvisations. She worked with several ensembles before releasing her solo debut *[Tear Drop](#tear-drop-gbn)* in 2022.[^bio][^mdw-0][^mdw-1][^mdw-4][^mdw-5][^tear-date]

## From biology to music

Shahyar played piano in Iran before moving to Canada. She studied biology at York University in Toronto and discovered improvisation, which opened another way of making music with others. In 2008 she moved to Vienna to pursue music.[^jazzhalo-0][^mdw-0][^jazzhalo-1][^db-0]

At the Vienna Conservatory, she began with classical singing but found that she could not fully express herself within that tradition. Studying with the singer and improviser Elfi Aichinger at the mdw’s Department of Popular Music helped her find a different path, connecting her musical roots with folk, jazz and experimental music.[^mdw-1][^mdw-2][^mdw-3][^mdw-4][^mdw-5] She later took up guitar because she wanted a musical space of her own, rather than depending entirely on accompanying musicians.[^jazzhalo-2]

## Ensembles in Vienna

In spring 2011 Shahyar began working with the guitarist Mahan Mirarab in Choub. She also sang with Sormeh, whose self-titled album came out in 2013. With Mirarab and percussionist Shayan Fathi she formed Sehrang; they made the album *[Dar Lahze](#dar-lahze-qfs)*.[^sarg-0][^sarg-1][^sarg-2][^lotus-1][^lotus-2][^lotus-3][^sehrang][^dar-lahze]

Her work with Mirarab continued in Golnar & Mahan, joined by percussionist Amir Wahba. They released *[Derakht](#derakht-m6d)* in 2017.[^derakht] These collaborations developed alongside her solo practice, in which she accompanied herself and made her own choices about songs and arrangements.[^jazzhalo-2][^tear-credits]

During the pandemic in 2020, Shahyar co-founded WE:Shape with composer Rojin Sharafi and conductor Yalda Zamani. The platform sought better working conditions for freelance musicians and a more inclusive music industry.[^db-1][^db-2][^mdw-6][^mdw-7][^mdw-8][^colleagues]

## Songs under her own name

In 2022 Shahyar sang the leading role in *Negar* at Deutsche Oper Berlin, her first opera. The same year she released *Tear Drop*, which she had recorded live in Vienna in December 2021. She composed, arranged and produced the album herself.[^mica-4][^mica-5][^mica-6][^tear-date][^tear-recorded][^tear-credits][^tear-produced]

For these songs she began at the piano or guitar, wrote the music first and added lyrics afterwards. “Ode to Trust” uses kell, a vocal cry; in “Maman Djan”, her grandmother becomes a figure for the lives of women of her generation, symbolised by the mythical bird Simurgh.[^mica-0][^mica-1][^mica-2][^mica-3][^qantara-0][^qantara-1][^qantara-2][^qantara-3]

She also recorded “Tell My Mother She Has No Daughter Anymore” live at Porgy & Bess in Vienna in 2022, arranging it with Mirarab.[^protest][^protest-arrangement]
