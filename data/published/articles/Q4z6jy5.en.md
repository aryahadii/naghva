Tar player Azar Zargarian leads Shahrzad Ensemble. In Venice in 2009, five women played folk music from different parts of Iran. Maral Pouya sang with Zargarian on tar, Najmeh Saghir on barbat and Parissa Kashefi on kamancheh. Naghmeh Farahmand played tombak, daf and dayereh. The Iranian cultural association in Venice filmed the group playing the Gilani melody “Kuhestani,” the Azerbaijani song “Ayriligh” and “Balal Balalam” from Shiraz.[^leader][^venice][^veniceplayers][^gil][^azeri][^shiraz]

At Tehran’s Niavaran Cultural Centre in 2014, Shahrzad prepared a programme for a women’s audience in memory of singer Gholamhossein Banan. With Homa Tehrani singing, Nazanin Pedarsani on tombak and Nazanin Ghanizadeh on kamancheh, they chose “Showgh-e Talab” for the Chahargah part of the concert and “Nay-e Bi Nava” for Mahur. Ghanizadeh also played with singers Rasoul Raho and Bahador Sehat during her years with the ensemble. Pedarsani’s films of Shahrzad at Niavaran include a trio and performances of the songs «قدح» and «بگو به باران».[^women][^benan][^tehran][^homa][^programme][^shoq][^nay][^ghani][^vnguqwqd9orc][^vaxcvvu6cvs8][^vbvhmrhuaalk]
