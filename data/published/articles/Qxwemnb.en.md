Houshmand Ebadi is an Iranian ney player based in Tehran. He began his performing career with Mohammad Reza Lotfi’s Sheyda ensemble. In [*Fifer*](#fifer-6sz) he recorded the ney alone, and as a member of [Sepidar Ensemble](/en/sepidar-ensemble-m6w/) he plays in a group devoted to Persian classical music.[^hbased][^hshayda][^hfirst][^tc]

## From Sheyda to solo ney

Ebadi began studying music at seven. He learned the ney from Reza Djamshidian and then Abdolnaqi Afsharinia. His first formal concert was with Sheyda under Lotfi’s direction. Ebadi worked with Lotfi until the latter’s death and went on to work with Daryoush Talai. When tar player Mazyar Shahi assembled *Leader of the Love Caravan* in memory of Lotfi, Ebadi played ney with the group and on his own.[^hstart][^hteacher][^hshayda][^hlotfi][^htalai][^hcara]

*Fifer* was Ebadi’s first collection of solo work. Kherad Art House released it as part of *Intimate Sentiments*. He opens in Shour, with an introduction, an unmetered section and then a faster, metered chaharmezrab, before moving on to Bayat-e Tork. He played in a different pairing for Hafdang’s [Naghme Roshan concert](#ney-setar-duet-persian-music-7gc), where Arjang Seyfizadeh accompanied him on setar.[^hfirst][^hfifer][^hduo]

## Later ensembles

Ebadi played on Sepidar’s *Tash*, a recording conceived by Imani as a small musical gathering, and joined the group when it performed in Geneva with Sepideh Raissadat. In November 2024 he toured Europe with Hossein Alizadeh’s Hamavayan Ensemble, playing at the Berlin Philharmonie. On [Araz Salek](/en/araz-salek-zqv/)’s [*Peripheries of Nahavand*](#peripheries-of-nahavand-78p), his ney appears on the last three pieces, among compositions drawing on Persian, Turkish and Arab modal practice.[^tcover][^ths][^gdate][^hgeneva][^htour][^hber][^hcredit][^hper]
