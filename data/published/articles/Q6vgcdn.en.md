Kamakan is an Iranian group founded in the early 2010s by singer and theatre artist [Mehdi Saki](/en/mehdi-saki-3gk/). Its music combines southern Iranian folk traditions with contemporary arrangements and poetry by Rumi and Ebrahim Monsefi.[^origins][^early] Its first album was arranged by [Makan Ashgvari](/en/makan-ashgvari-n8j/); in 2023, Saki developed an electronic trio with Ava Rasti and Axel Moon.[^early][^trio]

## Formation and first album

Saki grew up in Ahvaz listening to Arabic lullabies. He studied dance and theatre before bringing together musicians from Iran’s independent music scene to form Kamakan.[^early] The group performed at Tehran’s Azadi Tower complex in July 2013.[^debut]

The debut album, [*Kamakan*](#kamakan-mzz), appeared in April 2015. Musicians from southern Iran and Tehran worked together on southwestern folk songs and settings of Persian poetry, including Rumi’s verse.[^debut] Ashgvari arranged the album, whose compositions brought folk and jazz influences together.[^early]

## A second album and concerts

In January 2017, Kamakan performed at Silent Green in Berlin. It was the group’s first concert in Germany, attended by several hundred people, according to the event’s organizer Nikolai Blaumer.[^concert][^berlin]

By December 2017, a second album was ready. Producer Ehsan Rasoulof announced it under the planned title *Kamakan Baghi*, with arrangements by [Sardar Sarmast](/en/sardar-sarmast-gjg/), and said that Saki was performing parts of it at an interdisciplinary arts festival.[^second] The group’s booking agency later reported that a second album had not received authorization for release in Iran.[^albums]

The coronavirus outbreak interrupted an Iranian tour in 2020. During the pause, the musicians were composing and recording a new album.[^early]

## The trio

[*IS"*](#is-5ak) was released in September 2023.[^albums] That November, Kamakan took part in a residency organized by La rose des vents and Le Grand Mix in northern France. Saki, on vocals, guitar and percussion, worked with Ava Rasti on bass and electronic equipment and Axel Moon on keyboards, electronic equipment, saz and rubab. The three musicians reworked Kamakan as a trio with electronic sounds.[^trio][^residency]

## The Diver

Saki and Sam Solino, named in Persian as Sam Mazaheri, composed and arranged [“The Diver”](#the-diver-5j2) to words by Hossein Jannati. The group describes the song as a lament for the present world, “from Vancouver to Tehran”.[^diver]
