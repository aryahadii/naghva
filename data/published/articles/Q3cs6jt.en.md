Nima Ramezan is a guitarist and musician associated with Iranian alternative music. Cadence's introduction to its Balzan interview names earlier work with Comment, Pulse and O-Hum.[^career]

## Balzan

Bâlzan is his indie electro-rock duo with singer Amir Balafshan; Ramezan writes songs, composes and plays guitar in the duo.[^duo]

The interview introduction credits all the Balzan album's poetry to Nosrat Rahmani.[^poetry] In the published interview excerpt, Ramezan says he is no longer interested in playing solos and did not think about genre or audience size while creating the work.[^interview]
