Nima Ramezan is a guitarist and composer who played in the Iranian alternative bands [Comment](/en/comment-band-ec3/), Pulse and [O-Hum](/en/o-hum-6v5/). Around 2018 he and the singer Amir Balafshan formed [Balzan](/en/balzan-2t3/), an indie electro-rock duo.[^career][^duo][^started][^esfand]

## Writing with Balzan

Ramezan and Balafshan set poems by Nosrat Rahmani to music for their debut album, *[Balzan](/en/balzan-2t3/#balzan-b5x)*, released in early 2019.[^poetry][^started][^esfand] Looking back on the album, Ramezan said he had given little thought to genre or the size of the audience while writing it. He had also lost interest in playing guitar solos.[^interview]

The two continued to work with Rahmani’s poetry. In 2025 Balafshan adapted the poem “Namak” for “[Parandeye Abi](/en/balzan-2t3/#parandeye-abi-vjt).” Ramezan arranged the song and played the instruments, with Bardia Amiri on drums.[^parande][^parande-date]
