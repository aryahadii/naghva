The Yellow Dogs were an Iranian post-punk band formed in Tehran in 2006 and later based in Brooklyn. They sang in English and developed their music through years of rehearsing in a rooftop room, before appearing in Bahman Ghobadi’s *No One Knows About Persian Cats*. In the United States they recorded two EPs and played local venues and festivals. Guitarist Soroush Farazmand and drummer Arash Farazmand were killed in Brooklyn in November 2013.[^meeting][^music][^rehearsing][^doghouse][^film][^rhode-island][^venues][^deaths][^death-day]

## Tehran

The musicians met through a Tehran skate park in 2006, among skaters and punk-rock listeners. Their rehearsal room had soundproofed walls.[^meeting][^soundproof] For three and a half years, singer and guitarist Obash, bassist Koory, guitarist Looloosh and drummer Sina rehearsed for hours each day in the rooftop space they called the doghouse. Joy Division was among their influences.[^rehearsing][^doghouse][^joy-division]

They appeared in Ghobadi’s 2009 film, performing “New Century” in the doghouse. The film brought attention to their music and to Tehran’s underground scene.[^film][^new-century] They subsequently left Iran and settled in Brooklyn in 2010.[^brooklyn]

## Brooklyn and the EPs

The band became part of Brooklyn’s live music scene, playing venues and festivals.[^venues] They recorded *[In the Kennel](#in-the-kennel-ep-x9z)* and *[Upper Class Complexity](#upper-class-complexity-9m8)* in Rhode Island, releasing them in 2011 and 2012.[^rhode-island][^kennel-date][^credits]

On *Upper Class Complexity*, Keith Souza and Seth Manchester produced, mixed and mastered the recordings at Machines With Magnets in Pawtucket. Neverheard Inc. released the EP.[^credits] By the end of 2012 the band was working on new songs for a first full-length album.[^album-plan]

## The deaths of two members

On 11 November 2013, Soroush Farazmand and his brother Arash, the band’s drummer, were killed in their Brooklyn home. A third Iranian musician was also killed in the attack.[^deaths][^death-day][^third-victim][^brothers]
