The Yellow Dogs were an Iranian-American rock band formed in Tehran in 2006 and later based in Brooklyn.[^origin] They sang in English and cited Joy Division and Talking Heads among their influences.[^music] They also appeared in Bahman Ghobadi's film No One Knows About Persian Cats.[^film]

## Upper Class Complexity

The band’s *[Upper Class Complexity](#upper-class-complexity-9m8) EP* is available on its Bandcamp page.[^album] The page gives its release date as May 9, 2012 and names Neverheard Inc. as the label. Keith Souza and Seth Manchester are credited with production, mixing and mastering at Machines With Magnets in Pawtucket, Rhode Island.[^credits]

## In The Kennel

The band’s Bandcamp catalogue also includes *[In The Kennel EP](#in-the-kennel-ep-x9z)*, dated 17 May 2011, preceding *Upper Class Complexity*.[^kennel-date]
