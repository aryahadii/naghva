Rouzbeh Esfandarmaz is a clarinetist, composer and producer from Tehran who records as Rouzbeh Esfand. At the Art University of Tehran he studied Alban Berg’s clarinet music. He played with the Iranian Orchestra for New Music and the National Youth Orchestra. Meeting Makan Ashgvari and Omid Nemati at university led him to Pallett, where he began taking improvisation as seriously as his classical training.[^bio][^berg][^orch][^meet][^improv]

Esfandarmaz produced three Pallett releases, *Mr. Violet*, *Tehran, Smile!* and *Endless Ending*. He left the group in 2018 to study at the Royal Conservatory in The Hague. He heard a connection between the drumming, singing and repetition of Iranian rituals and the way electronic dance music brings people into a shared rhythm. At the conservatory he met Patrick Stewart, with whom he formed the duo Esfand. With Bushehri singer and percussionist Habib Meftah he developed another live project, Shibaali, which they brought to New York’s Nublu in 2023.[^pallett][^left][^degree][^repeat][^duo][^shib][^tour]

His music for the 2011 documentary *A Cinema of Discontent* pairs his clarinet with Faraz Aghili’s piano. A later work, “The Passing,” accompanied a photographic project about life in Iran during the coronavirus pandemic. For “Absence,” he and Aftab Darvishi shared the composition and played clarinet and piano respectively. In “Where the Light Remembers,” he drew on mourning rituals and Rumi’s poetry, using voice, gongs and an Osmose synthesizer in the Iranian mode chahargah.[^film][^piano][^covid][^darv][^clar][^pian][^living][^chah][^song]
