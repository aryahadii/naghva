Sepehr Alimagham is an Iranian-American DJ and electronic music producer who grew up in the United States. He made his debut album, *[Shaytoon](#shaytoon-9dr)*, in San Francisco and later moved to New York. In 2020 he started a label, also called Shaytoon.[^bio][^identity][^album][^credit][^move][^label][^first][^crown]

## Electronic music at home

Sepehr remembered an older cousin playing psytrance and hard house records on repeat. He traced his interest in electronic music to those listening sessions.[^cousin][^entry] In San Francisco he wrote, mixed and produced the eight tracks of *Shaytoon*, drawing on acid, electro, psychedelia and techno. Dark Entries released it in April 2020.[^sound][^credit][^debut-date][^move]

His EP *[Crown Jewel](#crown-jewel-v74)* followed in December as the first release on his own label.[^label][^first][^crown]
