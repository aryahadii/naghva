Sepehr is an American-Iranian electronic producer. In a 2020 interview he described himself as a diaspora Iranian who grew up in the United States between American and Iranian cultures.[^bio][^identity]

## Shaytoon

His debut album, *[Shaytoon](#shaytoon-9dr)*, was released under the name Sepehr Alimagham. Its eight tracks draw on acid, electro, psychedelia and techno. The release credits Sepehr Alimagham Tabari with writing, mixing and production in San Francisco.[^album][^sound][^credit]

PopMatters describes the cover as a reference to the sounds of pre-Revolution Iran.[^art]
