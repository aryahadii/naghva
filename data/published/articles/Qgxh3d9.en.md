Faravaz Farvardin is an Iranian singer and songwriter based in Berlin. Born in Tehran in 1990, she began playing guitar at twelve and later studied singing. After facing a prison sentence for singing solo, she left Iran. Her first album, *Azadi*, draws on her experiences in Tehran and in Germany.[^born][^guitar][^live][^sentence][^berlin][^record][^date]

## Singing in Tehran

Farvardin studied with musicians including the singer Sara Naeini. In 2016 she sang solo on *Dance with Me*. She was sentenced to a year in prison and chose to leave the country. She sought asylum in Germany in 2018 and lived first in Bad Reichenhall in Bavaria.[^bavaria] She later founded The Right to Sing, an organization that supports women who sing in Iran and Afghanistan.[^teachers][^live][^sentence][^found][^org]

## Songs in Berlin

The short documentary *My Orange Garden* explores how Faravaz sees her own body. For her, the restrictions placed on women’s bodies extended to the sound of their voices. On *Dessert*, a song from her 2025 album *Azadi*, she returned to a childhood memory of adults telling her she should feel ashamed of enjoying food.[^documentary][^body][^dessert][^date]

For *Saffron*, released in September 2026, Faravaz and Charlie McClean wrote three songs pairing Persian melodies with English lyrics.[^saff][^year][^words]
