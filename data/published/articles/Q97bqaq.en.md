Siavash Amini is an Iranian sound artist and composer.[^bio] His album TAR combines drone and modern classical music, using dreams and nightmares as a way to explore the relationship between an individual's fears and a collective state of mind.[^theme]

## TAR

TAR is available through Amini's own Bandcamp page.[^album] It premiered at the Fear Anger Love edition of Berlin's CTM festival in February 2017.[^festival] The release is dated June 9, 2017, with Hallow Ground as the label and Amini as the copyright holder.[^date]
