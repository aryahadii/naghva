Hadi Bastani is an Iranian composer, sound artist and anthropologist. He recorded post-punk in Tehran before turning to noise and computer music. His conversations with electronic musicians in Iran became part of his research and helped shape [*Emergence*](#emergence-v32), a double album released in 2020.[^first][^job][^punk][^noise][^study][^feedback][^emerge]

## Leaving the songs behind

In 2005 Bastani and his bandmates finished recording a post-punk album in Tehran and decided to leave it unreleased. Bastani no longer wanted to write songs that repeated a verse and chorus. He began exploring noise, glitches and computer music. Around 2010–11, in his Paris studio, he heard Umchunga's “Streets 1”. It made him realise that musicians back in Iran were listening to some of the same experimental sounds.[^punk][^leave][^verse][^noise][^paris][^other]

He went on to study that scene. It was through contact with composers and programmers in Iran that the material for *Emergence* began to take shape. Flaming Pines issued the album in June 2020.[^study][^feedback][^emerge]

## Performing and recording with others

At Berlin's tak Theater Aufbau Kreuzberg in June 2021, Bastani and Yalda Zamani improvised with electronic modules and code they wrote during the performance. The recording came out in August 2022 as [*TWO*](#two-live-at-tak-theater-aufbau-berlin-pcb).[^duet][^how][^when]

In [*trans.placed*](#trans-placed-8mt), his first collaboration with Maryam Sirvan, field recordings and electronics trace what happens to the sounds around a listener crossing from outside to inside. They started with a door opening and drew on recordings from Berlin and Tbilisi.[^walk][^places][^first][^door]
