Mahsa Vahdat is a performer of Persian vocal music, born in Tehran on October 29, 1973. According to her label biography, she has performed independently since 1995. It describes her contemporary style as rooted in Persian classical and regional vocal traditions.[^bio][^birth][^independent][^style]

## Poetry and collaboration

She composes most of her songs herself, drawing on poems by Hafez and Rumi, as well as modern poets Fourogh Farokhzad and Mohammad Ibrahim Jafari.[^poetry]

*Scent of reunion – Love duets across civilizations* pairs her with Mighty Sam McClain. Its publisher dates the release to September 11, 2009 and describes melodies written by Vahdat and three other composers in spring 2009. Recording took place in Oslo, Tehran and New Hampshire.[^duet][^date][^melody][^recording]
