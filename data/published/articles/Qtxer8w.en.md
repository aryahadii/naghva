Faarjam is a Netherlands-based pop-rock band formed in Arnhem in 2013 around Iranian frontman Massoud Faarjam Saidi. According to beehype’s 2017 profile, the group released its first EP, Awar E Rang, in its founding year.[^band]

## Formation

Saidi was born in Iran and grew up in the Netherlands. The profile traces his work as a singer and guitarist with different bands to 2003, followed by solo projects centred on Persian poetry from 2009, particularly the work of Hossein Panahi. Those projects developed into Faarjam.[^early][^poetry]

## Koala

Koala appears on the artist’s own playlist.[^koala] On Ba Chashmanam and Mordad, Faarjam performs vocals, guitar and effects, joined by musicians playing electric guitar, keyboards, trumpet, bass, drums and percussion.[^players]

## Releases

Apple Music carries the singles Amsterdam,[^release2] Amme,[^release3] Fathe Bagh,[^release4] Sabze Lajani[^release5] and Ghasedak,[^release6] as well as the EPs Awar E Rang[^release7] and Koala.[^release8]
