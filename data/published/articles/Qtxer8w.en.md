Faarjam is a pop-rock band formed in Arnhem in 2013 by Dutch musicians and the Iranian singer and guitarist Massoud Faarjam Saidi. The group grew out of Saidi’s solo work with Persian poetry and released its first EP, [*Awar E Rang*](#awar-e-rang-ep-w7e), in its founding year.[^band][^early][^poetry][^edition-release7]

## From poetry to a band

Saidi was born in Iran and grew up in the Netherlands. He began playing guitar and singing in bands in 2003. In 2009 he started working on Persian poems on his own, particularly those of Hossein Panahi. This was the music around which the band came together in Arnhem.[^early][^poetry][^band]

In August 2017 the group released its five-song EP [*Koala*](#koala-px6), opening with the title track.[^band][^release8][^koala] On “Ba Chashmanam” and “Mordad,” Saidi sang and played guitar with a band that included keyboards and trumpet as well as electric guitar, bass and drums.[^players]
