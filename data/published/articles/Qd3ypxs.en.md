Salome MC is an Iranian rapper and producer. Her own artist page also identifies composition and multimedia art among her fields of work.[^identity][^practice]

## Excerpts From Unhappy Consciousness

Her Bandcamp page presents *[Excerpts From Unhappy Consciousness](#excerpts-from-unhappy-consciousness-7h4)* as her third studio album. It offers the recording directly through her artist account.[^album]

Salome MC composed, wrote and performed every song, with mixing by Hussein Shahani.[^credits]
