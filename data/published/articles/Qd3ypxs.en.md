Salome MC is an Iranian rapper, producer and multimedia artist who began making hip-hop in Tehran in the early 2000s. After studying audiovisual art in Japan, she developed work across music, video and installation. In the United States she began teaching hip-hop and founded Seven Climes, a collaborative project supporting rappers from marginalized communities.[^a-identity][^c-beginnings][^a-japan][^s-workshop][^s-seven]

## Beginning in Tehran

Salome’s first hip-hop cassette was by Cartel, a group of rappers from the Turkish diaspora in Germany. In Tehran, she joined a small circle of hip-hop listeners who found each other through Yahoo chatrooms and began creating a local scene.[^c-cartel][^c-peers]

At first she worked with friends who knew more about recording and production. She gradually learned to handle the work herself.[^n-independence] She later explained that her wish for independence led her to learn composition and study sound engineering so that she could make music without depending on others.[^b-independence]

## Japan and audiovisual work

In 2010 Salome moved to Japan on a MEXT scholarship. She earned a master’s degree in audiovisual arts at Tohoku University of Arts and Design and began combining music with performance and audiovisual installations.[^a-japan] Living in Sendai during the 2011 earthquake and tsunami later led her to make “Aquaphobia.”[^c-aquaphobia]

In 2013 she released [*Price of Freedom*](#price-of-freedom-h3h), also titled *I Officially Exist*.[^price]

In summer 2015 she worked with the Japanese-American rapper Shing02 on [“Passenger”](#passenger-ere) for the 1+1 recording project. The two shared the beat credit; Norient’s studio report described her composing the instrumentals, bringing the production skills she had developed at home into their collaboration.[^n-project][^n-passenger][^passenger-beat][^n-composing][^n-independence]

## Albums and teaching

Salome moved to Washington State in 2016.[^a-washington] She planned *I Officially Exist* and [*Excerpts From Unhappy Consciousness*](#excerpts-from-unhappy-consciousness-7h4) as concept albums.[^c-album-question][^c-concept] She composed, wrote and performed all the songs on the latter, which Hussein Shahani mixed.[^credits]

In 2018 she established the youth workshop HipHop without Borders. Initially intended for visually impaired children, it expanded to students learning English.[^s-workshop] The following year she introduced [“Three”](#three-feat-srivani-jade-dej) in a Persian essay, explaining that it expressed the anxiety and alienation of her first three months after giving birth. She wrote and produced the song herself.[^b-three][^b-credits]

## Seven Climes

During the Covid-19 lockdowns in Seattle, Salome began Seven Climes to bring together young musicians from marginalized communities. The project uses hip-hop to preserve languages and oral history and to make the region’s cultural and linguistic diversity heard.[^s-seven] She moved to Austin in 2023.[^s-austin]
