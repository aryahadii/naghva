Kian Pourtorab is a musician, composer, lyricist, arranger and singer working in alternative rock and pop.[^profile] He helped form [Comment Band](/en/comment-band-ec3/) with guitarist [Nima Ramezan](/en/nima-ramezan-3cs/) and was its singer and lyricist.[^band]

## A solo direction

After [This Side](#this-side-e79), Pourtorab described a change in his work toward electronic pop, while also discussing further rock material with Comment.[^change]

Apple's catalogue dates This Side to 2018, Q to 2020, [Shabgard](#shabgard-z5s) to 2022, and both Gozar and [Maranjab](#maranjab-2s2) to 2023.[^first][^q][^shabgard][^gozar][^maranjab]
