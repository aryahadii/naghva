The three musicians in SASA met on a beach in Stockholm and discovered they shared a taste for The Police. At first they played the British band’s songs under the name POLIZEI. In 2023 they formed SASA to play their own material, with Safoura Safavi singing and playing bass, David Douhan on guitar and keyboards and Marcus Bouquelon on drums.[^beach][^pol][^formed][^members]

## From a poetry collection to a single

SASA released the five-track EP *Transcend* in November 2023. The following day they put out “Spillolja,” setting a poem from Jenny Wrangborg’s collection *Kallskänken* to music. Melody Safavi directed the video. She and her sister Safoura later shared the directing credit on “Amor Fati.”[^sasatrans][^sasaspill][^poem][^poet][^director][^amor]

“Bi Sedaa” and “Zaalem” from the EP also reached Persian-speaking viewers through Sina Valiollah’s television programme. SASA recorded their appearance in November 2023. The performance of “Zaalem” was broadcast in April the following year.[^sina][^aired]
