Salvaation is the Paris darkwave duo of Iranian singer Meshkat Mosavat, who performs as Meshcut, and French musician Christine Guin, also known as Oui Future. They met at Rosa Bonheur sur Seine, a music venue on a boat. Mosavat was looking for places to play and Guin was the artistic director. Mosavat recalled seeing a photograph of Depeche Mode in Guin’s studio and discovering that they shared a love of 1980s music.[^duo][^pair][^venue][^visit][^poster]

Before adopting the name Salvaation, they made an EP in 2025 for the project Teheran 69. Guin arranged, mixed and mastered it. The duo’s own [self-titled EP](#salvaation-66k) appeared in March 2026. On “Loop,” Mosavat sings over tremolo guitar, repeating synthesizer figures and a heavy drum beat. The four-song record also includes “I Would Want It All,” “This Is the End” and “Forever.” A September digital edition carried Meshcut’s name.[^before][^work][^debut][^voice][^riff][^four][^autumn]
