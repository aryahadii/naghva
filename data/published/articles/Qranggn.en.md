Golfam Khayam is an Iranian composer and improviser who grew up in an artistic family in Tehran. Her official biography describes how she incorporates native musical elements into contemporary experimental music. She earned a Master of Music at the University of Cincinnati's College-Conservatory of Music.[^bio][^origin][^study]

## Ravi

Khayam plays guitar on *Ravi*. According to its distributor, the Doberman-Yppan release features her interpretations and improvisations on the music of Dusan Bogdanovic. The distributor also describes an Iranian edition on Hermes Records.[^ravi][^music][^hermes]
