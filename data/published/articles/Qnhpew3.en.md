Inner Unity Ensemble first played together in Duisburg in February 2023, accompanying Iranian-Azerbaijani singer [Samin Ghorbani](/en/samin-ghorbani-ncx/). The group was founded by Yalda Yazdani, an ethnomusicologist born in Iran, and guitarist Mahan Mirarab, its musical director.[^debut][^found][^iran] The players draw on the intervals of Iranian radif and Arabic and Turkish maqam and arrange these melodies using harmonies from jazz and European classical music. Cellist Uygur Vural plays Turkish maqam melodies and takes the bass line when the arrangement calls for it.[^radif][^cello][^role]

## From Duisburg to Zurich

The Duisburg Philharmonic filmed the group's first concert. With [Shabnam Parvaresh](/en/shabnam-parvaresh-9as/) on clarinet, the ensemble accompanied Ghorbani on “Qara Qile” and returned for an encore with “Shahrashoob.”[^debut][^clar][^qara][^shahr] The following Nowruz, at Moods in Zurich, Ghorbani shared the vocal programme with Sakina Teyna, a Kurdish-Alevi singer from Varto in Turkey. [The venue's concert film](https://moods.ch/en/streaming/inner-unity-ensemble-sakina-teyna-2024-03-17) preserves their performance.[^nor][^singer][^film]

In March 2025 the group returned to Duisburg for *Female Voices United*. Teyna, Basma Jabr, Merve Akyıldız, Simin Tander and Golnar Shahyar each sang with the ensemble.[^united][^voices] In September 2026 the players joined the Gürzenich Orchestra at the Cologne Philharmonie for *Female Voices of Resilience*. Juya Shin conducted and Yazdani directed the programme.[^col][^stage]
