Hamid Saeidi is an Iranian composer, producer and santoor player. His biography traces his study of Persian classical music to the age of fifteen and names Madjid Kiani as his santoor teacher.[^identity][^training]

## Composing across media

Saeidi's work includes multimedia composition. His biography records commissions from the Farhang Foundation in 2011 and 2012 for Nowruz celebrations at the Los Angeles County Museum of Art.[^commission]

## Imaginary Knots

The Apple Music edition of *[Imaginary Knots](#imaginary-knots-7zw)* contains seven tracks and is dated April 1, 2013. Saeidi is listed as the recording’s copyright holder.[^album]
