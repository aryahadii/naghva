> Arash Zarabi is a music composer, double bass player, performer and film score composer based in Tehran. He started his musical journey playing the double bass in different classical orchestras including the Tehran Symphony Orchestra, Iran’s National Orchestra, and Iran-Austria (AISO). At the moment, he is exploring new influences in his music by incorporating jazz, folk, soul, and alternative.
