Ma'jara, episode 12: an interview with Sanam by the host Mohammad, with
performances. Uploaded 2020-11-29. Heard 2026-11-08 through an ElevenLabs
Scribe transcription, checked against Gemini where its parts were ready;
the transcriptions are kept outside the repository. Short quotations only.

> صنم معروف‌خانی، خواننده، شاعر و نوازنده مهمون این قسمته

01:21. The host's introduction.

> من موسیقی رو تو سن خودم فکر می‌کنم دیری شروع کردم حول و حوش شونزده، هفده سالگی

09:13. She began music late, at around sixteen or seventeen, studying
classical piano with a teacher whose name the two transcriptions spell
differently, then harmony with other teachers.

> یه سرکشی اومد سراغم

10:04–11:09. She enjoyed classical study but felt it was not her path, and
began performing in cafés, to her family's surprise.

> تو یه سری کافه می‌رفتم اجرا

11:09.

> براش موزیک نوشتم

13:47–14:21. She began writing songs to reach someone who would not talk to
her: the start, she says, of her singer-songwriting.

> روز کنکور تجسمی بود

14:22–15:20. On the day of the visual-arts university entrance exam, at
about eighteen, friends took her to Café Romance near Ferdowsi Square, where
she played her one verse at the piano and the whole café applauded.

> شروع کردن دست زدن

15:10.

> نزدیک یکی دو سال خیلی کم‌کار بودم

22:30. She had released little for a year or two before 2020.

> یک track افغان هستش به اسم نهان

22:56–23:17. Her next release, due within about two weeks: "Nahan", an Afghan
song she reinterpreted with Peyman Salimi, who rearranged and produced it.

> من تا مدتی خیلی طولانی فقط انگلیسی کار کردم

25:07–26:21. For a long time she wrote and sang only in English; putting
Persian on this kind of music was hard for her.

> همین فانوس دریایی اولین دمو من انگلیسیه

26:21. "Mesl-e Fanus-e Daryayi" (Like a Lighthouse), a song in progress,
began as an English demo that she rewrote in Persian.

> بچه‌های Jetpack فارسی رو انتخاب کردن

26:40. "Noghteh-ha", a synth-pop song, existed in Persian and English demos;
the Jetpack musicians chose the Persian one.

> من خیلی دنبال سادگی‌ام

28:04. On her lyrics in both languages.

> اول که ملودی رو می‌سازم

29:15–29:52. Her method: the melody first, then vowel sounds to find the
metre, then the words.

> تو چرا فارسی رو با لحن اونور می‌خونی؟

30:58–31:30. What listeners asked about a cover she recorded on short
notice: why she sang Persian with a foreign accent.

> سفری رفته بودم ترکیه

34:36–35:14. In Turkey, without her piano, she borrowed her housemate's
guitar, which she could play only three notes on, and wrote a song on them.

> به صورت پارت‌تایم توی یه باری اجرا می‌کردم

35:43–37:22. She earned money there by singing part-time in a bar; the
fantasy of playing for people turned into a routine.

> اسمش Silence بود ولی بعد تصمیم گرفتم اسمش رو بذارم Red Red Wine House

38:15. The song from that time, named after the bar; she performs it in the
episode.
