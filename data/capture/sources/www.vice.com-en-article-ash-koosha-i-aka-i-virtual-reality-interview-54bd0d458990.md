> When I went back to Iran, and I was listening to the CDs on my Walkman back then it was the first time I got introduced to Mogwai and Sigur Ros. A lot of crazy stuff. That was one of the points that changed my vision in music, especially the first Sigur Ros album, Von.

> It’s how I learned to play keyboard. I would make up shapes on the keyboard and memorize chords by shape.

> When I went to conservatory I learned how to read music, but I still think in terms of geometry.

> This is how I see music and it’s why I’m a little frustrated with the ways people listen to my music and can’t see what I see when I make it. It’s the reason for the VR. It puts people there with me.
