> Project leader: Hooshyar Khayam. Composers: Hooshyar Khayam and Bamdad Afshar.

> “Raaz” is the name of a project based on the music of Baluchestan, composed by Hooshyar Khayam in collaboration with composer and electronic musician Bamdad Afshar.

> The work was commissioned by the Iranian Heritage Foundation (IHF) in London.

> The work is for tape (pre-recorded music of Baluchistan), live vocalists, Baluchi ensemble, piano, string quartet, prepared piano and electronics.
