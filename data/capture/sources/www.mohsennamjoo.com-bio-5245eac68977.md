> Born in 1976 in Khorasan province, one of epicenters of poetry and music north east of Iran, Mohsen Namjoo is a Singer Songwriter, Music scholar and Setar (traditional Persian lute) player currently based in New York City.

> Mohsen Namjoo has released 19 albums and over 20 singles. His latest album, Magnetism is released on December 21, 2025.

> Seamlessly blending the Classical Persian music and scales with electric guitar,Rock and Blues vocal techniques with Persian Avaz (singing).

> Since his arrival to the U.S. in 2009, Namjoo has been touring the world
