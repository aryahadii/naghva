> Elian Amer Marjieh (sometimes credited as Margieh; Arabic: إليان عامر مرجية; born January 22, 2002),[1] known professionally as Elyanna (إليانا), is a Palestinian and Chilean singer-songwriter,[2] merging Arabic music with Latin rhythms to create an experimental Arab-pop sound that has captured a global audience.
