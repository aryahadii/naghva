> Ethnic fusion duo Vas are comprised of Iranian-born vocalist Azam Ali and American percussionist Greg Ellis ; after issuing their debut, Sunyata , in 1997, they resurfaced the following year with Offerings . In the Garden of Souls appeared in the spring of 2000, and Feast of Silence followed in 2004.
