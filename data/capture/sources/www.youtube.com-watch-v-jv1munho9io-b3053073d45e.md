## Interview, checked against two transcriptions

Cadence interview with Jetpack, part one, with Mohammad Shariat and Arian
Shariat; uploaded 2021-04-22. Their answers, heard 2026-11-08 through two
speech-to-text runs (ElevenLabs Scribe and Gemini) that agree on the
substance of each passage below; the wording quoted is Scribe's. The
transcriptions are kept outside the repository. Short quotations only.

> اینکه بگیم Daft Punk ایرانی به نظر من درست نیست

01:10. Mohammad: asked why people call them the Iranian Daft Punk, he says
Daft Punk inspired them but the label is wrong.

> اونم اولین ترکی بود که release کردیم

02:50. Mohammad: "Shab", whose vocal effects recall Daft Punk, was the first
track they released, and something of an experiment. (The platform dates in
the record put "Forbidden Love" a few days earlier; they do not settle the
order.)

> ترک‌های فارسیمون اونجوری نبود که خودمون خیلی ازش لذت ببریم و اونجوری هم نبود که خیلی شنیده بشه

03:56. Arian: their two Persian tracks pleased neither them nor listeners, so
they moved towards what they enjoyed.

> کلاً آریان بیشتر انگلیسی می‌خونه

04:23. Mohammad: the first two tracks followed a more commercial plan; Arian
sings mostly in English, so they made Jetpack an English project.

> من شعر انگلیسی می‌تونم بگم

05:14. Arian: he can write lyrics in English, which he could not in Persian.

> من این پروژه رو با مجید مطرح کردم گیتاریستمون و اون استقبال کرد. ما قرار بود اصلا دو نفری کار بکنیم

06:50. Mohammad: he proposed an electronic project to Majid, the guitarist;
it was meant to be just the two of them.

> مثلا با vocoder و اینا هم ضبط کردیم

07:30. Mohammad: as a duo they recorded with a vocoder, and it did not work
out as they wanted.

> روز به روز هم جریان موزیک پاپ تو ایران داره بدتر میشه

08:50. Mohammad: pop in Iran was getting worse by the day, so they chose to
make music they enjoy.

> آخر روز می‌بینم باز گیتار دستم نشستم دارم ساز می‌زنم

14:19. Arian studies computing, but music takes most of his life.

> خیلی از اوقات به من میگن که نمی‌دونن این بند کجاییه

18:57. Mohammad: friends abroad play their songs, and listeners cannot tell
where the band is from.

> مجید خودش واقعا ازش متشکرم

20:35. Mohammad thanks Majid for getting their songs onto Apple Music, Spotify
and YouTube.

> حتی یه ایده محمد بود که مثلا حداقل توی cover art‌هامون یه کم المان‌های ایرانی‌تر بیاریم

22:04. Arian: they thought about Iranian elements, at least in the cover art,
but their music does not suit them.

> چون اکثرا نمی‌دونن که ما ایرانی‌ایم

27:03. Mohammad: because they sing in English, most listeners do not know
they are Iranian, and they cannot count their real audience.
