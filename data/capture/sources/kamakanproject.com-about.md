> Kamakan (کماکان) is an Iranian fusion group formed in 2012 by
>           Mahdi Saki — a theatre-trained actor, singer, and composer born in 1974 in Khuzestan,
>           in Iran's south. Kamakan's music sets contemporary arrangements against the folk traditions of
>           southern Iran, drawing its lyrics from the poetry of Rumi (Mowlana) alongside the verses of poet
>           Ebrahim Monsefi. The group released its self-titled debut album,
> Kamakan
> , in 2015 to a
>           warm reception from audiences and critics, and has since performed at international venues and
>           festivals, including Berlin's Maxim Gorki Theatre.
