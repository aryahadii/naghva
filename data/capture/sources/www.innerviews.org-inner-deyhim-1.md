> How do you look back at
> Majoun,
> your 1996 duo album with Horowitz?

> I’d come up with vocal melodies first and then determine what kind of rhythms and melodies could be based on the poem.

> I might make things a little more abstract after that, or a little more electronic.

> We exchange sounds and groupings.

> I also have an infatuation with silence and the atmospheric elements of things.

> It was mostly Richard’s album on which I sang.

> You’ve done a lot of work with Bill Laswell. Describe your working relationship.
> When my album Madman of God came out, I was supposed to do a remix. I told Crammed Records that I wanted to work with Bill and they got really excited. Bill agreed, but really liked the album and felt it didn’t need remixing. But he got together with Karsh Kale and came up with some great ideas.
