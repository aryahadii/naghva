> This is a past event Persepolis After Dusk An evening of intimate music and immersive visuals. Iranian-born, Indian-raised Azam Ali has confirmed her place as one of the most prolific, versatile, and gifted singers, producers, and composers on the international stage today. Together with Iranian multi-instrumentalist/composer Loga Ramin Torkian, she pioneered the cutting-edge acoustic/electronic music group NIYAZ which became an instant world music sensation. Taking inspiration from the exhibition Persia: Ancient Iran and the Classical World , Azam and Loga have curated an intimate, acoustic performance featuring a collective of extraordinary musicians as well as immersive visuals projected on the facade of the Getty Villa Museum. Featuring: Azam Ali, vocals Loga Ramin Torkian, guitar viol and lafta Kourosh Moradi, tanbur Miisaq Eshaghi, kamancheh Javad Butah, tabla Tara Pandya, dance Jesse Gilbert, visual artist Persia After Hours is a collaboration between Farhang Foundation, UCLA’s Herb Alpert School of Music, and Getty.
