> On 2013 he founded Otaghkar as a platform for independent music, that organises events, workshops and music releases. More than a hundred people have participated in Otaghkar workshops. On 2017 Otaghkar started and managed a festival of independent music in Tehran, named Bazshenid, with 26 independent musicians performing.
