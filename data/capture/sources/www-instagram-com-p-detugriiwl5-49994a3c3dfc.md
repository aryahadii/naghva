> When I started Arsames in 2002, I was the drummer, and I never planned to become the band's vocalist.

> We started in underground basements in Mashhad, one of the most religious cities in Iran, where underground metal had almost no place to exist.

> Back then, finding a skilled metal drummer in Iran was extremely difficult. So, years before starting the band, I began learning drums because I wanted to be the drummer of my own band and make things happen.

> I kept playing drums with Arsames until 2008. But managing the band and playing drums at the same time was a big challenge. I decided it was time to pass the drums to a younger, more powerful drummer.

> Around the same time, our vocalist left the band, so I took on a new role and became the vocalist. And I've been doing it ever since!
