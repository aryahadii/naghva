Programme dates and credits (Voorstellingsinformatie), read 2026-10-15 and
2026-10-21. The Dutch National Opera & Ballet installation for WorldPride
Amsterdam 2026 at Studio Boekman combined new music, video and personal
stories; concept and composition Thomas van Dun, concept and direction Arnout
van Krimpen, libretto Fleur Pierets. Sanam is credited among the singers on
video: a recorded contribution, not evidence that she performed live at each
showing.
