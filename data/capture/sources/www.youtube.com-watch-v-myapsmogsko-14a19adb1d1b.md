## Interview, checked against two transcriptions

Cadence video podcast, episode 6, host Siamak Gholizadeh; uploaded
2023-10-20. Golsa's answers, heard 2026-11-08 through two speech-to-text
runs (ElevenLabs Scribe and Gemini) that agree on the substance of each
passage below; the wording quoted is Scribe's. The transcriptions are kept
outside the repository. Short quotations only.

> من بیست و هشت سالمه الان

07:05. Her age at the recording, which took place before the October 2023
upload: born about 1995.

> یه یارویی داشت کنار اسکله گیتار می‌زد و می‌خوند

05:11–05:42. In her third year of middle school, on a family trip to the
north, a man singing with a guitar by the pier moved her to tears; some time
later her father came home with a guitar for her. She took guitar lessons,
then learned piano and some theory from a relative.

> چند وقت بعدش بابام یه روز با گیتار اومد

05:42.

> مدیرمون اومد این برقشو قطع کرد

10:04. At high school she sang with her guitar at celebrations in the prayer
hall until the principal cut the microphone's power; she places it around
1388 (2009–10).

> من ورودی نود و دو بودم

11:05. She entered Shahid Beheshti University in 1392 (2013–14) to study
biology (marine biology, 17:20) and on her first day went looking for the
university's music club.

> یه زیرزمینی بود توی مثلاً معاونت فرهنگیش

11:31. The music club met in a basement of the cultural office, with
students from every department.

> خوندن و اینا که اصلاً فقط تو اون زیرزمینه می‌شد بخونی

12:15. Singing was possible only in the basement, not on stage.

> یکی از دوستامون می‌خواست apply کنه بره کانادا

14:15–15:08. Before a friend from the club left for Canada, the group played
covers (Anathema, Radiohead) for friends in a café: her first performance
for strangers, after which she wanted to keep performing.

> این یوکلله رو برام گرفتن برای تولدم

18:24. Her bandmates gave her a ukulele for her birthday.

> یه پیج جدید باز کردم و گفتم که خب این برای کارای موزیکم فقط

18:55–19:45. She opened a new Instagram page only for her music, starting
with "500 Miles", and its following grew from about a hundred.

> همه دوستای موزیسینی که پیدا کردم اینا همش از طریق اینستا بود

20:04.

> گروه بنفشم گروه پیمان بود

21:14–21:46. Before her own releases she sang backing vocals in Peyman
Abdali's group Banafsh, where she learned to improvise, met Soheil
Salimzadeh and worked with Mr Hekayati on *Les Misérables*: musical theatre,
backing vocals, whatever kept her close to music.

> این دیدو به من داد آرش که تو می‌تونی اون کاوره رو برای خودت بکنی

22:03–22:56. Working on covers with Arash Zarrabi taught her to make a song
her own; changing covers led her to write songs.

> کامل شد track بعد مثلا تازه همو دیدیم

23:22–24:32. She and Moody Moussavi made "The Poet" over the internet and met
only when the track was finished; many people know her from it.

> سایه رو خودت ساخته بودی

25:42–25:46. The host's question; she answers that she made "Sayeh" herself
and took it to the producer, who rebuilt the arrangement around her vocal.

> اون ورژن اولی به به فقط من و بهزاد و سردار و سالار بودیم

26:44–27:20. The first Bah Bah line-up; Mani and Jahan joined later.

> یهو همون شب سرگشته رو ساختم

27:41–28:52. She wrote "Sargashteh" one night, adapting a poem she found
online; about two years later Taha Mazaheri gathered musicians to arrange it.
It is her favourite of her songs.

> عید، عید releaseش کردم

29:01–29:40. The four songs, each arranged by someone different, became the
EP she released at Nowruz 1401 (March 2022).

> رامشگر که تازه‌ست، نی‌نیه، تازه چند ماهشه

34:49–35:03. Rameshgar was a few months old at the recording: Golsa, Taha
Mazaheri, Hamed, Farid and Mehrdad. They were arranging songs she had
written earlier and had not yet made one together.

> هر کدوم این همکاری‌ها مثلاً صدای یک دوره از منو داره نشون میده

36:43.

> با یه سری انتقاد روبه‌رو شدم از همین جامعه‌ی مثلا موزیسینی

37:02–38:21. Criticism from other musicians, for working with many people and
singing covers, checked her early enthusiasm.

> یه چیزی از درونم می‌خواد بریزه بیرون که حرفای منه

40:23. She describes moving from singing covers to writing her own songs.

> ما توی یه حبابی هستیم که نه صدامون به جایی می‌رسه نه اونا می‌دونن ما هستیم

44:43.

> یه نماینده‌ای هم از ایران باشه که از این حبابه یکم بیاد بیرون

45:26. What she hopes for: someone from Iran on an international stage.

> من اینا رو خیلی از سردار یاد گرفتم

48:22. She learned old jazz standards largely from Sardar Sarmast, playing
with him.
