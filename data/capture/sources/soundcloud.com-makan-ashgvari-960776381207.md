> Makan Ashgvari is a singer, songwriter, actor, director, art manager and
> teacher living and working in Tehran, Iran.

Page text, read 2026-10-19.

> He has published two personal albums (Hejdah and To Trucks) and has two albums named “Far” and “Then Go” with the guitarist Amir Saremi.

> He collaborated with “Naima Band” as the main vocalist in the album “Tehran’s Shore”, and he’s been the art director of the first album of the band “Kamakan”.
