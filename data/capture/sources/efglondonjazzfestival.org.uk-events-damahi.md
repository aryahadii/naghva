> Damahi | EFG London Jazz Festival

> Iranian band Damahi make their highly anticipated debut at the EFG London Jazz Festival.

> Formed in 2014, the band comprises of talented musicians including Dara Daraee on bass, Hamzeh Yeganeh on keyboards and benjo, Ebrahim Alavi on guitar and ud, and Reza Koolghani as the lead singer.

> Their unique musical style is a beautiful fusion of Iran's regional music, with a particular focus on the southern seashores, jazz, and a diverse range of international genres.

> Mark your calendars for November 17th and prepare to be enthralled by Damahi's performance at Rich Mix in London.

> This show is sold out, but a second show has been added on Monday 20 November, at World Heart Beat Embassy Gardens'.
