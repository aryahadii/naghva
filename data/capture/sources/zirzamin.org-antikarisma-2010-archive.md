> Artist: AntiKarisma
> Video: The Embryo of a New Band
> Info: A new rock band is being put together. The new band, Antikarisma, consists of Shahin Najafi, Babak Khazaei, Armin Mostaed, and Pejman Afshari who live in Germany. Najafi is mostly known for his hip hop works. The new band is another demonstration of Najafi's continuous innovation in music.
