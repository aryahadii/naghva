> کاوه صالحی، نوازنده درجه یک گیتار، با این ساز که نامش را لوتار گذاشتیم اجرا کرد.

Written Shargh interview with composer Fardin Khalatbari by Somayeh Ghazizadeh, reprinted in Sarpoosh. Answer discussing the recording process (lines298–299), read2026-11-13 in full. Album is Homayoun Shajarian’s Emshab Kenare Ghazalhaye Man Bekhab. Khalatbari describes commissioning a modified instrument with oudlike body and guitar neck after attempts to use harp and santur; Salehi played it and helped reach the sound he wanted. This is the composer’s testimony about his own collaborator, not an inferred connection to a namesake pianist.
