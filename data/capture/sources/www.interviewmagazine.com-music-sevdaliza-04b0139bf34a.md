> Although she released two electro-pop EPs in 2015, Sevdaliza considers her debut album to be her “first real body of work.” ISON, out now via Twisted Elegance, is, to her, an emotional “diary.”

> “I think the subconscious is the most important,” she says. “Whenever I go into a space with a preconceived idea, it never really works out. It’s about finding balance between seizing control and letting go.”
