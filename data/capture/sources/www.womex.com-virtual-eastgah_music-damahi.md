> Reza Koolaghani (Vocal, Guitar)

> Damahi was formed in 2014 with Dara Darai (Bass player, Bandleader), Hamzeh Yeganeh (Keyboard and Benjo player), Ebrahim Alavi (Guitar and Ud player), and Reza Koolghani (Singer).

> Released in 2015, “Damahi” was the title of the band’s first album.

> Damahi’s innovative style and its use of world music elements with the general pop format, have brought them a diverse range of fans.
