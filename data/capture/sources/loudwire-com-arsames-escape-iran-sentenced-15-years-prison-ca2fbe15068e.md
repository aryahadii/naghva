> We [were] arrested in 2017 when we were in our studio during rehearsal. They moved us to jail that day and [did] not [tell] our family about where we [were] for a week.

> Finally after nearly a month later we paid bail to come out of prison and they told us you should not work, release [or sell] your merch until your final court ... and do not talk with media!

> Our Instagram page, official website ... banned and they shot down all for a year, but we built a new Instagram again and [started] to be active until few weeks ago [when] the court called us again and they gave us 15 years [in] prison. So we had to escape from Iran.

> In a statement to Loudwire, Arsames wrote that they’re not a Satanic band.
