> Performing Persia After Hours at the Getty Villa remains one of my most cherished memories on stage. The concert, presented in collaboration with Farhang Foundation, with whom I have had the honor of working many times over the years, felt like stepping into a living fairy tale. That night, under a full moon that rose perfectly center stage, the projections of ancient Persia came alive around us, enveloping the audience in a tapestry of history, mysticism, and light.
