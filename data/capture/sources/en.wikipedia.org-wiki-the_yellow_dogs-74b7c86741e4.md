> The Yellow Dogs were an Iranian-American rock band, formed in 2006.[1] The band was formed in Tehran, and is now based in Brooklyn, New York.[2]

> They sang in English and played Western instruments, citing Joy Division, Talking Heads as an influence.

> They performed in Bahman Ghobadi's Cannes Un Certain Regard award-winning film, No One Knows About Persian Cats
