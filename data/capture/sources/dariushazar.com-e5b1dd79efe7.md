> DARIUSH AZAR — Musician · Singer · Composer · Contrabass player

> He began his musical studies at age 10 — Orff method, tombak, piano, clarinet, and classical vocals — before entering Tehran University of Arts to specialize in double bass in 2002.

> He released his debut album Blue Gray (آبی خاکستری) in 2019 and his second album 40 (چهل) in 2025. He is currently based in Montréal, Canada.

> The debut -comprises based on a personal love story set within an experimental electronic soundscape, blending vocals with electric instruments.

> A deeply personal second album - a meditation on four decades of life, craft, and self-reflection; creating a minimalist atmosphere through a blend of vocals and acoustic instruments.

> After graduation, he joined the ensemble of master Mohammad Reza Shajarian and later collaborated with Homayoun Shajarian. Between 2009 and 2016 he pioneered Iranian fusion music with groups Pallett, Dang Show, and Dal, touring across Europe and Iran.
