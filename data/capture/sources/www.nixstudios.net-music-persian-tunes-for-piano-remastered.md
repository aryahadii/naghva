> Arrangement /Piano / Production/Mi x/Master:: Hamzeh Yeganeh Rerecorded in Kargadan Studio - July 13th, 2024 Recording Engineer: Reza Farhadi

> Nix Interactive Studios Inc.

> This album, the firstin a series, introduces a unique pedagogical approach to piano playing, offering a fresh perspective on the rich Persian musical tradition. Starting from early beginner pieces like the beloved 'Majnoon Naboodom' and the iconic 'Elahe-ye Naaz,' the album lays a strong foundation by introducing left-hand rhythmic patterns, gradually building in complexity throughout. Each concept is reinforced in subsequent pieces, empowering students to incorporate learned techniques and explore their improvisational skills with the aid of chord symbols. Discover the beauty and intricacies of Persian melodies, embracing a new dimension of musical expression through the piano.
