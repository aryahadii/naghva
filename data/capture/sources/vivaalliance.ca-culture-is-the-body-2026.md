> The Suzuki Method positions the body as the foundation of expressive storytelling—using physicality as a bridge between internal intention and external action.
> Rooted in the philosophy that "Culture is the Body," this method employs disciplined physical exercises to unlock deeper emotional, mental, and expressive capacities. Drawing on elements such as sound, rhythm, movement, and gesture—universal across cultures—Mahdi Saki introduces innovative choreographies and practices that strengthen the mind-body connection in performance.
> Participants will engage in rigorous training that includes stomping drills, slow tenkan movements, and voice-breath exercises, all requiring intense focus, emotional control, and physical precision. These practices support a deeper alignment between inner intention and external expression.
