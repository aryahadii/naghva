> Porya Hatami (b.1981) is an experimental sound artist based in Sanandaj, Iran.

> His compositions explore the balance between electronics and environmental sounds, utilizing processed acoustic and electronic sources and field recordings.

> In 2014, Hatami started working with Berlin-based Uwe Zahn (Arovane), and so far, they released 6 albums together.
