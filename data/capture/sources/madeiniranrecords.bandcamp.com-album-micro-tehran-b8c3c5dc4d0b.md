> Micro Tehran by Unlucky Ninjas

> released July 11, 2023

> And also "micro" reminds of the use of microtonal sounds in this album and is the second album of Unlucky Ninjas after Tehran-Kabul.

> Micro-Tehran means a small Tehran and evokes the state of life and being stuck in oneself and this city.

> Micro-Tehran sounds are a type of conventional sounds in game consoles of the 80’s; Games where you always have to fight and lose and start over.
