> Ehsan Haeri is an Iranian poet, lyricist, educator, and creative director based in Canada. He is a founding member of the acclaimed music band Chaartaar, whose work has played a significant role in contemporary Persian music over the past decade.

> His teaching combines literary insight with practical songwriting, helping students develop poetic perception, strengthen their creative voice, and understand both the artistic and structural dimensions of lyric writing.
