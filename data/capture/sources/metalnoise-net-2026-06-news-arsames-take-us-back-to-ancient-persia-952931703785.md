> For this tribute to Iron Maiden’s legendary song “The Trooper,” Arsames chose a different path.
> Instead of simply covering the original track, we reimagined it through the sound of ancient Persia by incorporating two of the most iconic instruments of Iranian music: the Setar and the Daf.
