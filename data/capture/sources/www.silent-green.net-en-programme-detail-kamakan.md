> Kamakan
> Kamakan
> Sat 01/28/2017
> 07:00 pm Start
> Cupola
> Concert and Talk
> Mehdi Saki and his band Kamakan come from southern Iran and are Arabic-speaking Iranians. Their first album “Kamakan” was released last year by Noufe Music. Their second album, which they will present at this exclusive pre-release concert, highlights even more the Persian, Arabic, and African traditions of their home region.
> Looking forward to rocking the dancefloor at silent green, they sent the following message: “The future of the world is dark, they say. Perhaps, but we are still here, building a bridge with music to give hope to people, who want the world a better place.”
