> I was born in Tehran, Iran. That’s where I also grew up except for a few years in my adolescence when we moved to Northern California.

> When he left the country, I inherited some of his albums, such as Pink Floyd, The Eagles, Supertramp, and other rock bands.

> I studied architecture in Iran, but starting in high school, was active in the underground music movement in Iran. Later, I formed the band KIOSK.

> I decided to never write with censorship committees in mind. I realized I had become unconsciously conservative by censoring myself when I was writing because I wanted a song to get approved.

> That is when I decided to write as if I never would apply for permission. It was liberating. That’s when a new project later turned into the band KIOSK.

> He introduced me to Creedence Clearwater Revival, and most importantly singer/songwriter JJ Cale, who is an idol for me now.

> Decades later, I was thinking about all the people who shaped my musical taste.

> We recorded an album about his amazing journey as a concept album.
