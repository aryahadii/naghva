> “Por Kon” (“Fill up!”) by Golsa, a young jazz-soul singer on vocals and
> Behzad Danesh, Iranian-Canadian jazz musician and guitar player, composer
> and producer, is an electro-jazzy approach to the ancient poem while trying
> to be loyal to the idea of the poet.
