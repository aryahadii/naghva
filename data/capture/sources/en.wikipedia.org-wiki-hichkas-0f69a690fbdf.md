> Soroush Lashkari (Persian: سروش لشکری; born 9 May 1985), known professionally as Hichkas (هیچ‌کس

> He lived in Germany until he was two years old and then lived in the Vanak neighborhood of Tehran.

> Around 2003, he started his music career in the Vanak neighborhood, participating in Persian free-styling with people who also covered some English language songs in rap battles.

> Hichkas currently lives in London after emigrating to Great Britain in the aftermath of the 2009 Iranian presidential election protests. He is married to the British-based Iranian feminist activist Azadeh Akbari.
