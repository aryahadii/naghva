> I never wanted to be a singer, I wanted to be an instrumentalist. But when I was studying with my teacher, there were some passages that you had to learn to sing the part and play at the same time. So, when he would hear me singing, he would say: ”You know, there’s something in your voice, it makes me feel something. You should think about singing.” I always said no. Finally, when I went to college I took a course in singing and that teacher encouraged me too. And after a while people didn’t want to hear me play, they wanted to hear me sing.”

> The best songs I’ve written came by accident. You know, you are just in the studio, experimenting and you just let yourself go and be free and suddenly it’s like falling over something. ”Oh, what’s that?” And you stay with the accident, you make it more refined, you shape it and it becomes something.
