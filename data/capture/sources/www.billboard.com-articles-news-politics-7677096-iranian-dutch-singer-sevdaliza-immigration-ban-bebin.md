> Now unable to travel to the United States, Sevdaliza turned to music to vent her frustrations as well as extend love to others with her new song “Bebin,” which means “to look” in the Persian language Farsi.

> I wrote and composed “Bebin” about four days ago. Driven by something divine. Me and [the producer] Mucky finished “Bebin” within 48 hours.

> It was my first introduction to writing in Farsi, which felt extremely natural.
