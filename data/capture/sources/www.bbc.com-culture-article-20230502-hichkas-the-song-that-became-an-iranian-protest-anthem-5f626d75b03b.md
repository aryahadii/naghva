> In 2010, Hichkas released a wistful, longing anthem called A Good Day Will Come, shortly after millions took to the streets as part of the Green Movement

> Addressing the protests, Hichkas released They've Clenched Their Fists in 2019; on the record's cover was a long list of names of people who had been killed during the crackdown.

> the tension following the protests forced Hichkas's exile to London, and the track became his last to be recorded inside Iran

> Born in Tehran in 1985, Hichkas's early socially critical work gave an insight into life in Iran
