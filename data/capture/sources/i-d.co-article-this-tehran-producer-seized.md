> “Coming to Europe meant I found a lot of new music, a lot of electronic musicians that I didn’t know in Iran. Seeing how they perform and hearing the music through super loud speakers in venues, that changed a lot for me — even the way I look at music. Before that I had only heard music on studio monitors, in cars and on headphones. It’s a very different way of imagining music.”
