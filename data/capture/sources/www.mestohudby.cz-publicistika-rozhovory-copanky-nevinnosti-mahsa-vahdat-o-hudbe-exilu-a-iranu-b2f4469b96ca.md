## braids

> moje matka na pár vteřin nadzvedla můj šátek a ukázala mé copánky strýci

Answer about the childhood incident behind the title (lines114–117). In her recollection she was ten or eleven, visiting her politically imprisoned uncle at Evin; her mother briefly showed him her braids. Read2026-11-13.

## melody

> On jej napsal, já jsem k němu složila melodii

Same answer (line117). She asked her husband, composer/poet Atabak Elyasi, for a text about the memory; he wrote it, she composed the melody, and he arranged it for choir and harp. The 2022 album Braids of Innocence takes its title from the song. Read2026-11-13.
