> Born in 1983 in Tehran, Iran, Marjan Farsad is a New-York based animator, illustrator, singer, and songwriter.

> follow Marjan Farsad on

> Her debut album “Blue Flowers” was released in 2014, and her second album, White Tree, was released in 2020.

> The Center for Human Rights in Iran (CHRI) conducted the following interview with Iranian American musician Marjan Farsad ahead of the release of her new song, “Mahtab” (Moonlight), which was produced by CHRI.

> I don’t usually talk about politics in my work. But their cases compelled me to speak out, and I felt like I really had to do something about their unjust imprisonments, even if only just writing a song.

> It’s about two of my young and talented friends, Keyomars Marzban and Niloufar Bayani , who have been imprisoned in Iran for the past few years.

> My song is about stars that go missing every night. It represents the feeling of recent events in Iran.

> The song is more personal to me than political.
