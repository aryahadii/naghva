> The new album features Cuban Grammy award nominee pianist-composer Ivan Melon Lewis as producer and some of the finest Cuban and Iranian musicians.

> She has been acclaimed as an expatriate trailblazer for her extraordinary vocal technique and repertoire that fuses traditional Persian singing with Jazz, Latin, contemporary and experimental music.

> Determined to perform in public, Tara Tiba moved to Perth in 2012.

> A year later, studying jazz at the Western Australian Academy of Performing Arts, Tara Tiba formed her own band and performed on stage for the first time.
