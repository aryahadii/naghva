> Iranian composer & improviser, her career reflects her unique musical language through the integration of her native musical elements within a contemporary experimental musical framework.

> Golfam Khayam was born and grew up in Iran in a family of artists in Tehran.

> She holds “Master of Music” from the College-Conservatory of Music, University of Cincinnati (USA).

> She continued her studies at the HEMGE (Switzerland) , Interprétation Specialisée Solist and acquired the degree of Master of Composition.
