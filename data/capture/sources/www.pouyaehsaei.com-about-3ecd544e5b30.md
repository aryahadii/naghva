> Pouya Ehsaei (From the Lips to the Moon, Ariwo, Parasang) is a musician, sound designer, producer, curator and promoter from Iran, currently based in London.

> Pouya is the leader of the band Ariwo and the figure behind the electronic and live music club night Parasang .

> He is the founder and co-organiser of the collaborative music, poetry and live-visuals project From the Lips to the Moon .
