> Pouya Ehsaei (From the Lips to the Moon, Ariwo, Parasang) is a musician, sound designer, producer, curator and promoter from Iran, currently based in London.

> Pouya is the leader of the band Ariwo and the figure behind the electronic and live music club night Parasang .

> He is the founder and co-organiser of the collaborative music, poetry and live-visuals project From the Lips to the Moon .

Artist biography, read 2026-11-21.

> Pouya started his musical career in the underground scene of his hometown Tehran.

> With Ariwo he has recorded and produced two studio albums Ariwo & Quasi.

> During the lockdown in 2020 & 2021, he developed technologies that made it possible to play with other musicians, dancers and visual artists in real-time.

> The 12 shows he produced, under the Parasang moniker, were streamed on Twitch and Youtube.

> Pouya and Tara Fatehi (writer, poet and performer) inviete musicians, poets and visual artist to create immersive and intimate live perforamances.
