> Arash Sobhani is a songwriter and guitarist, known for his sharp lyricism and as the founder of Kiosk, a cornerstone of Iranian alternative music.

> Arash Sobhani, as the vocalist, guitarist, lyricist, and songwriter of the Iranian rock band Kiosk, has recorded eleven albums since the band’s formation in Tehran in 2003 and its subsequent relocation to the United States in 2005.
