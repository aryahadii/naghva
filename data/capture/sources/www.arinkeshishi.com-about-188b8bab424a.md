> Arin was born in 1992 to an Armenian family in Tehran-Iran.

> ARIN KESHISHI Bassist, composer and producer based in Amsterdam.

> He graduated from Tehran University of Arts with a Bachelor in Classical Double Bass before moving to the Netherlands to complete a Masters degree in Jazz Bass Guitar at the Conservatory of Amsterdam

> In 2015, Arin began directing his own band under “Arin Keshishi Project”, producing jazz/fusion music in collaboration with International artists and released his debute album "Self-Reflection" in 2023.
