> Dang Show is an Iranian music band formed in 2007 by two brothers: Taha Parsa, and Shaya Shoja.

> Other temporary members include Sepanta Mojtahedzadeh (vocal), Omid Nemati (vocal), Ali Zand Vakili (vocal), Milad Bagheri (vocal), Mahnaz Rad (Vocal), Milad Ghaleh (Guitar Electronic) Khashayar Ravangar (drums), Aidin Tarkian (kamancheh), Kasra Saboktakin (bass), Makan Khoinejad (cello), and so on.

> Dang show started having the first woman vocal artist in their shows and performances, Mahnaz Rad since 2019.
