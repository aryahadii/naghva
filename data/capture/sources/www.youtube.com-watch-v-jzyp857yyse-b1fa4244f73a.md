## Interview, checked against two transcriptions

Cadence video podcast, episode 16, host Siamak Gholizadeh; uploaded
2024-02-09. Koolaghani's answers, heard 2026-11-08 through two speech-to-text
runs (ElevenLabs Scribe and Gemini) that agree on the substance of each
passage below; the wording quoted is Scribe's. The transcriptions are kept
outside the repository. Short quotations only.

> یه کسی مثل علی خان حبیب‌زاده رو من بعد از ابراهیم منصفی شناختم

02:59. Ebrahim Monsefi came first for him as a teenager; the other masters of
Hormozgan's music (Ali Khan Habibzadeh, Ebrahim Shahdousti, Ebrahim Karivani
of Minab, Nosrat) he came to know later, through his work.

> ناصر از بچگی رو ما تاثیر داشت

05:40. Nasser Abdollahi shaped him from childhood, before Abdollahi was
famous.

> ناصر بهترین مجلس‌خون بندر بود

05:40. When Koolaghani was a child, Abdollahi was the best singer at
gatherings in Bandar Abbas, and wrote his own songs.

> باعث می‌شه که من از بندرعباس پاشم بیام اینجا که اینجا باید دیده بشم

11:20. The pull of the centre made him leave Bandar Abbas for Tehran, where he
had to be seen and recorded.

> من توی جزیره هرمز یا قشم یا هنگام بودم همه چیز تموم می‌شد پونزده سال پیش

39:53. About fifteen years before the interview (about 2009) he spent time on
the islands of Hormuz, Qeshm and Hengam, playing with friends around a fire.

> خلاقیت من اونجاها شکل می‌گرفت

39:53. His creativity took shape there; in Tehran he has to squeeze it out.

> تنها کاری که به این شکل تولید کردم یه دونه ترکه

19:15. Of Damahi's songs, only "Divaneh" was made in the easy, accented
"southern" style he criticises; he calls it a very personal song.

> همه چیش در لحظه اتفاق افتاد

22:42. "Divaneh" came together in the moment: the band added the vowel endings
and made some words broader in dialect.

> مهم‌ترین اشتباه زندگیمو گرفتن مجوز بوده

47:22. The biggest mistake of his life was seeking an official permit.

> اون چارچوب سانسور ساختم رو برای خودم

47:22. The permit meant building the frame of censorship for himself; he had
been singing in the mountains and deserts until then.

> ما چهار نفر بودیم. در اول پنج نفر بودیم

51:21. The band was five at first, and four later.

> ما از بین پنج نفر تنها آدمی که نشستیم جلوش و گفت که شما ایده‌تون چیه و شما می‌خواید چی کار کنید، اون آدم بود. احسان رسول‌اف

53:37. Of the five producers the band met, only Ehsan Rasoulof asked what the
band wanted to do; the others wanted to change their names, lyrics and song
lengths. The band chose him although his offer was the lowest, because he did
not interfere in their work.

> ما به جایی رسیده بودیم که آثارمون رد می‌شد

55:18. Even so, the Ministry of Culture began rejecting the band's songs, as
sexual or as social and political.

> سر این داستان‌های تهیه‌کننده اینا با من کار نمی‌کردن

1:09:56. Over the producer, close friends who had written for him stopped
working with him; by the time of the interview they were working together
again.

> دو تا آلبوم ما تولید کردیم سیزده تا قطعه

1:04:49. Damahi made two albums of thirteen tracks each, and three singles,
with only two full music videos.

> کلاً ما مثلاً چهل تا اجرا نرسیدیم. کلاً در هفت سال

1:06:49. In seven years the band gave fewer than forty concerts.

> از 2014 تا 2024. یک دهه

1:07:30. The band would turn ten in the spring of 2024: he dates its start to
2014.

> آلبوم خودم هست که به زودی حالا ضبط‌هاش داره انجام می‌شه

1:09:41. A new Damahi album was recorded and due soon, and he was recording an
album of his own.

> با امیر دارابی

1:09:53. His own album is with Amir Darabi.

> یه اجراهای خصوصی می‌رم

1:15:44. He was making his living from private concerts, in parts of Iran he
would not name.

> یه شکل هم گردشگریه که توش دست دارم در همون جنوب

1:15:58. He also works in tourism in the south.

> یه کارایی بتونم با خانم‌ها بکنم

1:16:56. He would like to work with women musicians, in time and not as a
trend.
