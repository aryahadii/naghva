> Sara Naeini (Saranin) was born in 1981 in Iran.

> As the first child of the talented Iranian traditional music singer Hourvash Khalili and her calligrapher father, she started singing when she was five.

> the limitations eventually forced her to leave Iran for the United States in 2012 in order to pursue her professional singing career.

> Performing in pop and jazz genres, Sara has collaborated with composers such as Reza Tajbakhsh and Reza Rohani, and has had numerous performances in California.

> In 2016, she began collaborating with Oceanic Blue and she is currently touring with them.

> Sara Naeini lives in Los Angeles and additionally works as a professional vocal instructor.
