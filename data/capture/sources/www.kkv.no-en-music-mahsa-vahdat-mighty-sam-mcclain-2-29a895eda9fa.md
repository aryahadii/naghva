> Scent of reunion - Love duets across civilizations

> Mahsa Vahdat, Iran Mighty Sam McClain, USA Sweet scent of Iran-American reunion on a new CD

> Release date: 11.09.2009

> The exchange of ideas became the lyrics of love duets, and the lyrics were given melodies by Mahsa Vahdat, Sigvart Dagsland, Knut Reiersrud and Jarle Bernhoft in the spring of 2009.

> The recordings were made in Kulturkirken Jakob in Oslo (Norway), in Fin Studio in Teheran (Iran) and in Cedarhouse Studio in New Hampshire (USA).
