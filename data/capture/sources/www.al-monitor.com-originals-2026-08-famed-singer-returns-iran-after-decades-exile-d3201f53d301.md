> Mohsen Namjoo, 50, whose work mixes Persian traditional music with more modern styles and had been living in the United States, flew into Tehran late last week, with his arrival welcomed by official media.

> Namjoo responded in a video published by the ISNA news agency on Sunday that while he had made the decision to return "a few months ago", he had kept the move under wraps.

> He hailed the "utmost kindness" he had encountered since his return and responded to criticism by saying "I have come to see my homeland and my family".

> In 2020, a series of anonymous allegations concerning sexual assault were made against Namjoo, though he has denied the accusations and was never charged.

> In 2009, he also sang in solidarity with protesters who took to the streets after the Islamic republic's disputed presidential elections.
