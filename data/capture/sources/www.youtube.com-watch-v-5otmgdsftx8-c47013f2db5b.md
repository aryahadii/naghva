## Interview, checked against two transcriptions

Cadence interview with Jetpack, part two, with Mohammad Shariat and Arian
Shariat; uploaded 2021-04-22. Their answers, heard 2026-11-08 through two
speech-to-text runs (ElevenLabs Scribe and Gemini) that agree on the
substance of each passage below; the wording quoted is Scribe's. The
transcriptions are kept outside the repository. Short quotations only.

> زندگی‌م از راه موزیک می‌گذره

01:56. Mohammad is the one who lives from music, producing and playing for
pop singers.

> آرین که دانشجوئه، مجید که کار دیگه‌ای داره، عرفانم که خودش استودیو داره

01:56. Mohammad: Arian is a student, Majid has another job, and Erfan has his
own studio.

> محمد، مجید، عرفان هم از من سنشون خیلی بیشتره

07:36. Arian: the others are much older than him and play many concerts
already, which is why Jetpack rarely played live.

> ما تصمیم گرفتیم که برنامه ریزی بکنیم واسه اجرا ولی استانداردای خودمونو داریم

09:28. Mohammad: after Arian raised it they decided to plan concerts, on
their own standards.

> مجید ریفشو ساخت بعد محمد رفت سراغ productionش. من ملودی وکالشو ساختم. عرفان درامز آکوستیکشو زد

11:12. Arian: "Off the Road" was a quarter each: Majid's riff, Mohammad's
production, Arian's vocal melody, Erfan's acoustic drums.

> از اولش قرار بر این بود که production رو من انجام بدم

12:38. Mohammad: from the start he was to produce and each member was to play
in his own style, without the others interfering.

> ما بیس که نداریم توی گروه، بیس‌ها تو پروسه کار خود من اجرا میشه

13:39. Mohammad: the band has no bass player; he plays the bass parts.

> به تعداد انگشتان شاید یک دست نمی‌رسن بندهایی که توی ایران

15:09. Mohammad: fewer than five bands in Iran produce everything themselves,
as Jetpack does.
