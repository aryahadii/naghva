> Born in Iran, raised in an English boarding school in India since the age of four, & currently based in Los Angeles, Azam first became internationally recognized for her work with Vas , the best selling world music duo she co-founded in 1996.

> Aside from her ensemble work, Azam has released five acclaimed solo albums, “Portals of Grace” (2002), “Elysium for the Brave” (2006), “From Night to the Edge of Day” (2011) that earned her a prestigious JUNO Award nomination in Canada, her critically acclaimed opus “Phantoms” (2019), and her brand new album “SYNESTHESIA” (2025).
