> All my lyrics are based on my experiences and philosophical outlook on life. You cannot connect with people through art unless you are expressing yourself from a very personal and honest place.

> It’s different for every project I work on. There are so many artists who have inspired me along the way and from so many different cultures. For Phantoms, I tapped into a lot of the music that had influenced me from the 80s & 90s, such as music on the 4AD label as well as some heavier industrial music from Wax Trax Records in Chicago. My main doorway influence was Depeche Mode. I loved their gloomy melodies & synth sounds. It’s the first band that made me fall in love with the idea of utilizing technology as a tool for creating music.
