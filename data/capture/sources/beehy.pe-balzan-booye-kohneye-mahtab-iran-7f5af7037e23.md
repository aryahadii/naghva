> Iran: Bâlzan (بالزَن)

> Bâlzan is an indie electro-rock duo formed by singer Amir Balafshan and Nima Ramezan as songwriter, composer and guitar player.

> The result of collaboration of these two musicians, which started about a year ago, is the recently released debut called “Bâlzan”.

> Amir Balafshan is both a singer and multi-instrumentalist from Rasht. He has worked with an indie-rock band called Makhula from his city. He also has his own solo project called Davvar.
