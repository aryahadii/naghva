# Stereotype

The band’s own Bandcamp lists Code, Foil, Horizon, and The New Normal. Apple ID 1524524030 confirms the early Foil tracks and the two separate Horizon remixes. The official site links Spotify and the now unavailable SoundCloud profile. The similarly named Bandcamp act with a release titled Welcome to My Spaceship is unrelated. OneBeat describes the original trio including Asal; the later German label credits two players, Meshcut on voice and synthesizer and XEEN on electronics and production. The band identifies Shahin Entezami as mixer on Code. The two musicians appear together in the Spotify portrait.
