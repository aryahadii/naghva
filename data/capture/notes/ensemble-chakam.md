# Ensemble Chakam

Concert bills document the move from the original Tehran-trained trio of tar, qanun and tombak to tar, qanun and viola da gamba. The mid-period portrait is the band’s own group photo. Farah Fersi guests for the Budapest recording. Eleanna Pitsikaki appears in the founder’s later roster. The March 2025 record features Christine Zayed. The RFI session includes two live pieces and a third played from CD. France Culture recorded two players. Official WOMEX and audience video documents of the 2024 showcase are attributed to their respective publishers.

Reviewed the performers’ original album pages, Apple Music and available Spotify editions, artist and group video channels, French public radio and independently issued performance films. Bandcamp contains material shared with Atine and Sowal Diabi. No additional artist-identified SoundCloud or Telegram edition was established in the original-language searches.
