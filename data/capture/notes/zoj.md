## Scope and unmatched services

Spotify and Apple Music attribute some digital editions to Gelareh Pour. Album identity is confirmed by the artist’s Bandcamp and corresponding titles, order and performance credits. Apple classifies Return as an EP. Apple’s Australian lookup and US searches did not return Give Water To Birds. Both Spotify and Deezer carry its six tracks. The matching [Deezer edition](https://www.deezer.com/album/991403981) carries barcode 882011968810.

The similarly named singles Haunted and Livin High have no verified link to the duo and do not occur in its catalogue. The Dome Sessions, suggested by Spotify, credits other musicians on all eleven tracks. Individual work by members and the MNMM ensemble are separate records. Searches for the duo on SoundCloud and Telegram yielded no verified additional release.
