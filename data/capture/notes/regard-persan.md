# Regard Persan

Montreal-based trio, founded in 2013. The verified Bandcamp group photograph shows the three performers with their instruments. Four distributor editions and all 39 tracks were reconciled; Spotify supplied three albums, the artist Bandcamp thirteen debut tracks. The original artist upload spells “Turquise” where the digital distributor uses “Turquoise.” The two cuts of “Inception” belong to different sessions. Mexican press documents the planned 2019 disc presentation years before the 2021 digital issue. The ten Ecos del Radif tracks explicitly bill the trio as guests. Native performer and presenter films include the remote winter session and separate Montreal concert takes. The 45-second Invisible trailer and presenter promotional shorts are excluded from the musical inventory.
