**9.2/10.** Sources 1.9/2, prose 1.9/2, recordings and services 2.6/3, images 1/1, gaps and reader experience 1.8/2. Article and catalogue ready. The Isfahan teachers and Norwegian commissions have independent biographical support, and the artist-held rights credit supports the independent release. The article explains how the Rumi Ensemble moved from setting poetry with Salar Aghili to an instrumental repertoire under its leader’s own name. The Persian and English accounts preserve that distinction and the historical 2009 issue. The late film paragraph breaks the otherwise chronological sequence. A thematic section heading would help a reader returning from the 2026 album to the 2009 film.

Checked 73 source URLs associated with the artist, the two articles and related releases. In addition to the commercial catalogue the inventory now includes the Mahoor excerpts, the two BBC Persian films, the older Karvan, Golestan, Parvaz and Bansal releases and documented individual collaborations. The Jazzland album account identifies both the Afghan dedication and Azerbaijani source melody. Contemporary Persian journalism gives the titles «پرواز» and «کاروان شرق» and identifies the full 2006 commission as «ماه در چاه». The 2009 festival programme directly confirms that Anne Hytta shares the documentary music credit. The later short-film programme and director interview distinguish a composition from a santur performance. Two catalogue websites denied a direct HTML download, and the film programme and original label booklet require PDF reading. The documentary and director credits were independently verified from their indexed originals. Unconfirmed track-level distribution on older physical albums remains documented.
