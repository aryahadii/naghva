# Kourosh Babaei

His own 2025 phonogram credits and the independent Canadian digital label Cambiata Arts establish publication eligibility. The artist’s discography, historical listings, album credits, 133 native channel films, the two different Bandcamp collaborations, Spotify and Apple Music were compared. The 2022 album Eastern Memory named on his CV and the eponymous digital single are separate editions. A numbered original release track list has not been found for the older recordings Tiba and Borderless.

Link Music Lab’s Bandcamp edition of Experimental Link 003 contains Steve, Kourosh and Maks. Apple’s Experimental Link EP instead lists Synthesis, Thesis and Antithesis. The releases are documented separately pending identification of matching masters. The 15 track titles on Cambiata’s Lights and Shadows were compared directly. Concert excerpts, alternate-resolution uploads and other artists’ reposts require individual review. The artist describes participating in more than 150 albums, a larger body of work than the named entries on his public CV.
