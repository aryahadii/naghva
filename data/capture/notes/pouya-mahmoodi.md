# Pouya Mahmoodi

Reused the Barad, Shahin Najafi and Habib Meftah credits on Qjwhj2t. The artist’s original label channel has 22 films. Eleven are the eleven tracks from Mehr, including the two Billy Cobham appearances, and four are separate Tehran and Zurich concert performances. The remaining seven are Saffron Moon recordings, including two uploads of “Flame.” The credits explicitly identify Mahmoodi and Derakhshan as the duo. The 2009 Sufi Ecstasy release on the same Apple artist page had no evidence for these musicians. Spotify’s Mehr edition has eleven exact track IDs. The artist’s twelve SoundCloud uploads match the eleven studio titles and durations, including two “Sare Koohe Boland” URLs with the same duration.

The 2024 Karaj Collective edition has six individually streamable pieces. Two credit Sahar Zibaei. Contemporary Iranian’s London programme names all three instrumentalists. Naghmeh Sharifi’s original Bandcamp has guitar and composition credits on “Tashvish” and “Darya” and composition, arrangement and production on “Bizaman.” Her “Bang Bang” credits Seth Duin on guitar. The releases with Behrouz, Erwin Khachikian and Habib Meftah retain their independently identifiable digital editions. The original artist portrait comes from Contemporary Iranian’s individual artist page. I checked the full-size 960 × 640 photograph against the page, which shows Mahmoodi at the microphone with electric guitar; Apple and Spotify supplied the Mehr cover as their profile image. The Barad live performance films are linked to the ensemble as well.

Second pass. Hermes’s bilingual personnel booklet for Rira names him on acoustic and electric guitar but does not allocate the two instruments by track. Ali Azimi’s bilingual “First Name” music film directly names him; all ten artist-uploaded Jens Sevom films name him on guitar, and each has a distinct Apple and Spotify recording. A physical Babylon Bar two-disc catalogue places “Sare Koohe Boland” eighth on disc two. The performer’s own and festival’s archives separately identify the December 2020 Roya Arab concert and Karaj Collective’s film at Gate of Tehran. Nogaam’s printed pp. 421–422 document the electric-guitar performance in the reconstituted Meera at Tehran’s Taraneh Music School in early 2006. The press release and syndicated biography give different birth years (1973 and 1975); neither was adopted. The guitarist names Hamed Derakhshan as co-founder of Faryaad.
