# Maryam Akhondy

Principal releases are credited under the singer’s own name, Banu, Ensemble Barbad and Paaz. Apple supplies separate edition and track credits for *Songs of Persian Women*, *Sarmast*, *Live at WDR* and the four-song *Hafez Ensemble*. The compilation *Many Ways* credits three individual songs. Christian Frentzen’s original album identifies the singers on “Freedom Song.” WDR documents the Schäl Sick Brass Band releases made during Akhondy’s tenure, and ACT’s own album credits confirm the voice on *Tschupun*.

The CultureMusicArt channel mixes her music with concerts by relatives, collaborators and unrelated performers. Its inventory of 79 uploads was checked for named performances; original recording titles and links are preserved. Interviews, publicity and other musicians’ solo demonstrations do not establish a performance by Akhondy. The festival and radio recordings remain distinct from the album editions wherever the source identifies a different setting.

The individual portrait shows Akhondy playing a frame drum in 2008. Cologne’s women’s history project publishes the photograph and credits Bernd G. Schmitz. The German broadcaster, local historians and Akhondy’s independent interviews in Persian and German document her ensemble leadership and explain her choice of repertoire.
