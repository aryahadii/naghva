# Ehsan Matoori

The original Phantasm digital edition identifies Ehsan Matoori as its 2019 phonogram owner. His own release credits name him as composer and Mohsen Namjoo as singer. The Persian Cultural Center of San Diego documented their joint release concert. The portrait is the artist’s Bandcamp profile photograph, showing him seated with his santoor.

The Apple artist lookup returned 41 editions. The album tracks, standalone single editions and their covers were checked on release pages. The two releases on his own Bandcamp profile, the original Spotify records matched so far, and the 2026 Rite of Strings album extend this inventory. The 2018 solo single Dust and the Moon and the 2026 ensemble album of the same name are distinct releases. Some of the 66 SoundCloud tracks use different spellings or earlier titles. The official video channel also carries interviews and instruction alongside original songs and live performances. Service reconciliation and independent review are in progress.
