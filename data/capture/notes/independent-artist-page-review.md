# Reviewing new artist pages

Use Sanam Maroufkhani (Qa84h2k) and Golsa (Qh745pn) as the owner's 9/10 examples. Sanam's account of recording two demos in different languages explains how she started singing in Persian. Golsa's account of the ukulele her friends gave her connects the breakup of a student group with the Instagram covers that introduced her to collaborators. Look for that kind of supported explanation. Match the depth to the musical life and available evidence.

An independent reader reviews the built page in Persian and English. Record the item ID, revision or reviewed file hashes, review date, reviewer, both page paths, points in each row, evidence, remaining gaps and follow-up verdict. Rescore changed dimensions after repairs and check that the built pages include the changes.

| Dimension | Points | What the reviewer checks |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Sources and claims | 2 | Establish Iranian connection and an independently released work. Open the sources supporting each claim. Check verbatim passages, locators, dates, independence of accounts, and evidence for each career period. Count distinct accounts and publishers. A syndicated biography counts as one account. |
| Article prose | 2 | Read each language through, one point per language. Check the lead, clear chronology, plain idiomatic wording, use of names, and parity of facts and uncertainty. Record specific awkward wording and a proposed revision. |
| Recordings and services | 3 | One point for reconciling releases, track lists, features and distinct versions against official catalogues. One for checking each relevant service including the six in the listen policy, artist links and regional services found in research. One for verifying exact recording links and resolving namesakes, duplicates, credits and dates. Record search scope, access failures and unmatched titles by service. |
| Images | 1 | Check the displayed artist photo and release covers, subject and edition, source and credit, and that each image loads whole in both page layouts. |
| Gaps and reader experience | 2 | One point for answering the material questions about how this artist started, made work and changed. One for distinguishing announced and completed events, reconciling conflicts, checking internal links and surfacing relevant relationships in the rendered page. Explain unresolved reader questions. |

Give partial credit with a specific deduction. Report the sum out of ten plus separate catalogue and article readiness. A known omitted work, unassessed catalogue, missing artist photograph, broken page, unsupported central claim or substantial bilingual discrepancy requires repair before counting the artist toward the requested collection. If a search was inaccessible, state what could and could not be verified and whether other evidence covers that gap. A site-wide validation pass supports a review but cannot establish a score.
