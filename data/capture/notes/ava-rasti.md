# Ava Rasti

Rasti was the founding bassist in The Finches, as she explains directly in her interview. Flaming Pines dates that role to 2015–20; the 2021 Shame EP after her departure is not attributed to her. The University of Lille documents her bass and electronics in a specific Kamakan trio. The identity was reconciled to the original person record. All sixteen tracks on her own linked SoundCloud handle avaarasti were reconciled, alongside her original Bandcamp, FatCat and Flaming Pines, Apple ID 1485164701, and Spotify 4s2iBVE1aD9bbMN3M0ljKY. A separate avarasti SoundCloud account has zero tracks. TCS Route and Apranik Eight Night were checked on the labels; Lucid Dream on For LA, Vol. 2 was checked on the compilation’s track page. Ginestra has Fabrica and artwork/engineering credits on the original Flaming Pines physical edition. The individual Bandcamp release dates The River to December 2024 despite a later FatCat blog calling it a 2025 release. Interviews and sources from different periods place Rasti first in Tehran and subsequently in Berlin. SoundCloud and Bandcamp are alternate issues for early titles, rather than evidence of new takes. Double Life and Presence differ. The three separate festival-documented films retain distinct composer credits; only the 1001 Frames credit list identifies individual performers. Her photograph is the artist portrait served by her own Spotify profile.
