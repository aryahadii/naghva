# Pouya Pour-Amin

The Iranian electronic composer and double bassist uses the spelling Pouya Pour-Amin on his website and Pannulus on SoundCloud. The SoundCloud portrait shows him playing his instrument. His interview traces his interest in low frequencies and the friendships he formed in Tehran’s Camerata orchestra. [Artist](https://pouyapouramin.no/about) · [Safemuse](https://safemuse.org/2025/06/20/pouya-pour-amin-an-artists-journey-from-fear-to-freedom/) · [Pannulus](https://soundcloud.com/pannulus).

- The label’s seven-part *Prison Episodes* has track-level Apple, Spotify and Bandcamp links. The original label credits Pour-Amin for composition and electric double bass and Lawrence English for mastering. The composer also credits himself for recording and mixing. Its 2026 staged adaptation uses the original album, with new theatre and choreography. [Label](https://flamingpines.bandcamp.com/album/prison-episodes) · [Production](https://www.baerumkulturhus.no/en/archive/2026-host/prison-episodes/).
- Nine personal SoundCloud uploads were checked. “Exterior Wash” also appeared on the Flaming Pines compilation *Absence*. Further releases include the independent Zabte Sote track, Baaroot remix, *To Night*, his original first album and his bass work on Siavash Amini’s *TAR* and Soheil Peyghambari’s *Gamaaneh*. No track-specific bass credits were located for the latter. His 2019 interview calls his first album *Lalaei/Lullaby*, Safemuse gives *Lullaby and Down the Quod*, and the CV uses a slash and spells the second title *Down the Quad*. [Interview](https://www.fluid-radio.co.uk/2019/07/interview-with-pouya-pour-amin/) · [Musician’s CV](https://pouyapouramin.no/Future/2026-8-21-2cc.pdf).
- The musician’s own project pages document published film or performance excerpts, work with Barbad Golshiri, and the score for Maryam Ashrafi’s *Eternal Sentinel*. The CV dates composition of that film to 2021 and the musician’s portfolio lists the film in 2022. Historical performance programmes alone do not establish that a separate recording circulated. *Oslo Chārgāh* is a documented live project; the artist announced a fall 2026 album, but a release could not be located. [Artist’s portfolio](https://pouyapouramin.no/) · [CV](https://pouyapouramin.no/Future/2026-8-21-2cc.pdf).
- Earlier work with Migrain Sq. is catalogued under the group. Pour-Amin’s CV documents the Spanish issue of *Polish Chair* under Migrain Blvd., and his recording and mixing credits. Unrelated Apple results credited to the Iranian pop singer Pouya Pourjalil were excluded.
- The composer’s old video channel has two uploads. One documents his Camerata orchestral work *A Persian Perfect Circle* at the cathedral in Aix-en-Provence and the other is the later Norwegian *The Secret Life of a Shameless Demon*. Kayvan Mirhadi conducts the performance. The *Gamaaneh* disc is also available under its English title *Assumption*. [Concert uploader](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B0UnXRH6c1k) · [Album edition](https://music.apple.com/us/album/assumption/916829001).
- Dast Cast’s native creator feed contains 13 programmes. Twelve list music and recording or editing in differing combinations, and several credit him in the spoken radio plays. The Azerbaijan-language episode explicitly identifies external repertoire by other artists. The native credits support his music and production roles on the series without establishing that the host composed the recordings it excerpts. The Iranian producer of the short *Neshaneha* separately credits Pour-Amin with sound recording and sound design. [Producer feed](https://anchor.fm/s/211d23a8/podcast/rss) · [Film producer](https://defcapp.ir/Movie/2412/).
