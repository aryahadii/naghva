# Araz Salek

Artist’s 2008 physical CD, 2010 David Sait compilation, Musicworks issue 138 CD, two distinct 2025 digital issues and all six independently playable album tracks are inventoried. Issue 138’s original printed contents lists the duo as track 10; the magazine’s later website feature labels its CD 139 incorrectly. The 2008 Edmonton concert, the 2009 Toronto concert excerpts and the original solo studio CD remain separate works. No verified streaming edition or first-edition artwork for the original CD was located. The solo portrait shows the artist playing tar onstage, from the artist’s own native Bandcamp image. Artist and producer Bandcamp liner notes were distinguished from the distributor’s generic composer/performer credits. In particular the track Ghazal names Salek for its avaz structure, and does not print a tar performance. Spotify and Apple exact tracks were joined by track order and title. The debut album name is displayed in its first-party English spelling.
