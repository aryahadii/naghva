# Dastgâmachine

Toronto trio with Iranian electric-setar performer Mahmood Schricker, Caleb Klager and Jason Rule, documented individually by its Toronto presenter, Wavelength. The artist-run Link Music Lab released the three issues on its Bandcamp. I checked the group against existing people, acts, articles and capture notes before binding the 13 previously documented recordings to the ensemble. The four 2025 EP tracks derive from a residency, the four 2026 EP tracks include earlier audio and three remixes, and Araq o Anar was recorded separately at Wavelength. Its members receive separate credits on the existing recordings. The full Tapestry and Revival films each name the group.

The label’s native issue page dates Tapestry to January 30, 2025. The concert presenter and a subsequent label biography say February. Both articles use the issue date. The label describes the 2023 show as the first performance before the group had a name. The group’s guest playlist for Wavelength includes individual radio commissions, including the 2023 Days of Steam episode now attached to Mahmood. It is a selection of works by others. I found no separate streaming profile confidently assigned to the group on Apple Music or Spotify. One label-published concert poster has only two musicians. The page uses a still from the publisher’s Tapestry performance film, visually checked against the Revival film and the label’s event imagery.
