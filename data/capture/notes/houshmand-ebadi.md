# Houshmand Ebadi

Three Spotify artist identifiers reflect inconsistent distributor splits, including the original spelling Hushmand. Apple Music and Spotify provide all Fifer tracks, Tash session credits and track-specific appearances on Khayyam Recital, Hozzan, Hazaran, Singing in Shadow, Luminous Sunshine, Peripheries of Nahavand and the Dastgahs of Persian Music series. The solo Fifer album is listed in 2019 by Labyrinth and both digital services. A Persian article instead dates the earlier physical issue to Tir 1397. Album-wide repeating guest metadata for Leader of the Love Caravan can include positions with separate instrumental solos. The archive credits the explicit ney solo, the album ensemble and identified ensemble cuts. Raha's upload is tied to the solo. On Peripheries of Nahavand, Apple Music and the band’s original Bandcamp credits place his ney on nos. 4–6. The 2024 videos came from performing percussionist Behnam Samani and Hafdang’s original channel supplies the separate Naghme Roshan and Homayoun recordings. No independent dedicated SoundCloud or Telegram profile was established. The two later member and guest relationships are distinguished per release and concert.
