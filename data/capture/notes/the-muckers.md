# The Muckers

The 2011 Tehran group and later New York lineup share the guitarist billed in 2012 as Amir Mohsseni and later as Emir Mohseni. The Atlanta Irish folk-rock band uses a separate Bandcamp account. The London and other namesakes were excluded. Sources include the original archived Iranian.com features, an interview with the musicians, the band’s Bandcamp, nine distinct native Apple editions, the fifteen-item original SoundCloud, label-distributed Spotify album, the two filmed concert sessions and the independent radio recording. No YouTube player resolved for the Iranian.com embeds of Attitude, Shoot Me Again or Tush; all three have specific contemporary host pages and original titles. The 2014 BBC appearance announced SXSW but does not confirm completed travel. No translated stage name or unnamed live performer was added.
