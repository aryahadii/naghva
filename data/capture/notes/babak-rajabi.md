# Babak Rajabi

Research and catalogue audit on 2026-11-21. The Isfahan-born singer identifies as an independent artist in his Persian interview with Kayhan London. He released Tanz o Tarab himself on Bandcamp. His 2026 statement about returning to Iran supersedes the Strasbourg location in older platform profiles.

The first Telegram reconciliation added 55 identified native recordings and exact links for four matching uploads already listed elsewhere. Thirteen of the native posts form a series of short comic songs, each in a different Iranian mode. Five posts document distinct performances or excerpts of Nakoone in Tehran and Isfahan. The channel also preserves a private rehearsal for a Paris concert, children's music and home recordings. Many other posts are announcements, lyrics, dialogue, interviews, forwarded material, unnamed clips or links to recordings listed elsewhere. The public archive alone does not identify the content and version of every video.

Four further Telegram links include an original children’s song, the duet with Sanaz Hamidi, an improvised Cologne performance of Pedarbozorg and the Georges Brassens cover in Sète. The artist credits Adamak as publisher of The Land of Song and identifies two songs from the children’s album حیوانات برای کودکان (complete track list unavailable).
