# Independent review of Farahnaz Hatam

The first local render available for inspection contained three panels and ten decoded images in each language. It preserved an individual portrait and distinguished her instrument built from vocal samples from her collaborator’s drumming. The 2018 Klangteppich presentation had been described in the article. The label issued the performance with Hani Mojtahedy as two recordings, each with an individual catalogue address. The author subsequently supplied additional individually documented work credits and the two missing joint recordings. Initial score **8.3/10** (sources 1.8, article 1.7, catalogue 2.1, imagery 0.9, reader experience 1.8).
