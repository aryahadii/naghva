# Rouzbeh Esfandarmaz

The musician uses both Rouzbeh Esfandarmaz and Rouzbeh Esfand. The prior Habib Meftah co-performer alias now resolves to the same person, preserving its public ID. The individual Spotify portrait corresponds to his performing image in his biography and Esfand’s label. The full Apple and Spotify solo catalogues, eleven artist SoundCloud uploads, two original YouTube uploads, twenty-seven embeds from the artist’s own discography, plus the concert and media pages were checked. The two Calling of the Land editions have different programme divisions and the released remaster of A Lament for the Living is distinguished. Sessions with Aftab Darvishi and the 2019 Shiraz live electronics performance are documented in his gallery. The original group gallery includes six films, including Robbert van Hulzen’s 2012 Darbast concert. Naaz’s Bark and Mehdi Saki’s Kamakan appear in his discography without identification of the session or track. Radio Farda names him in Kamakan’s live lineup. The original Emruz and Körber programmes specifically establish the Shibaali collaboration with Habib Meftah. His participation in Meftah’s 2020 studio album with the same title is unconfirmed. The Iranian singer’s source confirms clarinet on Khaneh Koo.
