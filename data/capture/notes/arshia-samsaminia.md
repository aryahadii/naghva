# Arshia Samsaminia

The artist’s SoundCloud inventory returned 24 actual tracks. The YouTube channel returned 31 uploads, including 30 music videos and one concert trailer. Direct studio recordings, commissioned works and festival films have attributed roles. The composer performs in Gravity and in the four-player prepared-piano work in Tbilisi. He made the metro recordings and sampled them for the audiovisual piece. The remaining named orchestral and chamber performances generally place him in the composer role, including Shifā, with Yalda Zamani conducting Collegium Novum Zürich. Some listeners see only the three performer-indexed panels in the current server’s presentation. The source records distinguish original composition, performance and previously recorded material used with permission.

Noise à Noise credits the FREQ.EXP excerpt in two sets. The artist’s historical three-track Bandcamp release Confessions Vol.1 lists 4OUR, SoundScapeS and Echopraxia in the indexed original edition; the live URL redirects to a one-track version named 4our. No current listen URL is assigned to the lost edition. An independent digital copy of the quartet track remains. The 2016 recording predates the identified 2017 Eufonija festival accordion performance. The older Bandcamp credits identify Mandana MelancholicDream for the graphic design. The historical cover itself could not be retrieved.

Apple and Spotify preserve In Eternity. Additional publisher records cover Access Contemporary Music's Micro-Moments IX and Ensemble Musikfabrik’s 2025 Micro-Moments IV single. Silo portem’s Samsaminia is its own 2020 album, edited from four improvisations by the two British musicians using the Iranian composer’s existing recordings; these production notes have been recorded at album level. The SoundCloud portrait was visually compared with the label’s dated portrait. The former personal web domain now serves a parked page.
