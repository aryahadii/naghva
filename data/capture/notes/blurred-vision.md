# Blurred Vision

The ensemble’s archived biography, original label, members’ contemporary interview, drummer’s credits and founder’s CD shop establish the Toronto and London group. Judy Totton photographed the original three-piece for the interview. The shop dates its formal founding to 2010 and closure to 2023. Hamid Naficy dates the brothers’ earlier group to 2007. The article describes the sequence leading up to the February 2010 protest video. [Band archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20181009172154/https://blurredvisionmusic.com/about/) · [Band interview and portrait](https://www.eonmusic.co.uk/blurred-vision-terry-brown-ramblin-man-fair-eonmusic-interview-july-2016.html) · [Founder’s account](https://sepposley.myshopify.com/collections/blurred-vision) · [Naficy’s study](https://cinema.iranicaonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Hamid-Naficy-02.pdf).

- The label’s 11-song *Organized Insanity*, the nine-song *Redemption* and 15-track *Manhattan* were checked against the group’s physical editions and each track on both Apple Music and Spotify. Four *Manhattan* tracks are new mixes of debut-album recordings and have separate identities. The single editions of “All I Wanted” and “Magdalena” retain their release dates. [Cherry Red](https://www.cherryred.co.uk/organized-insanity) · [Band’s shop](https://sepposley.myshopify.com/collections/blurred-vision) · [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/6Mjvjq51mGSCNwTpSWsD0H) · [Soundtrack editions](https://decibelreport.com/news/blurred-vision-release-soundtrack-to-manhattan-undying-film).
- The 2010 Pink Floyd adaptation, the 2017 “Democracy” single and the 2020 lockdown performance of “What Have I Become” extend beyond these albums. The latter was filmed with the Powerhouse Fellowship Gospel Choir. The band also backed the War Child concert. Subsequent live issues credited to Sepp Osley use the billing of each recording. [Original performance](https://youtu.be/INEj0RKQJ2Q) · [2017 single](https://realrockandroll.wordpress.com/2017/03/23/track-of-the-day-democracy-blurred-vision/) · [Drummer’s credits](https://jake-bradfordsharp-wzb4.squarespace.com/artists).
- The Apple profile also lists *Tales from the Red Hall* and “Valtea” from 2026. Neither is in the founder’s catalogue or on the independently verified Spotify profile. The former band’s involvement in either remains unverified. Full independent track listings on Amazon Music, YouTube Music, Tidal and Deezer have not been confirmed.

The 2012 *Democracy* EP was issued by Blurred Vision Music. The contemporary review identifies five of six tracks. “Famous Las Words” retains that source’s spelling pending a physical sleeve. The group’s separate five-track, self-titled Troubadour CD was sold at the London shows. The publicist confirms its format but does not name the individual tracks. The Toronto mobile-stage performance of “No More War” is a separate 2014 recording.
