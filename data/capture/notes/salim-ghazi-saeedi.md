# Salim Ghazi Saeedi

The composer’s discography distinguishes four solo albums from the first three albums he made with Arashk. His own production and phonogram credits appear on the original album notes. On *Yell* he played rhythm guitar and engineered the recording. The band’s original booklet identifies five guitar solos and his bass solo on “Mourning Scents”. All nine tracks are indexed with the album’s recording engineer credit. The collaborative single also survives on the two musicians’ joint Bandcamp page. [Original Yell booklet](https://www.arashkband.com/album-Yell.htm) · [Joint release](https://negarboubanandsalimghazisaeedi.bandcamp.com/track/when-there-is-more-beauty-in-the-contrary) His 2011 collaboration with Negar Bouban credits both composers and names the oud, electric guitar and keyboard players.

The original track lists were checked against 45 solo tracks in the US Apple catalogue and four complete Spotify albums, plus three Arashk albums on Apple. The seven SoundCloud sets embedded by the composer contain 65 uploads. Several are marked as demos and were retained separately from the studio versions. The author’s video index also lists a revisited Arashk composition, *Asiyeh*, the collaboration with Bouban, a promotional trailer and a review. Azadeh Shamee’s 2012 portrait comes from the composer’s own credited, CC BY-SA 3.0 gallery.
