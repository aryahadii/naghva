# Parsinava

The May 2024 Madrid presenter programme, original musician concert films and 2025 studio reel preserve distinct performances. The six named session personnel are identified in the original film description. The band confirmed the recording of an album at Camaleón Studio but had not supplied a public album title, release date or official distributed edition. Bidad from Café Berlín and El Intruso are separate. Searches of Bandcamp, SoundCloud, Apple Music and Spotify did not substantiate a separate artist profile.
