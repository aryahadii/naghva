# Shiraz Ensemble

Founders Sepehr Pirasteh and Sina Homaee are from Shiraz; they formed this independent ensemble in Philadelphia in 2023. The official site names its member musicians, lists its performances and links original concert publications by the composer, programmer and broadcaster. The concert image depicts the founding duo. Direct recordings include both separately dated Medukha collaborations and the single-composer June 2026 work at The Perch. The band's event listing and original UniLu film say August 17; its separate Film page says August 16, so the article uses August only. A recent event listing mistakenly dates a concert filmed and published in November 2025 to 2026. Other concerts on the artists' event lists have no identifiable full recording.

The five named performers on WPPM’s original programme and PhillyCAM film distinguish this ensemble from similarly named releases indexed by music platforms. The official Tehran-born guest and her March 2024 performance are linked across their pages. Native YouTube, the founders' sites, PhillyCAM, Spotify, Apple Music, Bandcamp, SoundCloud and Telegram were checked. No studio albums or additional issues belonging to this particular band have been authenticated.
