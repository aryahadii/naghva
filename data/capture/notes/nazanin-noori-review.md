# Nazanin Noori independent review

Build 70 rendered 42 panels in each language. All 37 images decoded in each viewport at 1280 px English and 390 px Persian. Verified the artist’s own works directory, label master, Refuge programme, Deutschlandfunk, exhibition and theatre sources. The two texts convey the artist’s use of continuous sound and the architecture of the protest exhibition.

First pass 8.6/10 (sources 1.9, prose 1.9, catalogue 2.2, images 0.9, gaps 1.7). Missing Mutant Radio’s November 2022 recorded guest set, the Tehran Contemporary Sounds trio with Azin Zahedi and Shabnam Parvaresh, and the Berliner Ensemble’s live Phaidras Liebe score. The artist’s interview credits Samin Ghorbani on the HOLLOW SONG SUSPENDED installation. Author has received the original sources and is reconciling these and the other radio archives.

Revised score 9.2/10. Sources 1.9, prose 1.9, catalogue 2.7, images 0.9, gaps 1.8. Both versions now render 56 panels, 39 decoded images and intact anchors. Mutant and eight other radio commissions are present, along with Doom Jazz, the Phaidras Liebe theatrical credit, Samin Ghorbani’s performance, the distinct versions of HAAL and the two Rupert works. Pomp’s original player is unavailable. The exhibition and theatre stills remain traceable to the original presenters.
