# Amen Feizabadi

The artist’s Vimeo account yielded 16 public videos. Three short films with identical credits and different lengths document Collective Op. 135; the ensemble also has its own published film. Four clips document Erzählungen aus der Lagune or the earlier short Lagune. The festival and theatre projects, alternate excerpts, exhibition, TV profile and trailers were distinguished by original credits. He plays live electronics in the 2018 Lagune and Collective Op. 135, in addition to composing them. Korhan Erel is the live electronic performer for the Banat project. The Iran Flute House preserves Koushyar Shahroudi's performance of the commissioned piece in five scenes. Two film scores are verified through the German Film Institute and the German Short Film Association.

The current Apple and Spotify artist pages identify a featured performance with Ankido Darash. The separate nineteen-movement live recording of Deep Siesta bills its composer separately from the voices, ensemble and conductor on the original Wergo release. Feizabadi and Maryam Takhtkeshian handled the production of the projected video. Direct album links in both articles lead to the release under conductor Yalda Zamani. The official portrait on Spotify shows the artist standing at the window with his papers. The Berlin TCS 2024 collaboration with Cymin Samawatie and Dilek Mayatürk was publicly programmed. No separately issued recording of that event was established.
