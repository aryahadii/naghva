# Sara Bigdeli Shamloo / SarrSew

The singer grew up in Tehran and performs solo as SarrSew, in Migrain Sq., 9T Antiope and Taraamoon. Her own Persian spelling and alias are preserved. Compared the 16 publicly listed tracks in the native SoundCloud user collection with the five uploads on her official Audius profile, the original 2013 stage and home recordings, her Apple and Spotify solo releases, two feature credits on Ali Sorena’s 2025 album, the Apranik and Zabte Sote compilation masters and the broadcasters’ actual programmes. The official videos of both guest songs are linked. Jaryan and Deep House Tehran issued her own mixes. BBC World Service credits her as curator of its separate December 2023 episode. Both articles link to the programme. Discussion and interview podcasts remain article evidence.

Confirmed named vocals and lyrics on original label editions of 9T Antiope’s Syzygys, Isthmus, Harmistice, Grimace and Horror Vacui, and the full original Low-Zi credits on Bādbān. The early co-credited stage opening with Nima Aghiani and Unperceived/Eilean’s named composer credits on the 2015 and 2017 albums also improve Aghiani’s page. Forecast’s festival documented a stage project and its separate proposed components. No independent commercial issue of an album called Loss in the Living Room was confirmed. The original Spotify image is a stage portrait of the performer with a microphone.
