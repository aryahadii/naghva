# Independent review of Kiyarash Davoudi

I retrieved 253 artist-channel posts across 392 message IDs, including 94 items with attached media or linked recordings. The initial catalogue missed original studio and concert performances, fragments posted by the artist, and the distinct 1395 Abu Ata and Bayat-e Tork performances. The revised edition retains the 27 publicly available artist SoundCloud tracks and reconciles three album sequences with the Apple Music, Iranian distributor and YouTube editions. It preserves two separately billed editions of Yademan 95. The artist's statements ground both the study chronology and how a phrase heard in a Lotfi performance became his own refrain.
