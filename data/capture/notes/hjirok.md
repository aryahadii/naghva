# HJirok

HJirok is both Mojtahedy’s fictional water spirit and her collaboration with producer Andi Toma. ACUD traces the partnership to the group's one-off 2021 Berlin presentation, and HKW bills those October performances as Forbidden Echoes. The Spore exhibition identifies The Mother of O and The Earth as Mojtahedy’s audiovisual works. Refuge Worldwide bills its 2024 broadcast as Hani Mojtahedy (HJirok); only the singer and credited project are attached. Its own programme data identifies the matching SoundCloud original. The official project portrait shows Mojtahedy in character and attributes the photograph as such.

The 2024 album includes eight tracks. Earlier Maly Men and Jin Bo Chie editions duplicate the same performances and retain their links and earliest verified issue dates. The label's 2025 Passaporte Bandcamp single precedes its May anthology upload on YouTube. The German critics’ own annual award, original label, presenter and artist interview substantiate the article. Meselek's authorized premiere on Inverted Audio is available as separate YouTube and SoundCloud links to the existing work. The project and named collaborators were searched across all six services; the project-level artist site links Bandcamp, Spotify and Apple Music. Its original films and producer/singer roles are corroborated by the issuing label.
