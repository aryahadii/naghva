# Nazanin Noori

The label enmossed issued the first album on cassette and digitally. Both sides have their own full-length Bandcamp players. The Chaperone flexi uses a vocal sample. Traumgarten’s 21-track Virgin Forest credits Noori on track 17. The album and her cut appear separately. Spotify searches by her full name, album, Chaperone and track returned no attributable release. Apple’s native artist and song search also produced no identifiable release. No Telegram artist-owned recording or account could be verified. Her official works list says the noise piece for Virgin Forest was available on Bandcamp only.

SoundCloud yielded the four attributable Refuge Worldwide station uploads, Andrea Belfi’s original 20-minute MacMillan rework and the original Mutant Radio recording embedded in CTM’s programme. Refuge Worldwide lists ten broadcasts. Two are the work of guests Başak Günak and Gisou Golshani. Noori’s eight have dedicated entries. The earlier station pages use their own original Mixcloud players. The work-specific Pomp playlist https://soundcloud.com/nznnsmmnr/sets/pomp now returns a generic shell without playlist data, and searches of Retreat Radio and Mixcloud did not establish individual tapes or episode titles. Pomp is recorded at series level. The artist lists separately dated programmes for other stations and brands.

YouTube holds the full Wired performance (existing Qzmeyzw) and Migros Museum’s film pairing Tanasgol Sabbagh’s text with Noori’s score. Gorki’s official Todesfuge trailer and production documentary establish her staging, while Andrea Belfi composed the music. Print-only texts, silent (NOT I), exhibitions with no individual recorded work, event listings without a recording, and unsourced takes have not been treated as album tracks. Her programme specifies the public Berlin HAAL live set and newly reworked radio issue as separate versions. Deutschlandfunk hosts the complete latter version in its own archive. The archive of original project pages records theatre and installation scores and identifies composer-only work separately from performance. Berliner Ensemble’s signed original programme credits Robert Borgmann and Noori live as 123 CEREMONY. Refuge Worldwide’s 2021 Laura Falafel portrait was inspected visually.
