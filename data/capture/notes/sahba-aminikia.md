# Sahba Aminikia

Qhqb38j is the same composer credited on the existing recordings of *Sound, Only Sound Remains* and the expanded *Salam Aleykom*. This is his first artist page. His independent studio catalogue spans Hermes Records and Mahoor, plus ensemble- and artist-issued performances. Creative credits distinguish composition, arrangement and his documented piano performances. The original portrait is by Ryan Lash, published by the artist with TED’s credit.

## Catalogue

99 credits reconciled below. Apple Music’s composer profile lists the eight *Being* tracks, the six-track *Miniatures*, *Song of Freedom* and selected compilation tracks. Spotify’s corresponding native profile also led to the Chicago Youth Symphony live edition. The three *No Era* movements have a second Spotify edition as indexed by the composer. *Bade Saba* with film and Areon Flutes’ subsequently released studio movements have separate entries. *Circadian Stories* supplies three named credits on the quartet’s Bandcamp and Spotify. Mahoor’s follow-up *بامداد بزم* and its five tracks have written original-release evidence. An authorized stream has yet to be located.

The artist’s home page, portfolio and [public works index](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/e/2PACX-1vSTs515hBUWUXxsscDEW_7qzdZ8_eOFZOvk3BC-OF4oDkG_rvMc3SEKS78VahkVTg/pubhtml?gid=307255595&single=true&widget=true&headers=false) are the inventory of compositions. Score-only and programme-only works, including *Four Seasons* in its full multimedia form, are recorded here as leads rather than represented as commercially or digitally issued recordings. The [composer’s official YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCy0cOY99d-be4q_Wh_h7M3A) and original ensembles’ videos supply independent sound and film editions. The Switchboard festival premiere and the composer’s edit of *Tar o Pood* share the same panel, as do the three extracts from the same *Tehrangeles* concert. A few films explicitly preserve performance excerpts. Discussions, trailers with unidentified music, loose TV interviews, fan reuploads and the original film used as imagery for *Bade Saba* are excluded. An embedded recording of Alizadeh’s *Torkaman* is a different work from Aminikia’s arrangement, which is documented in his portfolio. The *Boodan* page names the Mahoor collaboration, and the record uses *Being* only as its authenticated English title.

SoundCloud’s [official account](https://soundcloud.com/sahbakia/tracks) lists the expanded *Salam Aleykom* and an interview. Catalogue links include the former. Telegram search has not located an authorized artist or label collection. [Amazon’s licensed digital issue](https://music.amazon.co.jp/albums/B0D5GJCB51) provides an additional index for *Miniatures*. Amazon and Audiomack sit outside the archive’s listening-platform registry, and the archive pairs their editions with the allowed native links. The album date uses the 2012 edition as confirmed by the composer’s 2012 Radio Tehran interview. The Topic uploads reproduce licensed 2024 Hermes tracks. For *Placeless*, credit is limited to the six track names Aminikia gave on his project page, the partial-credit album and the confirmed video.
