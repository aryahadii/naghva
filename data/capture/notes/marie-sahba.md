# Marie Sahba

The Iranian connection is documented on Sahba’s official biography and in her interviews about her father’s upbringing in Urmia. The original 2019 Sway premiere states that Sahba Records is her label. The six-track Out of Control EP carries the matching rights notice.

## Catalogue scope

Twenty-nine already-issued Apple Music editions were compared to the artist's 1463066404 and 1193672017 accounts and the co-billed Blakhol releases. The second account is the Mert Harmankaya remix, also included in a 2026 DJ mix. Each of the twenty-nine has a matched Spotify issue or exact guest track. Oh God was listed for 25 November and remains a future release as of 21 November 2026. The 2019 Take Me Back is dated by release, distinct from the 2018 recording copyright. The additional three first-party Bandcamp issues are the Azeri Baby EP, the deluxe album and the Christmas single. They supply exact track links and resolve alternate titles. One Last Time lasts 3:57 as a single and 6:28 in its album version, both on the deluxe album under the same abbreviated streaming title. The groupings were checked against Bandcamp's explicit album-version designation.

All 60 tracks returned from her linked SoundCloud account and 43 uploads returned from her official YouTube videos tab were reconciled. These include three DJ mixes, five Oslo Sessions, the Istanbul acoustic versions, karaoke recordings, live diaries, a championship film and her lyric and vocal videos. Where official SoundCloud uploads duplicate track titles, the original artist asset links remain on their corresponding song. Edition-level duplicates of the same studio master on EP, single and deluxe releases retain their exact recording links. Two instrumental editions with the same date and title contain twelve and thirteen tracks. The thirteenth is Milyard Il (Instrumental), captured with its exact sources. After looking up a private SoundCloud premiere link for Sway, the current public artist catalogue was used. No separate artist-run Telegram music catalogue was verified. No published Persian spelling of the artist’s name could be corroborated from primary sources, so it is left in its attested Latin spelling.

The photographed person on the official biography was visually checked; the page credits Emre Köktaş. Cover images and video thumbnails come from the actual releases and uploads. The artist quotes establish how she developed material with Martin Vinje, why the Galata Bridge features in the title song, and why she used a heartbeat in Orumiyeh. The artist’s own distributor supplies February 16 for the debut and the retrospective interview agrees; the management biography and pre-launch promotion sometimes cite February 9.
