# Sepp Osley

The founder’s store and drummer Jake Bradford Sharp both describe the change from Blurred Vision to his solo career in 2023. RTÉ interviewed him about growing up between Canada and the US after leaving Iran, his first experience of hearing live rock, and “Sing”. His Spotify profile photograph shows him playing electric guitar. [RTÉ interview](https://www.rte.ie/entertainment/2024/0330/1438599-behind-the-music-sepp-osley/) · [Founder’s account](https://sepposley.myshopify.com/collections/blurred-vision) · [Drummer’s credits](https://jake-bradfordsharp-wzb4.squarespace.com/artists).

- The four-track *Love and Revolution* EP, digital *Dear John (Collaborations)* album and separate club/rock, studio/live and guest editions were compared on Apple Music and Spotify, with individual featured artists recorded. The personal channel retains the full concert film and separately published performances. The digital services give the 2020 and 2025 performances of “Dear John” different guests. [Apple artist](https://music.apple.com/us/artist/sepp-osley/1695793765) · [Spotify artist](https://open.spotify.com/artist/23oj7xVFF3zIyNgAcqDJaU) · [Artist’s channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCx5-eA9beLPBnk9eNIGOxBw).
- The direct download and CD contain seventeen tracks. The shop explicitly identifies thirteen on the commercial digital edition. The four extras are “Across the Universe” by Graham Gouldman, “Norwegian Wood” by Nick Van Eede, “I’m Only Sleeping” by John Illsley and “Power to the People” by Maxi Jazz. Their solo performance credits are retained here because the direct edition does not assign Osley a performance on those tracks. [Artist’s complete running order](https://sepposley.myshopify.com/products/dear-john-collaborations-digital-download).
- Blurred Vision’s original band recordings retain the band credit. The later solo EP contains a London performance of their Pink Floyd adaptation, and the original studio version has a separate solo digital issue. A complete historical listing from every international streaming storefront remains unverified. [EP credits](https://sepposley.myshopify.com/products/love-revolution-cd).

Osley’s 2003 account describes learning on a stringless guitar in his mid-teens and later taking over from borrowed singers. In 2024 he recalled starting to write and perform at thirteen. The page keeps the learning sequence without assigning it an age. Presenter Bob Harris dates the concert series to 2019 and documents the separate 2021 London and remote broadcast, later heard in the 2025 album. [2003 interview](https://iranian.com/Music/BlurredVision/) · [2021 presenter programme](https://bob-harris-2020.squarespace.com/bobs-events/3rd-annual-dear-john-concert-for-war-child-hosted-by-bob-harris).

Open Eyes Records released the charity single featuring Blurred Vision, Marriott and Frampton on 5 October 2021. The later solo album credits its own 2025 album mix. The available credits do not establish identical masters.
