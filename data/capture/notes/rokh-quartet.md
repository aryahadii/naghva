# Rokh Quartet

The quartet’s own photograph shows the later ensemble. The debut album and live four-track EP are separately credited. Live in Tehran was recorded later but distributed first. Sogol Mirzaei founded the group with Hossein Rad. Sanaz Sattarzadeh plays on the filmed Tehran programme and the later Yalda broadcast. The Fribourg and Paris film descriptions identify individual players and, where known, the origins of the songs.

Reviewed the performers’ original album pages, Apple Music and available Spotify editions, artist and group video channels, French public radio and independently issued performance films. Bandcamp contains material shared with Atine and Sowal Diabi. No additional artist-identified SoundCloud or Telegram edition was established in the original-language searches. Dates of video publication may differ from the concert dates.
