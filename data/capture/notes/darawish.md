# Darawish

The musicians self-released ten Madar tracks on Bandcamp on January 27, 2020. Apple distributed them on February 3. The album credits enumerate vocal and instrumental guests. The September 2020 Casa Árabe performance has two native recordings of the same concert. “Persarab” and “Persian Road” also circulated on the oud player’s channel, alongside an independently titled concert with guests Jasmina Petrovic and Jonathan Badichi. Their Bandcamp profile reuses the album poster. The earlier ensemble and the later Cardamom trio have distinct members and catalogues.

The [native Spotify edition](https://open.spotify.com/album/2pugCEut2UuiKEt0GvnVX0) preserves the same ten tracks in the same order. Each Spotify track now points from the same record as the [Bandcamp album](https://darawish.bandcamp.com/album/madar) and [Apple Music edition](https://music.apple.com/us/album/madar/1495102490).

The artist’s own channel supplies the following released performances. The full 2021 Córdoba excerpt and the festival organizer’s identical 7:40 upload share a single entry. The [festival programme](https://teatrocordoba.es/festival-guitarra-cordoba/eventos/darawish/) distinguishes that concert from the September 2020 issue. The original Darawish thumbnail for the El Intruso concert shows all five musicians and is the group’s portrait. The studio promotional reel and the winery making-of are documented as production footage.
