# ArtSaves

Arash Mollakarimi's existing item appeared on “Abyss.” The Persian spelling comes from the Fajr Music Festival’s performance report, where Kian Pourtorab and Mollakarimi performed on the same bill. MUTEK describes him working between Tehran and Vancouver. The festival photo has separate authorship. The Spotify profile photo used here shows Mollakarimi performing, in a dark shirt against large lettering. [Festival biography](https://montreal.mutek.org/en/artists/artsaves) · [2018 Red Bull Music Academy participants](https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2018/03/rbma-2018-participants/).

Kopi’s original recordings, the ten Apple and Spotify issues and forty tracks from the musician’s verified SoundCloud profile were reconciled by title and version. The label lists eleven songs on *SEUIL*, nine on *SEUIL Remixes* and six on *Molehead*. The printed “Choas before renewal” and digital “Chaos Before Renewal” are the same track. The first issue of “Lingering Unnoticed” and the digital edition spell the guest’s alias differently. The studio vocal and instrumental versions of “Abyss” are separate. The Idlefon rework retains its existing recording. The early SoundCloud material includes mixes and self-issued music. Three original Mixcloud uploads were checked separately, including the Tehran March 2014 DJ set and the 2016 TADAEX performance. The host is cited on the recording. The account also includes other musicians’ guest selections. [Label catalogue](https://kopirecs.bandcamp.com/music) · [Artist’s SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/artsvs) · [Early performance archive](https://www.mixcloud.com/artsvs/).

The artist uses artsvs on SoundCloud. The unrelated label channel uploading performances by Halal Sol is a separate project with the same name.
