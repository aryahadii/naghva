# Lowen

The group’s 2018 debut carries its own phonogram credit. Its six artist-issued Bandcamp entries include a physical spoken-word record with a digital preview. Apple distributes the original five-track debut under a different Lowen profile from the 2024 album. The five track titles and lengths match the band's release. Only the matched debut is used from that Apple profile.

The band's linked Spotify page supplies seven editions, including successive two-track and three-track digital singles in 2024. Its YouTube channel lists fifteen videos. The backstage film is documentary footage. The separate live festival performance, drum playthrough and acoustic improvisation were checked alongside the studio videos. The Temple of Antas film names Nina as the singer. The Bandcamp group photograph shows four people outdoors.
