# Darya Dadvar

Apple and Spotify carry the Berlin 2004, Toronto 2008 and Malmö 2014 concerts. The recordings keep separate identities even where titles recur across all three albums. The two digital editions of “Vatanam (Live)” have different issue dates and runtimes. “Dobareh” has collective Spotify billing. The artist’s homepage embeds SoundCloud account 73352414, which published three tracks. Its concert portrait is also the artist page image.

The 66 uploads on her own YouTube channel include television, rehearsal and concert material. Interviews and announcements are excluded from the recording inventory. The same Tehran opera film occurs twice, and the Babak Amini guitar arrangement of “Iran” appears in multiple uploads. Dadvar’s Paris-Est orchestral performance has different accompaniment. “Parastoo” to words by Fereydoon Moshiri and “Naz Parastoo” to words by Mehdi Akhavan Sales are distinct works. The artist’s written biography dates the Tehran opera appearances to summer 2002; her uploaded film description gives 2003. The film entry retains the venue without choosing a performance year.

The artist’s own biography, Radio Farda interview and live issue credits establish the account of her training, stage roles and changing repertoire. Her complete concert history and stage performances without a traceable recording are outside the recording count.

BBC Persian’s *Rangahang* adds the complete 2025 programme and individual songs listed in its chapters. “Raghse Azadi” and “Beboosam Mohkam” from that programme also appear on Dadvar’s own channel. The BBC Nowruz 1384 link on her press page is a retrievable four-page photographic report, not an audio issue. Her original page for *Love, Wisdom & Human* documents the five-city 2011 tour and embeds performance footage. The ARTE/Safar full concert at the Alhambra is distinct from the shorter clip on her channel. Bassist Leïla Soldevila’s original discography corroborates the Malmö ensemble.
