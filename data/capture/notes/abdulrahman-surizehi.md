The CNRS Jean During corpus yielded eleven named benju performances when checked at item level. Eight are distinct numbered sowt recordings of the Saravan ensemble made in November 1980. The 1995 epic recording with Qaderbaksh credits Surizehi in the opening only. The Paris concert tape credits him on benju beside Omar Sor in December 1998. The other 1995 epic item identifies Mazar Ebrahim. Several neighbouring archive records credit another musician with the same family name. The Karvan CD pairs Surizehi and Javid Afsari Rad in a 1998 Oslo recording issued in 2001. Six of the ten Karvan tracks can be individually attributed from an actual broadcaster’s programme. On Ocora’s 1997 CD and 2019 reissue, track-specific billing names Surizehi on two pieces.
