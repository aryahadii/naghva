# Karvan

Five musicians appear on the 1998 Oslo recording documented in Javid Afsari Rad's firsthand label essay. Etnisk Musikklubb's physical catalogue number, EM05, establishes the 2001 CD. The Spotify album linked by the CD publisher and the corresponding Apple issue each carry ten tracks. The broadcaster's six-track playlist identifies the two featured musicians by name. The solo percussion cuts carry their instrument-specific performers from the label roster. The 2005 sampler lists Sufi Dance plus a shorter version of Ghazal; its two tracks are kept distinct from the concert pieces. The streaming artist image reproduces the album cover and is used only for the release. No separately sourced group portrait was found.
