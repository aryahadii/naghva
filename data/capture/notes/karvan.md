# Karvan

Five musicians appear on the 1998 Oslo recording documented in Javid Afsari Rad's firsthand label essay. Etnisk Musikklubb's physical catalogue number, EM05, establishes the 2001 CD. The Spotify album linked by the CD publisher and the corresponding Apple issue each carry ten tracks. The broadcaster's six-track playlist identifies the two featured musicians by name. The solo percussion cuts carry their instrument-specific performers from the label roster. The 2005 sampler lists Sufi Dance plus a shorter version of Ghazal; its two tracks are kept distinct from the concert pieces. The label’s original 2001 sleeve incorporates an identifiable photograph of the ensemble onstage. The page uses the historical image as published and credits the label. No standalone image of the photograph or photographer name was found. [Physical edition](https://etniskmusikklubb.no/produkt/where-the-music-traditions-from-baluchistan-persia-and-india-meet/) · [Afsari Rad’s notes](https://etniskmusikklubb.no/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/20.pdf) · [Sampler](https://open.spotify.com/album/1qVia4obm7j5JVPEl1oxoC)

Matched the ten concert tracks and their parent album to the identically numbered Spotify and Apple issues also recovered for Afsari Rad. Each master has one record, including the two individually credited percussion solos. Both distributor and physical album titles are preserved.
