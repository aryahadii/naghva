# Farahnaz Hatam

The performing artist was born in Tehran. Solo performances, the partnership with Hani Mojtahedy and the larger duo are distinguished by the original Sharjah, Callie’s, Klangteppich and Villa Aurora credits. Her Bandcamp artist space is empty. Apple and Spotify posters and catalogue issues list the performing duo under LABOUR. Her individual catalogue includes the studio-recorded LP and both sides, an original Klangteppich quartet and recordings explicitly credited by name. NTS does not credit her separately as performer on all of the 76 hosted programmes. Both members appear together in the photograph, as identified in the caption. The 2019 Berlin Hatam and Hani concert and two separately advertised 2018 solo electronic sets have verified event credits but no confirmed publicly issued recordings. The Klangteppich label issued two separate excerpts from the 2018 concert, with Mojtahedy on vocals and Hatam on electronics. The Basel film credits LABOUR as composer and three Basel drum groups as performers. Earlier SoundCloud IDs embedded on the artists’ site are unavailable. The original festival films, Bandcamp, SoundCloud, YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music and Telegram were cross-checked with duo and presenter catalogues. No independent solo streaming discography was confirmed.
