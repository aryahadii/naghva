# Neda Mohamadpour

Reused Qbb46yz, already a credited artist on the recorded Small World Centre concert with Cinna Peyghamy and Mahmood Schricker. The vocalist’s own 2023 cohort introduction confirms her birth in Tehran, work in Iran and January 2022 move to Toronto. Her first full album was recorded live on 16 November 2024 and issued by the independent Toronto-based Link Music Lab in September 2025. The label’s event page identifies her portrait. The artist submitted biography uses the variant Mohammadpour; the record labels use Mohamadpour. A UK singer performing as Neda and the US music executive of the same first name are separate people.
