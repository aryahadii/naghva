# Nina Saeidi

The Lowen releases and native channel performances were checked against individual vocal and instrument credits. Mystras identifies track five on its 2021 album. Wyatt E. names her as singer and vocal composer on The Diviner’s Prayer to the Gods of the Night. The cover image on her page is a still from Lowen’s film identifying her singing at the Temple of Antas.

Wyatt E.’s original Bandcamp advertises a January 2027 release for Armour of Surrender. Only the advance track with playable audio was catalogued as available. The article records the announced status of the album. Band-wide credits do not establish an individual instrument credit for every track.
