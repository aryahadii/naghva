# Cardamom Trio

The March 2024 first interview distinguishes the named trio from the previous Darawish quintet and the separate March 2023 album, made by Sarvarian and Bitar. Taberna was their prior musical colleague, then joined the public concert project. Exact trio-billed films on the original musician’s YouTube channel and the programmes from Madrid and Portugal were checked against the group's own archive. No second released trio studio edition has been verified. The portrait on the musicians' own site identifies the photographer in its file path, and the three seated players are shown with percussion, flute and oud. The other two founders’ national origins are separately attested.
