# Siavash Imani

**9.2/10. Catalogue ready and article ready.**

Independent review on 22 November 2026. Labyrinth’s artist programme confirms the teachers, the radio work and the chronology of *Shahrood*, *Irā*, *Tash* and *So Faraway*. I verified all eight tracks on Imani’s SoundCloud profile and the Berlin film issued by CrossGeneration Media. The three physical CD catalogues from the Bibliothèque nationale de France identify voice and tar credits on volumes one and two, Sina Khoshk Bijari’s tombak for volume two, and three and six named modal groups on volumes two and three. I added those nine works and the specific credited roles. No authorized stream for the later two CDs could be verified. Apple’s 32 separately marked cuts of volume one and the eight physical divisions remain different indexes of the same issued material. The Persian prose explains the structure without losing the English chronology, and Labyrinth’s portrait depicts Imani at the tar.

Checkpoint 57 displayed 84 panels and 157 decoded images per locale, with the English viewport fitting at 1280 px and Persian at 390 px. The catalogued nine CD works entered after that render.
