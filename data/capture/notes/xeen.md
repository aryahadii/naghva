# XEEN

The five solo Bandcamp singles match all five uploads on her SoundCloud. The Shaytoon and Apranik compilations have track-specific original credits. The Noise à Noise cassette excerpt contains only the opening of the full Berlin to Istanbul performance, which the presenting partner identifies separately. Her five Herr Spectre entries keep their joint artist identity. OneBeat identifies Istanbul as her base during its residency; Fondation Fiminco identifies Paris later. Her own account describes her entry into The Finches in 2016, after the first singer left. Fiminco also calls her a founder; the early lineup evidence is insufficient to establish that. The founding-year discrepancy is preserved here. The Spotify artist portrait matches the musician shown by her official website. OneBeat mentions a benefit for an Afghanistan healthcare organization without naming a particular track.
