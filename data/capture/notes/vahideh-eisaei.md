# Vahideh Eisaei

Tehran conservatory, university and migration are documented independently by the musician and the Multicultural Centre for Women’s Health. The Letter String Quartet issue credits qanun and vocals. ABC Classic identifies her only on movements four and seven of Breathing Space. The Nexus Project specifies Karabach; the other seven titles do not credit her. The concert films, trio demos, theatrical scores and filmed productions are individually distinguished. The Boite’s dedicated performer page supplies her photograph beside the qanun.
