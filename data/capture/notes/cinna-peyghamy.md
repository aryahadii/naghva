# Cinna Peyghamy

The French artist and the earlier moniker Cikkun are the same person, documented in his signed Gamut credits and Festival de Marseille’s biography. The Persian programme identifies his name as سینا پیغامی. The Spotify artist portrait, checked visually, is a black-and-white stage photograph of Peyghamy playing his tombak beside his electronics. Born in France to Iranian parents, he describes speaking Persian at home and not having been able to travel to Iran.

The six Bandcamp releases, their individual track pages, the 19 native SoundCloud tracks under Cikkun, and all 11 videos in the personal artist channel were compared with Apple Music and both Spotify identities. The self-titled track and EP The Skin In Between are separate. Original album and concert versions with Azu Tiwaline were distinguished from remixes. The CD/streaming spelling Armure differs from the artist’s Armure II. Cikkun discography and the native footage establish that both concern the same three-track issue. Additional research included the full original Berlin Gaswerk concert, Genevan residency piece, the duo’s Draaimolen performance, Azu Tiwaline’s discography, Link Music Lab, and the guest credits of Saint Abdullah, Eomac, Rosso Polare and David August. The original label bills him on two tracks of the Toronto EP, Dorna and Speck Point. The solo tracks of its other two artists have their own credits. Mahmood Schricker’s original setar sample is on Divân, side B track 3 of the 2026 album. Khosrow Peyghamy’s voice is on Dar Shab; clarinet and co-writing on Slow Eaters are credited to Quelque Bourdon.

Tiwaline credits Peyghamy for tombak and modular arrangements on eight of nine tracks from The Fifth Dream without specifying all eight; the album is included under his guest credits. The E.P. Magnetic Service names exactly two shared works, Magnetic Service and Tight Wind. Their Canopée Imaginaire recording on his page is the Draaimolen festival performance. His 2025 Zurkhaneh premiere is in the article via the commissioning studio; unrelated performance clips require direct confirmation before attaching them to the work.
