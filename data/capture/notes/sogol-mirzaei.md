# Sogol Mirzaei

Mirzaei’s personal portrait shows her holding her tar. The ARTE television excerpt credits her as a player of two instruments and identifies Gilles Sivilotto as composer. The two 2025 Rokh releases have different tar players. The Sowal Diabi and Atine releases use their original attributions.
