# Atine independent review, 9.2/10

Catalogue and article pass. In checkpoint 51 each language rendered 15 recording panels and all 37 image elements decoded at the tested 1280- and 390-pixel widths. The Paris quintet and the earlier Mawazine quartet are distinguished. The Lille programme specifies Roshanak Rafani, while the original label artwork and recording list credit Saghar Khadem. Tar and viola da gamba interludes now have instrument-specific billing. I independently located the surviving WOMEX film from Porto and checked its new panel against the organiser’s channel. The album, Paris broadcast and Porto showcase remain separate performances. Scores for sources, writing, catalogue, images and gaps were 1.9/2, 1.8/2, 2.8/3, 1/1 and 1.7/2.
