# Idlefon

Hesam Ohadi, also credited as Hessam Ohadi on the original three-musician music catalogue. Idlefon is the music alias and Kino is the visual-art alias. MUTEK and the Calgary arts council identify both practices. The Spotify portrait was checked: a black-and-white photograph of the musician in front of a projection. [MUTEK biography](https://dubai.mutek.org/en/artists/idlefon) · [Calgary artist programme](https://www.icaionline.org/blog/napp-cohort-2-2022-2023).

- Reconciled the eleven-track *Intensive Collectivity Known As City*, *Submarine* and *COLDSTREAM* across their issuing labels, the musician’s own pages where applicable, Apple Music, Spotify and the artist-channel releases. Both early albums have label-issued covers and production credits. All 11 individually published SoundCloud uploads were inspected. The recording numbering in the SoundCloud titles of “Homecoming,” “Pretend It’s Spring” and “Pickers of Empty Cocoons” refers to the numbered album tracks. [Original duo of artist-published albums](https://idlefon.bandcamp.com/music) · [SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/idlefon) · [n5MD album](https://n5md.bandcamp.com/album/coldstream).
- Three people are credited on the digital edition of the early split album *Spotty Surfaces* (*Gereh*). CTM dates the initial Mahriz release to 2010, the Iranian press reviewed the CD in 2012, and the later digital edition uses the spelling Hessam Ohadi. Twelve tracks use the digital edition’s joint artist credit. None assigns sole composition to Ohadi. This edition is separate from the later *Girih* label compilations. [Participant interview](https://15questions.net/interview/fifteen-questions-interview-idlefon/page-1/) · [CTM retrospective](https://mobile-archive2013-2020.ctm-festival.de/archive/festival-editions/ctm-2017-fearangerlove/artists/p-t/siavash-amini/) · [Digital reissue](https://music.apple.com/us/album/spotty-surfaces/1804724753).
- Exact compilation appearances include “After the Quake” on the two Unexplained Sounds surveys, “Stonefall,” “Antmill N,” “Requiem for Bobby Baccalà,” the guest feature with SaffronKeira and Siavash Amini, and “Lost (with RON)” from the Kopi split with Pher. The 2021 Temp-Illusion rework retains its existing recording ID. Pher’s piece from the split is credited to Pher. [Kopi release](https://kopirecs.bandcamp.com/album/command-v3) · [Temp-Illusion recording](https://temp-illusion.bandcamp.com/track/two-lands-idlefon-rework).
- SET’s UNISON release is an artist-credited mix of other musicians’ recordings. The audio and cover have separate provenance. CJSW published the Black Ice live set. The MUTEK programme also documents the live project *Aesthetics of Survival*; a standalone issue could not be confirmed. [SET mix](https://soundcloud.com/set-fest/unison-mix01-idlefon) · [CJSW concert](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qs4NthX4diA).

Ohadi identifies four pieces on the original twelve-piece split. The digital rights holder bills all three on every track. The individual allocation is undetermined. His own biography also documents the unnamed contribution to Mahriz’s *Ear 5* and the score for Arash Radkia’s animation. [Musician biography](https://about.me/hesam.ohadi).

Photomat’s *Windowsill* appeared in 2013 on Oído. Its eight tracks have exact Apple and Spotify links. Two artist-uploaded demos and the PUSH session have separate entries. Herr Spectre’s first-party EP confirms the four songs and issue date. Its advertised 2023 Sled appearance was cancelled. Hailstones’ six-track 2026 album, earlier single, concert films and music video are included. The latter group's name also belongs to unrelated artists. [Photomat](https://soundcloud.com/photomatband) · [Herr Spectre](https://herrspectre.bandcamp.com/album/all-things-consoled) · [Hailstones](https://hailstones-band.bandcamp.com/album/the-weariness-of-hope).

Guest works now include Behbahani’s co-billed “Headless,” ArtSaves’ “Back to the Sun” rework and the sung version of “Abyss.” The original visual collaborator and MUTEK both describe the *COLDSTREAM A/V* presentation. [Flaming Pines](https://flamingpines.bandcamp.com/track/headless-idlefon-with-kamyar-behbahani) · [Kopi](https://kopirecs.bandcamp.com/album/abyss) · [MUTEK](https://montreal.mutek.org/en/artists/idlefon-amir-b-ash).
