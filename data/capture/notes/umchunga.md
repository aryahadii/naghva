# Umchunga

Nima Pourkarimi’s existing person item also holds his own artist name. The Bandcamp artist portrait is a photograph of the long-haired musician in low light. Sleeve artist Azin Nafarhaghighi is credited only for the debut album sleeve. The alias refers to Mira Calix’s “Umchunga Locks.” That recording belongs to Calix. [Artist’s label and debut](https://umchunga.bandcamp.com/album/should-have-been-done-by-now).

The two releases in his solo Bandcamp catalogue include the six-piece debut and the one-track *black square*. The 2018 Flaming Pines duo album has five tracks and precise collective writing credit. “Turbulence I” has two original label issues and a personal SoundCloud upload. “Turbulence II” is a distinct recording. Other label appearances include *Girih*, *Absence*, *Kollektive 1* and the two issues of his Tegh rework. The recording “Held (for Fateme)” and PUSH warehouse performance “CyTor” have separate credits. Nineteen original SoundCloud uploads and both Apple artist IDs were reconciled by title.
