# Sina Salimi

The songwriter’s Toronto showcase, his March 2010 Arta Gallery credit, his artist statements and his releases establish his Iranian Canadian identity. The Berlin-based filmmaker of the same name is a different person. The individual portrait on his Spotify profile was visually compared with his signed Bandcamp artist photograph and Tammuz’s named photograph. The existing Qt5sbqz identity and two existing catalogue items for his collaboration with Pedram Khavarzamini are retained.

The Bandcamp artist grid and its 53 album track pages, 17 Apple album and single editions (including two shared releases), 69 distinct track-specific Apple pages, and 73 Spotify track URLs were checked. The various guest artist and recommended track listings on digital platforms were filtered by performer. Apple and Spotify editions of the ten remasters were matched by track number against the original releases and kept distinct from the unmastered singles. The 2013 Apple edition garbles the final ellipsis in Perhaps There…; the artist’s Bandcamp supplies the attributable spelling. Seventeen musical uploads from his official channel are represented, alongside a concept-album trailer that does not constitute a separate recording.

Mahmood Schricker credits Salimi separately for lyrics, voice and santur on Shadows. Schricker’s SoundCloud public archive has a distinct Sin City home recording. Barzin credits him on the five-piece Studio 13 live session recorded in 2018 and issued in 2019; the album does not isolate his role for each piece. The studio’s broadcast interview about Salimi’s own career was not counted as music. Of 52 Link Music Lab uploads the exact Spring to Fall concert video matches the Experimental Link Music Nights Spotify track. One older venue YouTube link no longer resolved. The 2026 album’s booklet gives many of the performances by track number. The sixteen-musician, nineteen-instrument count and 71-minute length come from the final release, rather than the earlier 72-minute estimate on the lead single. Exact session and artwork credits have been added where available.

The native SoundCloud user archive returned 66 uploads. All have exact track links on the matching recording, including the original songs and the ten separately issued remasters. Two uploads of On the Road have the same duration to the millisecond and the same credited song text; both appear on its one recording. The Shahrema TV archive identifies Salimi and two other musicians in its Saba concert segment. It does not name the pieces or date the original concert. November 2024 is the archival upload, not a performance date. The article describes the planned Inner Scripts staging without asserting the cancelled Toronto Act 2 performance took place. Café Pamenar advertised the July 2026 Hafez programme as the second and final live recording. No public issue of that show has been identified.
