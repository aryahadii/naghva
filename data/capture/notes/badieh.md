The original Bandcamp debut was issued on 8 August 2021. Apple and Spotify classify their 12 August issues as an EP. The five-track catalogue search on Apple omits the second single Leyla Dar Vakon; Spotify confirms its separate July 2021 issue. The second record appeared on Bandcamp 15 November 2023; its label described the commercial release as 17 November in its October premiere. Paresh-e Jal has a separate 2020 Bandcamp single page. The identical durations of Rashid Khan / Tarke Diar / Rashid Khan (2:38) and Assadolla Jan / Assadullah Jan (4:46) distinguish alternate service titles from new works. The primary sources give the specific guests on each album track. The two era-specific label samplers reuse original studio performances. Five official Worlds Within Worlds videos link to their records. The SoundCloud and Telegram name searches returned no artist-owned group account; Badgir is a different act. The group’s Bandcamp portrait shows the two founders holding instruments among brick arches. The announced Budapest show was cancelled, and its status is explicit in the organizer archive. The 2025 Adelaide concert is confirmed by a published on-site review.

Christy Gallois documents the Brisbane concert as an attendee and photographed Gasco at the show. Her video is not an artist-, label- or organizer-published recording.
