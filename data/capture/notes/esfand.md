# Esfand

Rouzbeh Esfandarmaz and Patrick Stewart founded this duo in The Hague in 2020. The original artist ID for “Quiescence” has been retained. Its previous person type was inconsistent with the original Shouka credit to both musicians. The original Apple single is retained alongside the EP. The Spotify image shows the two members and matches the group billed by Pop-Kultur. [Original EP](https://esfand.bandcamp.com/album/h-l) · [Festival](https://2024.pop-kultur.berlin/en/artists/esfand/)

The five-track Shouka EP, eight-track 30M album, preceding Nuri remix and its parent issue were reconciled against original Bandcamp, the specific Apple and Spotify artist IDs, and original label videos. Hélé’s booklet and first edition give 4 March 2022. The later Piltan text retrospectively calls it a 2021 EP. Nuri’s dated remix record does establish the duo’s published work in 2021. The same-name SoundCloud DJ and separate Apple artist pages with unrelated recordings have been excluded. The published Berlin timetable establishes the 2024 performance. [2024 album](https://esfand.bandcamp.com/album/piltan) · [Nuri original](https://nuri.bandcamp.com/track/asfer-esfand-remix)

30M’s Bandcamp release message dated 26 April 2024 already offered Bibi Sāghi as the featured track for Piltan. The separate label announcement of 13 May called it the first single. The full release followed on 14 June. The Grey Space documents the Osmose residency and February public concert. The original Berlin Nachwuchs programme confirms the subsequent sample-production workshop.
