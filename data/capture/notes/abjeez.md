# Abjeez

Apple Music and Spotify profiles are linked by the band. Nine artist-led release pages were reconciled with the corresponding Spotify editions. Additional appearances on two Robbie Rulah collections and United Colors of Ska were checked track by track. The official YouTube channel has 73 uploads, including solo releases, interviews and concert excerpts. Forty-nine YouTube links were matched to works or distinct performances. A pass over 105 public posts on the group’s Telegram channel found directly playable editions of Estadium, Drömm, Freedom Call, Eyde, Eddeaa and To Va Man. Post 97 dates Hameh to 2006, whereas Apple dates the digital edition to January 2007. Radio Javan also lists Biyaa, Jedi Nagir and Hameh Baayad Bedoonan. These have artist-channel counterparts. The video descriptions identified the Urkult appearance, a 2009 tour rehearsal, the Stockholm live version of Tu Me Haces Falta, a Roxy Theatre remix, Mixed Nutz, and the Swedish television performance with Bo Kaspers Orkester. The Purple Rain performance credits Safoura and three other players. The second Rotterdam excerpt lacks enough information to determine whether it duplicates the first. Some untitled clips and rehearsals still lack an exact identification. The Spotify portrait shows both sisters.
