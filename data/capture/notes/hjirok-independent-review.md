# Independent review of HJirok

Both first renderings contain 11 recording panels and 29 decoded images. English at 1,280 pixels and Persian at 390 pixels have valid article anchors and no horizontal overflow. All eight original album tracks, the Passaporte film and the radio programme are represented. The original commissioning venue documents Forbidden Echoes as an installation and three October 2021 concerts (21–23). The February 2023 host identifies that commission as the beginning of the pair’s longer collaboration. Spore documents the later exhibition of two video and sound works and specifically credits Mojtahedy. The first article begins with their recollections and proceeds directly to the 2024 album, omitting the collaboration’s origins in performance and installation. Initial score **8.7/10** (sources 1.8, article 1.5, catalogue 2.7, imagery 0.9, reader experience 1.8).
