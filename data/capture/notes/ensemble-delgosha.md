# Ensemble Delgosha, Iran

The two-suite Delârâm studio recording has eighteen matching Apple Music, Spotify and distributor-delivered YouTube masters. The international digital editions identify guest soloists only where specified. The French programme identifies a different instrumental lineup during the two Louvre appearances. The two presenter films were published before the concert. In its later gallery guide Lyon calls the first one a festival recording, but its QR code decodes to the presenter’s February promotional publication, so no second live master can be inferred. The same-name 2015 Mexico City ensemble of Mehdi Moshtagh and Taghi Akhbari’s separate European ensemble have different personnel and were excluded. The 2018 concert is documented by the festival and the singer, who released a separate rehearsal excerpt. Iranian personal uploads reproduce the studio performance of Gol Ozar without confirming another edition. The original French label store closed in 2025. Native SoundCloud, Bandcamp and Telegram searches produced no group account; the credited singer’s Telegram clip is separate. The organizer’s photograph shows both vocalists and the four Paris players holding their instruments. No photographer is credited on the programme.
