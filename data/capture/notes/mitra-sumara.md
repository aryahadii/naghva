# Mitra Sumara · catalogue checked on 2026-11-21

The artist’s two albums, Tahdig and Dream, appear on both Apple Music and Spotify. Their 10 and eight tracks respectively matched in sequence. The artist's US Apple album lookup returned those two albums. Spotify also lists three singles under the group's name, each retained as a separate edition. The band’s Dream page on Bandcamp links all eight tracks. Its public Bandcamp catalogue resolves to that album. The label's SoundCloud account, linked from the band's own website, hosts “Helelyos”.

All four uploads on the official YouTube Videos tab were checked. Three overlap the existing song panels, while the Nublu performance of “Kofriam” is a distinct live recording. The Smithsonian’s concert video is separately identified. The museum gives its running order, including “Noruz Amad”, in its object record. These movements have one continuous official recording. A Telegram search in Persian and English did not locate a verified band channel. Amazon Music and Audiomack carry the two albums. The complete streaming history of earlier concerts is unverified.

The artist's official site presents its bandleader, Yvette Massoudi, in the photograph used on the page. Massoudi refers to her Iranian father and the origin of the group in her written Smith College interview. The band releases through Persian Cardinal Recordings and sells Dream through its own Bandcamp account. Grant support for producing that album came from the NYC Women's Fund.
