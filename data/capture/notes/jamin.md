# Jamin

ICORN identifies Jamin as an Iranian artist. The 2020 concert organizer places him in Tbilisi. His original channel documents both solo work and guest artists. His own phonogram credit is present on *Noosh* and on *Scenario*. The music video for Justina’s *Darim Miaym* separately credits him as producer. Piteå’s Festspelen and Swedish public television document the later festival and school performances. The portrait is the photograph on Jamin’s verified repertoire profile on Spotify.

The catalogue reconciles the artist’s 48 channel entries with the Apple editions and linked Spotify catalogue. Trailers are excluded. The *Sard* original and remix remain separate, as do filmed live versions and studio performances. Shared releases with Justina reuse existing records when the credits and performance agree. A musician billed only as Sina has not been identified with Sina Sae.
