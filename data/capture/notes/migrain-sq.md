# Migrain Sq.

Hadi Bastani interviewed founder Idin Samimi Mofakham for his doctoral research. Mofakham dates the cover band to about 2002 and names the early and intervening personnel. Singer Sara Bigdeli Shamloo dates her own entry to 2011. Contemporary interviews distinguish the studio trio from its four-person Madrid lineup with keyboard player Olka Hedayat. The group told a Spanish interviewer that it had recorded Polish Chair earlier. Neither that interview nor the searched native publications establishes a commercial issue or a track list for Polish Chair. The group plays the Lorca poem in Ahmad Shamlou’s Persian translation.

Seven songs on Oído Records’ 2012 album appear on both Spotify and Apple, with the physical CD independently distributed in Europe. The composer’s recollection of the year and title in the doctoral interview differs from the original release. The three songs on the artist’s only identified native SoundCloud profile were reconciled. “Love Story” had already circulated in August 2011, before the original album; its duration is within two seconds of the CD master. The authors did not establish a different arrangement. “History” has a video by its original director Ahmad Kiarostami with named guest musicians and individual credits; no concert film from Madrid could be tied to a group or presenter. No original artist Telegram, Bandcamp or Radio Javan artist edition surfaced under Migrain, Migrain Sq., Migrain Square, Madame Buttercry or the Persian titles. The Spotify group portrait is the original three-person image also published with the contemporary interview; the source credits Promociones sin Fronteras.
