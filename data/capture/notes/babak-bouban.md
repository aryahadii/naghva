# Babak Bouban

Candle & Fog’s author page identifies the santur player and supplies the photograph. His own account of lessons with Faramarz Payvar describes how the handwritten exercises became the introductory radif. BeepTunes identifies his tasnif studies with Mohammad Reza Shajarian, research on santur construction and his association for listeners. [Publisher profile and portrait](https://candle-fog.com/babak-bouban/) · [His account of the lessons](https://t.me/babakbouban/139) · [Iranian distributor](https://beeptunes.com/artist/3403799).

- The three albums on Spotify were compared track by track with Apple Music. *Fever* has four tracks, *The Sentinel* six and *Barren* nine. The original publisher’s Persian title for *Barren* is «سترون». For the two releases with Negar Bouban, her CV and the composer’s recordings identify his voice and santur and her voice and oud. Existing shared works retain their IDs. BeepTunes’ five-track *Sing*, with music and singing by Babak and arrangements by Sarvar Zarehgar, does not appear on his global artist pages. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/3zZ0DWSFfkvtXvaYHcpP6Z) · [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/artist/babak-bouban/555205515) · [Sing and original tracklist](https://beeptunes.com/album/73923970) · [Mahoor on Barren](https://t.me/mahoorinstitute/998).
- The musician’s public Telegram archive contained 320 visible posts in this pass. The Persian editions of 19 Bernstein programmes explicitly name Bouban as translator and narrator. His Payvar santur recording, the narrated *Carnival of the Animals* and *Peter and the Wolf*, and the introduction to modern music are also documented there. The works are credited by activity. Bouban provided historical Shajarian and Payvar tapes to a podcast from his family archive, without claiming a musical role in them. [Iranian editions](https://t.me/babakbouban/366) · [Payvar recording](https://t.me/babakbouban/258) · [Archival attribution](https://t.me/babakbouban/333).
