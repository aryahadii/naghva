# Sheen Trio

Two independently issued album track lists were reconciled to their original Bandcamp releases, seven Spotify editions and Apple’s two albums and early singles. The guitarist also recorded Cocoon separately for Distance with Parvaresh, under individual billing. Distinct recordings at Mühle der Freundschaft, Studio Nord Bremen and District Studios retain their issue-specific film credits. The group channel contains five films, including an album trailer. Jazzexzess and the 2026 Jazzfest Berlin radio broadcast are separately documented. No confirmed group Telegram release or separate group SoundCloud catalogue was identified. In her biography, Parvaresh dates the founding to 2020; the concert presenter says 2019, a year also suggested by an older group biography. The article attributes the year to the founder. The debut liner credits say “All compositions by Shabnam Parvaresh” but the adjacent album essay says all but one; composition is individually assigned only when further evidence identifies the specific work.
