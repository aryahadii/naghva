# Negar Bouban

Bouban’s official discography describes the five original solo albums, including which of the Tehran concert recordings appeared jointly on CD and DVD. The editions of *In Turn*, *Through* and *On Fire* carry the musician’s own phonogram credit. The 41 tracks across the five albums were matched across the services linked from her website. *Continu* has French, Persian and English track titles on different services. The digital issues bearing the names Negar Bouban, Negar Booban and Negâr Boubân were identified by the performer’s own links.

The artist’s 2024 and 2026 CVs identify collaborations that do not appear on her streaming artist pages. Album credits, including ensemble and orchestral performances, apply at the level documented. The official YouTube channel separates full performances, concert excerpts and teaching films. The *Meanders* playlist bills Gah Ensemble and the interviews distinguish the sung studio performances from concert arrangements with a guest singer. The performance portrait comes from Bouban’s SoundCloud profile. On “Simple One,” the ensemble director credits her with the theme and Tamara Lukasheva with composition and arrangement.
