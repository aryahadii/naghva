# Photomat

Tehran electronic/post-rock group formed around Hesam Ohadi, Siavash Amini and Nima Pourkarimi. Collaborator Avi Engel’s liner biography identifies the fourth formative musician, Pooya Bakhsheshi. Early songs involved two singers. The SoundCloud project avatar is a verified colour performance photograph showing musicians, laptops and projections. [Collaborator's sleeve](https://aviengel.bandcamp.com/track/harvest-siavash-amini-remix) · [Group’s original uploads](https://soundcloud.com/photomatband).

The available eight-track Oído edition, two pre-album demos, and the independently filmed PUSH set make twelve panels. The band's November 2012 SoundCloud upload calls the album forthcoming. A later participant biography dates the release to 2012, while Hadi Bastani’s doctoral thesis dates it to 2011. The group’s own dated post and the label’s March 2013 release credit give the clearest documented sequence. Contemporaneous Paris festival programming independently places “Night” in June 2012. Both available Spotify editions use the same recordings. Unrelated Hebrew-language industrial recordings on other profiles sharing the name belong to a different group.
