# Farshad Mohammadi

The concert and album credits identify this person as a santur player and composer. The Iranian cinematographer with the same name is separate. The Hafez Ensemble tracks were reconciled against existing Maryam Akhondy records. The origin of each concert matters: the Ealing Town Hall issue and Beykoz Kundura Cinema performances have separate original recordings, and the St Petersburg concert films feature a different lineup. An audiovisual teaser and uncredited orchestral clips have not been treated as performances by the uploader. Physical issue and studio master dates can differ from later distributor dates.
