# Soheil Peyghambari

Composer, sound engineer and woodwind player. His professional and recorded work includes Tehran’s Kargadan Studio, Quartet Diminished, international improvising ensembles, and his own recorded experiments with amplified winds and electronics. The Spotify portrait was visually inspected and shows him playing bass clarinet. [Musician profile](https://database.shareimpro.eu/en/person/soheil-peyghambari) · [Artist catalogue](https://music.apple.com/us/artist/soheil-peyghambari/586956766).

- Vasco Trilla’s recording in Tehran includes the four musicians’ instrumental credits and identifies Peyghambari as recordist and mixer. The Barcelona *Möhit* was issued digitally in December 2020. Its digital sequence divides the first part in two, while the label lists two pieces on CD. Subsequent recordings by the same trio from 2021 surfaced in February 2026 as *Sacred Birds*. Both projects are distinct from the Tehran quartet. The separate Radio France improvisation combines clarinet with Franck Dadure’s guitar and was recorded the day before broadcast. [Tehran quartet](https://vascotrilla.bandcamp.com/album/sadigh-peyghambari-trilla-fadavi) · [CD edition](https://www.sklep.gusstaff.com/en/reviriego-peyghambari-trilla-moehit.html) · [Sacred Birds](https://vascotrillaalexreviriego.bandcamp.com/album/sacred-birds) · [Radio France recording](https://franckdadure1.bandcamp.com/track/live-radio-france-franck-dadure-soheil-peyghambari-ikejime-duo).

The separately licensed music listed in the end credits belongs to other artists. The 2022 release *Hum Sa* names him on its seven issued recordings and Javan lists a physical edition. The international jazz projects include the two *Realm* albums and *Triple Helix* with Kevin Kastning.
