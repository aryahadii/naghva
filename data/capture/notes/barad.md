# Barad

Barad is the Tehran quartet on Hermes Records’ 2003 release. Its original bilingual booklet identifies all four musicians and the three guest musicians, gives the recording season and city, and credits producer Ramin Sadighi. The native 2024 Hermes feed on Apple backdates its edition to January 1, 2003. Because the physical sleeve says the band actually recorded it that summer, the original release is dated to the year alone. Kasra Saboktakin’s own discography links the original eight-track Spotify edition. BeepTunes supplies the corresponding Persian tracklist. The 2006 Rough Guide album names the same band and song; its exact Spotify track identifier is preserved separately. The group has one studio album, eight individual tracks, and the international compilation. The Persian transcriptions and the five individually named texts were checked against the original back cover. The booklet is published in full at [Khoobo](https://khoobo.com/barad-barad/).

The artwork is the original Hermes album cover and the official World Music Network album artwork. Current releases by the unrelated Avang pop singer Barad and his portrait do not match the band. Hermes’ own archived Barchive.htm identifies the band’s four nights at Tehran's Farabi Hall (5–8 February 2004) and embeds images/others/Band.jpg in the same table row. The Internet Archive preserves the entire label image. All four people are visually identifiable by comparison with the two original sleeve photographs that form the red design. The booklet credits Sohrab Daryabandari for its photographs. The online image has no separate photographer byline, so the published image credit names Hermes Records. The band’s guitarist, bassist, producer, guest singer Christophe Rezai, and three of its poets were reconciled with existing records. No full performance from the Farabi Hall benefit could be tied securely to the quartet. Farzad Golpayegani’s website calls it a benefit for the Bam earthquake but lists a January date before the earthquake itself, so neither that date nor an exact concert year was entered.
