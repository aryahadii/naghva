Bagheri studied electrical engineering at Guilan University, music technology in Tbilisi and sonic arts in Calgary. His original tapes and digital editions comprise Galoba and four separately listed tracks. His credited work on the two artists' albums includes engineering, mixing, mastering, production and composition. A Toronto vocalist bearing his name cofounded Chaam Trio, performs as a tenor, and was born in Kermanshah. Apple Music's vocal singles and The Confidant are associated with the Toronto musician. The available Kabin credits attached to Qhg477e supply insufficient identifying evidence to connect that record with the Calgary sound artist.
