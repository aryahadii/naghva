# Borderless Band

The 2019 concert organizer identifies Kourosh Babaei, Homayoon Nasiri and Ehsan Matoori as the original musicians and explains the cancelled tour. Matoori dates their collaboration to 2017. Diaspora Arts Connection places the July 20 Freight & Salvage concert in Berkeley. The two full-length edits on the musician’s channel have different runtimes but document the same event. The individual tracks use the titles on the issued recordings. The group photograph is a still published with the artist’s concert film.

The original musicians’ issued material, the concert films and the later Apple single credit are included. The search for earlier material and an independent review of the source and service inventory are in progress.

[Artist’s project history](https://ehsanmatoori.com/projects/borderless-band/) · [Organizer and lineup](https://www.diasporaartsconnection.org/events/2019/7/20/borderless-band-ft-mike-block-at-freight-amp-salvage) · [Concert film](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zwvkA5gCuyo)

The December 2018 fundraising page identifies Matoori as co-founder and documents the plan and the musicians’ decision to remain independent. Babaei’s own catalogue describes a 2018 album called Borderless, with no track list. It is entered under the performer whose catalogue attests it. The 2023 Canadian lineup includes Fariba Davody and guest vocalists. Babaei’s filmed Toronto-era performances are being matched across duplicate uploads. The unrelated 2026 Iranian group led by Kasra Ahadian is outside this record.

All 133 uploads on Babaei’s channel were checked for ensemble billing. Two four-minute uploads of Majnun Nabudom contain the same credits and duration and share a panel. The channel documents the Lawrence Park fundraiser, a trio performance in Durham and the Nowruz television repertory. In the published notes for Bani Adam, Babaei names both original leaders and identifies We Call It Peace as their planned first album. The three early album titles are retained as documented without equating their track lists.
