# Cymin Samawatie

Temas was recorded as ten duets in December 2018 and issued in June 2025 by the Istanbul independent label iKi Muzik. The label provides the musicians, instruments, poets, producer and studio. Her Apple Music and Spotify editions agree on the ten performances. The Spotify artist catalogue also distinguishes the two separately issued Trickster Orchestra singles and lists guest recordings on Nightingales in Berlin and Caveman. The latter has five explicitly billed pieces. Spotify credits her on Per Se by Cyminology. Group releases without individual track credits require separate research.

The portrait is from Beethovenfest Bonn’s original film of Samawatie conducting a rehearsal. Its performance report documents what her hand movements mean. Radio Farda’s interview gives the artist’s and quartet’s Persian names.
