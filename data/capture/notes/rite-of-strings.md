# Rite of Strings

The nine tracks and album were matched across the quartet’s Bandcamp edition, Apple Music, Spotify and the SoundCloud playlist embedded on the co-director’s project page. The Apple edition identifies Ehsan Matoori as phonogram owner, and Bandcamp names Sheed Records as publisher and credits both directors. The four members are shown in the group’s Bandcamp portrait. The 1990s trio with Al Di Meola, Stanley Clarke and Jean-Luc Ponty is a separate ensemble.

The band and Farhang identify the Qeshm and Dust and the Moon films. Matoori explicitly identifies the August 14 Xur film as the second single, names the same five players and credits Perez as arranger. The separate single and the August 28 album edition both appear. Bandcamp, the co-director’s ensemble page and the original Apple issue agree on August 28, 2026. His separate music listing gives August 26. The credited session dates are May 13 and 14.
