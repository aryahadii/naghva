# Farshid Arabi

The guitarist and singer leads his own Persian-language metal band. Cadence interviewed him alongside his drummer, Nima Navapour, in 2013 and revisited his film and theatre work in its video podcast. The Persian group interview about *Marge Khamoosh* includes his bassist, two lyricists and guest vocalist Ali Momenian. The portrait shows Arabi playing guitar and comes from his Spotify artist profile. [Cadence roundtable](https://khmohamad.wordpress.com/2013/07/16/interview-with-farshid-arabi-homayoun-majdzade-nima-navapour-armen-abramian-norik-misakian/) · [Band interview](https://www.sarpoosh.com/art-cinema/music/music960503945.html) · [Cadence video](https://youtu.be/6DWST5nDy1I).
