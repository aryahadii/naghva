# Nima Aghiani

The Tehran-born violinist, now in Paris, has an independent solo catalogue extending from 2013 to 2026. Petroglyph Music published the five parts of *Pacific Ocean* in 2013. The next year Hadi Bastani reviewed it in Persian for IranWire and identified its samples. Fluid Radio’s interview provides Aghiani’s own account of violin study in Tehran, Armenia and Odesa and his early orchestra and bands. The biography published by theatre collective kainkollektiv says he started at eleven, while Aghiani gives twelve in the interview. Both accounts support study in these places; the article avoids pinning down an age. His duo’s official Telegram supplies both Farsi names and the act’s preferred vocalized spelling تَرامون.

PTP, Zabte Sote, Low-Zi, Safir, Petroglyph and Karlrecords release pages cross-check his solo discography, beginning with *Pacific Ocean* and including *REMS*, *Convergence Zone*, *Kandoo*, *DEEV*, *Gharine*, the 2024 live excerpt released in 2026, plus multiple native mixes and guest credits. All 17 personal SoundCloud uploads survive and were matched to editions and other platforms. Separate SoundCloud publications include all three Migrain Sq. tracks and “False Blue” with Mez’Rab. “Jahel” is his track credit on Saeed Dehghan’s *Oryan*. The main personal Apple and Spotify profile does not include the earliest five-part album. The three component tracks of *Gharine* match the artist’s 15 November 2026 SoundCloud masters in title and duration. The two musicians are separately credited as individual performers, composers or producers where the original issues specify the role. A further recording by 9T Antiope or Taraamoon billed solely to the band has no automatic individual credit. *Harmistice*, *Grimace*, *Placebo*, *Horror Vacui* and *Bādbān* have explicit attributable roles.

The Spotify artist portrait shows Aghiani at his electronics, and SoundCloud has a separate portrait of him playing violin.
