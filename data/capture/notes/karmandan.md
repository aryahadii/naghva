# Karmandan

The founder’s biography calls this his solo music project, founded in 2010. His own 2019 anniversary video marks the tenth year of activity; the Dubai naming story dates to 2006. The BBC and Tirgan explicitly identify a four-person band and the founder describes changing local lineups. The catalogue follows the project billing. Founding names beyond Erwin were not expanded using unattributed online biographies. Tirgan’s image and BBC’s interview thumbnail show Erwin alone. The group image comes from the artist’s own Yerevan performance with Zara Gevorgyan.

Checked all four credited Apple and matching Spotify issues, the six original SoundCloud uploads and all 49 uploads to the combined founder/project channel. Original instrumental broadcasts, film and stage editions are distinguished where identified. The Apple track positions distinguish the two album versions of Engar. The channel also holds Erwin’s duo with Niaz and other separately credited work. Saman Abedi’s Lam O Be is produced by Karmandan Productions, not billed to the band as performer; the Money jam is explicitly credited to Erwin and Kasra. Both remain under their individually credited musicians.
