# Sepidar Ensemble

The 2015 founding dates come from the leader’s Labyrinth biography and the [Gulbenkian programme](https://gulbenkian.pt/musica/en/agenda/ensemble-sepidar/). The [agency](https://persianmusicheritage.org/index.php/portfolio-item/sepidar/) instead gives 2016. The group photo is April Renae’s and shows the later six-player lineup. Both Spotify profiles belong to the ensemble, one via album metadata and one via track credits. The label's original [2021 issue](https://mehrnews.com/xV6Lk) and [2024 digital sequence](https://music.apple.com/us/album/tash/1731420593) differ. The original coverage names six tracks including “Nam” and reports a duration of 67 minutes. The digital edition is 37 minutes and contains another “Saz–o Avaz” cut. The shared five names stay attached to their audible digital issue. The brief agency film supplies a public performance example. Geneva’s programme confirms an RTS recording, but availability of the full broadcast was unverified. No separate native account for the group was verified on SoundCloud, Bandcamp or Telegram. Concert listings separate the Geneva, Houdetsi, Bolzano and Maastricht programmes and the announced Lisbon date.
