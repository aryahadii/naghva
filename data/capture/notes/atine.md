# Atine

The original Atine album is distinguished from the Paris film for Farhang. Nosrat describes the Moroccan quartet and the subsequent membership of the gambist in her interview. Individual performances and songwriting have been kept apart: Rumi wrote the opening of the reed poem that Nosrat set for the Sowal Diabi project. The 2026 Common Routes CD and artist-led concert have their own credits. [Album and credits](https://atine.bandcamp.com/album/persiennes-diran) · [Farhang presenter](https://farhang.org/farhang-performances/atine-ensemble) · [Interview](https://themarkaz.org/aida-nosrat-of-atine-artist-at-work/) · [Common Routes](https://www.accords-croises.com/fr/catalogue/aida-nosrat-common-routes)
