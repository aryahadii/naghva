# Cheshmak

Tehran’s Iranian-Canadian folk and jazz trio consists of Arif Mirbaghi, Kaveh Ghaffari and Farhad Asadi. Toronto co-performer Rebecca Hennessy’s June 2025 programme identifies the band and the independent scene in Tehran. The original Koocheh festival interview shows the three players together with their interviewer at the table. Spotify displays the keyboard-in-a-car record cover as a profile image. The similarly named 1,696-video YouTube channel reports on construction in Kabul and is unrelated. The Telegram account Studio Cheshmak covers wedding videography.

The three musicians began the *Nostalgie* series in Tehran under their individual names. The original Qame Feraq issue documents three volumes and the individually credited guests. The article gives listeners the separately billed context, preserving the publisher's names. *Avalin Cheshmak* is credited to the group as well as the players. Its five original tracks and both native digital editions were reconciled. The Toronto Local Dish analogue seven-inch and the separately issued recording with Neda Mohamadpour have separate catalogue numbers and dates. The Toronto recut of “Hanuz Dar Darunam Zendast” has its own label-specific edition. *A Bruised Sunset* has ten distinct tracks, issued by Skill and Spotify. The lengthy interview for Gooshgir concludes with the band performing live, with named instrument credits. Koocheh’s separate interview was used for its portrait and documentation. Look for further concert footage via the group's public Iranian Instagram name cheshmak.mp3.
