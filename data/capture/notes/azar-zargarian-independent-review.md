# Azar Zargarian

**9.2/10. Catalogue ready and article ready.**

Independent review on 22 November 2026. The Casa della Cultura Iraniana programme confirms that Zargarian directed Ensemble Mehr, played tar, and divided its 2007 concert between Mahur and Bayat-e Tork. Its separate 2009 archive confirms her return with Shahrzad and the personal portrait shows her playing tar. The Niavaran presenter’s account supports the early teachers and years added during review. I verified four Mehr films and nine Shahrzad films in the surviving issue catalogue. The modal analysis and family connection describe her own work, and the Persian and English text retain the same timeline. Unissued pieces remain documented in the performance programmes.
