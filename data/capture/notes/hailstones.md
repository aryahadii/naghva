# Hailstones

The Calgary band grew out of Hesam Ohadi’s 2023 solo project. Festival and band biographies support different lineups, including Bo Scavvies in the earlier trio and Jill Shannon in the quartet with drummer Matt Doherty. The group’s own listing documents 2023 concerts at King Eddy, RailSpur Seattle, and Black Ice; it records dates earlier than the summary in its Bandcamp sidebar. The artist-published Bandcamp portrait shows the musicians under projected lights. [Band history](https://we-are-hailstones.com/about/) · [Group concert archive](https://we-are-hailstones.com/shows/) · [Festival’s trio biography](https://www.sledisland.com/2025/hailstones).

*The Weariness of Hope* is a six-track 2026 digital and cassette issue. The band's distributor profiles were obtained through its own website, excluding same-name projects. The 2026 festival biography dates the debut to 2025, but the band's own dated release and digital distributor identify September 2026, and a January 2026 broadcaster played “Treacherous” before the formal LP issue. The original website lists four videos: one short album teaser, two distinct Sled performances, and the October 2023 live “Cannibal Song.” The latter is separate from the November 2025 single and the 2026 album edition. Studio credits include Pat Palardy, Shahin Entezami, Wren Lovejoy and Amir B Ash.
