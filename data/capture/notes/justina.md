# Justina

The original artist channel lists 154 videos. The catalogue reconciles official audio, music videos, guest recordings and separately billed live, acoustic and karaoke versions. It excludes interviews, previews and behind-the-scenes material. Native credits on the Spotify artist's appeared-on editions identify work with EMEL, Faravaz and Basstard, Toomaj Salehi, Shayea, Milad S and Ho3ein. Her *Tatami* performance is billed on the film soundtrack. Apple lists *Chashm* with the artist’s own phonogram credit. The 2026 *Vahshi* EP contains three tracks.

Apple’s Justina profile includes recordings by other artists using that name. Cross-service matching uses the Iranian performer’s own channel and the Spotify profile she uses for this repertoire. Unconfirmed gospel, Tamil, Latin and other same-name recordings were left out. Her artist profile supplies the black-and-white portrait. Dates for digital reissues do not establish when the original performances were recorded. Madame Rap and the 2020 event biography differ on the age at which she first wrote poetry. Both identify poetry before rap, so the article does not assign an age to the first poems.
