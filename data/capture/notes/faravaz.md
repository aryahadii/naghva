# Faravaz

The Berlin festival organizer identifies Faravaz as an independent singer from Tehran. Her earlier biography describes learning guitar and studying singing. In a German radio interview she dates her move to Bavaria to 2018. Film-festival and music interviews document her film and first album. Her Spotify profile supplies the portrait.

The catalogue reconciles the artist’s Bandcamp, Apple Music and Spotify editions, including *Azadi*, *Taxi Driver*, *Butterfly* and *Saffron*, with issuer recordings under her own name and with Justina, Basstard, Indigo Dreamers and Puder. The original studio, acoustic, remix and instrumental variations of *Butterfly* retain separate records. The *Azadi* Apple edition and the artist's Bandcamp edition have different dates, which remain separately sourced. The channel biography mentions an earlier *WLF* EP. It does not provide an exact issue date or a track list.

Farhang’s Ramesh and Pouran tribute, its quarantine version of *Fatva*, the Diaspora Arts Connection concert and the 2018 *Adio Querida* festival arrangement complement the digital releases.
