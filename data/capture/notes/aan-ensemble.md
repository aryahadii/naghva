# Aan Ensemble

Original publication, nine credited players on Neshan, three independently named concert programmes and the film programme of the artist channel were compared. The five winter solo/duo uploads name only the featured musician and are catalogued against those players. Three behind-the-scenes films from the group are kept separate from the official concert selections. Changes of guest oud and voice were confirmed against the contemporary interview.

Checked native Iranian ensemble recordings, publisher and concert documents, Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube, Bandcamp, SoundCloud and Telegram. Rokh and protest masters reuse their previously issued identifiers. The portraits are originals credited to and individually captioned by the quartet. The stage image for Aan comes from its official Tehran performance.
